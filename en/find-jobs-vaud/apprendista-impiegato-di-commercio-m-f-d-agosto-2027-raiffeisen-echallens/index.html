Full description

28.09.2026 Echallens

Be part of our team!

Do you attach importance to solid basic training? You're a friendly and enthusiastic person, you have good manners and you're interested in the banking world? Then you may soon join our team!

What are you waiting for?

During your three years of training, you will discover different services and become familiar with the concrete daily life of the Banque Raiffeisen du Gros-de-Vaud. We offer training including a varied and dynamic practical work, complemented by comprehensive theoretical courses with the CYP (Centre of competence for inter-company courses). Our apprentices are constantly supervised by our attentive and experienced trainers. Discover a unique culture that combines entrepreneurial commitment, equity and personal life projects and then choose an employer that offers extremely modern conditions of engagement, particularly varied missions and a wide margin of manoeuvre.

What are you bringing?

  • successfully completed secondary school;
  • good or excellent academic performance;
  • interest and enthusiasm in banking and customer contact;
  • willingness to acquire professional and social knowledge;
  • autonomy and ability to work as a team,
  • home in the area.

A fantastic opportunity to start in active life! Sabina Sciulli, Head of Succession, Echallens Sabina Sciulli Head of succession, Echallens Why Raiffeisen?

We work in a friendly environment

Co-operative values are our pillars. Together. For each other. They shape how we work together and behave toward each other. The culture of mentoring promotes personal proximity and exchanges on an equal footing at all levels of function.

We live in diversity and equal opportunities

We encourage diversity of perspectives, experiences and skills within our teams. This diversity makes us rich and makes a decisive contribution to our success.

We encourage personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is an integral part of our culture. We create a space for the professional and personal development of each individual and support our employees in a targeted manner, through a wide range of in-house training and participation in external development costs.

We offer attractive social conditions and benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages over banking products, various discounts from third parties, as well as above average financial stability and old age insurance.
