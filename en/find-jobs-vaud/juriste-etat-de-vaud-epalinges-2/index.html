This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

As a lawyer at the Office de la consommation (OFCO), at an activity rate of 80 to 100% and as part of an indeterminate appointment, you provide legal expertise to support management, managers and business teams. You ensure the independent processing of complex cases, accompany administrative and litigation procedures, participate in strategic projects and contribute to the legal certainty of the Office's activities. This requires strong experience, an excellent sense of organization and the ability to manage multiple files and priorities simultaneously. With very good relational skills, you work closely with the Office's specialists and are able to quickly acquire expertise in the specific areas covered by its tasks, including water distribution, foodstuffs, usual objects and chemicals. You thus contribute to the sustainable strengthening of the legal function and the integration of legal issues into the activities and decision-making processes of OFCO

  • Train files and prepare administrative decisions and procedural documents related to OFCO
  • Provide legal, political and strategic support to the management of the Office and the legal entity of the Service in the respective areas of activity
  • Participate in the legislative and regulatory review process
  • Ensure all legal tasks on behalf of the legal entity of the service
  • Master's degree in law or equivalent training
  • Professional experience as a lawyer, if possible with strategic, policy and project management elements
  • High interest in food
  • Legal knowledge and interest in local economic reality
  • Decision-making and determination
  • Self-help and collaboration
  • Organizational sense and capacity for synthesis
  • Reactivity and proactivity For further information, please consult the Terms of Reference by clicking HERE or contact Ms. Fanny da Silva, HR Advisor at telephone number 021 338 47 86.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
