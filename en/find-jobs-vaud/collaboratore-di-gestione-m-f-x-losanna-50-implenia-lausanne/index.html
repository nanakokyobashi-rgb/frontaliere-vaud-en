Full description

For our site in Lausanne, tenants do not only feel at home – thanks to our new division of labour within the team, they also benefit from optimal and efficient support. It has advantages not only for tenants and owners, but also for you. Thanks to increased specialization within the team, we collaborate more closely and each can focus on its strengths.

If you have the passion for real estate, you are motivated, you are oriented towards goals and you appreciate the teamwork, then we look forward to receiving your application for the post of

Collaborator of Management (M/F/X) Lausanne, 50%

To which you contribute

  • You are responsible for the administrative support of the team on the site to ensure the smooth running of operations
  • As the first point of contact on site, you provide customer reception and manage the first contact with tenants
  • You are in charge of communicating with tenants, concierges, artisans and authorities to ensure an effective exchange of information
  • You are responsible for the completion of the exit accounts and the release of the deposits, as well as the demand and evaluation of the offers, the allocation of the mandates to the craftsmen and the monitoring of the works
  • Processing and re-billing of supplier orders and invoices and monitoring of debtors are also part of your responsibilities.

What you bring

  • You have a basic business degree and you have ideally already completed a real estate manager's training — or you are ready to do so soon.
  • A first experience in real estate or a similar domain, is an asset you have
  • Working as a team motivates you, you show stress resistance and your sense of service and customer orientation distinguish you
  • You know how to organize your work independently and oriented towards objectives. Thanks to your ease of communication, you interact easily with different people
  • You speak fluently in French, both oral and written, and you have good notions of German

Some of our benefits include

  • Working time: we live mobile work with individual (annual) hours of work and/or part-time work. Home Office possible up to 40%. Work-Life-Balance is crucial to us, because balanced and relaxed collaborators are important to us. Additional holiday purchase is possible
  • Allocation: you receive a contribution for the mobile phone. We also contribute to your meals
  • Training and development: encouraging employees is an important part of our culture. Benefit from generous support for internal and external training and development. We offer career planning opportunities in leadership, specialists and projects
  • Salary: We offer a market-compliant remuneration with absolute equal pay for all genders. Pension benefits are attractive and you can choose from different models. Accident protection insurance covers private division in hospital
