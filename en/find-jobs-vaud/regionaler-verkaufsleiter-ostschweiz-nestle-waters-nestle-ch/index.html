Full description

Location: Operation Area Eastern Switzerland Work model: field service / full-time Contract type: Perpetual

Be a force for good and accelerate change that improves lives and the planet.

As Regional Sales Manager – Eastern Switzerland at Nestlé Waters, your mission is to drive the further development of the out‐of‐home business in catering, hospitality, community catering, leisure businesses, events and regional trading partners. You make a difference by leading and developing a regional sales team, effectively anchoring the commercial strategy and bringing regional market knowledge and customer needs into the national out-of-home strategy. This role directly contributes to ensuring sustainable growth, profitability and a strong brand presence in the Eastern Switzerland region. This task offers you the opportunity to actively shape the future of drinks – and to create experiences that enrich the everyday life of many people.

How you work as Regional Sales Manager – Eastern Switzerland

  • Managing a regional sales team of 8-10 employees, including coaching, performance orientation, talent development and creating a motivating work environment.
  • Implementation of the national sales strategy into concrete regional measures, combined with objectives, prioritization, planning and control of all sales activities.
  • Introducing in-depth regional market knowledge, customer perspectives and competition observations to further develop the out-of-home strategy and optimally exploit local opportunities.
  • Monitoring sales, volume, margins and other key performance indicators as well as deriving effective measures to ensure sustainable and profitable growth.
  • Expansion of long-term, profitable relationships with customers, partners and sales outlets as well as identification, acquisition and development of strategically important new customers in the OOH environment.
  • Introduction of new products and initiatives with partners and sales outlets as well as close cooperation with marketing, supply chain, finance and key account management to strengthen regional market implementation.

What makes you successful

  • Experience in sales or key account management as well as a strong sense of market and customer needs, ideally in the beverage or catering industry.
  • Experience in the beverage, gastronomy or out-of-home environment and knowledge of its dynamics.
  • Experience in the management and development of employees, combined with the ability to create a performance-oriented, motivational environment and to promote talent in a targeted manner.
  • Negotiation and communication skills, as well as the ability to convince different stakeholders, combined with entrepreneurial thinking and a secure understanding of sales, margins and profitability.
  • Application of a structured, analytical and data-based way of working, supported by CRM, reporting tools and SAP.
  • communication strength in German (flowing) and French (good knowledge); English is an advantage.
  • Embodiment of our values: Care, Grit, Connection and Ingenuity.
