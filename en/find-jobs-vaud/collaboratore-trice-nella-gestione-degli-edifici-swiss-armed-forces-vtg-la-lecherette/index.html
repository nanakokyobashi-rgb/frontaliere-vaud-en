Put to the Point

They will be responsible for the inspection and maintenance of the La Lécherette buildings. In this role, you help ensure that the infrastructures can be used under the best possible conditions. Employing the Swiss army makes sense and brings many advantages. See our employment conditions – the advantages at a glance.

  • Entry date: by agreement
  • Type of employment: perpetual
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-21120
  • Location: Route de l'Hongrin 20, 1660 La Lécherette

You can make this contribution

  • Manage and monitor heating, ventilation and sanitation facilities
  • Carry out maintenance, cleaning, home maintenance and disinfection work in buildings
  • Premises intended for the force to be prepared, in particular in the framework of the transfer and withdrawal of infrastructure and equipment
  • Ensure that technical inspections are carried out in accordance with established maintenance plans
  • Perform snow clearance work
  • Ensure waste management

This makes them unique.

  • Federal certificate of competence as a specialist in maintenance or completed vocational training in engineering, construction or building maintenance
  • Category B driving licence (category C would be an additional advantage)
  • Motivated, responsible, independent and flexible person
  • Team spirit, enjoyment of working with the users of buildings and facilities, organized and conscientious working methods
  • Basic computer science knowledge (MS Office)

Your commitment to security and freedom The logistics base of the Swiss Army (LBA) is there for the force. It provides all logistical services for the Swiss army and manages the material, systems and infrastructure of the Swiss army with its approximately 3,500 employees and 12,000 militia members. Whether in the workshop or in the office, inside or outside: The LBA offers a wide range of workplaces throughout Switzerland. We have something for everyone.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.

Women are still underrepresented in our administrative unit. Your applications are therefore particularly welcome.
