Full description

Animator sociocultural/the 70-80%, 70%-80% The Département Plein Soleil de l'Institution de Lavigny, located in Lausanne, is dedicated to people with progressive neurological diseases or brain damage, and includes a 70-bed EMS, a 10-seat CAT, 12 sheltered accommodation and an outpatient therapy centre. It is a unique place of life and care in the Canton of Vaud, which offers personalized accompaniment, with care services and therapies, educational and socio-cultural activities, workshops for personal development and well-being, spiritual support and a service dedicated to the relatives of the people welcomed. Do you want to put your creativity and skills at the service of projects that make sense? Do you appreciate the field work, the meeting, the group dynamics and want to contribute actively to the quality of life and the social participation of the accompanied people? For our sociocultural service, Animation sector, we are looking for:

Social and Cultural Facilitator/70-80%

In a multidisciplinary team, you help develop a dynamic, inclusive and sociocultural offer tailored to the needs, needs and life projects of the beneficiaries. Your missions

  • Design, organize and conduct individual and collective socio-cultural activities;
  • Develop new activities that promote the expression, choice, autonomy and participation of beneficiaries;
  • Organize and accompany outings, events and projects that promote openness on the outside;
  • Participate in institutional events and projects;
  • Work with internal and external professionals and partners;
  • Participate in the development of sociocultural offer and follow up on projects for which you have responsibility.

Your profile

  • ES or HES training in sociocultural animation, social, health or training deemed equivalent;
  • desired experience with persons with disabilities and/or in the medical and social field;
  • Creativity, strength of proposal and ability to conduct a project from its conception to its realization;
  • Autonomy, organizational sense, adaptability and team spirit;
  • A strong interest in interdisciplinary work;
  • Availability for variable hours, including occasional evenings, weekends and public holidays;
  • Driver's licence category B essential.

We offer

  • A stimulating working environment within a unique place of life and care;
  • A varied activity giving real room to creativity, initiative and project development;
  • An interdisciplinary environment conducive to exchange and sharing skills;
  • Opportunities for internal and external training;
  • Working conditions according to the CLC of the parapublic health sector Vaudois;
  • Social benefits of a large institution: parking, restaurant and public transport on the site.
