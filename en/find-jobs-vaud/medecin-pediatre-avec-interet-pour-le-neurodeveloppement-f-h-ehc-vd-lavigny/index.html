Full description

Located in the charming municipality of Lavigny, in the heart of the canton of Vaud, the Pietro Sarto Medical Centre aims to improve the care and follow-up of children with neurological disorders, development and behaviour. By working in synergy with various healthcare professionals, we provide comprehensive and personalized care. We are looking for a: Pediatric doctor with interest in neurodevelopment (F/H) at 50-100% YOUR OBJECTIVES

  • Provide comprehensive neurological care for children and adolescents: assessments, diagnostics, therapeutic plans, longitudinal monitoring and network coordination.
  • Actively participate in the development of the specialized centre within our medical centres.
  • Promote the collegial and integrated functioning of the structure, working closely with other specialists and professionals of the centre.
  • To contribute to the continuous improvement of clinical practices and, as appropriate, to internal training or awareness-raising activities with partner teams. YOU MAY
  • A federal title of specialist in paediatrics, or equivalent recognized by MEBEKO, with additional training or interest in neuropediatry, child development, or experience confirmed in neurodevelopmental follow-up (TSA, ADHD, learning disorders, overall developmental delay, prematurity, etc.).
  • A proven competence in standardized clinical evaluation, coordination of interdisciplinary reviews (logopedia, occupational therapy, psychotherapy, psychomotority...).
  • A capacity to work as a multidisciplinary team and collaborate with families, school institutions and other stakeholders in the network.
  • A spirit of initiative, organization and real sensitivity to the special needs of children and adolescents.
  • A strong interest in the continuous updating of knowledge in the field of neurology, as well as enthusiasm for contributing to the dissemination and teaching of these good practices. WE OFFER YOU
  • A team of highly qualified trainers, teachers and doctors, providing high-quality coaching and professional development.
  • The reputation of the hospital and its doctors, providing a valuable and recognized work environment.
  • 5 days per year of "training leave" and 1'000 CHF of training costs reimbursed over 12 months of activity.
  • More than 20 recognized "training centre" disciplines.
  • 5 weeks of annual holidays, 6 weeks from the age of 50. Possibility of redeeming an additional week of vacation, as well as 10 holidays offered.
  • Several proposals providing flexibility (child care, unpaid leave, part-time, paid exchange time, etc.).
  • Sports activities (Pilates, walking races, discounts at various centres such as several fitness centres or Bains de Lavey), events for the children of collaborators, a company drink to share moments of conviviality during the beautiful days.
  • Remuneration based on the valuation of your previous years of professional experience as well as seniority bonuses within the EHC.
  • Internal transfer facilitated according to your desire for development. WE ARE
  • An institution comprising acute care and rehabilitation facilities, a private clinic, EMS, medical centres and permanences in the region of La Côte as well as the first clinical school of hypnosis dedicated to the care community in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A network of care allowing many opportunities for internal change according to the aspirations and development projects of employees.
  • More than 2,000 employees taking care of patients and residents of the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment conducive to the realization of projects.
  • A training company with approximately 70 apprentices in 13 different trades, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training. BE THE NEXT TALENT OF OUR CARE NETWORK.
