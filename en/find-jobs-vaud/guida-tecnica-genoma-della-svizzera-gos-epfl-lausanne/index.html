Role overview

Mission

The Technical Lead / Genomic Data Engineer is responsible for the design, implementation, operation and continuous improvement of the technical infrastructure supporting the Genome of Switzerland programme.

The position ensures the efficient, secure and scalable processing, management and delivery of genomic data and analytical resources.

Company and context

  • Ensure the secure ingestion, storage, organization, traceability and delivery of genomic datasets and associated metadata.
  • Oversee quality control procedures throughout the data generation and processing workflow.
  • Contribute to the automation, reproducibility and scalability of analytical processes and computational infrastructures.
  • Coordinate interfaces between sequencing platforms, analytical environments, data repositories and data-sharing infrastructures.
  • Support the integration, harmonization and preparation of genomic datasets for national and international initiatives, including Genome of Europe and controlled-access repositories.
  • Contribute to the implementation of data governance, security, access-control and compliance procedures.
  • Maintain technical documentation, workflow specifications and operational procedures.
  • Provide technical expertise and support to project scientists, bioinformaticians and collaborating institutions.
  • Evaluate, deploy and maintain emerging technologies, software tools and computational infrastructures relevant to large-scale genomics.
