Project Manager - Automation

Your Mission

Within our "Rail Projects" entity, you are piloting projects to renew and modernize the automation and safety facilities of our rail systems.

You guarantee control of the objectives, from the definition of requirements to the commissioning, you follow the control of costs, time, risks and quality, while ensuring compliance with Swiss railway requirements (OCF, DE-OCF).

You coordinate studies, suppliers and different partners, supervise the testing and integration of new systems and ensure their full compatibility with existing and future system components (rolling equipment and infrastructure). In close collaboration with engineering, operations and maintenance teams, you contribute to the performance, safety and evolution of our railway installations.

The function requires strong knowledge in railway safety installations including automation, signalling, interlocking, level crossings.

Your profile

  • Diploma in Engineering (HES, EPF) in Automation, Electrotechnical, Electronics, Industrial Computer Science or an equivalent discipline
  • Project Management Certification (IPMA, PMI or equivalent) is an asset
  • Professional experience of more than 5 years in conducting complex projects ideally in the field of metro rail or critical industrial systems
  • You know about public procurement, tendering procedures and contract management of projects
  • Autonomous and structured you are able to analyze complex issues, make recommendations and support project decisions
  • Your relational ease, negotiating capacity and your sense of communication allow you to unite people with diverse interests
