Head of general project - Direct line Neuchâtel-La Chaux-de-Fonds 60-100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Head of general project - Direct line Neuchâtel-La Chaux-de-Fonds Renens (VD) / Work Smart, 01.01.2027 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

Make a difference on a new major railway line (investment > CHF 1 billion) in the heart of a region with exciting and strategic projects. At the SBB, we continuously upgrade the infrastructure to ensure fluid connections, point trains and welcoming stations. Following the completion of the preliminary project, we are currently strengthening our team to take a key step: the preparation of the investigation file. Why would you like this job? You will contribute to reliable, safe and sustainable mobility for an entire region while influencing national traffic. This is a flagship, technically and organizationally ambitious project. - with challenges such as 5% without racks, more than 30 km of tunnels and integration of ETCS Level 2 - offering a rare scale. You will also evolve in a stimulating environment, within a multidisciplinary team surrounded by advanced railway technologies. What you can move.

  • You join a growing team and support the Program Director to achieve ambitious quality, cost and timeliness goals while ensuring safety.
  • On a daily basis, you keep an overall vision of the project, but also pilot technical works and lots (tunnel, track, catenary, ETCS signage, etc.) by ensuring that the nuisances for customers and residents are limited.
  • In charge of the coordination and management of internal agents and specialists, you ensure the quality of deliverables and compliance with deadlines.
  • You prepare and structure the technical records required for the granting of building permits by ensuring their regulatory and administrative compliance.
  • You define the procurement strategy and conduct major tenders under the Public Procurement Act (PMO/LMP).
  • You identify and propose pragmatic and achievable solutions compatible with deadlines and budget envelopes.

What you bring.

  • With a strong sense of responsibility and team spirit, you make thoughtful and factual decisions.
  • You also have excellent coordination and communication capabilities with various stakeholders.
  • Thanks to your experience, you have gained experience in managing stakeholders in multi-stakeholder environments. You have a good ability to align different interests.
  • You have at least 5 years of experience in managing complex projects, experience in the railway and/or underground (tunnel) field would be an important asset.
  • You have a higher education, university or HES/EPF in a technical field (e.g. civil engineering) or equivalent training.
  • You have mastered French (minimum C1 level); an intermediate level in German (des B1) represents an advantage.
