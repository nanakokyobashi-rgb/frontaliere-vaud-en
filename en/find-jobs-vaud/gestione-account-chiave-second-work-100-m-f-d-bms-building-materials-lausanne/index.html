Full description

BMS Building Materials Switzerland is a wholesaler of construction materials for the large and second work. Our group of companies includes renowned specialists such as Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz-Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced specialists advise, support and inspire construction customers and private customers with a great deal of commitment on 110 sites throughout Switzerland. Close to the daily construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Fast, simple and competent.

In a constantly changing environment, we are looking for a Key Account Manager capable of embodying our business ambition and actively contributing to the development of our business Second Work for the Lausanne and La-Côte region.

This position is intended for a field personality with a strong business sense, an excellent understanding of market issues and a real ability to develop lasting relationships with key stakeholders.

Missions

  • Deploy Business Strategy Second Work on Your Region
  • Develop sales in the areas of health , building technology and floor and wall coverings
  • Identifying and implementing new business opportunities
  • Develop a strategic client portfolio while strengthening existing relationships
  • Steering your revenue and margin targets
  • Support sales teams in the field and contribute to regional dynamics
  • Working closely with the various internal players: business managers, shops, halls, exhibitions, supplies and transport
  • Actively participate in trade developments in your region
  • Administrative monitoring of client, credit and litigation files

Your Profile

You are a soul hunter, oriented results, able to create opportunities and turn your network into a business.

You bring in particular

  • A solid experience in a similar business function, ideally in the construction or second job sector
  • Basic technical or commercial training related to construction
  • A proven experience in management or support of sales teams
  • An excellent knowledge of the economic fabric of the Lausanne region
  • A strong commercial temperament, with real skills in negotiation, influence and network development
  • Leadership, energy and strong field orientation
  • An entrepreneurial spirit and a real ability to move the lines
  • A good mastery of the usual IT tools, knowledge of an ERP such as Navision being an asset
  • The knowledge of German and/or English is a more
  • Collaborate closely with the various internal actors: business managers, shops, halls,

We offer

  • A well-structured network of craft centres and close collaboration with the sales department.
  • A function vehicle with petrol card - can also be used privately

  • 5 weeks of holiday (6 weeks from 50 years old) with the possibility of purchasing an additional holiday up to 10 days a year, additional benefits such as an independent and well-funded pension fund, as well as discounts on our entire product range and additional external benefits.
