Full description

The Directorate of Architecture and Buildings (DAB) is responsible for the planning, programming, development, construction and maintenance of all buildings in Vaud State. As a project assistant, you support domain managers and project managers in the administrative monitoring of construction projects and contribute to the smooth administrative functioning of the team.

  • Search, select and collate the elements necessary for the preparation of invitations to tender, prepare and monitor internal files
  • Manage administrative follow-up of construction cases and ensure administrative maintenance of project schedules
  • Monitor and process contract proposals, work orders and invoices, while ensuring their management in the database
  • Verify document compliance in accordance with internal requirements and directives for delegation of signing authority
  • Inform the interlocutors about the procedures and monitoring of construction cases and ensure liaison between the various internal and external actors
  • To provide administrative support to project managers and domain managers, including the organization and preparation of meetings, the rereading of documents, the management of e-mails and telephone calls, the filing and archiving of files, and the updating of various lists and documents. For more information, you can consult the specifications here or contact Mr Claudio Iglesias, Director DAB at 021 316 73 21.
  • Bachelor or equivalent training in a field related to construction
  • Further training in project management
  • Work experience of at least 5 years in construction and at least 10 years in complex case management and project monitoring
  • Mastering computer and office tools, organizational and planning skills, excellent editorial skills.
  • Autonomy, sense of priorities, relationship ease and interest in public service.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
