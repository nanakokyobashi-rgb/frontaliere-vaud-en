Full description

Mission At Decathlon, sport is at the heart of our daily lives, and every member of the team can invest and grow. Here, team spirit is essential: it is by playing together that we achieve our goals together. Join a company where it is good to live, and integrate a motivated and united team. Come and express your initiative, take responsibility and help us make sport accessible to all. Your playground: The Federal Workshop is responsible for repairing and repairing Decathlon customers' products for all Decathlon subsidiaries in Switzerland (e-commerce site, warehouse and 40th stores). Created in 2022, the Federal Workshop consists of a team of 20 people working daily to develop the country's circular hub strategy. To complete the current team, we are looking for a 100% personalization technician. The site is currently based in Onnens (VD) but will be relocated to Niederwangen (BE) from March 2027. Your missions:

  • Pre-printing & Graphic Design: Verification and vectorization of logos, preparation of files and creation of BAT for validation.
  • Production & Floating: Adjusting machines, cutting, stowing and placing on hot press (temperature/pressure adjustment depending on the material).
  • Control & Quality: Checking the final rendering (centering, colors, marking holding) before shipping.
  • Maintenance & Stock: First-level maintenance of equipment (cleaning, settings) and monitoring of consumable stock.
  • Customer order management: Timeliness monitoring and compliance with Load Taking (PEC). Profile Your profile:
  • You have equivalent professional experience in the area of personalization and/or graphics.
  • You have a true artistic and graphic sensitivity, you will be able to accompany and advise a B2B clientele with professionalism.
  • You are autonomous and able to work on a variety of tasks.
  • Your sense of detail is irreproachable.
  • You are rigorous and organized.
  • You're passionate about sport, company DNA!
