Full description

The Directorate-General for Territorial Affairs and Housing (DGTL) is responsible for spatial planning ( cantonal master plan, planning and allocation, agglomeration project), cadastre and geoinformation ( cantonal geoportail), permits for building permits and housing policy at cantonal level. Join a team that is united, collaborative and passionate about housing policy. You will evolve in a dynamic working environment with modern and bright offices.

  • Support for work related to cantonal housing policy
  • Support for the implementation of the Housing Vision, in collaboration with the Head of Mission
  • Development and structuring of statistical monitoring of housing policy
  • Cross-cutting support For further information, please contact Sarah Meyer, Director, Housing Directorate, at sarah.meyer2@vd.ch
  • University or HES training, completed or recently completed, in a relevant field: political science, public administration, economics, law or training deemed equivalent
  • Aisance with numbers
  • Professional experience in administrative records management
  • Excellent knowledge of computer tools word, excel, powerpoint
  • Autonomy, professional awareness, discretion, sense of organization, spirit of self-help and collaboration Please submit a complete file (CV, cover letter, work certificates, diplomas relevant to the function)
