Full description

For Raiffeisen Bank Nyon-La Vallée, a firm firmly anchored in the region and growing, we are looking for a Mortgage Finance Assistant as part of a future retirement.

Aufgaben

  • Private Client Advisor Support for the Processing of Funding Applications
  • Processing of financing requests for clients' wealth management
  • External coordination with clients and notaries in particular
  • Internal coordination in particular with advisors, credit analysts and credit administration
  • Visit of real estate objects
  • Participation in complex interviews
  • Mortgage Portfolio Management Support (renewal, file update)

Anforderungen

  • Banking CFC, Bachelor of Finance or equivalent.
  • Experience confirmed of minimum 3 years in the area of mortgage financing or real estate credit.
  • Perfect mastery of regulatory standards and local real estate market.
  • Excellent presentation and ease of contact with customers thanks to your natural interpersonal skills, listening and empathy
  • Good management of stress, delays and priorities
  • Demonstrated interest in teamwork while cultivating an entrepreneurial spirit
  • Very good organisational capacity to ensure high quality standards
  • Mother tongue French, English is an asset

Co-operative values are our pillars. Together. For each other. They shape how we work together and behave toward each other. The culture of mentoring promotes personal proximity and exchanges on an equal footing at all levels of function.
