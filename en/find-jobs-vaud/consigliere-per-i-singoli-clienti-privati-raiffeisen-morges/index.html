Be part of our team!

La Banque Raiffeisen Morges Venoge - booming bank and well established in the region - research in order to strengthen its private customer advisory department, an individual private customer advisor to join its Morges agency, with a weekly presence at the La Sarraz agency.

What are you waiting for?

  • Overall advice to private clients and more specifically in the area of mortgage financing
  • Advice to private clients on insurance, investment, insurance and taxation
  • Analysis and exploitation of customer potential through proactive and continuous portfolio monitoring
  • Customer development through targeted acquisition
  • Representation tasks in the area of activity
  • Preparation of funding files.

What are you bringing?

  • Strong experience in the field of pension, investment and life insurance
  • Higher training of type HES or equivalent, supplemented by specific counselling modules
  • 3 years of experience at a similar post in a Swiss bank are essential
  • Good knowledge of the economic fabric of the region
  • Excellent relational skills and sense of communication
  • Business activity and stated customer orientation
  • Dynamic, organized, autonomous, able to take initiatives while integrating into a team.

If you are interested in providing quality advice and human relations, don't hesitate and join us! Stéphane Guisolan Customer consulting team manager Why Raiffeisen?
