# IT Technician Support

He tuo futuro inizia who

For one of our international customers based in Vevey, we are looking for an IT Support Technician who is passionate about technology and user service. Within a dynamic team, you provide IT support for outlets throughout Switzerland. You intervene autonomously in the field to ensure the availability of IT equipment and the quality of service offered to users.

  • Provide local computer support to users
  • Diagnosis and resolve hardware and software incidents
  • Install, configure and replace IT equipment
  • Follow-up of requests and communication with users
  • Guarantee a quality user experience thanks to your sense of service and your approach oriented solutions
  • Self-intervening in different outlets across Switzerland
  • Various position combining user support and technical interventions in the field with regular trips throughout Switzerland
  • A great autonomy in organising your interventions in a dynamic environment with direct contact with users

Your profile

  • Information technology CFC or equivalent
  • Experience in community computer support appreciated
  • Ability to analyse problems and find pragmatic solutions
  • Excellent relationship and customer orientation
  • Initiative, autonomy and responsibility
  • Current French and good English knowledge
  • German is a real asset
  • Compulsory driving licence (Opportunity to benefit from a function vehicle)

We offer the possibility to work in one of our offices in Switzerland or at your home. You will use agile working methods and the latest technologies. We offer flexible working hours tailored to your personal needs. As a Swisscom employee, you have attractive advantages that will enrich your professional experience.
