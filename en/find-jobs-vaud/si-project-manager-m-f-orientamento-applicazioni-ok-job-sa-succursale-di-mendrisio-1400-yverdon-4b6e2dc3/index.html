SI project manager, (M/F) applications orientation back to list In order to strengthen our teams, we are looking for an SI Project Manager, (M/F) application orientation. Job description

Your mission

  • Pilot IT projects with a dominant application, from the scoping phase to the production stage, ensuring that objectives, deadlines, budgets and expected quality are respected
  • Collect, analyse and formalise the needs of the various departments in order to propose efficient digital solutions adapted to the company's challenges
  • Ensure the interface between users, technical teams and external partners to ensure project success and smooth communication between all stakeholders
  • Coordinate software suppliers, integrators and publishers, monitor the progress of services and ensure the proper integration of applications into the existing IT ecosystem
  • Organize testing, validation and deployment phases of solutions, while supporting users in leading change and adopting new tools
  • Actively participate in the evolution of the information system by contributing to the optimization of the application portfolio, the continuous improvement of processes and the modernization of working methods
  • Play a key role in the digital transformation of the company by providing a career-oriented vision, performance and user satisfaction

Profile

Profile desired

  • HES Bachelor in Management Information Systems, Information Systems, Computers or Training deemed equivalent
  • Further training in project management (HERMES, IPMA, PMI, PRINCE2 or equivalent)
  • Minimum 5 years experience in piloting IT projects oriented to business applications
  • Strong experience in needs analysis, drafting specifications and functional specifications, and managing the testing and revenue phases
  • Very good knowledge of ERP environments, business software, application interfaces and data governance
  • Confirmed experience in coordinating IT providers, suppliers, software publishers or integrators
  • Excellent mastery of project management methods, with strong ability to prioritize, plan and follow several projects simultaneously
  • Ability to communicate with technical and trade partners, to federate teams and to create ownership around projects
  • Analytical spirit, organizational sense and ability to transform complex operational needs into pragmatic solutions
  • Independent, proactive and results-oriented personality with an excellent sense of service and a real ability to accompany change
  • Experience in public transport, infrastructure or multisite services is an important asset
