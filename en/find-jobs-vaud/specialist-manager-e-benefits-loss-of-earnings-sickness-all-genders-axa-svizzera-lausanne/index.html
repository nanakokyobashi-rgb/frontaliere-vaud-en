What's waiting for you

  • As the main interlocutor for our clients, you place their satisfaction at the heart of your work. You respond in a personalized way to their needs and keep them regularly informed by telephone of decisions regarding their files
  • You take care of complex daily allowance files in case of loss of earnings due to illness. You make the appropriate strategic decisions and proactively manage these files
  • You accompany insured and insured persons in a situation of illness throughout their recovery journey, mobilizing the necessary specialists and partners
  • Your curiosity and innovation spirit allow you to actively contribute to improving your working environment

What you bring

  • You have commercial or equivalent training (preferably in the insurance sector)
  • Experience in the area of daily sickness benefits and/or medical knowledge would be beneficial
  • With French mother tongue (or equivalent), you ideally have excellent knowledge of German (B2)
  • You are energetic, have a positive spirit and good communication skills
  • Independent personality, you know how to make decisions with an entrepreneurial spirit and rigorous working methods
  • You have the ability to work efficiently in dynamic environments and keep calm in situations of high loads
