Full description

For our team from the Technical Bureau within the Research & Development department, we are looking for our future intern for a period of 6 months, starting in February 2027. During your internship, your main mission will be to support our Watch Development team to manage an innovative or improvement project.

In this context, you will be responsible for

  • Create and develop a project based on a specification including the creation of the concept, the various ideas for development, the design and monitoring of the project's supplies
  • Present your work to the entire office and management
  • Participate in the technical bureau's recurring activity: data entry, digital archiving of plans and property intelligence in our technical database
  • Perform various administrative work in Excel
  • You hold or are in the process of obtaining a university degree from an engineering school in microtechnics or mechanics
  • You are interested in joining the technical office world
  • Excellent communicator, you have good relational skills and you perfectly master French in both oral and written language
  • Proactive and organized, you are recognized for your analytical spirit and attention to detail.

  • The ability to work remotely depending on the function
  • A business restaurant
