Join an organization in the midst of transformation La Vaudoise leads many transformation initiatives in a rich and varied technological environment. In order to support the evolution of our tools, processes and working methods, we are looking for a Senior Project Leader capable of piloting complex projects with a strong IT component, closely linked to the trades, and effectively mobilizing stakeholders. Within the Delivery Transverse Division, you will join a team of project leaders and program managers, contributing to strategic initiatives with high impact for the company. Your Mission You are piloting strategic projects with a strong end-to-end IT component, from launch to production and hypercare. You ensure that the objectives of quality, cost, timeliness and business value are met. As such, you will be required to:

  • Lead complex projects, from scoping to hypercare, within defined objectives.
  • Structure and drive project governance by facilitating decision-making.
  • Coordinate actors with diverse profiles and unite stakeholders at all levels of the organization.
  • Ensure timeliness, cost, risk and quality of deliverables.
  • Link business needs to technical solutions, understand system interactions and anticipate impacts and dependencies.
  • Ensure that the architectural, security and data management aspects of projects are taken into account.
  • Adopt a product-oriented approach when context requires it, contributing to priority setting and backlog management.
  • Plan and coordinate project team activities.
  • Prepare quality submissions and files for governance and decision-making bodies.
  • Produce clear and relevant reporting through appropriate steering indicators.
