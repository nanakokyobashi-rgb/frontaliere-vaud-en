Company description

Located on the shores of Lake Geneva and overlooking the magnificent view of the Alps, the Fairmont Le Montreux Palace is a distinguished hotel establishment, a member of the Accor group and the Fairmont brand, and affiliated with Swiss Deluxe Hotels.

Boasting an unparalleled worldwide reputation, 236 luxurious rooms and suites, and one of the most refined culinary experiences, Fairmont Le Montreux Palace is truly an enchanting place.

Its six restaurants and bars offer a wide choice of delights that will satisfy the most discerning palates.

Le Montreux Jazz Café is the must-visit place to experience the Festival 365 days a year in the magical setting of Fairmont le Montreux Palace, MP’s Bar & Grill is an upscale restaurant with a relaxed atmosphere specializing in grilled dishes that has no equal in the region, and Funky Claude’s Bar is the undisputed place to end up late into the night to enjoy an incredible line-up of traditional and exclusive cocktails to the rhythm of the best live scene in town.

Fairmont Le Montreux Palace also specializes in hosting conferences, meetings and seminars.

The Fairmont Spa is a 2000 m² wellness space dedicated to fitness, relaxation and beauty.

Job description

At Fairmont Le Montreux Palace, our kitchen team creates unforgettable dinners through flavorful cuisine and flawless organization. As a kitchen assistant, you will play a key role in preparing dishes as well as at the staff cafeteria, where you will be a key point of contact, in direct contact with all hotel employees.

Your attention to detail and commitment will contribute to preparing dishes, while you provide a warm welcome and quality service. You will thus have the unique opportunity to interact daily with all employees, contributing to the success of our establishment.

Together, let’s transform every moment into a memorable culinary experience for everyone.

Summary of responsibilities

Reporting to the Executive Sous-Chef, the responsibilities and essential functions of the position include, but are not limited to:

General functions

  • Provide professional, welcoming and attentive service while assisting colleagues
  • Know and apply the LQA technical and emotional standards
  • Comply with the department’s policies, procedures and service standards
  • Comply with hygiene rules and apply HACCP standards
  • Comply with ergonomic work standards in accordance with Health & Safety requirements
  • Be able to manage your work independently, always under the supervision of a superior.
  • Any other tasks that may be assigned by supervisors

At Polyglotte (staff cafeteria)

  • Serve the employees who come to eat at Polyglotte
  • Set up the Polyglotte buffets and clear them away
  • Collect meal vouchers from external guests
  • Clean small equipment and large equipment
  • Clean the Polyglotte (clean the tables, empty the bins, do the dishes)
  • Keep the Polyglotte pantry and equipment in good working order and clean
  • Ensure that employees have the necessary plates and cutlery and that the tableware is clean

In the kitchen

  • Participate in service as well as carving certain dishes during a buffet or cocktail reception
  • Participate in setting up banquet buffets and clearing them
  • Clean small equipment and large equipment
  • Clean cooking surfaces, roasting boards, braising pans, smoke extractors and fryers
  • Clean, polish and restore S&D (Chafing dish) after service
  • Keep the kitchen and equipment in good working order and clean
  • Ensure that the cooks have the necessary plates and cutlery and that the customers' tableware is clean
  • Ensure the cleanliness and maintenance of all work areas, utensils and equipment

Credentials

  • Knowledge of French
  • Previous experience in a kitchen or as a kitchen porter is an asset
  • Experience in a 4or 5 hotel is an asset
  • Apply hygiene and safety policies (HACCP)
  • Highly responsible and reliable
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team
  • Flexibility and availability with regard to working hours
  • Well-presented, neat and conscientious

Physical demands of the position

The physical demands of the position include, but are not limited to:

  • Constant: standing and walking throughout the work period
  • Constant: lifting and carrying loads
  • Occasional: kneeling, pushing, pulling, lifting
  • Occasional: ascending or descending stairs and ramps
