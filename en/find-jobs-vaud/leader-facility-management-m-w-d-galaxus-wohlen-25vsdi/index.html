Full description

Do you want to lead and develop a versatile technical area? In this role, you will be responsible for Facility Management in Wohlen, lead your team and several project managers and ensure the reliable operation and further development of infrastructure, building automation and processes.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Hauptlager Wohlen, Industriestrasse 21, 5610 Wohlen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • You are responsible for facility management and ensure the reliable operation of the buildings and infrastructure
  • You lead and develop the FM team, set priorities and ensure clear structures and high service quality
  • Planning and coordination of maintenance, repairs and improvement measures – also for parallel projects and disruptions
  • You optimize processes and use operational data to transparently control services and exploit potential
  • Close cooperation with the location teams and central contact person for all FM topics
  • Professional and disciplinary management of several project managers: as well as prioritization and coordination of their projects
  • Ensuring legal and safety requirements in fire protection
  • Negotiation with the site owner as well as strategic development of the site including construction measures and investment planning

Requirements

  • Min 3 years: Facility management, building maintenance or in a comparable technical operating function
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): Facility Management
  • Several years of professional experience in facility management, building maintenance or in a comparable technical operating function
  • Leadership experience and joy in developing employees and finding solutions together
  • Ideally, a degree or further education in facility management, building technology or a related field
  • Good technical understanding, networked thinking and a structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Experience in managing service providers and dealing with MS Office
  • Knowledge of CAFM systems is an advantage
  • Experience in dealing with prioritization and resource conflicts in several parallel projects
  • Self-employment, determination and responsibility as well as a pronounced service orientation
  • Building trust through consistent and reliable actions
  • Using creativity and initiative to solve problems and achieve goals
  • German (flowing)
  • English (good knowledge)
