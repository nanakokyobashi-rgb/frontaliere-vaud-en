Full description

Make visible the sustainable economy and the companies that engage, the projects that change the situation, the results that matter: this is the challenge that awaits you in the Communication Unit of the Service for the Promotion of the Economy and Innovation (SPEI). Your mission? Ensure that service delivery is clear, accessible and attractive to businesses and their partners, in particular around the sustainability component of the Economic Development Support Policy (EDSP) and the Sustainable Economy Support Fund (SEF) that are the core of your portfolio. In concrete terms, you imagine and deploy the content that makes you want to commit to sustainability: campaigns, publications, corporate portraits, digital formats. You lead the service channels, highlight supported projects and organize meetings that bring companies and partners together around these devices. A transversal position, at the crossroads of content creation, event and mandate management.

  • Design and implement communication plans, content and media adapted to different audiences and channels.
  • Develop and animate digital communication in support of the sustainable economy: websites, social networks, newsletters, direct mail campaigns.
  • Valorize companies, projects and results supported by the service.
  • Design, organize and promote meetings and events for companies and partners.
  • Lead external mandates, coordinate your portfolio partners, and measure the effectiveness of actions. For further information please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Elisabeth Kim, Communications Manager, at 021 316 60 10.
  • Specialized training in digital communication, corporate communication, marketing or public relations.
  • At least 3 years of experience in managing communication projects, including digital communication, editorial production and service provider management.
  • Excellent editorial skills and mastery of digital communication tools.
  • Ability to manage multiple projects, coordinate various partners and adapt content to audiences and media.
  • Strong interest in sustainability, innovation and business support.
  • Knowledge of the Vaudian economic and institutional fabric as players in the desired sustainable economy.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
