More than 1,000 employees at 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe: the CSD Group is a dynamic and growing company. Thanks to the ingenuity, skill and multidisciplinarity that characterize our teams, CSD offers innovative and sustainable solutions for our customers with a "plus" for quality of life and the environment. We are looking for our Environment branch in Lausanne (VD) or Geneva (GE), a Project Manager Water Treatment (f/h/d) 80-100%.

  • Your missions
  • Pilotage and implementation of projects in the field of wastewater treatment and drinking water : Sewage treatment plant, sludge incineration furnace of STEP, drinking water treatment plant throughout Switzerland Romande
  • Managing large multidisciplinary projects
  • Coordination of the different phases of the project: studies, design, calls for tenders, implementation and commissioning
  • Hydraulic dimensioning and processing processes
  • Preparation of technical reports, calculation notes and project documents
  • Work direction, site monitoring and stakeholder coordination
  • Monitoring of project costs, timelines and quality
  • Participation in business development and maintaining relationships with clients and partners
  • Your profile
  • Engineer/EPF/HES in environment, civil engineering or equivalent training, with specialization in water treatment
  • Minimum 10 years of experience in an engineering office or construction company, ideally in Switzerland
  • Experience in managing and coordinating major multidisciplinary technical projects
  • Good knowledge of SIA and ATV-DWA standards
  • Mastery of French; knowledge of German is an asset
  • Analytical capacity, synthesis and sense of responsibility
  • Dynamic, autonomous and proactive personality, with good relational and organizational skills
