Your mission

  • Ensure the installation and installation of tailor-made arrangements with a variety of clients (individuals, shops, offices or public buildings)
  • Installation of kitchens, dressing rooms, cabinets, doors, woodwork and other interior fittings

  • Good physical condition
  • Organizational sense and ability to work independently as a team
  • Driver's licence category B is an asset
