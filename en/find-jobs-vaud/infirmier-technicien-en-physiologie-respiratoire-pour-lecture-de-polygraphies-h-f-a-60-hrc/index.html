Full description

The Riviera-Chablais Hospital, Vaud-Valais (HRC), is a reference intercantonal hospital with over 2,000 collaborators. Rennaz Hospital has over 350 acute care beds. It is complemented by two Geriatric and Rehabilitation Clinics and two medical clinics. All employees invest on a daily basis to provide quality care for patients, embodying the institution's values of authenticity, exemplaryness and innovation.

In order to strengthen the team in the Pneumology Unit, we are looking for a:

Nurse Respiratory Physiology Technician for Reading Polygraphs M/F 60%

The main tasks of this post are

In close collaboration with Pneumology Unit physicians, your activity will be mainly devoted to reading and technical analysis of respiratory polygraphs, in accordance with procedures defined by pneumonologists.

You may also be required to participate in the conduct of examinations of pulmonary explorations, thus providing a multifunctional activity within the unit. Training and support can be offered to develop your skills in this area.

We ask

  • A HES diploma in nursing or equivalent qualification with CRS recognition, or a technician's training in respiratory physiology or equivalent
  • Training or experience in lung functional exploration, respiratory polygraph or sleep medicine
  • An Experience in Pneumology
  • Analysis capacity, rigour and accuracy in data interpretation
  • Autonomy in the organisation of work and a sense of responsibility
  • Motivation to develop a versatile activity and also participate in lung function examinations
  • An ease with computer tools and specific software
  • An excellent mastery of French, both oral and written
  • Team spirit, good relationship skills and ability to collaborate with different professionals

We offer

  • An activity specialized in the field of outpatient pneumonia, within a dynamic and multidisciplinary team
  • A position mainly dedicated to reading and analyzing respiratory polygraphs
  • A versatile activity also allowing to develop your skills in lung functional explorations
  • A real possibility of teleworking for part of the activity of reading polygraphs, according to the needs of the organisation and at a rate to be defined and agreed with the employee
  • A work organisation offering some flexibility between on-site clinical activity and remote reading activity
  • A technical environment with quality equipment
  • Close collaboration with pneumonologists and an experienced multidisciplinary team
  • Opportunities for continuing training and skills development
  • Attractive conditions of engagement adapted to your experience

Information can be obtained from Ms. Aida Marques, ICS of ambulatory medicine, by e-mail: aida.marques@hopitalrivierachablais.ch
