Watch Assembly & Development (M/F) back to list In order to strengthen our watchmaking teams, we are looking for a (M/F) Assembly & Development Watchmaker. Job description

Your mission

  • Make the assembly of calibres and watchmaking functions in the development phase, then participate in their technical validation
  • Produce or adapt certain parts and subassemblies using traditional workshop machines
  • Collaborate with the various technical stakeholders to optimize the solutions chosen during the design phases
  • Bring your professional expertise to support assembly and watchmaking activities
  • Document tests performed and develop general assembly procedures
  • Support the increase in skills of employees and external partners on the methods of assembly of products
  • Develop in a versatile environment with a wide range of projects and responsibilities

Profile

Profile desired

  • Holder of a Clock CFC or equivalent training
  • Significant experience in creating unique parts, developing new products or mounting high precision watchmakers
  • Ability to work independently while easily integrating into multidisciplinary teams
  • Excellent thoroughness, organizational sense and ease of transition from one activity to another according to priorities
  • Strong interest in innovation, continuous improvement and sharing of technical knowledge
  • Want to pass on your know-how and contribute actively to the development of the skills of your professional circle
