Full description

For our Innovation & Design team, we are looking for our future intern for 6 months from February 2027. During your internship, your main mission will be to contribute to the identification of innovation opportunities and the design of new experiences, products and services focused on user needs.

In this context, you will be responsible for

  • Participate in user research through interviews, observations and field analyses to identify needs, irritants and innovation opportunities
  • Contribute to the design and development of new concepts using Thinking, Agile Innovation and Co-construction approaches
  • Make models, prototypes or proofs of concept, participate in user testing and contribute to continuous improvement of proposed solutions
  • Take part in innovation and transformation projects by carrying out benchmarks, market analyses and recommendations for different stakeholders
  • You are or are in the process of obtaining a Bachelor or Master in a field related to design, innovation, engineering, entrepreneurship or digital technologies.
  • You have a strong sensitivity to user-centered approaches, Thinking Design and innovation, and have a strong interest in new technologies
  • You have excellent analytical, synthesis, communication and storytelling capabilities, and are comfortable with design tools such as Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, Miro or equivalent
  • Curious, creative and with a strong spirit of initiative, you enjoy evolving in a collaborative environment and participating in the design of new experiences, products and services
  • You have a perfect command of French both orally and in writing, English is a plus

  • A contribution to your sports or cultural activities
  • The ability to work remotely depending on the function
  • A business restaurant
  • An advantageous PBL
