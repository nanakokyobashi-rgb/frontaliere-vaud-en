Careers that change live start here. Medtronic is a global leader in healthcare technology with a Mission to alleviate bread, restore health, and extend life. A Day in the Life Inventory specialist is responsible for the availability and technical management of spare parts necessary to maintain the reliability of production equipment.

He works closely with Maintenance, Engineering, Logistics and suppliers to ensure the identification, standardization and availability of critical parts. It helps reduce unplanned shutdowns, improve equipment reliability and optimize maintenance costs. Responsibilities may include the following activities and other activities may be assigned:

Technical management of spare parts and maintenance data

  • Create and maintain the list of spare parts in the CMMS.
  • Standardize components to reduce the diversity of references.
  • Identify components becoming obsolete and develop alternatives.

Procurement and supplier management

  • Create and track purchase requests related to spare parts until they are received.
  • Work with suppliers to improve procurement times.
  • Ensure rotation of calibrated and/or maintained elements at an external supplier (delivery and receipt)

Stock management

  • Receive spare parts in CMMS
  • Executes physical inventory on a regular basis
  • Prepare work orders (parts output)

Continuous improvement

  • Participate in Maintenance Excellence (MEx), Kaizen and Lean initiatives.
  • Identify cost reduction opportunities while maintaining the expected level of reliability.
  • Develop standards and good practices for spare parts management.

Knowledge and Experience Required

Training

  • Technical CFC or equivalent training in mechanics, automation, electrotechnical or industrial maintenance.

Experience

  • Experience in logistics, industrial maintenance or equipment reliability.
  • Experience in GMP or MedTech environment appreciated.

Competences

  • Good knowledge of industrial equipment and its components.
  • Knowledge of CMMS tools.
  • Mastery of Excel and computer tools.

Personal suitability

  • Rigor and sense of organization.
  • Analytical mind.
  • Internal customer orientation.
  • Ability to work transversely.
  • High interest in continuous improvement and reliability of equipment.
