Full description

The park of Beausobre is nestled in the park of the same name in the city of Morges, and offers a personalized welcome for 82 residents. Designed to foster intergenerational ties, the EMS is surrounded by a sheltered housing residence, a Child Living Centre and two colleges. The Beausobre Park also has a restaurant and cafeteria, open every 7 days/7, a wellness area for residents (jacuzzi and sensory baths) and an aromatic garden with a fully secure perimeter.
