What you bring to our mission

Within the administrative branch located in Montagny-près-Yverdon, you direct, respond, and filter requests from beneficiaries as well as those from internal/external partners by phone, physically at reception, and by email.

A word from your future manager, Géraldine

"With solid experience in public administration, both in managing complex cases and in team supervision, I joined EVAM 6 years ago as head of the Reception and Service Division. Since 2024, I proudly lead the Montagny Branch.

Every day, I work with a close-knit and caring team, where mutual aid is not an empty word but a reality. The mission at reception is to welcome, guide, collect, and inform beneficiaries and partners. If you share our values of solidarity and respect, and want to invest yourself in a meaningful profession, we will be delighted to welcome you among us!"

Your key missions

  • Welcome and guide requests from beneficiaries, collaborators, and internal and external partners at reception.
  • Management of the site's cash register.
  • Process basic transportation benefit requests.
  • Management of access badges and the site commissary.
  • Processing incoming and outgoing mail.

Your profile

  • CFC for commercial employee or title deemed equivalent.
  • Minimum 3 years of experience in administrative field, ideally in a similar role.
  • Proficiency in French, spoken and written, English an asset.
  • Proficiency in common office tools.
  • Sense of service.
  • Adaptability and stress management.
  • Ease in interpersonal skills, listening, and a collaborative spirit.
  • Interest in the field of migration and social action.

What EVAM offers you

  • An engaged work environment
  • A continuing education policy to advance your career
  • 5 weeks of vacation (6 from age 50)
  • Very good social benefits
  • Meaningful projects in a stimulating intercultural context

The recruitment process in 3 steps

  1. An interview with the recruitment officer and the team leader
  2. A role-playing scenario
  3. A half-day immersion to concretely discover your future work environment

At the end of the process: request for a criminal record extract, special extract, and certificate from the prosecution office (blank documents required).
