100%, Lausanne / Smart Working

Shape your future with us!

AXA-ARAG, one of Switzerland's leading legal protections, is expanding. Our legal department, with approximately 200 employees and collaborators located in three locations in Switzerland, processes over 100,000 legal requests from our clients each year. Our ambition: to continuously improve the customer experience to a high level of demand, innovation, thinking differently and fully embracing digital transformation.

Our corporate culture is based on openness, flexibility and mutual recognition. With us, your opinion counts – really. We offer you modern work models and real opportunities for personal development. Do you like taking responsibility and aspire to play a central role in your team? Do you want to support our clients in their legal challenges, explore innovative approaches and evolve with digital transformation?

– So you're the personality with the good mindset we're looking for!

What's waiting for you

  • Oral and written advice to our Private and Business clients, especially in the following areas, with a portfolio of cases currently under way: Labour law (contract of private and public law, dispute with the joint commissions...); Unemployment insurance
  • Loss of gain insurance
  • Legal advice with a modern touch: you analyse various situations and solve problems in a simple, pragmatic and in the interest of the clients, examining the facts in the fields concerned
  • Conduct of negotiations, on the telephone and in writing, with policyholders, opposing parties, technical experts and authorities
  • Using AI: In your daily work, powerful AI tools are at your disposal, allowing you to work efficiently and focus on the essentials.

What you bring

  • University education in Swiss law (Master of Law), experience in the relevant fields, the lawyer's patent is an asset
  • Flexibility and adaptability
  • Good management of a large volume of work and autonomy in the way of working
  • Stress resistance
  • Rapid and pragmatic analysis of problems and the search for precise and appropriate solutions
  • Taste of customer contact and teamwork, as well as ability to work independently, accurately and targeted, even in times of high demand
  • Excellent communication skills, negotiation skills and very good written expression
  • Good knowledge of English and/or German is an asset

What we offer you We are convinced that satisfied employees achieve better performance. That is why we propose salary supplements that bring tangible benefits beyond the ordinary.

Flexibility and freedom: Smart Working (flexible working model), annual working time, five weeks of holidays (six weeks from 50 years), two additional weeks of holidays offered every five years, possibility to buy up to ten additional days of holidays per year

Work and family life: family-friendly meeting hours, 22 weeks of maternity leave, 8 weeks of leave for the other parent, subsidies for day care, company family allowances, days of leave for caring for relatives or in difficult personal situations

Insurance and insurance: an attractive pension fund offering extended overcompulsory benefits and savings options, and up to 30% discount on insurance products

Flexible benefits: AXA Shareplan (staff participation), CHF 500 per year on the Swibeco benefits platform, lunch lunch or lunch card, Smart Working allowance of CHF 200 per year

Mobility: half-rate subscription refund, Reka-Pay up to CHF 1000 per year (20% discount), fleet discount, free electric charging station, AXA bikes to borrow

The right balance at work: free health checkups, health weeks every year, varied physical activity offers (on-line and on-site), massage offers, rest rooms, Employee Care, online health portal, various seminars and events around health prevention and mental health

Development: three days for individual training, wide range of learning and development, free coaching, financial participation in continuing training, promotion of internal mobility, offers of talent development, seminars for the over 55 (AXA Horizon), engagement in communities on different themes, participation in innovative projects (Innoday, AXA Hackathon, UnConference), two days dedicated to volunteer missions (environment and social)
