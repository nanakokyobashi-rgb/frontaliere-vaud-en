Full description

The Riviera-Chablais Hospital, Vaud-Valais (HRC), is a reference intercantonal hospital, which brings together more than 2,500 collaborators. Rennaz Hospital has over 350 acute care beds. It is complemented by two Geriatric and Rehabilitation Clinics and two medical clinics. All employees invest on a daily basis to provide quality care for patients, embodying the institution's values of authenticity, exemplaryness and innovation.

As part of the development of its activities and the creation of a new position, the Human Resources Directorate of the Hospital Riviera-Chablais is seeking, for its HR Management & Development unit based in Rennaz, one of the following:

HR specialist (80%) – CDI

The main tasks of this post are

For the services for which you will be responsible, you ensure the missions of:

  • To advise and support managers and employees on HR issues, including in the application of legal, treaty and regulatory provisions, as well as their respective rights and obligations.
  • Support long-term absences by monitoring cases, coordinating interdisciplinary networks and implementing measures to maintain or return to work.
  • Coordinate reintegration and gradual recovery, in collaboration with management, Occupational Health, Disability Insurance and other relevant partners.
  • Support managers in the management of complex HR and managerial situations, including recruitment, conflict management, framing, employee development and management of sensitive individual situations.
  • Support the development and updating of specifications, analyse functions and propose their classification in accordance with the remuneration system of the institution.
  • Analyze and present HR indicators and dashboards to service managers, identify issues and propose appropriate improvement measures.
  • To develop the HR and managerial skills of management , by disseminating good practices, providing methodological advice and conducting awareness-raising or training activities.

We ask • You have a Master's degree in Labor Psychology and Organisations, or an equivalent university degree. • You have at least 5 years of proven professional experience in HR consulting, HR specialist or HR manager, ideally with a strong executive support dimension. • You have strong skills to support complex HR situations, including conflict management, absentee management and executive advice. • You have good knowledge of Swiss labour law, social insurance and treaty provisions. • You are comfortable with the compensation mechanisms, the assessment and classification of functions and the analysis of HR indicators. • Additional training in HR management, mediation, case management or coaching, as well as the federal HR specialist certificate, are assets. • Knowledge of the health sector and the hospital environment is desired.

You distinguish yourself by your ability to analyze, your discernment and your relational qualities.

With a strong advisory posture, you can accompany managers in sensitive situations with diplomacy, pragmatism and sanctity.

Your autonomy, your ability to step back and your ability to propose concrete solutions are essential qualities for success in this function.

We offer

Joining the Hospital Riviera-Chablais is integrating a health institution that makes sense, at the service of the population, in a complex, multidisciplinary but passionate professional environment.

Within our Human Resources Directorate, you will have the opportunity to perform a varied function, with direct contact with managers and teams, and to make a concrete contribution to the support of employees and to the evolution of managerial practices.

The HRC encourages skills development and further training,

You will benefit from attractive terms and conditions of employment and many benefits.
