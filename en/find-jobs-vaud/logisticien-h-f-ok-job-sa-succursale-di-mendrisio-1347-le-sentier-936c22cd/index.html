Logistician (M/F) back to list In order to strengthen our teams, we are looking for a Logistician (M/F). Job description

Your mission

  • Organize and coordinate freight flows (receipt, storage, preparation, shipment)
  • Planning supplies according to needs and stocks available
  • Oversee inventory management and conduct regular inventories
  • Optimize warehouse organization (location, flow, storage space)
  • Monitor deliveries and resolve any problems or delays
  • Use logistics IT tools to monitor operations
  • Ensure timeliness, safety and quality standards

Profile

Profile desired

  • Logistics training (CFC, federal patent, ES/HES diploma in logistics) or training deemed equivalent
  • Prior experience in a similar function desired
  • Good mastery of IT tools and logistics management systems (ERP, Excel), knowledge of a WMS being an asset
  • Organizational sense, rigour and anticipation capacity
  • Self-reliance and management of priorities, especially during periods of high activity
  • Driver's licence category B, an asset
