Location: Vers-chez-les-Blanc, Switzerland Work model: Office-based Contract type / duration: 2 years limited contract

Shape the future through scientific breakthroughs and innovation.

As a Junior Research Assistant in Packaging Safety at Nestlé Research, your mission is to develop, validate and apply analytical methods for novel coated paper packaging materials. You will make a difference by collaborating closely with teammates, research colleagues, operations teams, quality managers and external partners. This is your opportunity to turn ideas into sustainable and innovative solutions for people, pets, and the planet.

How you’ll make an impact as a Junior Research Assistant in Packaging Safety

  • Contribute to the development of robust analytical methods that help assess the safety and performance of innovative packaging materials.
  • Perform packaging characterization analyses that support reliable research outcomes and project decisions.
  • Consolidating and interpreting data and writing clear analytical documentation that enables quality, research and project teams to act with confidence.
  • Participate in transferring analytical methods to operations and supporting end users throughout the method lifecycle.
  • Contributing to quality, safety and knowledge-sharing practices that help teams work consistently across sites.
  • Engage with internal and external partners to stay at the forefront of packaging safety science and analytical technologies.

What will make you successful

  • Master’s degree in chemistry, analytical chemistry or a related scientific discipline, combined with a strong interest in analytical sciences, packaging safety and sustainable packaging technologies.
  • Applying knowledge of gas chromatography-mass spectrometry, liquid chromatography-high resolution mass spectrometry or material characterization.
  • Using hands-on laboratory experience from academic projects, internships or research activities to deliver reliable experimental work.
  • Collaborating with curiosity, attention to detail and strong data analysis, problem-solving and scientific reporting skills.
  • Working proficiency in English and French.
  • Bringing collaboration, fresh thinking, bold questions, respect, and a positive, wellbeing-oriented mindset.
