Main responsibilities

  • Your key responsibilities
  • As a PJP, you demonstrate personal purpose around improving the patient journey, as well as strong passion to create value and impact on the ecosystem.
  • You think from an Enterprise level, beyond boundaries and borders, in order to bring best possible outcomes to both the local health environment, and more broadly, across Roche globally.
  • Visionary: defining and embedding the strategy around creating improved patient journeys Mindset:
  • Strong entrepreneurial mindset with outstanding partnering capabilities (coaching, consulting, ability to deeply listen, question and understand)

  • Self managed and self directed - altruistic in supporting the global network

Company and context

  • As a Patient Journey Partner (PJP) you are at the center of the Roche organization, turning upside down the role we play in society - from mostly offering world-class medicines towards much more partnering, innovating and leading generative disruption.
  • By doing so you will be directly accountable to deliver better outcomes for patients, the ecosystem and the organisation, faster.
  • We are a dynamic and vibrant team that is passionate about delivering outcomes while maintaining a work life balance and having fun.
  • In this role, you will directly contribute to Roche Pharma’s Ambition of delivering 20 transformative medicines addressing diseases with the highest societal burden by 2030.
  • The PJP is an empowered leader and the local face of Roche.
  • You partner closely with all stakeholders directly touching the patient journey (in a given therapeutic or disease area), gaining a deep understanding of their needs in order to connect Roche’s capabilities to co-create meaningful solutions.
  • This role is locally embedded in the ecosystem, it is an integrated role and functionally agnostic.
  • You are a system thinker, deep listener and with excellent stakeholder engagement skills including partnering with Disease area experts and organizations.
  • Experience partnering with Patient Advocacy Groups is highly desired.
  • You have experience leading a Cross-functional team, formulating strategies out of market and disease driven insights and orchestrating cross-functional teams to seamlessly execute your plans.

Additional details

  • Join Roche, where every voice matters.
  • Visionary: defining and embedding the strategy around creating improved patient journeys
  • Ability to say “No” to stakeholders when the value is not predictable/foreseen.
  • You bring to the team a strong business acumen and expertise in commercialization, medical and customer experience excellence including an understanding to leverage digital tools in stakeholder engagements.
