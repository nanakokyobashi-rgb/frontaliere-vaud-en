Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. True to its cooperative nature and values, it encourages the creation of a healthy, inclusive and inspiring work environment, ideal for developing your talent. Join a team at the heart of actuarial issues and digital transformation. Within our Actuarial Life team, you are actively involved in key actuarial production exercises while contributing to the modernization of tools, methods and processes. In a changing environment marked by the development of data, automation and artificial intelligence technologies, you play a key role in transforming actuarial activities and continuously improving our practices. Your Missions Participate in actuarial production and risk management exercises:

  • Realize financial loops.
  • Calculate the Swiss Solvency Test (SST) ratio and estimate the risks Life.
  • Simulate future-oriented scenarios and achieve ORSA.
  • Calculate the technical provisions. Contribute to the optimization and automation of actuarial processes:
  • Develop and develop analytical, modelling and reporting tools.
  • Use data to improve the quality, reliability and efficiency of processes.
  • Work closely with business teams, IT and Data to design innovative solutions that meet the needs of Life Actuarial.
  • Contribute to the integration of new technologies, including data science, automation and artificial intelligence. Your profile
  • University training in Actuarial, Mathematics, Engineering, Data Sciences or equivalent.
  • First experience in life actuarial, risk management or quantitative modelling.
  • Good understanding of the regulatory and prudential requirements of the insurance sector, an asset.
  • Strong skills in quantitative analysis and complex problem solving.
  • Good knowledge of programming languages such as Python and SQL.
  • Knowledge of Data environments and tools, including Databricks and Git.
  • Use and valuation of data, with particular attention to their quality.
  • Ability to automate processes and develop decision support tools.
  • Curiosity for new technologies, artificial intelligence and their new uses.
  • Excellent mastery of French and English, level C1. What we offer you
  • A role combining actuarial expertise and technological innovation.
  • The opportunity to participate in transformation projects with high added value.
  • A collaborative environment that promotes continuous learning and knowledge sharing.
  • The possibility of having a concrete impact on the evolution of the actuarial practices of tomorrow. We look forward to receiving your complete application package digitally (with curriculum vitae, cover letter, copies of work certificates and diplomas).
