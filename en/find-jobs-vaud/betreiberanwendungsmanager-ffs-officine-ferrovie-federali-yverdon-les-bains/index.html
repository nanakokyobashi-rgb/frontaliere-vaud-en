What you can move.

  • Assumes, for the West Region, the role of the Facility Management team as a central point of contact with project leaders to coordinate and plan interactions between these two entities.
  • Develops operating and maintenance standards and requirements documents to integrate them early into projects.
  • Defines the requirements for operation and maintenance in the context of investment projects in technical installations and buildings.
  • Supports and supports projects from the development of specifications to reception with a focus on operation and maintenance.

What you bring.

  • Advanced training successfully completed (e.g. federal patent with technical specialization, engineering degree, etc.) in the fields of technical management of installations and buildings.
  • Very good knowledge of the directives in force in the fields of technology (CVCS, electricity, etc.), machinery (standards, EC, etc.) and buildings (fire standards, safety...)
  • Perfect mastery of MS-Office computer tools and specific computer diagnostic tools.
  • Have a strong spirit of analysis and anticipation. Be rigorous, organized and recognized for your ability to deliver the projects entrusted to you.
  • Perfect mastery of the French language with good knowledge of German (B1-B2).
