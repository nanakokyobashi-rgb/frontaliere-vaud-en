Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Join the first universal bank in the canton of Vaud, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Private Banking division, we are looking for a:

Advisor or Advisor Private Banking International - Global Accounts

  • Your main missions: managing a portfolio of clients domiciled outside Switzerland know the main tax, estate and asset approach aspects where a tracking responsibility is established loyalty to your customers and gain portfolio share maintain and develop customer contacts in accordance with the legal and regulatory framework optimize the profitability of your portfolio while managing the inherent risks being constantly up to date on the banking regulations in force
  • Your profile: higher education (university or banking economist), strong technical knowledge in tax, legal and financial matters, confirmed business experience in the field of wealth management, excellent relational ease, developed business sense and real understanding of customer needs, perfect mastery of French, very good knowledge of English (level C1).

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
