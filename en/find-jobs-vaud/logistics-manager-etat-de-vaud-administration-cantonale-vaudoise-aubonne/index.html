Full description

As a logistics manager, you participate in the good running of the school in Aubonne and surrounding area and collaborate continuously with teachers and students. In this way you will contribute not only to the administrative efficiency, but also to the overall success of the educational mission entrusted to the Vaudois compulsory education.

  • Ensure inventory management of school supplies and equipment.
  • Order, prepare and distribute school supplies and equipment.
  • Ensure respect for financial resources and maintenance of the economy fund.
  • Work with school management, teachers and community staff to ensure good management of the economy.
  • Ensure administrative monitoring of the economy. The function involves handling heavy loads and requires the use of handling equipment. Working time is annualised to compensate for a number of school holidays and a higher workload, particularly at the beginning and end of the school year. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here . Do you have any questions? Mr. Serge Martin, Director of the Institution, is pleased to be at your disposal at 021 557 16 30.
  • Logistician CFC (storage, transport or distribution) or title deemed equivalent required
  • Professional practice of 3 years since obtaining the EFA, or experience at a similar position in logistics
  • Driver's licence required and carist's licence desired
  • Knowledge of standard office automation tools (informatics) and inventory management software required
  • Knowledge of the school environment
  • Responsibility, customer orientation and public service ethics
  • Listening and communication, self-help and collaboration
  • Ability to organize and manage time, self-control and stress management
  • Flexibility and ability to adapt to a fluctuating workload

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
