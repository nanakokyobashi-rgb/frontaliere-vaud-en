Full description

In order to strengthen its Research Centre, Swiss Visio is seeking a motivated, rigorous and autonomous clinical research attaché to participate in the development and coordination of its scientific activities.

  • Your Mission

Attached to the Medical Directorate of the Swiss Visio Research Center, you ensure the coordination and follow-up of clinical studies and the development of the research projects of the centre. You work closely with investigators, clinical teams, sponsors and academic partners to ensure the quality and compliance of projects. Your main responsibilities

  • Establish, coordinate and monitor clinical studies.
  • Ensure compliance with regulatory, ethical and scientific requirements (ICH-GCP).
  • Prepare submissions to Ethics Commissions and manage the literature of studies.
  • Participate in monitoring visits, audits and inspections.
  • Control the quality and traceability of clinical data.
  • Contribute to the drafting of protocols, publications and scientific communications.
  • Monitor research projects and academic collaborations.
  • Participate in partnership development and funding search.
  • Administrative monitoring of research projects.
  • University training in health, biomedical sciences or equivalent.
  • Experience confirmed in clinical research, ideally in multicentre studies.
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss regulatory framework and ICH-GCP standards.
  • Experience in following up clinical and multicentre studies CRA
  • Excellent editorial, organizational and relational skills.
  • Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously with autonomy and rigour.
  • Mastery of French and good command of English. German is an asset.
  • The opportunity to join a network of state-of-the-art dental centres
  • Motivating activity in a pleasant work environment
  • A corporate culture focused on excellence and the quality of human relations

We pay great attention to the diversity of life paths of our collaborators. Our positions are open to both women and men.

Swiss Visio Network is a network of leading-edge dphthalmology centres dealing with all eye diseases. Our teams guarantee personalized, first-class care for patients, placing them at the centre of attention. Swiss Visio is distinguished by its pleasant working environment and entrepreneurial spirit. The network now has 22 centres in French-speaking Switzerland, 3 in German-speaking Switzerland and 1 in Ticino and continues to expand in Swiss territory.
