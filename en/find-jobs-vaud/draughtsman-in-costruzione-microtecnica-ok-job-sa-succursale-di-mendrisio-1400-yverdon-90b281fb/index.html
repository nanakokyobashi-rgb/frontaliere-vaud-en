Draughtsman in microtechnical construction back to list In order to strengthen our teams we are looking for a cartoonist in microtechnical construction. Job description

Your mission

  • Design and draw microtechnical parts, subassemblies and mechanisms in 2D and 3D
  • Model parts and assemblies on computer assisted drawing software (DAO)
  • Plan machining operations according to established standards and procedures
  • Ensure interface with production, quality control and suppliers to ensure industrial feasibility
  • Evaluate different design parameters to improve processes in collaboration with other professionals involved
  • Control and update technical documentation
  • Meeting industry quality standards

Profile

Profile desired

  • Holder of an CFC for a draftsman in microtechnical construction or equivalent training
  • Experience in a similar position is an asset
  • Mastery of computer assisted drawing software (DAO) such as SolidWorks, Creo or equivalent
  • Ability to work in a multidisciplinary environment (mechanical, materials, production, quality, etc.)
  • Autonomy in the management of design projects, from outline to production
  • Space representation capacity and ease of drawing
  • Rigor, accuracy and thoroughness
  • Ability to adapt to technological change
