Full description

Amstein + Walthert is an engineering company with approximately 1100 employees. As engineers, planners and consultants, we deal with all aspects of building technology and infrastructure throughout their life cycle. Our solutions are innovative, elegant and durable. Your missions:

  • Abstaining profiles with less than 7 years of experience as a project manager in Switzerland Conducting projects
  • Management and coordination of CVC projects autonomously, from the design phase to the implementation (SIA phases 2, 3, 4, 5).
  • Piloting a project team: project managers/projectors/planners
  • Defining technical concepts, carrying out CVC studies and sizings, drawing up outline principles and comparing technical variants.
  • Perform or verify hydraulic and aeral dimensioning calculations.
  • Draft project and project documents and cost estimates.
  • Develop submissions and pre-metals.
  • Pilot the spatial coordination of CVSE facilities and defend the technical solutions selected from the project partners.
  • Control hydraulic and aeral planes.
  • Participate in technical choices taking into account operational, maintenance and execution constraints. Implementation monitoring
  • Provide on-site monitoring services.
  • Coordinate the interventions of the installation companies and the various project stakeholders.
  • Monitor the compliance of the work performed with contractual and technical requirements.
  • Organize and conduct technical worksite sessions.
  • Perform technical approvals, final tests and commissioning.
  • Ensure management of business costs and financial monitoring of work.
  • Plan and administer technical records in accordance with ISO quality system. Management and coordination
  • Ensure technical, financial and temporal progress of projects.
  • Ensure sound financial management of mandates and monitor financial indicators.
  • Ensure contractual monitoring of projects and anticipate technical, financial and calendar impacts of benefit changes.
  • Control the content of contracts, including services included, excluded and the responsibilities of the various stakeholders.
  • Organize and coordinate the activity of projectors and internal resources in collaboration with the discipline managers.
  • Work closely with architects, design offices, general enterprises, total enterprises, installation companies and other project partners.
  • Manage the performance of agents and monitor their costs and quality.
  • Ensure documentary compliance according to ISO requirements. Your skills and qualifications:
  • HES, EPF or equivalent training in heating, ventilation and air conditioning.
  • Minimum 7 years of experience as a CCV project manager in engineering offices in Switzerland.
  • Excellent knowledge of SIA standards, applicable Swiss requirements and construction processes.
  • Strong skills in multidisciplinary coordination and contractual management.
  • Relationships with clients, architects, companies and agents.
  • A sense of responsibility, autonomy and ability to manage multiple mandates simultaneously.
  • Additional experience in a technical installation, general enterprise or total enterprise is an asset.
