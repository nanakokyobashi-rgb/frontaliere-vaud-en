Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Join the first universal bank in the canton of Vaud, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. At the border between operations and IT Linked to the Operations Operations and Projects Support Sector of the Operations Department, the ROA service (operational responsibility applications) - DOP (Operations Department) manages Department applications. To strengthen this team, based at the Prilly Administrative Centre, we are looking for a IT Business Analyst Announcement - Market Operations domain Your main missions:

  • To ensure the operational and functional responsibility of a critical platform covering inter alia foreign exchange activities (FX), money market, cash products and derivatives.
  • Be the preferred contact point for Front Office, Treasury, Back Office, Accounting and external suppliers users for all applications related questions under your responsibility.
  • Ensure the availability, stability and performance of applications by ensuring daily monitoring of production, incident management and coordination of corrective actions.
  • Piloting the functional evolution of applications: gathering and analysing needs, drafting specifications, coordinating developments, organizing tests and accompanying change.
  • Coordinate production, release and relations with external publishers and partners.
  • Identify, analyse and reduce operational risks by implementing controls, monitoring mechanisms and automation solutions.
  • Develop and maintain monitoring and control tools (SQL, scripts, automation) to improve operational efficiency and quality of processing.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of processes by simplifying manual activities and offering high added value optimizations.
  • Ensure documentation, application governance and support during internal and external audits. Your profile:
  • University, HES or equivalent training in finance, management computing or similar fields.
  • Experience in a role of Business Analyst IT, Application Owner, Applied Manager or Applied Support at a banking institution, market room or financial solutions publisher.
  • Good knowledge of financial products and processes Front-to-Back (FX, money markets, securities, derivatives, cash).
  • Good control of relational databases and SQL language
  • Linux/Unix knowledge, scripting (Shell) and monitoring tools are an important asset.
  • Strong skills in functional analysis, specification writing, test management and change management.
  • Demonstrated ability to analyze complex incidents, coordinate multiple stakeholders, and ensure continuity of service for critical applications.
  • Spirit of continuous improvement, autonomy, sense of responsibility and internal customer orientation.
  • Excellent communication capabilities to interact with business and technical interlocutors.
  • Perfect mastery of French and good professional English (minimum B2); German is an advantage.
  • First experience or strong interest in blockchain and IA topics a plus.
  • Swiss resident or willingness to settle permanently in Switzerland.
