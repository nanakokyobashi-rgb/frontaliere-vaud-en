Full description

Are you an experienced and motivated psychologist with the idea of joining an interdisciplinary team? So you are the person we need to complete our team at the Community Psychiatry Consultation in Vevey as

Associate psychologist or end-of-training assistant 80% THE MISSION Psychologists at the Community Consultation provide outpatient psychiatric and psychotherapeutic consultations for persons from East Vaudois. With interdisciplinary care, they are involved in intensive care of patients with a psychological pathology associated with a decrease in their psychosocial functioning by offering individual or binomial interviews.

WE OFFER

The Community Consultation of the Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy Service (SPPA) of the Nant Foundation aims to offer outpatient psychiatric and psychotherapeutic consultations to adults. Working within this service is to evolve in a dynamic working environment at the service of the patient, offering a psychotherapeutic treatment suitable for everyone. WE SEEK A psychotherapist who holds the title (or a maximum of 18 months of his or her qualification), enabling him or her to play a specialist role in psychology, psychotherapy and psychopathology. The candidate must have an interest in working as a multidisciplinary team and ideally master projective evaluations. Moreover, the ability to be at the service of the patient , his colleagues and the institution is important to work as a psychotherapist in the consultation. Finally, we want to hire a person motivated by the mission of this unit and loving the work as an interdisciplinary team.
