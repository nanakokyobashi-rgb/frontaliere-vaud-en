Full description

The EVAM (Vaudian institution for the reception of migrants) is a major player in the voodoo public service. With more than 1,000 committed collaborators and an annual budget of 360 million francs, we ensure the reception, support and integration of migrants in the canton of Vaud.

Every day, our teams work with openness and responsibility to address key social issues. Joining EVAM means putting your skills at the service of a human mission and of public utility, in a stimulating, diverse and constantly evolving professional environment.

As part of a departure, we recruit one

Social Assistant (80%) – CDI Location: Lucens (60%) + Home support nearby (20%) Entry into office: As soon as possible [Salary class: 7](https://www.evam.ch/app/uploads/2026/06/Bareme-salarial 2026.pdf) (This scale refers to validated prerequisites, failing which classification is done at grade level -1)

What you bring to our mission

In connection with a multidisciplinary team, you play a central role in providing individualised social support, organising care according to the standards in force, contributing to the life of the home and its proper functioning.

The word of your responsible future, Ismael

"As a manager, I base my work on the trust and autonomy given to my employees. I make sure to create a clear, secure and caring framework, while giving everyone the space to fully exercise their skills. Available and listening, I attach great importance to dialogue and team cohesion. I am convinced that the quality of support is based on solidarity and collective commitment. I am looking for a social assistant with real sensitivity to migration. A person invested, humane and motivated to contribute actively to meaningful accompaniment."

Your key missions

  • You are in charge of administrative monitoring and social interviews.
  • You set up a custom action plan.
  • You support beneficiaries in social, professional and administrative integration.
  • You coordinate with internal and external partners.
  • You make sure the rules of the home are respected.

Your profile

  • You have Bachelor HES in social work (recognition SEFRI if foreign diploma).
  • Minimum. 3 years of experience in a similar function.
  • You have a good knowledge of the Vaud network and social insurance.
  • You have an excellent Frenchman (oral and written); Mastering English or other languages is an asset
  • You have a strong interest in migration.

What EVAM proposes

  • A committed working environment
  • 5 weeks of holiday (6 from 50 years)
  • Very good social benefits
  • Meaningful projects in a stimulating intercultural context

The three-step recruitment process

  1. An interview with the head of the household and the.e.
  2. Situation
  3. Half a day of immersion to discover your future working environment

At the end of the process: request for an extract of the criminal record, special extract and certificate from the prosecution office (virgin documents required).

Do you recognize yourself in this mission? We look forward to discovering your candidacy and building together a solidarity future.
