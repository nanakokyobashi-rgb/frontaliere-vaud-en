A place where you can do what you love. And love what you do.

As a Senior Key Account Manager at Purina , your mission is to lead the strategic and financial performance of key retail customers, with full accountability for customer P&L, driving sustainable sales, profitability and market share. You’ll make a difference by collaborating closely with senior customer contacts and colleagues across sales, marketing, finance, supply chain and commercial operations. This is your opportunity to deliver meaningful results that enrich the lives of pets, the people who love them, and the planet we share.

How you’ll make an impact as a Senior Key Account Manager

  • Owning customer business performance to achieve sales, profitability, market share and availability goals
  • Leading joint business planning that turns Purina’s strategy and market insights into shared customer growth
  • Driving high-impact commercial execution across promotions, launches, distribution and merchandising
  • Defining negotiation strategies ,investment choices and customer growth priorities that strengthen customer value and sustainable profitability
  • Building trusted relationships with senior customer stakeholders to unlock long-term opportunities
  • Leading cross-functional teams around clear priorities, timely decisions and measurable business outcomes
  • Act as a senior commercial leader within the Sales team, sharing best practices, developing commercial capabilities and providing leadership across priority customer topics.

What will make you successful

  • Bringing extensive key account management expertise in FMCG and a track record in strategic customer leadership, complex commercial negotiations and customer P&L ownership. Applying commercial and financial insight to manage customer profit and loss, trade investment, forecasting and return on promotions
  • Using customer, category, shopper, market and financial insights to lead effective business plans, forecasting and negotiation strategies
  • Collaborating with and influencing senior stakeholders while making timely decisions in complex, fast-moving environments
  • Communicating fluently in German and English
  • French is an advantage
  • Bringing Purina’s key traits to life such as passion, pace, progress, pioneering spirit, purpose, and pride, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value
