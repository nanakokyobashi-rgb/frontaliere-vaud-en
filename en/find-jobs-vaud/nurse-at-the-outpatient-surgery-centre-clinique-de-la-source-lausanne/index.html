Full description

The Clinique de La Source is a unique multidisciplinary acute care facility. It belongs to the La Source Foundation, an independent and private non-profit institution that also oversees the Institut et Haute École de la Santé La Source. The independence that this status gives it allows it to continuously invest in human and technological aspects in order to guarantee its patients the best possible care. We are looking for

for our Ambulatory Surgery Center

a 100% Nurse

Key information about the position

Reference: CLS/RH/2026.767 / Type of contract: 100% permanent contract / Opening hours: Monday to Friday from 6:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. at the latest / Check-in date: as soon as we find the right person / Location: in the heart of Lausanne (M2 stop "Riponne" or Bus 2, 3 or 21 stop "Beaulieu-Jomini")

Why do we need you?

A nurse position in an outpatient surgery center is becoming available: join a team committed to the Day Clinic where your expertise is recognized, your autonomy valued and your role in the patient journey fully supported.

What will you have to do?

  • Ensuring the comprehensive care of patients in surgery and outpatient medicine, in the pre- and post-operative phase
  • Perform clinical assessments, ensure continuous monitoring and adapt care according to the evolution of patients' conditions
  • Administer and monitor prescribed treatments, including complex infusions
  • Preparing, accompanying and monitoring patients for interventional radiology procedures
  • Apply department-specific nursing care while anticipating and preventing potential complications related to treatments and interventions
  • Ensuring caring communication and personalized support for patients and their loved ones throughout the care pathway
  • Work closely with physicians and interdisciplinary teams to ensure coordinated and safe care
  • Ensure the rigorous transmission of information and the complete traceability of care in patient records
  • Mentor and support nursing students in their clinical learning and professional development

Who will you be working with?

In the Day Clinic, our team has 10 nurses and 1 nursing assistant in endoscopy and outpatient service. It is a supportive, versatile team, always smiling and dynamic. The department's watchwords are "Mutual aid, empathy and greed".

In Interventional, our team is made up of 8 nurses and 4 technical assistants in endoscopy and outpatient service. It is a supportive and dynamic team, experienced and always curious to develop new skills and activities. The watchwords of the department are "Good mood, rigor and gentleness".

What do we expect from you?

The essentials

  • Diploma in nursing from the University of Applied Sciences or title recognised by the Swiss Red Cross
  • Proven experience of at least 3 years in a hospital setting
  • Rigorous control of safety, hygiene and aseptic rules
  • Interpersonal skills and real ability to collaborate within an interdisciplinary team

Your strengths

  • Experience in an outpatient setting, ideally in day surgery
  • Versatility and ability to evolve in a technical and dynamic environment
  • Excellent adaptability and quick integration into a committed team

Why choose Clinique de La Source?

  • Join the largest private clinic in the canton of Vaud and evolve in a dynamic environment, recognized for its expertise and know-how
  • A 24-bed overnight centre for multidisciplinary patient monitoring
  • 3 interventional rooms dedicated to flexible endoscopy and non-invasive surgery
  • To benefit from a human-sized working environment, while benefiting from the richness and diversity of the activities of a future-oriented health institution
  • Benefit from attractive employment conditions and advantageous social benefits (welcome and integration, LaMal participation, advantageous BVG, discounts, etc.)
  • Work-life balance, with part-time work opportunities, up to more than 7 weeks of vacation, unpaid time off, compensated public holidays and the valuation of exchange time
  • Sharing values that resemble us: a Unique, Respectful, Innovative and Responsible clinic
