  • Key Responsibilities Contract Management

  • Agreement Analysis: Thoroughly assess executed agreements to identify key financial and non-financial obligations, ensuring they are accurately captured and maintained in our centralized licensing system (eBDS).
  • Execution Monitoring: Proactively manage and oversee the end-to-end fulfillment of contractual obligations, both internally and externally.
  • Dispute Resolution and Legal Support: Collaborate with internal stakeholders and provide data and expert assistance in the event of contractual disputes or necessary legal actions. Controlling
  • Guidance: Provide technical or procedural guidance to staff on use of templates and database systems used for documentation of royalty obligations both financial and non-financial
