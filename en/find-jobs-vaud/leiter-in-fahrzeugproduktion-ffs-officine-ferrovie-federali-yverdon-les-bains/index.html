Head of “Vehicle Production” 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Head of “Vehicle Production” Yverdon-les-Bains / Work Smart, December 1st, 2026 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. At the Yverdon-les-Bains plant, a team of around 250 people carries out the mid-life renovations and overhauls of the vehicles in the “Passenger Transport Production” division – from dismantling and rebuilding to commissioning. The “Vehicles Yverdon” area combines production and industrialization projects. As a unit manager, you lead operational teams and can specifically improve quality, adherence to deadlines and cost optimization

  • and at the same time contribute to the future development of the plants. This unit is managed by two production managers who are jointly responsible for the area. You can move that.
  • You lead six team leaders and two project managers for “Revision Industrialization” and have overall responsibility for around 120 employees. You are responsible for implementing the safety culture and adhering to company values.
  • As a unit manager, you ensure that changes and revisions to the vehicles are implemented correctly in terms of quality, deadlines and costs.
  • You continuously develop your production lines towards operational excellence, for example using methods such as Kaizen or by using new technologies. Investment projects and their implementation on site are also part of your tasks.
  • As a member of the department and plant management, you will actively work on further development, represent the unit in various committees and maintain close, productive cooperation with other organizational units in order to strengthen our position as a reliable partner. This is what you bring with you.
  • To be successful in this role, you have a solution- and customer-oriented mindset as well as a strong awareness of quality. Leadership skills and convincing communication are among your strengths. - You have several years of management experience in operational areas such as production or maintenance
  • Experience in the rail vehicle sector is an advantage. - You have completed a degree (FH/Uni/ETH) in a technical or business area and have an affinity for the other area. - For this role, business fluency in French (at least level C1) and good knowledge of German (at least level B1) are required so that you can exchange ideas with your colleagues from the Olten and Yverdon-les-Bains plants.
