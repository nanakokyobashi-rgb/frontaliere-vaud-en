Full description

Mission

Innovate4Life is the innovation program of the School of Life Sciences (SV). It supports the next generation of entrepreneurs through education, mentorship, and funding, to translate life sciences discoveries and technologies into t tangible healthcare solutions and societal impact.

Working closely with SV Vice-Dean for Innovation, you will manage and grow the program supporting innovators and connecting them with innovation ecosystem.

Main duties and responsibilities

  • Strategy : Contribute to the vision and development of Innovate4Life program with the Vice-Dean for Innovation, in line with SV, VPI and EPFL strategies.
  • Mentoring : Be a first point of contact for early-stage innovators, help them clarify their next steps, and connect them with the right EPFL resources and mentors.
  • Innovation calls : Organize funding calls and support programs for life science projects, from preparing the call to coordinating selection and following up with funded teams.
  • Education and events : Organize courses, workshops and networking events that bring the SV innovation community together.
  • Partnerships : Help develop collaborations with industry and clinical partners and liaise with EPFL's Startup Unit and Industry Liaison Office.
  • Budget : Follow the program's budget and expenses, with the support of SV finance and administration.
  • Philanthropy and fundraising : Support the SV School in seeking philanthropic and external funding and help maintain good relationships with donors and foundations.

Your Profile

  • PhD or MD in life sciences, medicine, bioengineering or a related field.
  • Experience in pharma, biotech or medtech industry and/or in clinical setting (hospital, clinical research, clinical trials)
  • Experience in fundraising and knowledge of the innovation ecosystem is a plus.
  • Interest in entrepreneurship and in bringing life science research to society.
  • Well organized and autonomous, with a collaborative open mindset.
  • Good communication skills in English, French is a plus.

We offer

  • A dynamic and international research environment within a leading scientific institution.
  • Opportunities to contribute to cutting-edge life sciences innovations that impact healthcare and society.
  • Competitive salary and benefits.
  • A collaborative network of top-tier researchers, clinicians, and industry professionals.
