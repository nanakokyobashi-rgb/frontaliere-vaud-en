Full description

As an educator at the "Les Léchares" juvenile and young adult institution, you are involved in the two tasks assigned to the prison service: to provide supervision and supervision of detained persons and, more specifically, to the task of rehabilitating and preventing the recurrence of minors and young adults. You are an integral part of the development of the identity of a dynamic institution and evolve within a multidisciplinary team. You guarantee a clear and reassuring living environment for detainees and ensure their physical, moral and emotional integrity. You act at all times in accordance with our values: "Loyality, Solidarity, Credibility".

  • Ensure a consistent and reassuring living environment for minors and young adults, mainly on cell units, including by ensuring their physical, moral and emotional integrity.
  • Implement individual strategies for socio-educational intervention.
  • Actively contribute to the organization of the daily operation of cell units.
  • Preparation and preparation of various educational reports, reviews or notices requested by the Directorate or the Criminal Authority.
  • Organize individual or collective activities that meet both the interests of prisoners and socio-educational objectives.
