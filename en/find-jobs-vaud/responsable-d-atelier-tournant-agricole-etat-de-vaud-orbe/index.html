Full description

Do you want to grow in one of the largest agricultural fields in the Canton of Vaud and work in both animal and plant production? The Orb Plain Establishments (EPO) area includes, among others, about 353 ha of cultivated land, 300 cattle (milk and fattening), 350 pigs and 5000 chickens, and a plant-specific machinery fleet. In order to complete our team, we are looking for a future manager for a rotating agricultural workshop within the Orb plain establishments. As such, you accompany and train detained persons in the various professions of the land. You also ensure the care of animals and crops, through our various workshops (domains, vineyards, pigs, poultry slaughterhouse, mill, etc.). You act at all times in accordance with our values: "Loyality, Solidarity, Credibility".

  • To train and supervise persons detained in workshops in the agricultural sector in which you provide replacements;
  • Ensure the management of animals (bovine, poultry, pigs) and the cultivation of crops on the basis of instructions from the head of the sector in compliance with legal standards of hygiene and safety at work;
  • Ensure the welfare of animals and the application of directives on the use of veterinary products and strictly implement the Animal Protection Act;
  • Provide supervision, security and supervision for prisoners working in the workshop and ensure that discipline is maintained;
  • Develop regular behavioural reports and assessments of the skills and acquisitions of supervised persons and work with stakeholders in different sectors (social, security, professional, etc.) to ensure adequate care for detained persons. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Eric Joly, Head of Agriculture, at 024 557 24 10. By clicking here , you will find more information about working in detention in the agricultural sector.
  • Holder of an CFC in an agricultural trade;
  • 10-year professional experience in agriculture and/or market gardening;
  • Federal patent of detention officer or undertaking to acquire him in the course of employment;
  • Mastery of the French language both orally and in writing;
  • At ease in taking charge of a group, you know how to motivate teams;
  • Good mental and physical skills to manage sometimes difficult situations or conflicts while keeping a distance suitable for your function;
  • Mastering common computer tools;
  • Ability to manage stress;
  • Good teaching skills, strong emotional mastery and open mind;
  • Driver's licence holder. Our terms and conditions of employment require you to be a Swiss citizen or holder of a licence C, free of a deed of default of property (an extract from the Office of Prosecutions must be presented during the process), and your criminal record must be blank. There is a right to consult the criminal record under the Federal Criminal Records Act by the engagement authority for this function.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
