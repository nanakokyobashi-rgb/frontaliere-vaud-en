Role overview

At the heart of the Vaudian Judicial Order (OJV), you have a leading strategic and managerial role as the director of human resources of an entity of more than 1,800 collaborators and magistrates working in 33 judicial authorities and offices.

You develop and implement the OJV HR strategy in line with the needs and challenges of the various missions and in line with the HR strategy of the Cantonal Administration of Vaudoise (ACV), particularly in terms of the succession and development of the trades.
