Full description

As secretary of a decentralized unit, you participate in the good running of the primary and secondary school of Chavannes-near Rennes and St-Sulpice and collaborate continuously with teachers, students and parents. In this way you will contribute not only to the administrative efficiency, but also to the overall success of the educational mission entrusted to the Vaudois compulsory education.

  • Participate in the day-to-day management of the school secretariat;
  • HR monitoring of the teaching and administrative staff of the school;
  • Managing school accounts;
  • Enrolment and administrative monitoring of students;
  • To perform other tasks related to the life of the establishment. The work schedule is annualized and flexibility is requested to meet the needs of the school. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here . Do you have any questions? Mrs Stefania Hofmann, Director of the school, is happy to be at your disposal at 024 557 48 60.
