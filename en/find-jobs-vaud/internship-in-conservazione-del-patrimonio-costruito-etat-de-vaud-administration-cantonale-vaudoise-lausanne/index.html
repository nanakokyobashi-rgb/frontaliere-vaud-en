Role overview

As an intern, you will have the opportunity to do some of your Master's work within our Monuments and Sites Directorate (DMS) in order to bring practical elements to the theoretical knowledge acquired during your Master's studies in architecture, MAS in cultural heritage or any other equivalent training.

On a daily basis, you would be required to meet with conservators of built heritage whose activities are related to the analysis of building permits in connection with buildings or sites protected by heritage.
