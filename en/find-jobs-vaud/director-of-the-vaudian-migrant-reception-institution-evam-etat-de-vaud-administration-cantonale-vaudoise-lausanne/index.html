Full description

At the head of the EVAM, you run a major public institution at the heart of the Vaudian policy of welcoming, hosting, accompanying and integrating migrants. In a demanding context marked by the evolution of migration flows and a significant increase in its reception capacities, you define and direct the strategic directions of the establishment and drive the changes necessary for its development. Through your leadership, you federate and evolve the leadership teams and develop trusting relationships with the authorities and institutional partners in the service of the general interest.

  • To define and bring the establishment strategy into line with the guidelines of the State Council and the applicable legal framework.
  • Ensure the institution's overall governance, financial sustainability, risk control and efficient and responsible use of public resources.
  • Direct the Board of Directors and lead the organizational, digital and cultural transformations necessary for the establishment's development.
  • Ensure continuous improvement of reception, accommodation, support and integration services, adapted to the needs and in line with the tasks of the institution.
  • Pilot the implementation of the establishment's real estate strategy in order to ensure long-term reception capacity adapted to changing needs.
  • Represent EVAM with authorities and partners, develop trust relations with stakeholders and ensure credible and coherent institutional communication. For more details, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Vinciane Frund, Deputy Head of Service at the Population Service (SPOP) at + 41 21 316 46 96 / Human Resources of the Department of Economics, Innovation, Employment and Heritage (DEIEP) at the e-mail address: rh-deiep@vd.ch.
  • Master's degree in law, economics or public administration.
  • Further training in management.
  • Experience at the head of a large organisation subject to high standards of governance.
  • Evidence in terms of strategic leadership, change management and organizational transformation.
  • Strong financial management skills and ability to lead an institution in a particularly exposed institutional and political environment.
  • Knowledge of policy issues related to migration and asylum, as well as real estate, social and integration issues specific to the field.
  • Global and strategic vision, excellent analytical, synthesis and editorial capabilities, strong decision-making capacity, sense of responsibility and public service ethics.
  • Excellent relational skills, recognized leadership, developed political sense, strong sense for cross-sectional collaborations and excellent communication, negotiation and representation skills.
  • Mastery of French as well as very good knowledge of German and English. Miscellaneous
