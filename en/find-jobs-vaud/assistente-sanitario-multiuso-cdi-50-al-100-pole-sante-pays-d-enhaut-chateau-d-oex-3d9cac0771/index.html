Full description

Located at 1000 meters above sea level, the Country-D-Enhaut Health Centre offers a wide range of hospital services, accommodation, accompaniment for the elderly and home care. According to the so-called integrated care approach, this innovative organization represents an opportunity for health professionals to work in small multidisciplinary teams, for better continuity of care and in response to the health needs of the population of the Upper Country. In the heart of a privileged natural environment, less than an hour away from Lausanne or Bern, the Pays-d'Enhaut is renowned for its quality of life and offers a very wide range of sports, leisure and cultural activities, to live summer and winter! For its Accommodation Sector, the Health Centre of the Country-d-Enhaut is looking for several:

Multi-purpose health aids (CDI, 50 to 100%)

Your mission

  • Provide basic care;
  • To maintain the living environment of residents;
  • Collaborate with the multidisciplinary team to transmit the information inherent in the care of residents.

Your profile

  • Diploma of Auxiliary of Health (or title deemed equivalent by the Swiss Red Cross);
  • Quiet personality, showing empathy and patience;
  • Interest in the elderly.

We offer

  • Participation in the development of an integrated care model, involving the coordination and collaboration of different health professionals;
  • Work in a pleasant environment within a human-sized structure;
  • The benefits of living in the mountains while remaining close to urban centres (to be discovered under www.alpesvaudoises.ch/en/stories/ paysdenhaut)
  • Working conditions in accordance with the CLC san-vaud (13th salary, training, 5-6 weeks of vacation per year, etc...)
