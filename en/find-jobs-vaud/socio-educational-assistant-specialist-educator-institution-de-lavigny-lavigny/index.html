Social-educational assistant or specialized educator Deployed at several sites, the Lavigny Institution consists of a neurological hospital, a department of accommodation for adults with neurological impairment, a department of accommodation for people living with intellectual impairment and associated disorders, a special education school and a workshop and vocational training sector. La Passerelle is a specialized teaching school at the Lavigny Institution for children and adolescents aged 6 to 18 years in difficulty with development and learning. The objectives of the Gateway are to provide the child with comprehensive care, including school training, therapeutic interventions and follow-up and educational support. For the Special Education School La Passerelle, site de la Préformation à Rolle , we are looking for a Socio-educational assistant or specialized teacher 12 months to 60% CSD La Passerelle has two pre-training centres, located in Morges and Rolle. They welcome adolescents from the Vaud region who have completed their compulsory education. Before entering vocational training, these centres offer them time to explore and experiment with the world of work. For a varying duration, but at the maximum until their majority, young people are accompanied not only in their professional project, but also in the choices necessary for their adult life. The proposed route allows them to develop their critical spirit, their social and cultural life, guiding them towards autonomy guaranteeing them a true "place" in our world.
