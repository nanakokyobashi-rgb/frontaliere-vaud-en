Full description

What awaits you: Do you like human contact, personalized advice and business development? Do you want to support your customers during the important moments of their lives by offering them adapted insurance and pension solutions? Join our general agency Riviera and actively participate in our development. What you can achieve with us: As an insurance and pension advisor, you are responsible for monitoring, retaining and developing a portfolio of private and corporate clients in the Pully region and its surroundings. Listening to your customers' needs, you support them with professionalism and proximity by offering them personalized solutions in the areas of:

  • personal and property insurance;
  • private and professional insurance
  • Thanks to your network and your commercial sense, you develop new relationships of trust and actively contribute to the growth of the agency. What you bring:
  • Great professional ethics
  • AFA insurance intermediary certificate (all branches profile) or motivation to obtain it
  • Several years of professional experience, ideally in insurance consulting, foresight or financial services
  • Excellent sense of customer relations and commercial development
  • Regional network and local roots constituting a real advantage
  • Dynamic, positive and convincing personality
  • Ability to work independently while enjoying teamwork
  • French mother tongue or excellent command of French; any other language is an asset Working with us: We offer you varied and demanding work, offering great autonomy, in an exciting and diverse environment. To exercise this function, the law on insurance supervision requires possession of the AFA insurance intermediary certificate, profile for all branches. If this certificate is not available, we will be happy to help you by organizing tailor-made internal training courses. What we offer: Participation in the result Our success also benefits our employees. Each year, we decide on the payment of a profit sharing and its amount. Continuing training and development We offer a wide range of internal courses. We contribute to continuing training related to work. Benefits We offer in particular up to 25% off premiums, discounts on mobile phones, free flat-rate mobile subscriptions and various discounts on cultural events. Interested?: We look forward to receiving your application by email. We thank all the candidates for their interest; however, only applications matching the position criteria will receive a response.
