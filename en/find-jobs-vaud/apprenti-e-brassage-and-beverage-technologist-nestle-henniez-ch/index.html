Full description

Nestlé Waters (Switzerland) is the Swiss leader in the mineral water market with brands such as Henniez, S. Pellegrino, Vittel, Contrex, Perrier and Acqua Panna, as well as other leading brands of alcohol-free beverages. Nestlé Waters Switzerland trains about 20 apprentices per year.

Training in brewing and beverage technologist

(Food Technologist)

August 2026 – July 2029

Nestlé Waters (Switzerland) SA, Henniez

Summary of position

  • We offer a stimulating and varied learning environment to get your SWC and good training for your future
  • Learning is done in our brand new syrup factory, on the production line and in our laboratory
  • Very good coaching by qualified trainers
  • Various advantages: REKA cheques, half-rate offered train, fitness discount, Henniez vouchers, Nestlé Shop discount, in-house courses (informatics, stress management, postulation)
  • Six weeks of vacation
  • Attractive wages

One day in the life of a brewing and beverage technologist

  • Preparation of syrups and recipes for different drinks (fruit juices)
  • Receiving raw materials
  • Regular controls of machinery and products during the production process
  • Compliance with all safety measures and good manufacturing practices
  • Performance of day-to-day maintenance of facilities
  • Sampling for various chemical and microbiological analyses
  • Quality control

What Will Make Your Success

  • End of compulsory schooling with good results in math
  • Technical sense and taste for numbers
  • Manual capability
  • Be ready to work in shifts later
  • Ability to withstand working conditions with noise and heat
  • Position also open for retraining for professionals who have worked in the food industry (blanger, milk technologist, pastry maker)
  • French mother tongue

Looking for a varied apprenticeship with a good working atmosphere? Don't hesitate to apply! (Letter of motivation, CV and bulletin of notes for the last two years)
