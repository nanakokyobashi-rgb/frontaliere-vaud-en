Your role as Dental Assistant

  • Clinical assistance: To assist the dentist during the consultations, to perform certain practices according to his/her specialization. Disinfect and sterilize the instruments, to perform the x-rays
  • Care Management: To welcome patients, assist in the preparation and management of treatments, by monitoring patient records and compliance with clinical protocols.
  • Technical Innovation: Use state-of-the-art equipment and adapt quickly to new dental care technologies and methods. Collaborate with the Lab Lab Ardentis Laboratory.
  • Education and Prevention: Advise patients on good oral hygiene practices and contribute to their education.
  • Development and training: Participate in continuing training to keep up to date on the latest developments in the sector.
  • Various responsibilities within the clinic. Your profile:
  • Holder of a CFC dental assistant, with a confirmed experience (minimum 5 years).
  • Equivalence recognised by SEFRI with a minimum of 3 years of professional activity in Switzerland.
  • Passion for patient care, availability and ability to build a relationship of trust.
  • Has an interest in developing in a dynamic environment, with the emphasis on optimal working conditions, promoting knowledge sharing and mutual support, while seeking a fulfilling and enjoyable work experience.
  • Perfectly comfortable in French, good English skills a bonus, and an easy and engaging approach.
  • Excellent computer tool mastery.
