Full description

Mission

EPFL's Science Promotion Service (SPS) develops programs and activities for schools, young people, families and the general public to generate interest in science and technology and to strengthen the dialogue between science and society.

We are looking for a communications officer to coordinate and produce the communications activities of the service. In collaboration with the SPS teams, institutional partners and EPFL's communication services, this person will contribute to enhancing the visibility, coherence and impact of the SPS's programmes through creative communication, accessible and tailored to its various audiences.

Main tasks and responsibilities

  • Implement the SPS's communication actions to promote its activities among the various target audiences.
  • Design, produce and coordinate content for digital and print media.
  • Manage and animate websites and social networks.
  • Ensure consistency of messages and respect for EPFL's visual and editorial identity.
  • Design practical models, guides and tools to help teams produce coherent communication media that are consistent with EPFL's identity.
  • Contribute to the monitoring and improvement of communication activities.

Profile

You are an organized, creative and autonomous person with excellent editorial skills and a strong interest in scientific communication and promotion of science. You like to produce quality content, work as a team and coordinate projects with multiple stakeholders.

  • Bachelor or Master in communication, marketing, public relations or training deemed equivalent.
  • Experience in digital communication and project management.
  • Excellent writing skills in French and good command of English. Knowledge of German is a real asset.
  • Mastery of digital communication tools (management of websites, newsletters, social networks) and graphic creation tools (Adobe Creative Suite, Canva or equivalent).
  • Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously, define priorities and meet deadlines.
  • Excellent relational skills and sense of teamwork.
  • Organizational sense, rigour, autonomy and initiative.
  • Creativity, sense of message and ability to adapt communication to different audiences.
  • An experience in an academic, educational or institutional environment, as well as a good knowledge of the Swiss educational landscape, is an asset.
