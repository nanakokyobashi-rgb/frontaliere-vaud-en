Full description

Close to Morges station, in brand new premises, GO2, the Romaned Center of Surgery and Medicine of Sport and Arts was created by a specialized and passionate team. It is intended for adolescents who regularly engage in physical activity, regardless of their level, whether in leisure, club or elite. This team of doctors, surgeons or therapists also advises any teenager who wishes to start or resume physical activity.

In order to strengthen our versatile team, we are looking for the GO2 Centre, a-e:

Medical Assistant (M/F) at 80%

CDI, effective 1 December 2026

WE OFFER YOU

  • 5 weeks of annual vacation, 6 weeks from the age of 50. Possibility of redeeming an additional week of vacation, as well as 10 holidays offered.
  • Remuneration based on the valuation of your previous years of professional experience as well as seniority bonuses within the EHC.
  • Several proposals providing flexibility (unpaid leave, part-time leave, paid exchange time, etc.).
  • Sports activities (Pilates, walking races, discounts at various centres such as several fitness centres or the Bains de Lavey), events for the children of collaborators, a company drink located in Morges to share moments of conviviality during the beautiful days.
  • Internal transfer facilitated according to your desire for development.
  • Many in-house or certifying training courses available to you.

YOU MAY

  • A medical assistant CFC or equivalent.
  • Prior experience in a medical practice, in paediatrics an asset
  • Competencies in blood sampling and laboratory analysis.
  • A strong interest in administrative tasks, logistics and patient coordination.
  • Flexibility and versatility in order to provide doctors with quality support in carrying out routine and exceptional activities.
  • A good team spirit and a strong sense of responsibility.
  • Recognized skills in the use of computer tools and excellent spelling.
  • A strong team spirit, autonomy and the desire to evolve in an environment conducive to collegiality.

YOUR OBJECTIVES

  • Provide patient care and guidance.
  • Ensure a patient experience of excellence on a daily basis.
  • Provide care, provide clinical supervision of patients and assist physicians in specific interventional and non-interventional care.
  • Perform common diagnostic services: laboratory, ECG, Remler, Apnealink, etc.
  • Manage hotline and message take.
  • Manage billing of medical procedures.
  • To monitor all day-to-day administrative tasks involved in the proper functioning of the centre, such as managing emails and planning.
  • To guarantee the preparation, monitoring and archiving of patients' physical medical records
  • Conducting the typing, rereading and transmission of medical reports and test results to physicians in a timely manner.

WE ARE

  • An institution consisting of acute care and rehabilitation institutions, a private clinic, EMS, medical centres and permanences located in the region of La Côte and the first clinical hypnosis school dedicated to the care community in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A network of care allowing many opportunities for internal change according to the aspirations and development projects of employees.
  • More than 2,000 employees taking care of patients and residents of the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment conducive to the realization of projects.
  • A training company engaged in succession training, with about 80 apprentices in 16 different trades, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.
