Be a force for good and accelerate change that improves lives and the planet

As a Corporate Learning Excellence Lead at Nestlé, your mission is to define the learning standard, shape the curriculum and elevate the learner experience at Rive-Reine. You’ll make a difference by collaborating closely with programme leaders, capability-building partners, learning teams, campus colleagues and external academic partners. This is your opportunity to take ownership, grow your expertise, and deliver meaningful results that make a difference at scale.

How you’ll make an impact as a Learning Excellence Lead • Leading the Rive-Reine learning community and helping programme leaders deliver consistently high-quality learning experiences. • Shaping a future-focused curriculum that responds to evolving business and leadership capability needs. • Defining and embedding learning design standards across in-person, online and hybrid programmes. • Advancing learning technology and innovation to create engaging, effective experiences for senior leaders. • Maintaining Rive-Reine accreditation and quality assurance so learning programmes deliver recognized, measurable value. • Strengthening the Rive-Reine learning brand and its reputation within the global learning community.

What will make you successful • Extensive expertise in strategic learning design with the ability to translate business and leadership capability needs into compelling learning portfolios and the ability to set clear, practical quality standards. • Applying strong facilitation, programme and portfolio management capabilities, with quality assurance skills to complex learning portfolios. • Harnessing AI, learning technology and outside-in innovation to create more engaging, effective and future-focused learning experiences. • Bringing strong stakeholder influencing skills to collaborate confidently with senior leaders, cross-functional partners, learning communities and external institutions. • Communicating fluently in English; working proficiency in French is preferred. • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.
