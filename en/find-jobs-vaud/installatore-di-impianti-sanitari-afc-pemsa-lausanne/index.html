Your role

As a hydraulic installer, you will work in new construction, renovation, as well as in maintenance, repair and after-sales service. Reliable, clean and complying with current standards, while guaranteeing professional and quality service to customers.

Your main responsibilities

  • Pose, connect and put into operation the pipes of drinking water and drainage, as well as the different sanitary equipment
  • Perform cutting, welding, gluing, clinging and fixing according to the rules of art
  • Integrate systems into walls, floors and ceilings with precision, care and safety
  • Mount and connect the sanitary fixtures: WC, washbasins, showers, bath tubs, water heaters, washing machines, etc.
  • Make adjustments, seal controls and pressure tests
  • Strictly record the results of the controls
  • Diagnose faults and perform necessary repairs
  • Replace defective components, rape pipes and perform descaling jobs
  • Quickly intervene in case of emergency, while guaranteeing customer satisfaction
  • Respect environmental standards for differentiated collection, waste management and disposal

And now we're talking about you? You have a federal capacity certificate (CFC) as a health plant installer or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland. You have proven experience in the plumbing sector and master both new installations and renovation, maintenance, repair or after-sales service. Rigorous, autonomous and quality oriented, you can intervene effectively both in the yard and with customers, while guaranteeing an accurate, reliable and compliant job to the requirements of the profession.

Your profile

  • Federal Capacity Certificate (AFC) as hydraulic installer or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland
  • At least 3 years of proven experience in Switzerland in the hydraulic sector
  • Experience in municipal networks, water, disposal and public transport: a real advantage
  • Good mastery of installation, welding, connection and control techniques
  • Ability to read and understand technical plans or schemes
  • Strict, precise and quality oriented work
  • Autonomy in the performance of tasks
  • Good team spirit
  • Sense of service and professional attitude towards customers
  • Good physical fitness
  • Valid driving licence
  • Personal vehicle: an appreciated advantage

You are a reliable, attentive and conscientious person, able to intervene effectively both in the yard and in the repairs, with constant attention to the well done work and the satisfaction of the customer.
