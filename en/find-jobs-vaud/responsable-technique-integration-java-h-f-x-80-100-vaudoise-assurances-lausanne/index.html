Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. True to its cooperative nature and values, it encourages the creation of a healthy, inclusive and inspiring work environment, ideal for developing your talent. Convinced that the future is also in the digital world, Vaudoise Assurances has launched an ambitious digital transformation project with the aim of becoming #1 customer satisfaction. At the heart of our data recovery strategy, we are looking for a Technical Lead Java Integration - 80-100%. You integrate a team that develops and operates orchestrations and integrations via APIs between our operating systems with Java, Spring Boot and Azure. Your role is to be the technical reference in an experienced team based in Lausanne and Portugal. Proactive, open and curious, you like to work as a team in a multidisciplinary setting. Of an enthusiastic and client-oriented nature, you have ease of communication. You will play a key role in inspiring and empowering our team to embrace change, develop new skills and thrive in a changing technological landscape. The missions that await you:

  • Develop and maintain APIs and integrations with Java and Spring Boot
  • Deliver and maintain with Terraform, Terragrunt Azure infrastructure for team perimeter
  • Interact with the corporate architect, the technical architect, the project manager and the product owner to review the proposed architecture and provide solutions on implementation issues, with a view to defining an achievable and quality LLD (Low Level Design) meeting business needs
  • Control and organize the quality of the code
  • Be the technical reference of the team to help its peers in their activities Your profile:
  • Experience confirmed as a Java developer and Spring Boot back-end: API, microservices, system integration, Azure Container Apps, Azure Kubernetes
  • Experience with Terraform and Terragrunt to be able to deliver infrastructure as code.
  • Excellent understanding of the functioning of REST APIs, JSON models, HTTP calls and response status
  • Ability to read and understand technical specifications, architecture or code diagrams (Java, Swagger/OpenAPI...)
  • Experience in writing user stories and test cases in an agile setting (Scrum/Kanban)
  • Languages: English and French, minimum level B2 We offer you :
  • A healthy work environment within a mutual company certified Great Place to Work
  • Attractive conditions and competitive social benefits
  • Flexibility in work organisation
  • A stimulating framework for skills development and continuous improvement We look forward to receiving your complete application package digitally (with curriculum vitae, cover letter, copies of work certificates and diplomas)
