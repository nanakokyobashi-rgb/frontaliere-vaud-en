Full description

Policlinic, emergency, outpatient care, hospitalizations: from birth to old age, the Morges Hospital offers a wide range of care services. The establishment has 240 acute care beds, one maternity, with about 1,500 births per year, and nearly 30,000 emergency consultations. In all, more than 40 medical disciplines have been practiced in its walls for more than 150 years. Surrounded by vineyards, with an incredible view of the mountains and the lake, the Morges Hospital welcomes you in a warm and dynamic setting. We are looking for the Hospital of Morges, a: Chronic patient care coordinator and complex F/H situations at 80% to 100% YOUR MISSION In collaboration with family physicians, you independently monitor and coordinate patients with chronic diseases or with complex medical-psycho-social situations. With your sense of organization and initiative, you anticipate the needs of patients and actively contribute to improving their care path. You play a central role in the organisation of this journey to ensure the continuity, quality and economic efficiency of the services. WE OFFER YOU
