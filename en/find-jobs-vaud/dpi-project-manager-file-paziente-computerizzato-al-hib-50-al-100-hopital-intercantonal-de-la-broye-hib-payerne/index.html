Full description

Thanks to its 800 employees, the Broye Intercantonal Hospital (HIB) is responsible for acute care and rehabilitation. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the broyard population. As part of the deployment of the new Epic hospital information system, the HIB is looking for:

DPI project manager (computerised patient file) at the HIB 50% to 100%

Your Mission

  • Pilot and coordinate the preparation and deployment of Epic within the HIB, consistent with the objectives and schedule of the central programme
  • Plan and monitor local project activities, ensuring timeliness, resources, deliverables and budget within assigned responsibilities
  • Ensure coordination between the HIB Directorate, relevant departments, technical teams, trade reference staff and the central programme
  • Organize project governance, prepare follow-up sessions and report to the Directorate, including progress, risks and decisions to be made
  • Support the preparation of services and coordinate actions necessary to adapt processes, train and support users
  • Coordinate technical work and local integrations, particularly in relation to computer systems, biomedical equipment and external partners
  • Prepare and coordinate implementation, ensuring business continuity, risk management and follow-up of corrective actions after start-up

Your assets

  • Advanced training in project management, information systems, engineering, health or equivalent
  • Experience in conducting complex projects involving several trades and stakeholders
  • Experience in hospitals or in the highly desired health sector
  • Experience in deploying a clinical information system or computerised patient record; an implementation experience of Epic is a major asset
  • Good control of project management methods and tools, including planning, monitoring, reporting and risk management
  • Collaborative leadership, excellent communication skills and ability to unite stakeholders with diverse profiles
  • Acute sense of organization, rigour, autonomy and anticipation capacity
  • Excellent mastery of French, both oral and written; knowledge of English is an asset

Entry into office: to be agreed
