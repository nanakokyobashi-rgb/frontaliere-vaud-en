Location: Vers-chez-les-Blanc and EPFL sites, Lausanne, Switzerland Work model: On-site, with flexibility to work across both locations Contract type / duration: Internship, 6 months Activity rate: 100% Target start date: As soon as possible

Be a force for good and transform your potential into something extraordinary

As a Cell Biology and Nutrient Absorption Intern at Nestlé Research, your mission is to explore how dietary lipids are absorbed using an advanced human intestinal cell model and to generate insights that strengthen nutrition and health research. You will make a difference by collaborating closely with scientists, laboratory technicians, research assistants and multidisciplinary partners across Nestlé Research and local research facilities. This is your opportunity to make an impact while gaining hands on experience and mentorship to kick-start your career.

How you’ll make an impact as a Cell Biology and Nutrient Absorption Intern

  • Investigating how dietary lipids are absorbed using a human stem cell-derived intestinal model
  • Generating high-quality experimental data and mechanistic insights on lipid uptake, metabolism and bioavailability
  • Optimizing lipid preparation, cell-based experiments and accurate analytical quantification
  • Connecting experimental findings with scientific literature in intestinal biology, lipid metabolism and nutrition
  • Communicating results through clear analyses, reports, presentations and discussions with multidisciplinary partner

What will make you successful

  • Being a student or fresh graduate in Biology, Biotechnology, Biomedical Sciences, Nutrition, Life Sciences, or a related field.
  • Bringing a strong interest in gut physiology, lipid metabolism and advanced laboratory models that recreate biological processes
  • Having practical familiarity with molecular and cellular biology techniques, alongside data analysis and basic statistical tools
  • Being able to review and interpret scientific publications, while working with curiosity, precision, initiative and a collaborative mindset
  • Communicating fluently in English
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.
