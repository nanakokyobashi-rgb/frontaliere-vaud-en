Driver of motor vehicle cat. B100 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Make Switzerland move with us. Driver of motor vehicle cat. B100 Denges, 01.09.2026 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you. You will find varied work in a motivated team on interesting projects in the Neuchâtel region. We make it a point of honor to offer attractive working conditions, even with irregular hours. You view irregular hours and shift work as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. With us, night and weekend work entitles you to additional pay (e.g. 16 francs per hour for Sunday work). You also benefit from time increases. This means that you have more free time than employees who do not work nights or weekends. To find out about the other benefits we offer, such as child allowances, free 2nd class GA or even discounted travel abroad (also for families), go to our website under the “Employee benefits” section. What you can make move.

  • As a motor vehicle driver, you drive construction trains safely, while respecting the regulations in force.
  • You guarantee a high degree of safety within the team and respect work processes.
  • You are responsible for preparing and monitoring trains.
  • Your field of activity also extends to the capture and transmission of data relating to the operation of freight trains using the IT tools made available to you.
  • You carry out services related to our businesses according to production needs. What you bring.
  • You demonstrate great motivation, you are ready to take responsibility and you invest yourself with passion in your tasks. You focus your work on the pursuit of your objectives and set high standards for its quality.
  • Your communication skills allow you to transmit complex information in a clear and understandable manner and to meet the needs of different stakeholders.
  • Preferably, you have completed an apprenticeship (CFC) in a technical field and you have a cat driving license. B.
  • You want to invest in your professional development and you have successfully completed the B100 locomotive pilot training.
  • You communicate with ease in French and you have knowledge of German.
  • Your color vision is perfect (neither protanomaly nor deuteranomaly) and there is no entry in your criminal record or the prosecution register.
