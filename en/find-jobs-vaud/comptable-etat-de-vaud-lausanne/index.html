Full description

As an accountant within the departmental financial unit at the General Secretariat of the Department of Finance, Territory and Sport (SG-DFTS), you are responsible for coordinating and controlling the accounting management of the department and the SG-DFTS. You ensure that accounting transactions are maintained on a regular basis and that accounts are closed. You ensure the accuracy of accounting charges, compliance with accounting standards and rules, and follow instructions and procedures. You work closely with the other members of the Departmental Financial Unit and you are involved in the development of budget monitoring. You make sure that the inventory of state subsidies is compiled by monitoring and consolidating the information provided by the departments.

  • Monitor, record or validate the accounting operations of the SG-DFTS, the Office for External Affairs and the SG-Grand Conseil (Providers, debtors and creditors, banking, general and analytical accounts) as well as transactions related to unallocated incoming payments and internal transfers of the department.
  • Prepare, analyze and present the financial statements of the SG-DFTS, justify the balances and ensure quarterly closures and annual closure of the service accounts.
  • Coordinate quarterly closure and annual closure of departmental accounts. Perform the required departmental controls, ensure the compliant establishment of the closure annexes by the services and ensure the departmental consolidation in accordance with the instructions.
  • Prepare, analyse and present departmental accounting statements (balance sheet, operating accounts and annexes).
  • Accountancy advisor to the various departments of the department, support the Head of the Departmental Financial Unit and respond to enquiries. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Ms Nadine St-Pierre, Departmental Financial Officer at 021 316 45 28.
  • Holder of an SWC of a business employee.
  • Federal patent of financial specialist desired.
  • Minimum 5 years' experience in accounting.
  • Very good knowledge of Excel.
  • Use of IT management and office automation tools and databases.
  • Ability to work independently and as a team.
  • Resistance to stress, very good time management and priorities.
  • Compliance with standards and procedures.
  • A sense of public service responsibilities and ethics developed.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
