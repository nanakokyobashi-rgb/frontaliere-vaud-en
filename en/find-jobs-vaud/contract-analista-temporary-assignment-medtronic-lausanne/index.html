Full description

Careers that change lives start here. Medtronic is a global leader in healthcare technology with a Mission to alleviate pain, restore health, and extend life. Our 95,000 employees work across more than 150 countries to put patients first — developing innovative medical technologies that improve the lives of 72+ million patients each year. Your unique talents will help shape the future of healthcare while building a career grounded in purpose, growth, and impact. A Day in the Life The International Neuromodulation team partners with healthcare professionals (HCPs), healthcare organizations and internal stakeholders across Europe to support medical education, professional engagement and business initiatives. The team manages a high volume of HCP agreements and related operational processes, ensuring activities are executed in accordance with company procedures and compliance requirements.

This position supports the administration, coordination and tracking of HCP engagements across multiple markets, working closely with Commercial, Marketing, Medical Education, Legal, Compliance, Finance and Strategic Partnerships teams.

The role can be based in Tolochenaz, Switzerland, or Prague, Czech Republic, and follows a Flex work model. Up to 30% travel may be required for team meetings, training and business activities. This position provides operational support for the management of healthcare professional agreements and related engagement activities across International Neuromodulation. Working alongside a Senior Contract Analyst, the role coordinates contract workflows, maintains accurate records, supports reporting activities and collaborates with cross-functional stakeholders to ensure timely execution of HCP engagements.

Primary Responsibilities

  • Support preparation, processing, amendment, tracking and documentation of healthcare professional agreements.
  • Coordinate with internal stakeholders to collect required information and ensure timely progression of contract requests.
  • Monitor contract milestones, renewals, expirations and outstanding actions to support operational compliance.
  • Maintain accurate contract records, documentation repositories and system data.
  • Partner with Commercial, Marketing, Medical Education, Legal, Compliance and Finance teams to address contract-related inquiries.
  • Support reporting activities, budget tracking and healthcare professional service-cost monitoring.
  • Escalate contract issues, data discrepancies and process risks to appropriate stakeholders.
  • Contribute to process improvement, digitalisation and workflow efficiency initiatives.

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree with 0 years of experience OR Associate's degree with 2+ years of experience OR High School Diploma with 4+ years of experience in contract administration, operations, project coordination or related business support functions.
  • Fluent English communication skills, written and verbal.
  • Experience using Microsoft Office applications, including Excel.
  • Experience managing administrative processes, documentation or data records with a high degree of accuracy.
  • Experience coordinating activities across multiple stakeholders and priorities.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Degree in Business Administration, Management, Finance or Law.
  • Experience in contract administration, contract management or healthcare professional engagement processes.
  • Experience with HCP Engage, Onit or comparable contract lifecycle management systems.
  • Experience with reporting tools, workflow automation or digital process improvement initiatives.
  • Additional European language proficiency.
