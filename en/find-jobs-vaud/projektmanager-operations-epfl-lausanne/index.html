Full description

Mission

The Operations department manages and operates the real estate infrastructure and services to EPFL users. It is composed of about 80 employees active in CVSE-MCR techniques, Building Maintenance as well as in concierge and stewardship services.

Main tasks and responsibilities

Your duties and responsibilities as Project Manager Operations:

  • Develop tenders in collaboration with EPFL's purchasing department for the acquisition of services, equipment or supplies for operation, as well as for the execution of studies or works.
  • Conduct or participate, in collaboration with internal or external project managers, in studies to define or develop technical infrastructure in the field.
  • Manage and implement projects for the transformation and renovation of technical infrastructure in the field.
  • Participate in the coordination of the operation of technical infrastructure in large-scale projects.
  • Implement an active technology watch on technology, standards and methodologies that impact exploitation, propose developments.
  • Create or modify technical documentation to standardize infrastructure and to ensure effective interaction between project and operation.
  • Provide technical support to unit heads and heads of operating groups.

Profile

Ingénieur.e HES, technician ET or equivalent training and experience in the relevant fields.

  • Confirmed experience in project management in relevant areas
  • Strong social and communication skills to interact effectively internally and externally
  • Strong sense of service, cross-cutting collaboration, ability to find solutions and manage multiple priorities under pressure with a range of stakeholders
  • Excellent organizational ability, logical mind, rigorous and methodical
  • Very good mastery of French
  • English level B1, an asset
