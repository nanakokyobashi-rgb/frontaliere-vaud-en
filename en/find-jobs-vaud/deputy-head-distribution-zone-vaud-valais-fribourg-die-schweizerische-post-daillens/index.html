This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

You combine leadership, an eye for the big picture and results orientation and thus contribute to the management and further development of the distribution zone. You have direct responsibility for multiple distribution areas and play a key role in performance, organizational development and cross-divisional projects. With you, we drive our delivery organization forward sustainably!

You can do that.

  • Three to four managers are directly subordinate to you. Together with them, you will assume leadership responsibility for 700 to 1200 employees. You set goals and accompany the development of the leaders and their teams.
  • You are responsible for the operational, qualitative, financial and human results of the distribution areas assigned to you and ensure the efficient use of resources.
  • You support zone management in the control, management and further development of the distribution zone.
  • Preparation, moderation and follow-up of management meetings contribute to clear decision-making and efficient functioning of the distribution zone.
  • You lead or accompany cross-divisional projects and orders at regional and national level and support the implementation of organizational changes.
  • In the absence of the zoning management, you ensure the deputy within the framework of the delegated competences to ensure the continuity of the management and coordination.

You bring that with you

  • You know the delivery processes and the operational environment of delivery very well and have proven experience in the management of medium to large organizations with more than 100 employees.
  • A higher vocational training at the level of higher specialist examination or higher technical school, a Bachelor or an equivalent education form your professional basis.
  • You have proven competences in leadership, coaching and leadership development as well as in performance management.
  • You convince with strong leadership skills and understand how to assume leadership responsibility in the line, in technical topics and across departments – even if there are different interests or conflicting goals.
  • You are familiar with the management of budgets, costs and resources, as well as the development of decision bases for complex questions and their implementation.
  • You communicate orally and in writing very well in a first national language and have good knowledge of a second national language.

In order to promote gender diversity, we are particularly pleased about applications from women.
