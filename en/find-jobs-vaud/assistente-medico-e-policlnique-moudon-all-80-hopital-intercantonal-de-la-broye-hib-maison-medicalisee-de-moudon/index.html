Full description

Thanks to its 800 collaborators, the Broye Intercantonal Hospital (HIB) is responsible for acute care and rehabilitation. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the broyard population. To meet the many challenges ahead, the HIB is looking for:

Medical Assistant-e Policlnique Moudon at 80%

Your Mission

Ensure the organisation and operation of medical and paramedical consultations within the HIB Policlinic in Moudon.

Your activities

  • Welcome, guide and announce the arrival of patients to the different doctors
  • Organize all administrative aspects related to Policlinic (preparation of patient file, management of insurance reports, scanning of the various medical documents)
  • Organize physician agendas
  • Write consultation reports and billing
  • Care for patients (technical and care assistance for counselling, ECG, sampling and injections and basic care)
  • Conduct laboratory analysis and development of digital X-rays

Your assets

  • You benefit from a Federal Certificate of Medical Assistant Capacity (FCC) as well as a solid experience in medical practice.
  • With a sense of listening and contact, your organizational and relational skills allow you to offer a quality reception and to adapt to the demands of the field of a clinic.
  • For this position, a very good command of French (spoken and written), as well as the use of the usual computer tools is indispensable. (Institutional software: Opale, Soarian, Ultragenda and Winscribe.

Entry into office: immediately or to be agreed
