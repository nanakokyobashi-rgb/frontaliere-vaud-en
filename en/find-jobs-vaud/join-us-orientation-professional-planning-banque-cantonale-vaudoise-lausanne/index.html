Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Join the first universal bank in the canton of Vaud, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region.

Announcement The program Join us offers the opportunity for people with no experience in banking or foresight to train for a year in the course of employment, to embark on a promising new career in banking. Your opportunity:

  • Achieving a global vision of the Bank through intensive theoretical and practical training
  • Valuing your past achievements and experiences
  • Step by step building a new career Join us: Orientation Professional planning Your one-year training will consist of a gradual immersion in the services of the Professional Planning Department, which brings together all the activities of administration of the bank's pension funds in this field. This phase will allow you to acquire the basic knowledge of administrative management, benefit management, pension fund accounting, in particular their regulatory and procedural dimensions. Co-coached by a department manager, you will be required to develop your skills in the area of the 2nd pillar, as well as your autonomy and your ability to solve the practical cases of the day-to-day professional provision. At the end of the training, if successful, the knowledge acquired (tools, rules and processes) will allow you to move to many positions within the Bank to obtain an indeterminate employment contract. Your profile:
  • Administrative training or equivalent work experience
  • Bilingual German / French
  • Learning and adaptability
  • Experience in insurance, sickness funds or an administrative field, an asset Do you adhere to BCV values? Are you enthusiastic in nature and that you feel ready to invest in demanding and quality training? Your journey and aspirations match the criteria? Please send us your application. This program will begin on September 1, 2026 on our Prilly site.
