Full description

GHOL is the reference hospital for the German West with its structures on Nyon, Rolle and St-Cergue. Tomorrow, we propose to continue your professional development by involving you in your new missions and by participating in the development of GHOL.

Description of the post

Within the Finance and Administration Department, you coordinate and support the secretariats of the Specialized Consultations. You are the operational relay between the secretaries, the executives of the Outpatient Centres and your hierarchy. You work closely with the head of outpatient medical secretariats. In your new duties, your missions have an impact on the quality of patient care. Your role will include:

  • Managing the proximity of day-to-day planning according to the different secretariat posts
  • Perform secretariat duties according to planning, including replacing absent secretaries
  • Set up consultation rooms
  • Train and support secretaries
  • Develop ways to optimize secretariat practices
  • Identify and resolve operational problems encountered
  • Update secretariat procedures

As a referent, you

  • Work closely with the head of outpatient medical secretariats, who provides overall secretariat planning
  • Ensure proximity management of daily planning: organization of breaks, management of absences and assignment of positions according to workload
  • Ensure delegation of duties in case of absence, among secretaries
  • Master the tasks and practices of the medical secretariats and ensure their knowledge by the secretaries
  • Ensure the quality of secretariat services and timeliness
  • Follow existing institutional procedures
  • Respect and enforce schedule, schedules and breaks by secretaries
  • Represent a constructive and motivating force to the teams

Profile required

We are confident of your rapid success in your future functions through your:

  • At least 2 years experience as medical, administrative or executive secretary
  • At least 2 years experience in team management
  • Good knowledge of the functioning of medical secretariats
  • Excellent mastery of computer tools and calendar structuring
  • Mastery of French and medical terminology
  • Ability to analyse practices and propose adjustments, plan and meet deadlines
  • Assistance in the development of procedures and working materials and in the implementation of projects
  • Polyvalence
  • Leadership, anticipation, proposal strength, institutional vision
  • Commitment, adaptability, professional awareness, stress management, perseverance
  • Equity, respect, listening, pedagogy and confidentiality

Level of training

  • Diploma of medical secretary and CFC trade, or training deemed equivalent
  • Team management training is an asset

Benefits

You perform interesting and diverse tasks, in direct collaboration with the medical staff of the department. You are in a central position, within a dynamic service and constantly looking for developments for the quality of patient care.
