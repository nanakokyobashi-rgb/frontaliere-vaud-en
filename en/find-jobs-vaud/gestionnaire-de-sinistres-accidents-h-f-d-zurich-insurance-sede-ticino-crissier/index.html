137038380 - 100%Crissing, Vaud

The idea of offering our customers a positive and reassuring experience from the first exchange motivates you?

Within the LAA and LAAC accident management team of the Life Insurance Department at Zurich, you are the first point of contact and you manage our customers' accidents.

You take care of the files with listening, professionalism and sense of responsibility. For you, accompanying our clients is not just about dealing with a disaster: it is also an opportunity to create and maintain a lasting relationship of trust.

At Zurich, you have a varied and stimulating job, while continuously developing your skills. Taking care of our clients and working closely with your team are an integral part of your daily life.

You are organized, efficient and have experience in managing personal insurance claims? Then we'd be happy to meet you.

Your missions

  • Independent and client-oriented management of LAA and LAAC accident records, including decision writing.
  • Application of the payment of benefits in accordance with legal and contractual provisions.
  • Close collaboration with our various internal and external partners
  • Correspondence management and interviews with the various parties involved in the settlement of a claim: insured persons, employers, medical services, other insurers, lawyers, etc.
  • Identification of remedies with responsible third parties and other insurers.

Your profile

  • Business training completed; A federal social insurance patent is an asset.
  • Professional experience in managing claims files, ideally in personal insurance.
  • Knowledge of the Swiss social and private insurance system.
  • Reliable and empathic personality, with excellent negotiating skills and a professional attitude towards customers.
  • Mastery of French, both oral and written; good knowledge of German and English is an asset.
