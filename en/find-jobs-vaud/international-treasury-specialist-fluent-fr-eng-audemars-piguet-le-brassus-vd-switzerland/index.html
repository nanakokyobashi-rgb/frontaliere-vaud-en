Full description

Under the responsibility of the Director Tax & Treasury you will contribute to the development of the Group's Treasury function within its various subsidiaries. You will participate in activities related to intra-group financing, liquidity monitoring, cash forecasting, financial coordination and cash reporting. You will play a key role in strengthening the links between the Group Treasury, Accounting, Taxation, Legal and Local Finance teams, fostering effective collaboration between the various stakeholders. In a context of transformation and structuring of the Treasury function, you will contribute to improving the Group's cash visibility, monitoring intra-group flows and the deployment of treasury processes and tools to the various international entities.

In this context, your activities are

  • Prepare recurring cash reports for subsidiaries, cash forecasts, intra-group positions and Group liquidity.
  • Participate in the coordination between Treasury and Accounting, including through the reconciliation of financial data, periodic closures, audit work and continuous improvement of controls and treasury processes
  • Act as a point of contact Cash for subsidiaries and coordinate cash flow with local Finance teams.
  • To monitor liquidity positions, cash requirements, short-term financing as well as intra-group loans, deposits and flows, also participating in intra-group interest calculations and cash flow planning.
  • Contribute to improving the quality of cash flow forecasts, analyse variances between forecasts and accomplishments, and identify key explanatory factors
  • Participate in the deployment of the future Treasury Management System (TMS) to subsidiaries by contributing to data preparation and validation, user testing, training, post-deployment support and identification of opportunities to automate and standardise cash processes.
  • You have a Bachelor in Finance, Accounting, Economics or equivalent
  • Specialized certification is an asset.
  • You have 3 to 6 years of experience in finance, accounting, treasury, control or audit.
  • You have evolved in an international, multi-entity and multi-currency environment.
  • You have experience in cash forecasting, liquidity monitoring, intra-group flow and financial reporting.
  • You have a good knowledge of accounting processes, financial closures and audits.
  • You master Excel and are comfortable with financial data analysis.
  • You know best ERP, reporting or treasury tools (TMS) such as M3, Tagetik or equivalent.
  • You are able to document processes and contribute to their continuous improvement.
  • You are fluent in French and English
  • German or any other language is an asset.
