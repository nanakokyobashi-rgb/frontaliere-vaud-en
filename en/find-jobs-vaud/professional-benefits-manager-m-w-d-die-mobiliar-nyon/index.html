This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

What awaits

In this position, you work within the Benefits team of the Planvance in Nyon who is responsible for managing the benefits linked to the 2nd pillar for our clients (pension funds, collective foundations and joint fund).

What you can do with us

Your main task is to assess, process and coordinate 2nd pillar benefits.

You are also responsible for processing customer requests (by telephone/in writing) in collaboration with different interlocutors (daily allowance insurance, doctors, employers, disability insurance, accident insurers).

Thanks to your open and solution-oriented personality, you help strengthen our "Dear Mobilier" image by putting the customer at the centre of your concerns.

What you bring

  • SWC Commercial Employee (Federal Social Insurance Specialist Patent is an asset)
  • Some years of experience in social insurance or occupational insurance
  • Curiosity and interest in the topics of welfare and social insurance
  • Dynamic personality, autonomous with a strong team spirit
  • Excellent knowledge of German and a good knowledge of French

Working with us

The Professional Planning Service Services team is made up of motivated specialists who look forward to counting you among them. They work together in a pleasant atmosphere and with a strong team spirit.

Your occupancy rate is 100% with access to flexible working time models and home office. Your workplace can be chosen between Nyon and Zurich. Below you will learn more about our commitment as an employer.

What we offer

Balance of working life - privacy

We apply flexible work models and allow 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave. We also offer above-average family allowances and a sustainable pension fund.

Continuing training and development

We offer a wide range of in-house courses and allow you to participate in possible programs abroad. We contribute generously to external continuing training related to work.

Benefits

We offer up to 25% discount on premiums, half-rate subscription, discounts on mobile phones, free flat-rate mobile subscriptions and discounts on cultural events.
