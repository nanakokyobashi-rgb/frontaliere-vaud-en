Mission Your future playground You will join the Lausanne Bel-Air store, opened in October 2024 , made up of a team of 28 employees. Passionate about sport and challenges? Integrate Decathlon Lausanne Bel-Air: a growing store and a unique working environment in the heart of our Olympic capital. At the heart of our strategy, the Caisse et Accueil area, composed of 3 passionate athletes, is not only a simple crossing point, it is here that you will act as a true ambassador to make every athlete live a unique and 100% personalized customer experience. Join an adventure that continues to grow Decathlon in Switzerland, it is a success story that started in 2017 in Neuchâtel. Since then, we have opened about ten stores a year to reach 50 outlets today. And we're not going to stop there! Our expansion continues, especially in German-speaking Switzerland, offering career opportunities throughout the country. Why is this job for you? Looking for a moving profession, where you share your passion for sports from morning to night? You want to be proud to advise the right equipment and see your client go away with a smile? Do you like action, terrain and teamwork? Your 3 great benefits to us:

  • You're at the heart of the action. Your daily life is 100% in the field. You're Decathlon's face. Whether it's welcoming a beginner or advising an expert, you share your passion and create the link.
  • You're inventing tomorrow's business: You're not just selling. You offer durable solutions: hardware rental, second-life buyback or digital orders. You're acting for sport and the planet.
  • You are autonomous and responsible: It's your department! You manage your inventory, you showcase your novelties and you organize your business operations. Your impact is immediately apparent on your customers' satisfaction. What You Will Do Every Day: Your Missions
  • The advice above all: You welcome, listen and direct the athletes to the perfect product, whether on the shelf or through our digital tools. You also offer our loyalty program and manage the cash flow.
  • An impeccable ray: You install the products, manage the "facing" and make your sales space attractive, dynamic and adapted according to the seasons.
  • Stock control: From the reception of the pallets to the dispatch, you guarantee that the customer always finds what he wants. You're also preparing web orders.
  • Circular economy: You are the ambassador of our new models, rental and second-hand purchase to offer a second life to equipment for example.
  • Specific missions: Depending on your wishes, you can become a reference on a subject, trainer for your colleagues or even help with the overall management of the store. This job isn't for you if...
  • You'd rather sit behind a screen all day in peace.
  • You don't like going spontaneously to others.
  • You think unpacking pallets, tidying a radius and processing merchandise "that's not your problem."
  • You don't want to work on Saturdays.
  • You are looking for fixed and routine schedules: here we live in the rhythm of sport and our schedules adapt to the activity (even if we communicate them to you 6 weeks in advance so that you can organize!). Profile What we expect from you:
  • You have an excellent relationship. Customer satisfaction is your compass.
  • You're bold, curious, and you always want to learn.
  • You're effective and you know how to manage your priorities in a fast-moving environment.
  • You're passionate about sports: it's company DNA!
  • You have perfect command of French. Mastering English/German is a real plus. What we offer: (Small accuracy: the amounts and benefits below are valid for a 100% and will logically be proratized according to your activity rate!). We list below the main advantages.
