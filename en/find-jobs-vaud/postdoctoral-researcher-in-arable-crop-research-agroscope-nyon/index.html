Additional Information

The Agricultural Technology and Varieties Research Group develops sustainable production techniques as well as new varieties adapted to climate change for the most important arable crops, which are grown according to various production methods (integrated production, labels or organic farming). To fulfill this mission, the group conducts trials, mainly in the open, from Lake Geneva to Eastern Switzerland.
