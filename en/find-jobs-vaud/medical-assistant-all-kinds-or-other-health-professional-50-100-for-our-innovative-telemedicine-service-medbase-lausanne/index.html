Welcome to Medbase Lausanne-Malley

Located a stone's throw from the Prilly-Malley train station, the Medbase Lausanne-Malley medical center offers a full range of medical and therapeutic services ranging from general medicine to gynecology and pediatrics, to the care of sportspeople and athletes of all levels. In our medical center, we value communication, adaptability, reliability and empathy. You will join a team with a pleasant, active and supportive atmosphere, where mutual assistance is essential. Due to the different medical specialties present in our center, you will have the opportunity to manage a multitude of tasks, making each day unique and stimulating. We work in a modern environment, ideally located close to all amenities and easily accessible by car or public transport. Medbase Lausanne-Malley favors a good balance between professional and private life. It is possible to work part-time in all functions and the working schedule can be arranged flexibly. For doctors, collaboration is possible under employee or independent status, to be agreed during the interview.

Your missions

  • Be the first point of contact for patients and represent the image of Medbase Telehealth
  • Ensure the triage of requests and making appointments
  • Participate in the organization and coordination of daily activities
  • Manage correspondence with patients
  • Carry out advisory interviews according to internal procedures, after appropriate training
  • Actively participate in continuous improvement and quality assurance processes
  • Collaborate closely with medical centers and pharmacies of the Medbase group

Your profile

  • Training as a medical assistant, ASSC, pharmacy assistant or other recognized training in the health field in Switzerland
  • At least two years of professional experience
  • Very good command of German; any other language is an asset
  • Ability to work independently, rigorously and reliably
  • High sense of responsibility and service
  • Flexibility and ability to adapt to a dynamic and constantly changing environment
  • Team spirit and ease in interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Good command of IT tools and modern means of communication
  • Excellent interpersonal skills, empathy and patient orientation

What we offer you

  • A good work-life balance thanks to flexible hours and teleworking possibilities
  • Opportunities for continuing training and development within the Medbase group
  • The opportunity to participate in the development of innovative health solutions
  • A modern working environment within a growing company
  • The opportunity to make a concrete contribution to accessible and sustainable healthcare

#WeAreMedbase

To accomplish great things, you need to have a vision for the future. 20 years ago, Medbase was founded with the idea of ​​creating a healthcare network for all of Switzerland. From a medical practice with three employees in 2001, we have today grown into a 360-degree healthcare provider, with around 4,000 employees across 170 locations, operating in four business areas: medical and dental centers, pharmacies, drug distribution and digital health platform. Take the opportunity to continue this success story with us, as Medbase continues to grow and improve the future of healthcare in Switzerland every day.
