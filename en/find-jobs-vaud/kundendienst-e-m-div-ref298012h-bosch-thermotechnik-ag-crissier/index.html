Your tasks

  • You organise and coordinate the daily planning of the technicians.
  • You enter and update customer data and systems in SAP.
  • You enter and process service reports in MODI.
  • You receive telephone calls and respond to customer inquiries.
  • You handle customer enquiries by telephone, e-mail or other communication channels.
  • You actively participate in the continuous improvement of our processes.
