Your assignment

  • Drive a rubberized or cyngled hydraulic excavator depending on the needs of the yard.
  • Perform earth movement, excavations and leveling.
  • Perform excavation work in compliance with the required projects and levels.
  • Participate in work related to networks, pipelines and external accommodation.

  • Collaboration with the teams on the ground to ensure the proper conduct of the work.
  • Strictly apply safety standards on site.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Owner of the M2 operator license recognised in Switzerland.
  • Experienced in driving hydraulic excavators.
  • Good mastery of land and excavation work.
  • Ability to work independently and accurately.
  • Versatile, reliable, punctual and conscientious person.
  • Good team spirit and compliance with safety standards.
  • Driving license and vehicle: an advantage.
  • Immediate availability
