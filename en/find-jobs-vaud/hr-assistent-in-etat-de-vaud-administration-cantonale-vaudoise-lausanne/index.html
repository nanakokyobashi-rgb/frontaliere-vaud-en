Full description

Directly attached to the HR Unit of the General Secretariat of the Department of Institutions, Culture, Infrastructure and Human Resources (SG-DICIRH), the future HR Assistant will play a key role in the management of the administration of SG-DICIRH staff, related entities, as well as the Cantonal Child Care Service (SCAJE).

  • Assume the administration of DICIHR General Secretariat (SG-DICIHR) staff and related entities as well as the Cantonal Child Care Service (CJEC) and perform the day-to-day duties of the HR Unit Secretariat.
  • Independently manage processes related to time management, absences and training and contribute to the advice of managers, collaborators and collaborators.
  • To ensure recruitment processes for SG-DICIRH collaborators and related entities, as well as SCAJE with line lines. Provide administrative support in recruitment processes and arrange for the reception of new collaborators.
  • Provide specific activities and support to the departmental HR manager in the implementation of HR processes, promotion and improvement of HR tools and practices within the SG-DICIHR and the department.
  • Assume the role of Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) Correspondent for the SG-DICIHR.
  • SWC employed in commerce supplemented by the Personnel Management Assistant Certificate or equivalent.
  • Work experience of 3 years in a position of HR assistant, ideally within the cantonal administration of Vaud.
  • Innate sense of confidentiality, discretion, precision and rigour
  • Perfect oral and written mastery of French.
