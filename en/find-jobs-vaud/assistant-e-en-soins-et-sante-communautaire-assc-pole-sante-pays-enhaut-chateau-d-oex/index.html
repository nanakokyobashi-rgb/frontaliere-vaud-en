Full description

Located at 1000 meters above sea level, the Country-D-Enhaut Health Centre offers a wide range of hospital services, accommodation, accompaniment for the elderly and home care. According to the so-called integrated care approach, this innovative organization represents an opportunity for health professionals to work in small multidisciplinary teams, for better continuity of care and in response to the health needs of the population of the Upper Country. In the heart of a privileged natural environment, less than an hour away from Lausanne or Bern, the Pays-d'Enhaut is renowned for its quality of life and offers a very wide range of sports, leisure and cultural activities, to live summer and winter! For its Home Care Sector, the Country Health Department is looking for a:

Community Health and Care Assistant (CHS) Activity rate 80 - 100%

Entry into office: 1 October 2026 In principle within the home care sector, you will also have to carry out one-off missions within the accommodation and hospital sectors.

Your mission

  • Provide basic care and medical-technical care within your competence
  • Accompanying clients in daily activities
  • Observe client health and provide relevant information to the team
  • Participate in the implementation and monitoring of care plans
  • Promote the autonomy and home care of clients
  • Ensure thorough and complete documentation of the services performed
  • Actively participate in quality and continuity of care
  • Collaborate with the multidisciplinary team in client care

Your profile

  • Holder of an SWC of Community Health Care Assistant (CHS) or an equivalent title recognized in Switzerland
  • Interest in home care and support for people with varied needs
  • Autonomy, responsibility and adaptability
  • Good relational skills and listening
  • Ability to work as an interdisciplinary team
  • Organization, reliability and flexibility
  • Valid driver's licence and personal vehicle available for service

We offer

  • Participation in the development of an integrated care model, involving the coordination and collaboration of different health professionals
  • Work in a pleasant environment, within a human-sized structure
  • The benefits of living in the mountains while remaining close to urban centres (to be discovered under www.alpesvaudoises.ch/en/stories/ paysdenhaut)
  • Working conditions in accordance with the CLC san-vaud (13th salary, training, 5-6 weeks of vacation per year, etc...)
