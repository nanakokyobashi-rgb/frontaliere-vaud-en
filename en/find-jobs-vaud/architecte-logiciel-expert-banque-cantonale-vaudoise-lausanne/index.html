What if your professional history goes on at BCV's IT? Put your skills and experience at the service of one of the few banks that develops, maintains and ensures the security of its own Information System. The endless and varied professional opportunities within the 3 IT departments offer you the opportunity to carry out strategic projects, which will contribute to the success of the entire bank. For our IT Departments, we are looking for one or more: Announcement Architect Experienced software - Digital Banking with a solid technical experience and a good understanding of security issues, to support strategic projects within our bank's digital ecosystem Your main missions

  • design architectures to meet business needs in close collaboration with Product Owners and development teams,
  • integrate safety, resilience, performance and scalability aspects into the proposed solutions from the design stage,
  • ensure alignment of solutions with the Bank's corporate architecture strategy and standards,
  • actively participate in the technological choices and modernisation of the digital platform (containerization, API, DevSecOps, Microservices...),
  • contribute to the SRE / DevOps / Agile culture of the team as a technical expert and referent in architecture. Your profile
  • Graduate degree in computer science (Master or equivalent),
  • proven experience in digital solution architecture (microservices architecture, web/mobile frontends, REST APIs, cloud patterns, CI/CD...),
  • proven knowledge in application cybersecurity (OWASP, IAM, cryptography, mobile security...),
  • experience in the Swiss banking or financial sector and a good understanding of the associated regulatory constraints,
  • customer orientation, sense of service, ability to popularize technical issues,
  • mastery of French and English. German is an asset,
  • Software architecture or application security certifications are most appreciated. If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
