Specialist water disposal facilities and connection channel manager (m/f/d) 60-100%

Move Switzerland with us. Specialist water disposal facilities and connection channel manager (m/f/d) Renens VD / Work Smart, 01.01.2027 or suitable, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

We ensure that the railway infrastructure remains safe and available: we monitor the condition of the tracks, the traction current, the structures and we quickly launch the necessary measures to ensure their safety and availability. We represent the owner's interests during third-party work on and near the tracks, and guarantee the safety of the yards. After projects, we manage fault-free facilities and correct anomalies and provide data management teams to improve quality. What you can move.

  • On a daily basis, you ensure the availability, safety and regulatory compliance of water disposal facilities (track drains, emergency valves, flow limiters, retentions, exits), as well as connecting line structures, and intervene when necessary.
  • You prepare, mandate and supervise external service providers as well as internal teams for maintenance and repair of water disposal facilities, ensuring cost, timeliness and quality. You continue to enrich the inventory in the dedicated tool.
  • As a project manager, you monitor the safety of in-house constructions and third-party structures affecting the track or discharge of water and verify that there are no faults before they resume.
  • You plan the monitoring of connecting routes, conduct state assessments (road, drainage, works of art) and provide the owner with the necessary maintenance and investment measures from the inspection data.
  • You manage maintenance budget and service billing, announce major projects to Project Management and contribute to continuous process improvement.

What you bring.

  • Autonomous, responsible and endowed with a great ability to learn, you make quick decisions and act effectively on the ground.
  • You have excellent relational ease and initiative, and you do not hesitate to contact and coordinate external contacts.
  • Several years' experience in the operation, maintenance or management of railway infrastructure, water disposal systems or works of art is required; experience in provider coordination and project management is an asset.
  • You have advanced technical training (HES, ES, university) or CFC-type training with several years of experience in the rail sector.
  • You are fluent in French (minimum C1 level). A good knowledge of German and/or Italian would be an important asset in order to be able to participate in different working groups at national level.
  • You are available to travel in the western region.
