Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. True to its cooperative nature and values, it encourages the creation of a healthy, inclusive and inspiring work environment, ideal for developing your talent. Attached to the Head of Transverse Delivery, the Head of Release Management & Testing ensures the quality and control of the company's IT deployments. At the head of teams dedicated to testing and releases, you define practices, pilot delivery plans and accompany the evolution of the field in a logic of continuous improvement, automation and innovation. This position is intended for a leader able to combine recognized expertise in testing and release management, both dimensions being at the heart of the function The missions that await you:

  • Define and evolve the Release Management & Testing strategy to ensure the quality, reliability and control of IT deployments
  • Pilot testing activities (functional testing, integration and automation) ensuring continuous improvement of methods, tools and practices
  • Build and orchestrate the annual release plan, ensuring alignment and coordination of the various stakeholders of Delivery
  • Supervise deployments in test and production environments, while ensuring effective risk and incident management
  • Ensuring the availability, stability and governance of the environments required for project and technical teams
  • Contribute to the transformation of the field by conducting a reflection on the operating model, automation, the evolution of testing practices and the opportunities offered by the AI
  • Manager and develop an organisation composed of internal and external teams, promoting the development of skills, commitment and talent Your profile:
  • You have a proven experience in the areas of Release Management and Testing, with a strong understanding of the deployments in the testing and production environment
  • You master the driving of internal and external testing teams as well as the different types of tests (functional, integration, NFT, NRT), and know how to move the practices towards more efficiency and automation
  • You have demonstrated your ability to build, plan and enforce a release plan in an environment involving multiple stakeholders.
  • A recognized leader, you can recruit, develop and support your teams while promoting collaboration and skills development.
  • You are able to synthesize complex topics and communicate effectively with a variety of people, both in writing and in oral form.
  • Curious and continuously improving, you are interested in the evolution of testing practices, automation, new tools and opportunities offered by AI
  • You have advanced computer training and a good knowledge of testing tools such as Tosca and GT-F

Professional English completes your profile We offer you

  • A healthy working environment in a mutual company labeled "Best Employer"
  • Multiple social benefits including flexibility in work organisation
  • Working in a dynamic and passionate team
  • Human values focused on the well-being of employees We look forward to receiving your complete application package digitally (with curriculum vitae, cover letter, copies of work certificates and diplomas).
