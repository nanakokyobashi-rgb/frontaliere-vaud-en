Full description

Attached to the Chef d'Atelier and under the direct supervision of the Team Leader, you join our Pre-mounting workshop within the Movement department. Your main mission is to make beading of watch components (bridges and plates), thus contributing directly to the level of finishing and the excellence that characterize our parts.

In this context, your main responsibilities will be

  • Ensure the commissioning, adjustment and execution of operations on bead machines.
  • Perform decoration operations in compliance with quality standards and production requirements.
  • Ensure self-control of your production and guarantee the conformity of the completed parts.
  • Organize your activity according to the objectives of quality, quantity and time.
  • Actively participate in the continuous improvement of the Workshop.
  • Collaborate effectively with the various actors in production to contribute to collective performance.
  • To benefit from a successful experience in the watch industry, you have good practical knowledge of beading.
  • You have at least two years of experience in a similar function.
  • With excellent manual skill and a high degree of detail, you perform precise work that meets our quality requirements.
  • Organized, rigorous and autonomous, you know how to manage your priorities while respecting the standards expected.
  • You have a good team spirit, flexibility and constructive communication.
  • In self-control, you guarantee the quality of your work and the rigorous application of the established criteria.

What we offer

  • Health insurance (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual holiday weeks and 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • A contribution to your sports or cultural activities
  • The ability to work remotely depending on the function
  • A business restaurant
