Full description

We are looking for an Enterprise Architect with a strong focus on Cloud and Integration to support the evolution of a complex application ecosystem. You will play a key role in defining the target architecture, supporting transformation projects, and ensuring consistency across multiple business and IT initiatives. This role requires a strong technical background, with the ability to understand, challenge, and guide architecture and integration decisions. While the position is not primarily hands-on, being technically comfortable is a real asset. Key responsibilities

  • Map the existing application landscape, systems, and their dependencies.
  • Define, maintain, and improve architecture documentation.
  • Provide architectural guidance across multiple transformation projects.
  • Challenge solution design, integration patterns, and technology choices.
  • Identify cross-system dependencies and assess their business and technical impact.
  • Bring a high-level, strategic view to help prioritize initiatives and architectural recommendations.
  • Support the ongoing transition towards a Cloud-based ecosystem.
  • Review and advise on CI/CD, deployment, and integration best practices.
  • Proven experience as an Enterprise Architect in complex IT environments.
  • Strong understanding of application architecture, system integration, and API-based ecosystems.
  • Experience designing or evolving Cloud architectures.
  • Good knowledge of CI/CD principles and modern deployment practices.
  • Ability to engage with both technical teams and business stakeholders.

Experience with several of the following is highly desirable

  • ERP, PLM, PIM, and WMS solutions.
  • Cloud platforms and hybrid architectures.
  • Integration architecture (APIs, middleware, event-driven architectures).
  • Digital platforms / e-commerce ecosystems.
  • Enterprise application landscapes involving multiple interconnected systems.

Fluent English required
