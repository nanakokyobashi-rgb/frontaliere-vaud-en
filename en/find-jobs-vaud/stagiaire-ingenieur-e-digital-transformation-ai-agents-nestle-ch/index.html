Reimagin what coffee can be. Reimagin what you can become.

As a Trainee Engineer Digital Transformation & AI Agents, you will participate in the digital transformation of the factory by developing artificial intelligence agents capable of improving the operational efficiency and experience of employees.

How will you have an impact as a Trainee Engineer Digital Transformation & AI Agents?

  • Identify opportunities for improvement by working with business teams to identify processes that can be optimized through artificial intelligence.
  • Develop and deploy AI agents using Microsoft Copilot and SharePoint Agents solutions to meet the needs of different departments.
  • Support the adoption of AI by forming and supporting a network of referrals, while promoting good use practices.
  • Measure the impact of the solutions implemented and contribute to the future roadmap for the digital transformation of the plant.

What Will Make Your Success

  • Student in the final year of engineering school or Master's degree in specializations such as: Data & AI, Information Systems, Digital Transformation or Business Analytics
  • Analytical spirit and ability to quickly understand business issues.
  • Relationship strength and ability to transform operational needs into digital solutions.
  • Autonomy, curiosity and taste for innovation.
  • Knowledge of Microsoft Copilot, Power Platform, Power BI, SharePoint, SQL, Python or general AI tools is an asset.
