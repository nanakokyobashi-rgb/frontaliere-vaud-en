Role overview

Policlinic, emergency, outpatient care, hospitalizations: from birth to old age, the Morges Hospital offers a wide range of care services.

The establishment has 240 acute care beds, one maternity, with about 1,500 births per year, and nearly 30,000 emergency consultations.

In all, more than 40 medical disciplines have been practiced in its walls for more than 150 years.
