Full description

80-100%, Lausanne / Smart Working

Shape your future with us!

Are you passionate about customer contact? Join a dynamic team where your initiative, autonomy and sense of service will be truly valued. Every day, you will have the opportunity to make a difference and contribute to a motivating environment.

Join our Distribution sector. You'll be the point of contact with our clients. Every day, you will give the best to meet their needs, by offering human advice, oriented towards action and the future, in keeping with the motto "Customer first" of AXA.

What's waiting for you

  • Management and administrative monitoring of a portfolio of assigned brokers
  • Tracking and processing of business-related cases
  • Establishment and monitoring of tenders
  • Regular contacts with different brokers
  • Coordination of files with the various internal services (subscription, claims, litigation)
  • Occasional visits to brokering partners
  • Close collaboration with Broker Consultants

What you bring

  • You have commercial or equivalent training, ideally acquired in the insurance sector
  • You consistently place the customer at the heart of your concerns and make satisfaction a priority
  • You have excellent communication and negotiation skills. You appreciate customer contact and like to accompany your interlocutors in the search for suitable solutions
  • You are open to change and know how to perceive it as an opportunity for development
  • Team spirit, reliability, autonomy and efficiency are qualities that define you every day

What we offer you We are convinced that satisfied employees achieve better performance. That is why we propose salary supplements that bring tangible benefits beyond ordinary.

Flexibility and freedom: Smart Working (flexible work model), annual working time, five weeks of holidays (six weeks from 50 years), two additional weeks of holidays offered every five years, possibility of buying up to ten additional days of holidays per year

Working and family life: family-friendly meeting hours, 22 weeks of maternity leave, 8 weeks of leave for the other parent, subsidies for day care, company family allowances, days of leave for caring for relatives or in difficult personal situations

Provision and insurance: an attractive pension fund offering extended overcompulsory benefits and savings options, and up to 30% discount on insurance products

Flexible benefits: AXA Shareplan (staff participation), CHF 500 per year on the Swibeco benefits platform, lunch lunch or lunch card, Smart Working allowance of CHF 200 per year

Mobility: half-rate subscription refund, Reka-Pay up to CHF 1000 per year (20% discount), fleet discount, free electric charging station, AXA bikes to borrow

The right balance at work: free health checkups, health weeks every year, varied physical activity offers (on-line and on-site), massage offers, rest rooms, Employee Care, online health portal, various seminars and events around health prevention and mental health

Development: three days to train individually, wide learning and development offer, free coaching, financial participation in continuing training, promotion of internal mobility, offers of talent development, seminars for the over 55 years (AXA Horizon), engagement in communities on different themes, participation in innovative projects (Innoday, AXA Hackathon, UnConference), two days dedicated to volunteer missions (environment and social)
