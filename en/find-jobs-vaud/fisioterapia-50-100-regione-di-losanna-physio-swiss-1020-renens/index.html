FoxPhysio brings together 3 developing physiotherapy offices in Renens, Romanel-sur-Lausanne and Lausanne (canton de Vaud). We offer a modern work environment in recent premises, fully equipped with latest generation equipment, including fitness facilities. We take care of a varied patient, ranging from elite athletes to patients in general physiotherapy (orthopaedics, traumatology, rheumatology, geriatrics), as well as gynaecology. Our approach focuses on efficiency, quality of care and active management.

WHAT WE RECOGNIZE

  • Driving permits for home travel
  • Languages: French level B2 minimum / a second language is welcome
  • Dynamic, positive and rigorous
  • Demonstrate organization and flexibility
  • Ability to work as a team and independently

WHAT WE OFFER

  • Attractive wage conditions
  • 5 weeks of vacation per year
  • Secured parking space
  • Paid and continuing training
  • Individual and closed processing booths
  • Team trips/weekends (teambuilding)
  • Sports equipment available
  • Possibility of having temporary accommodation for arrivals in Switzerland

Your mission will be to integrate you into our young and dynamic team, to respect our values whose priority is to care for our patients.
