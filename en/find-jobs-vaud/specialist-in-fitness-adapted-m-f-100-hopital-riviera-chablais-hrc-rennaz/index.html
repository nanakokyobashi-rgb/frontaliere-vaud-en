Full description

The Riviera-Chablais Hospital, Vaud-Valais (HRC), is a reference intercantonal hospital, which brings together more than 2,500 collaborators. Rennaz Hospital has over 350 acute care beds. It is complemented by two Geriatric and Rehabilitation Clinics and two medical clinics. All employees invest on a daily basis to provide quality care for patients, embodying the institution's values of authenticity, exemplaryness and innovation.

In order to complete our interdisciplinary team in geriatric rehabilitation at the Monthey site, for an indefinite period, we are looking for a:

Specialist in Adapted Physical Activity (APA) M/F 100%, with experience in the geriatric field

The main tasks of this post are

  • Ensure the quality and continuity of stationary and ambulatory therapies
  • To cooperate in the organization of work and in the smooth running of the service's activities
  • Participate in the supervision and evaluation of students and trainees
  • Contribute to an interdisciplinary approach to care and teamwork
  • Promoting physical activity within and outside the institution

We ask

  • A Master's degree in Movement and Sport Sciences, orientation Adapted physical activity in health or an equivalent title
  • Full recognition by the Swiss Red Cross for foreign titles (cases without recognition or in the process of recognition will not be considered).
  • A willingness to acquire and develop new skills
  • An open and dynamic personality, wishing to integrate into the team already in place and to collaborate with the professionals of the various services

We offer

  • Interdisciplinary team work or the specialist in Adapted Physical Activity is recognized as an essential link in the care of patients hospitalized in geriatric and rehabilitation clinics.
  • A varied activity essentially group but also individual.
  • Project development is encouraged and likely to change the position.

In 2021, the HRC was twice ranked among the best employers in French-speaking Switzerland by the magazine Bilan and the newspaper Le Temps, placing it as a leading institution.
