Full description

The Pharmacie des Hôpitaux de l'Est Lémanique (PHEL), spread over several sites, is responsible for the supply of medicines and pharmaceutical assistance to hospitals, as well as to various health facilities, in the East of Germany (including the Hospital Riviera-Chablais, Vaud-Valais).

On our main site in Rennaz, we are looking for

Pharmacist/not wishing to complete FPH training in 100% clinical pharmacy

The main tasks of this post are

· Develop the skills necessary to become a confirmed pharmacist/ne clinician/e (further HPF clinical pharmacy training described under https://www.gsasa.ch/en/10141/fph-pharmacie-clinic/programme-training-complementary/ )

We ask · A federal diploma in pharmacists/ne · A willingness to invest in an interprofessional environment · Initiative, communication skills and good coping and organizational skills · Good knowledge of French and common office tools

We offer

· Professional and personalized support throughout your training · Work in a dynamic team with recognized experience in clinical pharmacy · A stimulating and innovative working environment within the Rennaz Hospital Centre · The opportunity to work closely with different care units in our partner hospitals · An ISO 9001:2015 certified hospital pharmacy and RQPH 2.1

Duration: A 24 month contract

For further information: Ms. Marine Roussel, clinical FPH pharmacist, at 058 773 45 77
