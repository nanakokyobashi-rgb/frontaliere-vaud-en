Road maintenance technician (Mechatronician/Mechanic)

Your Mission

As a road maintenance technician, you ensure the reliability and availability of our fleet of vehicles (buses, trolleybuses, electric E-Bus) by performing maintenance and repair operations according to an established schedule.

Your main responsibilities

  • Perform preventive and corrective maintenance on our vehicles.
  • Conduct periodic interviews, revisions and adjustments.
  • Make changes and repairs.
  • Diagnosis and resolve mechanical, electrical and electronic failures.
  • Intervention on innovative technologies (battery electric buses).

Your profile

  • Training: mechanic CFC, mechatronician or equivalent.
  • Experience: minimum 1 to 3 years in maintenance, preferably on heavy, agricultural or light vehicles.
  • Powers:
  • Mastery of common computer tools.
  • Diagnostic and repair on mechanical and electronic systems.
  • Permit: Permit B vehicle required.

In this position, you will be required to pass Licence C. For this, a blank criminal record extract and a blank SIAC extract are required.

  • Hours: Day + about one week of night every 7 to 8 weeks.

  • What we offer:
  • A modern and stimulating technical environment.
  • A dynamic and passionate team.
  • Opportunities for further training on new technologies (E-Bus).
