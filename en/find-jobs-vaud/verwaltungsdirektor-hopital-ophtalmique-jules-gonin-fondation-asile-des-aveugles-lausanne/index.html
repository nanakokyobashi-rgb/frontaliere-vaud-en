Full description

PORTAILS is part of the Asylum Foundation for the Blind, which also includes the Jules Gonin Ophthalmic Hospital, the educational centre and a medical-social institution. The vocational integration platform takes care of young people and adults with a visual deficit in their career path of guidance, reorientation and training, throughout French-speaking Switzerland. PORTAILS brings together the Foundation's various services oriented towards integration and professional retention: support for training, social service, rehabilitation and low vision, technical centre for adaptation and accessibility, psychological service for guidance, reception of trainees, in order to promote their social and professional inclusion and develop their autonomy.
