UFA SA is committed to productive animal production. Skilled and motivated collaborators work in regional teams across Switzerland and support animal owners by offering products and services. Modern and efficient factories are the pillars of our success.Join us and come to strengthen our team!

Exciting tasks

  • Drive and monitor grinding and mixing facilities
  • Operating and monitoring press and expansion lines
  • Monitor and operate blending facilities
  • Maintain and clean machines and workstations
  • Depending on your abilities, it is possible to move to a shift leader position in shift schedules.

The profile sought

  • CFC completed, ideally as a miller, plant operator or in a comparable technical, mechanical or agricultural profession
  • Ability to work in 3 x 8 team and on weekends
  • Ability to work independently and a sense of responsibility
  • Team spirit.
