Specialised teacher and coordinator Volunteer, PPF and EEF, 60% CDI, 60%

Deployed at several sites, the Lavigny Institution consists of a neurological hospital with outpatient consultations at two sites, two departments for adults with disabilities, with an intellectual disability with a physical problem, a specialized school and a workshop and vocational training sector respectively.

The socio-educational housing department is recruiting. If you want to work in interdisciplinarity around strong values that are:

Benevolence, fairness, respect and responsibility in a collaborative managerial approach.

You can join our educational teams.

We are looking for one

Specialised teacher and coordinator Volunteer, PFP and EEF, 60% CDI

At the institution of Lavigny, we accompany adults with disabilities daily to various places of life: polyhandicap, intellectual disability, aging.

Your Mission

Under the management of the Head of Pôle (RP), you ensure the operational coordination of volunteerism as well as of practical training arrangements: practitioners trainers (PF), trainers in professional practice (FPP) and trainers in business (FEE).

Your main responsibilities

  1. Volunteer coordination, 40%
  • Develop, promote and coordinate the policy of volunteering within the institution.
  • Recruit, accompany, mentor and retain volunteers.
  • Organize and coordinate volunteer activities.
  • Participate in organizing, coordinating and facilitating institutional events.
  • Ensure administrative monitoring and management of volunteer data.
  • To monitor the financial resources allocated within the framework defined by the PR.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of practices and the development of volunteerism in collaboration with the various internal partners.
  1. Coordination of FPs, trainers in FPP and trainers in FEE, 20%
  • Develop and maintain the FP/FPP/FEE network and ensure that reference lists are updated in collaboration with internal partners.
  • Inform and support students in their efforts, their dual journey between training and operational activities and in their further development.
  • Inform and accompany PF/FPP/FEE in their function.
  • Coordinate, monitor and contribute to the continuous improvement of practical training arrangements.
  • Organize and coordinate annual meetings between students, the EEF/PF/FPP and follow-up of examinations and selected themes.
  • Ensure relations and follow-up with external partners (schools, apprenticeship commissioners, etc.).
  • Support the management of potential difficulties or conflict situations between students and PF/FPP/FEEs and seek the PR where necessary.

Your profile

  • Diploma of Educator ES or Bachelor of Arts HES-SO in Social Work or recognized equivalence.
  • Case of Practitioner.ne trainer.ce.
  • Experience in accompanying people with disabilities.
  • Experience in partnership development and networking.
  • Initiative, creativity, dynamism and excellent relational skills, with a capacity to mobilize new partners and volunteers.
  • Driver's license.
  • Mastery of common computer tools and ease of writing.

We offer

  • Working conditions agreed by the Comité du secteur social parapublic Vaudois.
  • A salary in line with the function and in line with the scales of the CLCs in the voodoo parapublic sector, with benefits related to mobility (parking, participation in public transport costs).
  • In-service training and specialized supervision (including TSA and challenge behaviour), internal mobility, possible funding of diplomas and certifications.
  • A business restaurant offering varied meals at preferential rates, in a pleasant business environment encouraging exchanges.
  • A wide choice of sports and relaxation activities (yoga, fitness, aquagym, pilates etc.) as well as massages, while enjoying regular business life events
  • Flexible and continuous schedules across sectors, with the possibility of part-time work for optimal work-life balance
