Put to the Point

The Centre shall contribute to two publicly funded research projects. The first project will develop models for predicting virus infection risk in seed potato certification, taking into account historical certification data, weather variables and varietal susceptibility. The second project will develop operational models for predicting potato dormancy and germination during storage, using data on varieties, climate and storage over 35 years. Together, these projects combine artificial intelligence, machine learning and digital agriculture to provide decision support for the Swiss potato industry. The projects are financed by the Federal Office for Agriculture (BLW) and implemented in close cooperation with Agroscope, Swisspatat and other partners.
