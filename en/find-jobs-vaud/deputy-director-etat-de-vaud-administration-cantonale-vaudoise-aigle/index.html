Full description

As deputy director of the new Gymnase d'Aigle, which will open its doors at the beginning of August 2027, you will assist the director, in order to ensure the smooth running of the institution at the educational, organisational, financial and logistical level.

  • Assume the tasks delegated by the Director in his task of implementing cantonal and/or federal training policies. In particular, in the educational conduct and annual organisation of the institution and in its relations with teachers, pupils, parents and other partners of the institution.
  • Support the Director in the management of the institution's human resources in order to foster a conducive and enjoyable working environment. Support the Director in the financial field and work closely together on the efficient financial management of the institution.
  • Manage information systems (Administrative IS), ensure project development related to administrative IS and monitor updates.
  • Supervise the activities of the various services (student support network, concierge, cafeteria, BCUL) and contribute to the administrative, technical and logistical organization of the institution, particularly for safety issues at work.
  • Maintain the necessary external relations and ensure the representation of the institution for the tasks entrusted to it. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Mr Michaël Gelsomino, Director-designate at 021 557 46 60 or by e-mail: michael.gelsomino@vd.ch
  • Higher, master level or equivalent (University, EPF or HES)
  • Educational title for secondary education II.
  • Several-year vocational courses in the field of education, vocational, secondary II or tertiary.
  • Experience of several years of management and organization in a responsible position
  • Training in management and management of training institutions (training can be taken in the course of employment).
  • Experienced managerial skills, negotiating skills, ability to act, communication skills. Disclosure of possible criminal history is required for this function.
