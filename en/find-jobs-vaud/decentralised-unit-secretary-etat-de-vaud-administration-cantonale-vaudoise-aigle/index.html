Full description

Join our team as a decentralized unit secretary! Are you passionate about the field related to the choice of occupations and careers of young people at the end of compulsory schooling, gymnasiums, adults? This job is for you! As secretary of a decentralized unit, you carry out the assistance tasks related to the various missions and services of the cantonal Office for Educational and Vocational Guidance OCOSP. You ensure the activities and tasks related to the services provided (Compulsory education-Gymnase-Guichet regional transition 1-Adults) with the maintenance and updating of databases. You manage the reception and reception of the public, evaluate applications, inform or send the consultants to the appropriate person. You process and organize all administrative tasks in collaboration with the various secretariats of the centre.

  • Self-executing and multi-purpose assistance tasks related to the various tasks and services of the cantonal Office for Educational and Vocational Guidance OCOSP
  • Independently perform the tasks related to the Case Management for Vocational Training (CMFP) and the T1 Regional Window
  • Manage reception and public reception, evaluate requests, inform and contact consultants in the right place.
  • Processing and organizing all administrative and reception tasks of the office in an autonomous and versatile manner according to the organization set up
  • Ensuring the quality of secretariat and assistance work
  • Manage and update the Apprenticeship Exchange (BPA) For further information, please consult the terms of reference by clicking here and contact Thomas Ehret, Head of Centre, 021 557 14 72.
  • Commercial employee CFCs
  • Professional experience in a similar field an asset
  • Ability to work as a team
  • Knowledge of the regional economic fabric
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss and Vaudois training system
  • Database management skills
  • Good knowledge of user computer science required. Possible disclosure of criminal history is required for this function. The workplace is located at Chemin du Verger 10 in Vevey.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
