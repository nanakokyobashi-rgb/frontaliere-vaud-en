Full description

Nurses at 80-100%, 80% to 100% Integrated into the heart of a large recognized institution, the Hospital of the Institution of Lavigny is one of the reference Romanesque centers for patients with brain damage requiring neurological rehabilitation, or with difficult-to-treat epilepsy. Open since 1 July 2024, the University Neurorehabilitation Service (SUN) is an innovative structure linking two institutions, CHUV and Hôpital de Lavigny. With 75 beds and nearly 200 collaborators, the SUN provides highly specialized and interdisciplinary care in academic neurorehabilitation, for more than 500 in-patients and nearly 2000 outpatients per year. It is in this context that the hospital department develops, while keeping alive the values dear to it: Benevolence, fairness, respect and responsibility in a collaborative managerial approach We offer you the opportunity to discover an exciting work in neuro-reeducation, at the heart of interdisciplinarity, in a partnership with patients and their loved ones.
