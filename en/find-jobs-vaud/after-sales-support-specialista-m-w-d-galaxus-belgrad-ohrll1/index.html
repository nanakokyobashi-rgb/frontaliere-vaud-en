Full description

In After Sales Operations, we ensure that our customers are well looked after even after the purchase. For this purpose, we control a network of service centers for more than 9 million products. As a support specialist, you keep the cases in motion: from the guarantee to the credit. And because the role is deliberately broad, your area of responsibility grows with you.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Belgrade, Makedonska 44, 11000 Belgrade

Mansioni

  • After-sales cases capture, monitor and solve even if the standard solution does not fit
  • Invoices and credits are checked, processed and booked
  • Proactively accompany warranty cases and claim and track credits from service partners
  • Check customer documents such as cost estimates and transparently return them
  • Stay tuned to service centers, forwarders, suppliers and customers by telephone, e-mail and ticket system
  • Evaluate and track feedback from our after-sales customer survey
  • shaping and improving processes where manual work can be replaced by automation

Requirements

  • 1-3 years: After Sales, Customer Service or Accounting experience
  • Basic or continuing education: Commercial education
  • Secure handling of ERP systems and ticketing tools such as Zendesk as well as a precise working method
  • Keep calm, even if many cases are on the table at the same time and make decisions themselves instead of waiting for releases
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • The ability to adapt to continuous changes in the working environment
  • in German (negotiation certain)
  • English (advantageous)
  • French (advantageous)
