Full description

BMS Building Materials Switzerland is a wholesaler of construction materials for the large and second work. Our group of companies includes renowned specialists such as Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced specialists advise, support and inspire with great commitment construction customers and private customers on 110 sites throughout Switzerland. Close to the daily construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Fast, simple and competent.

As of February 1, 2027, we recruit a rigorous management controller, ready to become a key financial partner. Join a dynamic team to drive financial performance and contribute to strategic decisions.

Missions

As a Controller or Manager, you will be the strategic partner of the Commercial Excellence teams, and the main point of contact for all departmental financial matters.

Your role will be to analyze business performance, identify growth and profitability levers and inform decisions.

Attached to the Head of Management Control, you will be the preferred interlocutor for financial matters related to commercial activities and commercial excellence.

In close collaboration with business teams, you will transform your analysis into concrete recommendations to support decision-making. By being close to the operational teams, you will help transform the data into actions and strengthen the business performance of the company.

Key responsibilities

Monthly closure

  • Conduct the monthly review of the product portfolio, analysing pricing, margins and operational aspects.
  • Monitor changes in turnover and margins, explain differences and identify factors influencing results.

Performance reviews

  • Participate in monthly reviews with the management team.
  • Performance and profitability by region, customer segment and product range.
  • Present actual results, explain variances from budget and previous year, define and monitor corrective action plans in collaboration with relevant officials.
  • Build and challenge business models in collaboration with business teams.

Estimates and budgeting

  • Take responsibility for the budget planning process (budget and forecasts): preparing the budget file, collecting information from stakeholders and integrating it into the management software, ensuring reliability and consistency of data.

Business cases and projects

  • Develop financial business boxes for operational initiatives (e.g. productivity improvement, profitability of outlets).
  • Monitor deliverables, achievement of benefits and timeliness to ensure projects achieve financial targets.

Cost analysis

  • Collaborate with operations to identify improvements and cost optimization opportunities.
  • Gaps in objectives and identification of avenues for improvement.

Ad hoc analyses

  • Conduct scenario analyses, cost-of-service studies and other ad hoc financial analyses as required.

Your Profile

  • University degree (Licence/Bachelor) in Finance, Accounting, Management or equivalent
  • Specialisation in management control is an asset.
  • Minimum 3 to 5 years of experience in finance/management control, including monthly closing and gap analysis, with proven experience in business partnership.
  • Strong analytical capabilities, with proven experience in carrying out business analyses (margin, profitability, etc.).
  • Strong analytical and communication skills
  • You know how to explain complex issues in a clear and accessible way, including financial matters, and how to adapt your communication to a variety of people
  • Ability to influence and collaborate with non-financial interlocutors.
  • Advanced Mastery of Excel; ease in the extraction and analysis of data from an ERP; knowledge of SQL appreciated.
  • Languages: German, French or English, with professional mastery of the second

We offer

  • A challenge within a changing company
  • A stimulating, versatile and interesting activity in a good working environment, where active collaboration is valued while encouraging autonomy.
  • An intensive integration programme is planned, allowing our new colleagues to benefit from product and system training within the company.
  • Support for the professional and personal development of our employees.
  • A well-structured network of craft centres and close collaboration with the sales department.
  • 5 weeks of holidays (6 weeks from 50 years) with the possibility of purchasing an additional holiday up to 10 days a year, additional benefits such as a self-employed and well-funded pension fund, as well as discounts on our entire product range and additional external benefits.
