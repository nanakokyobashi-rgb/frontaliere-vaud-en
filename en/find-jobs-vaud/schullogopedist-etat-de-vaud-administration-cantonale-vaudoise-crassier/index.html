Full description

You are sensitive to the challenges in the field of school logopedia and want to help children and young people in difficulty in the Vaudian training system, join us to strengthen our team of Crassier!

  • Prevent, diagnose and treat disorders of original and/or organic language. Conduct a specific intervention with children and young people in schools in the field of communication, oral and written language with a view to promoting their development and social, educational and vocational integration.

  • Work with parents, school workers and other stakeholders involved. Possible disclosure of criminal history is required for this function. Please send us the complete application package including the CV of diplomas and certificates of employment. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Ms.Laurianne Craig, Team Leader, who is happy to be at your disposal at 022 557 58 13.
  • Master in logopedia or title deemed equivalent.
  • Professional experience, interest in multidisciplinary and group work.
