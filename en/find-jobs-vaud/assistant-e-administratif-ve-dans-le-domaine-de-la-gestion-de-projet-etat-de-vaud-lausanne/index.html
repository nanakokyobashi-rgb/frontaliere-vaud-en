Full description

The Social Support and Guidance Centre (PASO), attached to the Directorate for Integration and Solidarity (DIRIS) of the Directorate-General for Social Cohesion (DGCS), is responsible for the monitoring and development of projects and programmes in the field of reception, counselling, support and social guidance. In order to strengthen its team, PASO is looking for an Administrative Assistant in the field of project management. For further information, please consult the specifications here or contact Ms Léa Fazenda, Head of the Social Support and Guidance Unit (PASO), at the e-mail address: dgcs.recruitment@vd.ch.

  • Ensure the administrative management and follow-up of contracted hotels.
  • Support the person responsible for training concerning administrative tasks in connection with the organisation of training.
  • Producing and monitoring the conventions and amendments relating to organizations subsidized by the Centre.
  • Support project managers and mission managers in their relationship with the internal and external partners at the Centre: organization of meetings, taking of decisions and monitoring of projects.
  • Provide cross-cutting administrative tasks related to pole management.
  • Trade employee's CFC, commercial maturity or equivalent, required.
  • Professional experience of at least 5 years in the administrative field.
  • Good knowledge of the cantonal administration of Vaud and the field of social policies, desired.
  • Excellent ability to work independently.
  • A sense of public service responsibilities and ethics.
  • Capacity for organization, time management, adaptation and flexibility.
  • Very good editorial capacity.
  • Listen and communicate.
  • Good oral expression and spirit of mutual assistance and collaboration.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
