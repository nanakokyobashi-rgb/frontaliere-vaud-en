Intern Engineer Engineering

Location: internships available at our 3 production centres (Romont / Avenches / Orbe) Rate: full-time 100% Type of contract: fixed-term contract (6 months) When: desired start 1st quarter 2027

Reimagin what coffee can be. Reimagin what you can become.

As an Engineer Engineering Trainee, you will participate in operational maintenance reviews, the preparation, implementation and monitoring of indicators and improvement projects. You will also support production and logistics support teams.

How will you have an impact as an Engineer Engineering Intern?

  • Define and apply the appropriate methodology to manage a cross-cutting project
  • Participate in continuous improvement
  • Ensure a daily presence on the ground to support the rise in competence and identify opportunities for improvement.

What Will Make Your Success

  • Student in School of Engineering in Mechanical / Industrial / Robotics Engineering
  • Field experience in a engineering or production department, an asset
  • Project management experience, an asset
  • Ability to analyse and solve problems
  • Ability to work in a multidisciplinary team.

Benefits

  • Opportunities for internal training and development
  • Pension fund with excellent employer contributions
  • Financial contribution to health insurance
  • Beneficial parenting policy
  • Business restaurant on site with menus at subsidized prices
  • Free parking and charging stations for electric cars
  • Many advantages at the Nespresso Club
