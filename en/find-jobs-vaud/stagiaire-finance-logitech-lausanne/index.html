Logitech is the Sweet Spot for people who want their actions to have a positive global impact while having the flexibility to do it in their own way.

Finance Intern - Personal Workspace Location: Lausanne, Switzerland Team: BG Finance Duration: 6 months

The Role

At Logitech, our FP&A team plays a vital role in driving strategic and financial insight across the business. We’re now looking for a Finance Intern to join our Personal Workspace (PWS) business groups. In this role, you will help drive the sales growth, margin improvement as well as strong controls over the financial planning, analysis and reporting. The objective is to drive financially sound decisions that are consistent with the Business Group’s (BG) priorities & strategies and more generally the interest of the Company overall.

In this role, you will primarily interact with the PWS FP&A Lead, Financial Analysis Lead, BU Leads & Teams, Corporate finance. From a functional side, there are frequent interactions with Regional Finance, Operations, and other functional teams. From a commercial standpoint, the position will give you an opportunity to work in close contact with the realities of world famous names from the consumer electronics industry. You will live daily with the pace of innovation, technology, product design and the demands of the international financial markets. With a fast-moving, innovative culture and strong balance sheet, Logitech is a world leader in products that connect people in a natural, intuitive way to the digital experiences they care about.

We’re looking for the right person to join our finance team. Is that you?

Your Contribution

Be Yourself. Be Open. Stay Hungry and Humble. Collaborate. Challenge. Decide and just Do. These are the behaviors you’ll need for success at Logitech.

In this role you will

  • Analyze performance trends across revenue, margin, and operating expenses & providing insights to support PWS Finance Lead in making financially sound decisions
  • Assist in annual budgeting and quarterly outlook cycles, ensuring alignment with strategic objectives
  • Provide insightful perspective to PWS Finance and BUs teams on financial matters for existing and new product line launch
  • Maintain and improve financial models and dashboards, enhancing visibility into business performance
  • Drive process improvements, helping the team streamline workflows and increase data accuracy
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams (Sales, Marketing, Operations) to collect inputs and validate assumptions
  • Support ad-hoc analysis and presentations for PWS Finance, BUs teams and key stakeholders

It is an opportunity to expand the job scope to suit aspirations/potential of high caliber candidates.

Your Skills

For consideration, you must bring the following skills and behaviors to our team:

  • Currently pursuing or recently completed a degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, or a related field
  • Proficient in Excel, experience with Power BI, Tableau, or similar tools is a plus.
  • Fluent in English, both written and spoken.
  • Capable to prioritize and bring structure while enabling pace and controls
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with attention to detail.
  • Self motivated and agile in embracing fast changing business context
  • Excellent communication and interpersonal skills with a collaborative mindset
