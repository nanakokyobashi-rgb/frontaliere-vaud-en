Full description

You are passionate about health and want to play a key role in the proper functioning of the different care units, so join our team of Nurse Poolist. GHOL is the reference hospital for the German West with its structures on Nyon and Rolle. We offer you a job in a pleasant setting, in new premises, surrounded by dynamic, motivated, volunteer colleagues who will welcome you and accompany you in your various missions.

Description of position

In your new functions, you strengthen your care teams according to your preferences by:

  • Surgery, Orthopaedic Medicine
  • Hemodialysis-Emergency-Intensive care
  • Pediatric/Maternity
  • Pneumology (Role site)
  • EMS (Role site)

Your benefits

  • You organize your schedule at your convenience by managing your working time and rest time
  • You are taking on an activity more or less gradually
  • You expand your knowledge by being oriented in different services
  • You participate in the various training courses offered according to your needs. A catalogue of continuing vocational and personal development training is offered to employees of the Department of Care
  • You will have after a given time the opportunity to get to a fixed position as a priority
  • You will receive a flexibility and availability bonus in addition to your salary
  • Continuous hours of 12 hours / hours on a coupe/ day / night / WE

Profile required

We are convinced of your rapid success in your future functions thanks to your:

  • Ease of adaptation, responsiveness to requests
  • Want to expand your knowledge and skills
  • Use of IT tools (care folders, mobile applications, etc.).
  • Your few years of nursing experience will be a real asset for rapid integration.

Level of training

Bachelor of Science HES-SO in Nursing required or diploma deemed equivalent recognized by the Swiss Red Cross Possible specialization in one of the specific areas of care (Intensive care, Anaesthesia, Emergencies, Pediatrics, Gerontology, etc.)
