Full description

Our specialized Spitex/Service Care "Service Management" is responsible for the technical assessment of Spitex needs clarifications.

Your mission

  • As a Spitex/care specialist, you work with the benefits department and care providers.
  • Besides processing and evaluating Spitex cases, you make decisions according to legal standards.
  • In addition, you conduct assessments and inform benefit providers and insured persons.
  • You also advise employees from other divisions on Spitex issues.
  • The management of a demanding level of correspondence, the drafting of orders and re-considerations, and the processing of complaints are also part of this interesting activity.
  • In addition, you are called upon to collaborate on various projects.

What you bring

  • You have basic training in the field of care, particularly as a nurse, and you can attest to professional experience in the field of help and home care, possibly also in the psychiatric field.
  • You are interested in health insurance issues or already have professional experience in this area.
  • Very good knowledge of German is an asset.
  • In addition, you are proactive, creative, committed, flexible and oriented towards the company's objectives. You work efficiently.
  • With an analytical mindset, you also have a good linguistic understanding.
  • Work flexibly: Adapt your work every year to your lifestyle, with part-time opportunities and other flexible models such as telework, to name but one.
  • Enthusiasm your purse: Benefits such as bonus, Reka cheques, preferential health insurance premiums, etc., which can quickly reach several thousand francs per year.
  • Choose your holiday model: Five or six weeks of vacation? You choose. You work 41 or 42 hours a week, respectively.
  • Keep training: We want you to continue training internally or externally and generously support you with time and money.
  • Keep the form: ergonomic work places, promotion of psychological well-being and many other offers related to health.
  • Engage in social matters: Is life good for you? So make others enjoy it and broaden your horizons, alone or as a team.
