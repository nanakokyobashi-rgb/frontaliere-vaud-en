Full description

Mission

The Switzerland Project

Since 2017, Decathlon Switzerland has been an entrepreneurial laboratory. Our goal is to shape the future of sporting goods retail through local autonomy and digital excellence. We are looking for a team member to take responsibility for the strategy of our most important online category: Hiking & Camping.

The Challenge

Hiking & Camping is the engine of our digital business. This is not a purely administrative role, but a key leadership position in which you will drive the strategy for our most successful digital category. You will join as maternity cover for Tania while she is on maternity leave. To ensure a smooth transition and thorough onboarding, the recruitment process will take place at the end of September.

Framework conditions

  • Start date: 1. November
  • Standort: Bussigny, VD
  • Contract: Fixed-term until May 2027 (with the possibility of extension)

Your responsibilities

  • Market strategy: You drive market share growth by specifically adapting our business and advertising strategies to Swiss market trends.
  • Business Planning: You are responsible for the online business plan as well as the marketing strategy in close collaboration with the Sales and Communications teams.
  • Revenue growth: You are responsible for online revenue and manage it through data-based forecasts and decisions. You analyze performance daily and flexibly adjust the commercial action plan ("Commercial Animation Plan") to market trends and inventory levels.
  • User Experience: You optimize product categorization, navigation and availability to maximize the conversion rate.
  • Acquisition & customer retention: You work closely with the Traffic and CRM teams to increase new customer acquisition and visit frequency (web, app and stores).
  • Cross-functional leadership: You manage strategic projects (e.g. Wellness, App) and collaborate through functional leadership with the entire ecosystem (Marketing, Business Units). You act as the bridge between the sports teams (Offer/Supply) and the Digital teams (Acquisition, Tech, CRM, Data).

Profile

Your profile

  • We prefer internal candidates with a strong entrepreneurial drive and high digital competence.
  • Proven experience in Digital / E-Commerce (Category Management, Digital Marketing or similar) is mandatory.
  • You have a deep interest in the Hiking & Camping market as well as a sound understanding of user behavior in the sports ecosystem.
  • You feel comfortable in a multilingual environment. Fluency in German and French is required, together with English.
  • You act proactively and navigate confidently in a complex environment where you convince and guide others even without direct hierarchical authority.
  • You are data-savvy, organized, think entrepreneurially ("Test-and-Learn" approach), and adapt quickly to new circumstances.
