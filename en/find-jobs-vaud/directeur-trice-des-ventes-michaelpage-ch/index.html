Full description

About Our Client A historic player in Swiss real estate for more than 125 years, deRham has opened a new chapter in its development and wants to strengthen its organisation in order to support its ambitions for growth and transformation. In this context, deRham recruits a Sales Director, who is responsible for managing all of the group's transactional activities (residential, plan sales, high-end sales and real estate performance), while reflecting prospectively on the evolution of the Swiss real estate market, both on its traditional and premium segments. Beyond commercial performance, the challenge is to contribute to the sustainable repositioning of transactional activity by anticipating market developments (digitalization, new uses, customer expectations, sustainability) and developing a differentiated offer.

Job Description Attached to the CEO, you take responsibility for Sales and manage all transactional activities of the group. You define and implement the business strategy, develop the teams and accompany the evolution of the business in a constantly changing market.

As such, your main tasks are to

  • Develop activities on strategic segments (high-end residential, real estate performance, plan sales) and strengthen business performance.
  • Structure an organization that is efficient, customer-oriented and results-oriented.
  • To support the evolution of supply and practices by integrating market transformations.

Leadership and management

  • Lead, federate and develop sales teams.
  • Pilot the P&L of the activity.
  • Create a culture of performance, collaboration and demand.
  • Support staff development and recruitment.

Trade strategy

  • Define and deploy the business development strategy.
  • Analyze the evolution of the Swiss real estate market and adapt business guidelines.
  • Prioritize growth segments and develop partnerships.
  • Monitor performance indicators (business figures, margins, portfolio).

Business development

  • Obtain and pilot strategic mandates.
  • Develop and maintain relationships with institutional and private clients (investors, promoters, family offices, private clients).
  • Mobilize your network to identify new opportunities.

Organization and evolution of activity

  • Optimize processes, piloting tools and business practices.
  • Promote the use of digital solutions, data and CRM to improve performance and customer experience.
  • Contribute to the evolution of supply in connection with the changes in the sector.

Coordination

  • Work closely with other branches of the group.
  • Coordinate external partners involved in promotion operations.
  • Ensure regular reporting to the Directorate General.

The Successful Applicant Experience and skills

  • Experience confirmed in sales and real estate brokerage in Switzerland (ideally 10 to 15 years).
  • Strong experience in business development, transaction negotiation and team management with P&L responsibility.
  • Excellent knowledge of at least one of the following segments: high-end residential, real estate performance or plan sales.
  • Professional network established among the main market players.

Strategic vision

  • Good understanding of developments in the Swiss real estate market and their impact on transaction activities.
  • Ability to integrate issues related to digitalization, new uses, sustainability and customer expectations into business strategy.
  • Ability to combine strategic vision with operational delivery.

Leadership

  • Unifying leadership and results oriented.
  • Collaborative management style, promoting the autonomy and development of teams.
  • Entrepreneurial spirit, sense of initiative and excellent relational qualities.

Specific criteria

  • Impeccable reputation (references, extract from the criminal record and the Office of Prosecutions required for registration in the Trade Register).
  • Experience in an entrepreneurial environment and a human-sized structure.

What's on Offer A unique opportunity to shape a role that combines commercial direction and construction of a strategic vision of the sector, running as premium, this in good conditions.
