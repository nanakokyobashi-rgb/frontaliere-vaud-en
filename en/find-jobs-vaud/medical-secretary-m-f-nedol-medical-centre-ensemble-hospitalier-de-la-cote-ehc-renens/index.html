Full description

The Ensemble Hospitalier de la Côte — EHC is a health network that includes, among other things, 35 medical centres including the NEDOL Medical Centre in Renens. The latter offers consultations in general and visceral surgery, vascular surgery, angiology, endocrine surgery, and nephrology (reins). Its multidisciplinary team receives by appointment in brand new premises on the 4th floor, one floor above the NEDOL Dialysis Centre, and offers consultations that benefit from the latest technologies to ensure optimal care, combining medical quality and personalized support.

To strengthen this team, we are looking for a

Medical Secretary (M/F) at 60%

CDI, starting as soon as possible

WE OFFER YOU

  • 5 weeks of annual vacation, 6 weeks from the age of 50. Possibility of redeeming an additional week of vacation, as well as 10 holidays offered.
  • Several proposals providing flexibility (unpaid leave, part-time leave, etc.).
  • Remuneration based on the valuation of your previous years of professional experience as well as seniority bonuses within the EHC.
  • Sports activities (Pilates, walking races, discounts at various centres such as several fitness centres or the Bains de Lavey), events for the children of collaborators, a company drink located in Morges to share moments of conviviality during the beautiful days.
  • Internal transfer facilitated according to your desire for development.
  • Many in-house or certifying training courses are available to you, as well as a training grant.
  • Office hours.

YOU MAY

  • A training of medical secretary.e, CFC trade employee or title deemed equivalent.
  • An excellent knowledge of medical terminology.
  • Recognized skills in the use of computer tools and excellent spelling.
  • Flexibility and versatility in order to provide doctors with quality support in the execution of all the activities of a medical secretariat.
  • A strong team spirit, autonomy and the desire to evolve in an environment conducive to collegiality.
  • Rigor, detail care and good management of priorities allowing you to cope with an ever-changing activity.
  • A relational ease allowing harmonious collaboration with the various medical-care workers.

YOUR OBJECTIVES

  • Provide patient reception and guidance. e.
  • Follow up on all day-to-day administrative tasks involved in the proper functioning of the centre, such as managing emails, managing the schedules of different doctors and answering the telephone.
  • To guarantee the preparation, monitoring and archiving of patients' physical medical records
  • Conducting the typing, rereading and transmission of medical reports and test results to internal and external physicians within appropriate time frames.

WE ARE

  • An institution consisting of acute care and rehabilitation institutions, a private clinic, EMS, medical centres and permanences located in the region of La Côte and the first clinical hypnosis school dedicated to the care community in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A network of care allowing many opportunities for internal change according to the aspirations and development projects of employees.
  • More than 2,000 employees taking care of patients and residents of the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment conducive to the realization of projects.
  • A training company engaged in succession training, with about 80 apprentices in 16 different trades, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.
