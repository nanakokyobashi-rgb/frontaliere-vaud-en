Full description

The Monitoring, Audit and Investigations Unit (UCAE) of the Directorate-General for Social Cohesion (DGCS) is a dynamic team composed of some 20 collaborators in charge of monitoring our services. The section of the Interdisciplinary Monitoring of Visits to Health and Social Institutions (CIVESS), which is attached to the UCAE, has the specific task of monitoring and inspecting health and social establishments in the canton of Vaud. In order to carry out the following tasks, we are currently looking for an assistant to the Head of the Monitoring, Audit and Investigations Unit (UCAE) for the section on Interdisciplinary Monitoring of Visits to Health and Social Institutions (CIVESS).

  • Provide strategic leadership in collaboration with the Head of UCAE and representation of CIVESS.
  • Ensure response and expertise regarding CIVESS operational processes and procedures.
  • Monitor compliance with legal rights and requirements, safety and quality standards in health institutions (including in particular EMS, CMS, CAT, HNM, psychiatric hospitals) and socio-educational (including in particular SEA, workshops) Vaudoises.
  • Support the Head of Unit in his missions and replace him in the conduct of the unit. For further information, please consult the specifications here or contact Mr Giuseppe Farina, Head of the Monitoring, Audit and Investigations Unit (UCAE), at the e-mail address: dgcs.recruitment@vd.ch
  • Master's degree or socio-health profession master level or HES or equivalent.
  • Specialisation in a social or health field (patient rights, public policies, etc.).
  • Professional practice in the field and in supervision or in the socio-health quality of minimum 5 years.
  • Control of the functioning of public administration and the management of public institutions required.
  • Very good editorial, analytical and synthesis skills.
  • Global vision and sense of perspective.
  • Adaptability and flexibility.
  • Customer orientation, spirit of mutual support and collaboration.
  • A sense of public service responsibilities and ethics developed.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
