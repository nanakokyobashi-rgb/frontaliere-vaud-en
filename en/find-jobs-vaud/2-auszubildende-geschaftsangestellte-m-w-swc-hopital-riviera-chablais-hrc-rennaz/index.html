Full description

The Riviera-Chablais Hospital, Vaud-Valais (HRC), is a reference intercantonal hospital with over 2,000 collaborators. The Rennaz Hospital, inaugurated in late 2019, has over 300 acute care beds. It is complemented by two Geriatric and Rehabilitation Clinics as well as two medical clinics in Vevey and Monthey.

All employees invest on a daily basis to provide quality care for patients, embodying the institution's values of authenticity, exemplaryness and innovation.
