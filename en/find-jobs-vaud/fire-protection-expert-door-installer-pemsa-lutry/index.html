Your assignment

  • Ensure installation and installation of fire doors in new construction and renovation sites.
  • Install fire protection systems in accordance with technical requirements.
  • Perform the work of adjustment, fixing and finishing of the doors and their frames.
  • Install fire compartment elements according to the requirements of the construction site.
  • Check the correct operation of doors, door locks and closing devices.
  • Make the necessary adjustments to ensure installation in accordance.
  • Locate any anomalies and perform the necessary corrections.
  • Respect the executive projects, the requirements of manufacturers and safety standards in Switzerland.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Experience proven in the installation of fire doors and/or fire protection.
  • Good mastery of laying, fixing and adjustment techniques.
  • Knowledge of Swiss standards and regulations on fire protection
  • Ability to read and interpret technical drawings.
  • Autonomous, precise, organized and conscientious person.
  • A sense of responsibility and attention to the quality of the work done.
  • Good team spirit and ease in the relationships on site.
  • Driving license and vehicle: an advantage.
  • Immediate availability
