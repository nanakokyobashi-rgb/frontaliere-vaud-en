Full description

BMS Building Materials Switzerland is a wholesaler of construction materials for the large and second work. Our group of companies includes renowned specialists such as Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced specialists advise, support and inspire construction customers and private customers with a great deal of commitment on 110 sites throughout Switzerland. Close to the daily construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Fast, simple and competent.

Starting March 1, 2027, we are looking for our future Head of IT Operations. An inspiring field leader to drive and evolve our IT infrastructure in Switzerland, with the aim of ensuring a reliable, secure and efficient environment. At the heart of management and transformation challenges, you will play a key role in stabilizing the existing, modernizing our platforms, strengthening our cyber-resilience and accelerating automation. An ideal opportunity for a leader who can combine vision, operational requirements and concrete impact.

Missions

As Head of IT Operations, you will be at the heart of the performance and transformation of our technological environment, with the mission of piloting infrastructure, managing teams and ensuring a high level of quality of service, security and reliability. Your missions will be as follows:

Key responsibilities

  • Continuously operate and improve data centre services, servers, storage, virtualization, network, workstation, identity, collaboration and remote access, including supervision, patching, backup and documentation.
  • Automate repetitive operations and manage on-premise, cloud or hybrid migration and modernization decisions.
  • Manage the network and data centre infrastructure (LAN/WAN/Wi-Fi, firewalls, VPN, certificates) and ensure the stability, security and automation of Citrix platforms and users.
  • Pilot major incidents and maintain proven recovery and continuity capabilities, with restoration tests and a clear IT/OT border for connected equipment.
  • Implement group security controls and local plans (MFA, privileged access, vulnerabilities, hardening, data protection) with Group IT and regional security.
  • Manage suppliers, contracts, licences and budgets; conducting tenders; ensure rigorous use of the ITSM with clear communication to the trades.

Leadership and mode of operation

  • Develop a culture of responsibility, responsiveness and service orientation, with clear roles, stress/suppleence models and development plans.
  • Effectively conduct operational reviews, individual interviews and governance forums, developing the team through coaching and documentation.
  • Combining strategic leadership with concrete involvement when complex incidents or diagnoses require it.

Your Profile

  • University diploma or higher vocational training in computer science, or equivalent qualification; strong long-term work experience will also be considered.
  • At least 8 years of significant experience in the fields of IT infrastructure and operations, including several years in the management of technical teams within a multi-site organisation.
  • Experience in managing critical infrastructure for activity and major incidents; strong knowledge of high availability, backup/recovery, cybersecurity and operational risk management.
  • Experience in piloting external providers and operators, as well as in managing contracts, licences, budgets and tenders.
  • A pragmatic approach to automation, with experience in scripting, orchestration, DevOps or Infrastructure as Code.
  • Current knowledge of English and very good knowledge of German or French (experience in both linguistic regions in Switzerland is an asset)
  • Driver's licence valid and available for travel, as well as outside of office hours.

We offer

  • A challenge within a changing company
  • A stimulating, versatile and interesting activity in a good working environment, where active collaboration is valued while encouraging autonomy.
  • An intensive integration programme is planned, allowing our new colleagues to benefit from product and system training within the company.
  • Support for the professional and personal development of our employees.
  • A function vehicle with petrol card - can also be used privately
  • Fixed salary x 13 + bonus on goals
  • 5 weeks of holiday (6 weeks from 50 years old) with the possibility of purchasing an additional holiday up to 10 days a year, additional benefits such as an independent and well-funded pension fund, as well as discounts on our entire product range and additional external benefits.
