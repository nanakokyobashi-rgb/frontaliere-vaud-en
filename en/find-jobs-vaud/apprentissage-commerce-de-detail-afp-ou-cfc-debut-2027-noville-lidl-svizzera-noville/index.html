Full description

Do you already have an idea of what you will do after receiving your school-leaving certificate? Take the next step into your future with us and benefit from the development opportunities at Lidl Switzerland. Stores are at the heart of our business. As an apprentice in retail (with a focus on creating shopping experiences), in an AFP or CFC program, you contribute to the success of your Lidl store. Start/End AFP: August 1, 2027 – July 31, 2029 Start/End CFC: August 1, 2027 – July 31, 2030

Your mission

  • Operating the cash registers and preparing cash reports
  • Ensuring freshness, quality and cleanliness
  • Stocking and presenting goods
  • Checking the receipt of goods in the stockroom and assisting your team during inventory counts
  • Helping to plan and implement promotions and seasonal displays
  • Determining merchandise requirements and ordering goods in the 3rd year of apprenticeship (CFC)
  • First experience in team leadership in the 3rd year of apprenticeship (CFC)

Your profile

  • Average to very good grades in the lower secondary school-leaving certificate
  • AFP: A1 level in a foreign language and CFC: at least A2
  • Enjoyment of working with people
  • Independent working style and a quick grasp
  • Team spirit, reliability and a sense of responsibility
  • Willingness to learn and availability
  • Physical stamina
  • Good command of French

Our offer An apprenticeship that opens up many paths. After successfully completing your apprenticeship, and if you have good personal skills, we will help you progress to a management position in the store. In addition, you will benefit from the Lidl collective labour agreement and numerous attractive benefits
