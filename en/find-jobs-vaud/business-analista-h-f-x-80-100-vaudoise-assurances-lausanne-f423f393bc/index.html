Full description

Vaudoise Insurance is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. A strong, forward-looking company, it builds on its cooperative values to provide its employees with an inclusive and stimulating healthy working environment conducive to the development and development of talent. Strongly committed to its digital transformation, Vaudoise Assurances wants to strengthen its teams in charge of the evolution of its applications. To accompany us, we are looking for a Business Analyst (m/f/x) 80 - 100% within our customer service team with a strong affinity for web front solutions. The mission is to create value-added user experiences for our customers through digital technology. In this position, you play a central role between business and IT, as the preferred partner for the Product Owner and the project manager. The various missions that await you:

  • Prepare and moderate sessions to identify and define needs
  • Write user stories and conduct analysis work on features
  • Advise stakeholders on business and technical alternatives (internal and/or external)
  • Translate requirements into technical specifications, related to IS and architecture
  • Actively collaborate with Product Owner, Trade Experts and delivery team
  • Accompany and participate in functional testing sessions and UAT (User Acceptance Testing) What makes you unique:
  • Higher education type EPF, HES, HEC, UNI or other title deemed equivalent in IT / technical field with equivalent IT / professional experience
  • 3 years to 5 years of experience in the development of omnichannel digital platforms type e-commerce, customer area, Website, SEO etc...
  • Knowledge of common digital tools and solutions (CMS, HTML, CSS, Analytics, API etc.)
  • Successful experience in agile project management, insurance an asset
  • Ideally business certification analysis and understanding of global IT architecture and integration issues
  • French current, English (C1 minimum), German an asset You are organized, proactive, curious and enthusiastic, and you like to work as a team in a multidisciplinary setting. You are also able to drive a subject autonomously, and finally thanks to your sense of communication, you have a very good relationship We offer you:
  • A healthy working environment in a shared enterprise labeled "Best Employer"
  • Multiple social benefits, including flexibility in work organisation
  • Working in a dynamic and passionate team
  • Human values focused on the well-being of employees We look forward to receiving your complete application package digitally (with curriculum vitae, cover letter, copies of work certificates and diplomas).
