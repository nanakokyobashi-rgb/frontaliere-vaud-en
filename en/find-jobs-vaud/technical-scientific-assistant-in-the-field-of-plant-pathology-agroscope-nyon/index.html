Good food, healthy environment

Agroscope is the federal government's centre of excellence for research in the agriculture and food industry. The researchers carry out their activities at various locations in Switzerland. Its headquarters are in Bern-Liebefeld (from 2026: Posieux FR). Agroscope is based at the Federal Department of Economic Affairs, Education and Research EAER. Additional information

The Mycology research group is working on innovative research topics to combat the epidemic spread of fungal diseases in the long term. These include i) epidemiological studies, ii) the development of new bio-based fungicides, iii) the development of predictive models and iv) the use of fungi in degradation processes and mycore mediation.
