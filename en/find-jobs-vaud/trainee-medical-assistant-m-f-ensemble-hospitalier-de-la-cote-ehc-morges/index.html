Full description

The Hospitaller of the Coast — EHC has been a health network at your service for over 20 years. It brings together institutions with complementary missions of acute care, rehabilitation, accommodation in EMS and first resort medicine. We deliver more than 40 medical disciplines in order to take care of each patient and resident, thanks to our 2'050 professionals working on a daily basis to provide them with quality care.

We are looking for our medical centers in Aubonne, Bussigny, Simplon in Renens, North-South and Arbora in Crissier, Chavannes, as well as the Permanence des Halles in Morges and the North-South Policlinic in Crissier:

7 Interns Medical assistants 100% (M/F)

From 1 August 2027.

WE OFFER YOU

  • The opportunity to discover the world of work in an evolving institution whose mission is to take care of all our patients and residents.
  • The opportunity to benefit from close monitoring during your internship, by involved and experienced trainers, who will accompany you towards the success of your SWC.
  • A mobility plan including participation in your annual public transport subscription or on your bike or basketball trip to your workplace, for environmental reasons.
  • Discounts at various centres such as several fitness centres or the Bains de Lavey), numerous institutional events and a business drink to share moments of conviviality during the beautiful days.
  • The possibility of having a fixed time at the workplace to prepare your final exams.
  • Professional opportunities within the institution after your apprenticeship.

YOUR POSITIONS

  • A compulsory school leaving certificate.
  • Validation of your medical assistant training at a recognized school.
  • A well-defined professional project, thanks to an observation workshop that allowed you to confirm your choice.
  • A real motivation to get involved in a medical centre and support patients who are weakened in their health, while carrying out various administrative work.
  • Open, listening and empathetic, you show maturity and interest in teamwork.

OBJECTIVES

  • Participate in the reception and care of patients in the medical centre.
  • Provide hotline, inform and guide patients.
  • Perform various administrative tasks.
  • Perform care and assist physicians in certain procedures.
  • Conduct laboratory and radiographic examinations.

WE ARE

  • A training company engaged in succession training, with about 80 apprentices in 16 different trades, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.
  • An institution consisting of acute care and rehabilitation institutions, a private clinic, EMS, medical centres and permanences located in the region of La Côte and the first clinical hypnosis school dedicated to the care community in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A network of care allowing many opportunities for internal change according to the aspirations and development projects of employees.
  • More than 2,000 employees taking care of patients and residents of the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment conducive to the realization of projects.
