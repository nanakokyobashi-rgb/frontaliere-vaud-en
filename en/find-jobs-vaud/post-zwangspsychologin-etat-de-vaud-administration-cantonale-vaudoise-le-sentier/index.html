Full description

You are sensitive to the challenges in the field of psychology in schools and want to help young people in difficulty in the Vaudian training system, join us to strengthen our team at the Path!

Please submit the application file in a single PDF (CV, diplomas and work certificates). For more information, please consult specifications by clicking here or contact Carole Henry, Team Leader, who is happy to be at your disposal at 079 701 25 04.
