Your assignment

  • Ensure installation, installation and installation of fire doors and systems in different yards;
  • Perform laying of fire doors, tanks, shutters and other fire protection elements;
  • Perform fire sealing work around cable passages, pipes, canals and other technical crossings;
  • Making several passive fire protection systems;
  • Apply fire-retardant products and materials according to the technical requirements and regulations in force;
  • Perform work related to sealants, foams, malts, mastics and other fire products;
  • Ensure the preparation of the supports before laying and ensure a clean and accurate finish;
  • Read and interpret projects, schemes and technical data sheets for work;
  • Use the tools and equipment necessary for installation correctly;
  • Check and finish in order to ensure the conformity of the plants;
  • Strictly comply with safety instructions and fire protection procedures;
  • Working in collaboration with the different teams present on site;
  • Keep the material and the working area clean and in good condition.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Good ability to read and understand technical projects and cards;
  • Able with hands and at ease in the use of electrical tools;
  • Independent, precise, careful and meticulous profile;

  • Serious, reliable and punctual;
  • Ability to work both alone and on team;
