Role overview

The Engineering and Sustainability Directorate (DID) provides technical and strategic expertise within the Buildings and Heritage Branch (RPB) in energy and environmental matters.

As a heating technician, you contribute to the operation and energy quality of the heating facilities of the Vaud State housing stock and ensure the technical link between engineering and building operation.

Perform technical visits to the heating facilities of the cantonal housing stock, prepare complete and documented site records, assess the general condition of the equipment, identify malfunctions and make recommendations
