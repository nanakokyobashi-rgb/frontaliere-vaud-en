THE HOSPITAL PEDIATRIAL SERVICE OF THE RESEARCH

This unit, consisting of 5 beds, accommodates children up to 16 years of age for crisis treatment and child-psychiatric assessments.

SKILLS – REQUIREMENTS REQUIRED

  • Have a diploma in nursing recognized by the Swiss Red Cross. Having a mental health CAS would be an asset
  • Have desired professional experience in child psychiatry, psychiatry or pediatrics
  • Have the capacity to implement a therapeutic project
  • Working with multidisciplinary teams
  • Have knowledge of the extrahospital child-psychiatric care network
  • Experience in counselling, support, supervision in the implementation of good practices, while ensuring the organisation and implementation of activities would be an asset
  • Have the capacity to work in the fall while demonstrating ease in organizing and collaborating in maintaining and developing interdisciplinary work
  • Have a sense of contact and ability to manage groups of children and adolescents

WE OFFER

  • A varied activity
  • Opportunities for continuing training
  • A salary according to the scale of the Vaudian Hospital Federation (FHV)
  • Excellent social benefits
