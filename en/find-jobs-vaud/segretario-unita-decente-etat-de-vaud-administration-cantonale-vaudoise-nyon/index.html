Full description

As secretary of decentralized unit you participate in the good running of the Nyon Gymnase and collaborate continuously with the teaching staff, students and parents. Thus you contribute not only to administrative efficiency, but also to the overall success of the educational mission entrusted to post-compulsory Vaudian education.

  • Collaborate in the day-to-day management of the school secretariat, reception, telephone and e-mail
  • Provide administrative management of a flight of students from the School of Maturity, collaborate in the organization of exam sessions and prepare the promotion ceremony
  • Ensure the preparation of the brochure of optional courses and administratively manage the related registrations, as well as manage absences for student leave during university visits
  • Prepare all the circulars inherent in school life and print legitimation cards
  • Manage spontaneous offers, local rental, duplicates, ... Fixed working days, Tuesdays and Fridays all day and half day on Thursdays. The disclosure of judicial history (virgin judicial case) is required to perform the function.   • Spirit of mutual assistance and collaboration
