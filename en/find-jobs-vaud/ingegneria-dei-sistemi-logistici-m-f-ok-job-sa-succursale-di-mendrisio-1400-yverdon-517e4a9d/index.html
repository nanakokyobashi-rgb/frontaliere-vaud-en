Logistics Systems Engineer (M/F) back to list In order to strengthen our teams, we are looking for a Logistics Systems Engineer (M/F). Job description

Your mission

  • Ensure availability and smooth operation of applications supporting logistics activities and automated flows
  • Monitor system performance and develop reporting tools for effective monitoring of operations
  • Act as a technical reference for warehouse management solutions, automated equipment control and associated interfaces
  • Participate in the analysis, design and implementation of functional and technical changes
  • Contribute to projects for migration, modernization and improvement of industrial and logistical systems
  • Identify the causes of malfunctions, coordinate corrective actions and follow up until resolution
  • Prepare and maintain technical documentation to ensure sustainability of solutions
  • Promote knowledge sharing and support the development of technical skills within the team

Profile

Profile desired

  • Advanced training in industrial computer science, system engineering, computer engineering or equivalent
  • Experience in an industrial, logistical, production or supply chain environment
  • Strong knowledge of relational databases and data analysis tools
  • Controlling data exchange, application interfaces and systems integration platforms
  • Ability to understand complex architectures and to intervene independently on various technical issues
  • Experience in driving or participating in industrial information system development projects
  • Analytical spirit, organizational sense and ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously
  • Relationship building for collaboration with technical and trade partners
  • Excellent mastery of French and good professional English
