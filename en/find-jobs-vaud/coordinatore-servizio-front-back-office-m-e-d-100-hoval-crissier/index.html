Full description

We are looking to strengthen our internal service team in Crissier!

As a leading technology company, Hoval has inspired people for decades with pioneering system solutions for heating and air conditioning technology. In order to offer our wide range of services, we need motivated people who are happy to be the first point of contact for our customers and service technicians.

You will be responsible, within a team at our regional centre in Crissier, for optimal customer service and coordination of the professional assignments of service specialists.

Activities

  • Optimal planning of the activities of service technicians
  • Telephone reception for customer requests (professional and private)
  • Preparation of tenders
  • Processing of warrants and service invoices
  • Supervision and processing of intervention reports

What we offer you

  • Various and responsible tasks in a prosperous and progressive family business
  • A hub function with extensive skills
  • An open and dynamic team
  • Attractive Terms and Conditions of Employment

Your profile

  • Good business experience
  • Organizational talent with first experience in field intervention planning and affinity for heating technology
  • Familiarity with current Microsoft Office and SAP applications
  • Service-oriented personality, with listening and understanding
  • Ability to impose and work under pressure
  • A place of residence in the vicinity of the Regional Headquarters and good German knowledge are desired

Are you a passionate and well-organized person and looking forward to this challenge? We look forward to receiving your complete application (with CV, all work certificates, diplomas, etc.) online.
