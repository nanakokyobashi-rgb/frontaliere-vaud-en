Full description

As part of the temporary strengthening of our team, we are looking for a future agricultural workshop manager within the Orb Plains Establishments (EPO) under a one-year fixed-term contract. As such, you accompany detainees in their daily work by ensuring the replacement of the incumbent workshop managers in the various agricultural workshops. You thus contribute to the continuity of activities, while ensuring that production, safety and supervision objectives are met. You pass on your knowledge to them, enabling them to develop new social and professional skills, and thus help them reintegrate. Through your commitment, you contribute to the protection of the population by providing supervision and supervision of detainees. You ensure the care of animals and crops, guaranteeing animal welfare and strict enforcement of the Animal Protection Act and the guidelines for the use of veterinary products. You will also be required to participate in the mill's activities, including the drying and storage of cereals from internal production and the preparation of feed mixtures for animals in the field. You act at all times in accordance with our values: "Loyality, Solidarity, Credibility".

  • Train and mentor people detained in agricultural workshops where you provide replacements;
  • To ensure the management of animals (bovine, poultry, pigs) and the cultivation of crops on the basis of instructions from the head of the sector in compliance with legal standards of hygiene and safety at work;
  • Ensure the welfare of animals and the application of directives on the use of veterinary products and strictly implement the Animal Protection Act;
  • Provide supervision, security and supervision of prisoners working in the workshop and ensure that discipline is maintained;
  • To prepare regular behavioural reports and assessments of the skills and skills of supervised persons and to work with stakeholders in the various sectors (social, security, professional, etc.) to ensure adequate care for detained persons.
