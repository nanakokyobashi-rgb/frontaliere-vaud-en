Role overview

As a CIUS, you are a key player in the organization and management of care activities within the rehabilitation clinic, and as such:

Mission

  • Actively participate in the management of the health care team (planning, mentoring, coaching of staff, management of priorities).
  • Ensure the fluidity, quality and reliability of the information transmitted within the institution and among the different actors in the care.
  • Ensure the efficiency of patient care, ensuring optimal organization of resources and adequate burden sharing.
  • Ensure quality and safety of care, in accordance with institutional standards, protocols and requirements.
  • Contribute to the development, optimization and harmonization of care processes.
  • Participate in the implementation, evaluation and monitoring of rehabilitation protocols, in collaboration with multidisciplinary teams.
  • Support teams in the field and in complex situations.
  • Promote a positive working environment and constructive team dynamics.

Qualifications Required

  • The Bachelor of Science HES-SO in Nursing or equivalent title (Red Cross recognition for foreign degrees)
  • Professional experience of at least 5 years in nursing, ideally in rehabilitation
  • Experience confirmed in team management
  • Management training (or ongoing) is an asset
  • Good understanding of organizational and financial issues in the health sector
  • Excellent communication and coordination skills
  • Ability to make decisions, manage priorities and deal with situations

We attach great importance to the diversity of the lives of our employees. Our positions are open to both women and men.

About us Anchored in the panorama of the Riviera since 1905, the Valmont Clinic offers personalized rehabilitation stays for people with neurological, orthopaedic and back damage, in an exceptional setting, facing Lake Léman and the Alps. Today, it has become a flagship rehabilitation facility, due to its unparalleled therapeutic efficiency, competent teams and advanced technical plateau.

The rehabilitation doctors are collaborating with a team of multidisciplinary experts, composed of physiotherapists, e-sweet therapists, neuropsychologists, logopedists, sports masters and dietician. In 2006, the clinic joined Swiss Medical Network, one of the two main Swiss groups of private clinics.
