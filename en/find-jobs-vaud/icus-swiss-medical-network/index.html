Role overview

As a CIUS, you are a key player in the organization and management of care activities within the rehabilitation clinic, and as such:

  • Take an active part in the management of the health care team (planning, mentoring, coaching of staff, management of priorities).
  • Ensure the fluidity, quality and reliability of the information transmitted within the institution and between the various actors in the care.
  • Ensure the efficiency, efficiency of the care of patients, ensuring an optimal organization of resources and an adequate distribution of the workload.
  • Ensure the quality and safety of the care, in compliance with the institutional standards, protocols and requirements.
  • Contribute to the development, optimization and harmonization of care processes.
  • Participate in the implementation, evaluation and monitoring of rehabilitation protocols, in collaboration with multidisciplinary teams.
  • Support field teams and Üsz in complex situations.
  • Promote a positive working environment and a constructive team dynamic.

  • Good understanding of the organizational and financial challenges of the health sector
  • Excellent communication and coordination skills
  • Ability to make decisions, manage priorities and deal with complex situations

What we offer We attach great importance to the diversity of life paths of our employees. Our positions are open to both women and men.

The Clinique Valmont has been in the panorama of the Riviera since 1905, offering personalized rehabilitation stays for people with neurological, orthopaedic and back problems, in an exceptional setting, facing Lake Léman and the Alps. Today, it has become a flagship rehabilitation facility, due to its unparalleled therapeutic efficiency, competent teams and state-of-the-art technical platform.

The rehabilitation doctors are collaborating with a team of multidisciplinary experts, composed of physiotherapists, e-sweet therapists, neuropsychologists, logopedists, sports masters and dietician. In 2006, the clinic joined Swiss Medical Network, one of the two main Swiss groups of private clinics.
