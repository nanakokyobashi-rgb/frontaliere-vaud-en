# PiBs: Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Computer Science

He tuo futuro inizia who

Do you wish to enter working life immediately after your maturity, without giving up a high school degree? So you found the perfect solution for yourself. The Bachelor course integrated into practice (PiBS) offers many career opportunities in the digital world.

General conditions

In collaboration with the Ecole d'Ingénierie et de Gestion du Canton de Vaud (HEIG-VD), Swisscom offers you a unique training course. By combining education and professional practice, you become a highly sought-after IT professional and prepare for your entry into the labour market. In addition, you use your computer knowledge on interesting and topical topics such as Big Data, Cloud Computing or information security. At the same time, you deepen your knowledge in software development, application programming, web technologies and computer networks.

Admission requirements

  • You have gymnastic maturity
  • You have not yet more than one year of professional experience in a field related to
  • In this case, you already meet the conditions for admission to higher specialised schools and your profile can no longer be chosen for the PiBS.
  • You have not yet obtained a graduate degree in computer science or have completed a maximum of three semesters in a high school (HES, university, ETH/EPFL). The credits are determined by the head of the study programme. It is not possible to spend in a higher semester (lack of practical experience).
  • Before enrolling, learn about the study model, course content, possible specializations, and procedure – e.g.: www.ffhs.ch. You will find detailed information about the PiBS in computer science and you will be able to register for a first interview with the school guidance service.

Training process

You work up to 24 hours a week at Swisscom and take a practical IT training on different projects. The other days of the week are devoted to your computer studies because the course is based on the Blended Learning model: You're taking face-to-face classes one day a week. For the remaining study time, you work on the goals yourself using an interactive and multimedia online learning platform.
