Role overview

As an alternate cantonal chemist, you hold a key position in the Office of Consumer Affairs (OFCO), attached to the Department of Agriculture, Sustainability and Climate and Digital Affairs.

A true partner of the Cantonal Chimist, you support her in the management of consumer health protection missions and ensure her replacement in the operational and strategic conduct of the Office.

You are involved in coaching the teams attached to the position, coordinating complex and cross-cutting issues, and actively contributing to risk management and institutional decisions.
