Your assignment

  • Drive construction machinery according to the needs and instructions of the manager.
  • Perform light earth movement, leveling and surface preparation.
  • Participate in the excavation and accommodation of the working areas.
  • Ensure handling and moving materials on site.
  • Collaboration with the workers on the ground to ensure the proper conduct of operations.
  • Check the operating status of the machine and provide its ordinary maintenance.
  • Respect the safety standards and procedures in force in the yards.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Owner of the license of operator of machinery M1 recognized in Switzerland.
  • First positive experience in driving construction machinery.
  • Good mastery of compact machines and earth-moving works.
  • Versatile, motivated, reliable and punctual person.
  • Good physical form and attitude to teamwork.
  • Ability to work independently and conscientiously.
  • Driving license and vehicle: an advantage.
  • Immediate availability
