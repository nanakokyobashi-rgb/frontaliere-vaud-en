Full description

Background As part of development and application development projects, our client is looking for a QA Test Lead capable of coordinating and executing all testing activities to ensure the quality of the delivered solutions. Main missions

  • Define and evolve the testing strategy.
  • Design, maintain and execute test plans and cases.
  • Organize and coordinate functional testing, integration, validation, non-regression and exploratory campaigns.
  • Monitor, qualify and prioritize anomalies.
  • Produce quality indicators and test reports.
  • Work closely with trade, development and project teams.
  • Participate in continuous improvement of testing processes and repositories.
  • Contribute to the automation of tests where relevant.

Profile sought

  • Experience confirmed in software quality assurance and application testing.
  • Mastery of test methodologies (ISTQB, TMAP or equivalent).
  • Experience in managing test campaigns and quality control.
  • Good knowledge of test management tools such as XRay or Octane .
  • Ability to analyze functional and technical requirements.
  • Excellent communication and coordination skills.

Assessed competencies

  • Agile / Scrum Environment Experience.
  • Knowledge of testing automation (Selenium, UFT or equivalent).
  • Sensitivity to performance tests and CI/CD chains.

Deliverables expected

  • Strategy and test plan.
  • Reference of tests and data sets.
  • Campaign reports and quality dashboards.
  • Monitoring anomalies and recommendations for improvement.
  • Quality assessment and continuous improvement plan.

Why join Talan Switzerland?

  • Participate in the growth of a rapidly expanding Data & AI practice.
  • Play a key role in structuring our Data Management offer.
  • Intervention on strategic programs with major Swiss accounts.
  • Benefit from an entrepreneurial environment offering autonomy, impact and opportunities for change.
  • Collaborate with experts Data, Analytics, Data Governance and Artificial Intelligence.
