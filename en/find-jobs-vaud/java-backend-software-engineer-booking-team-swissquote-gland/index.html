Full description

About the team The Booking team owns the backend systems triggered after an order is executed: creating and updating client positions, applying fees and commissions, and making sure every trade is booked correctly and reported to the banking package. The system now support securities and crypto assets. We also build the internal tools Business Operations rely on to book manually traded products and handle mistrades.

You role will include

  • Translate business needs into clear functional and technical specifications, together with Business Operations and product stakeholders
  • Design and document the technical architecture of new features, and define the related business test scenarios
  • Build and evolve our Java services within the existing architecture
  • Design new APIs and data models to decouple Booking from external components
  • Own quality end to end: unit and integration tests (JUnit), end-to-end tests, code reviews and validation in pre-production
  • Investigate and fix Level 3 production issues, and address root causes
  • Use AI tools daily to work faster and better, and proactively propose new AI skills, agents or automations that improve the team's productivity.
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or equivalent, with around 5 years of professional Java backend experience
  • Strong skills in modern Java (17+), Spring Boot, Hibernate/JPA and Maven
  • Willingness to embrace AI-assisted development (Claude codes) as part of your everyday workflow
  • Solid knowledge of relational databases and SQL
  • Experience with event-driven architectures and messaging (RabbitMQ or Kafka)
  • Hands-on experience with Docker, CI/CD (Jenkins) and Git/GitHub
  • Good practices around testing, clean code and code reviews
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, especially when investigating production issues
  • Clear communication with both technical and business people
  • Rigorous and autonomous, while being a true team player
  • Interest in financial markets; experience in securities trading, booking or settlement is a strong plus
  • Fluent in English
  • French is a plus
