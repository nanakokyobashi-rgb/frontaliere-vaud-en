We make spaces where it is good to live.

With more than 25 years of experience in solar photovoltaics, Solstis has built the largest solar power plants in Western Switzerland while responding to the growing demands of individuals for domestic solar systems.

Your area of activity

Solstis SA is looking for a motivated, autonomous and focused electrician solutions to strengthen its team in the field of renewable energies.

Your Mission

  • Perform, connect and commission photovoltaic installations.
  • Install and connect storage batteries and charging terminals.
  • Ensure maintenance, control and troubleshooting of facilities.
  • Diagnosis faults and perform re-service work.
  • Perform wiring, measurement and component replacement work.
  • Write intervention reports and provide quality service to clients.
  • Respect electrical standards and safety rules.

Your profile

  • Installer-electrician CFC.
  • Experience in photovoltaics, maintenance or troubleshooting, an asset.
  • Knowledge of batteries and charging stations, an advantage.
  • Autonomous, accurate, reliable and oriented solutions.
  • Good customer contact and team spirit.
  • Driver's licence required.
  • Interest in new technologies and renewable energies.
