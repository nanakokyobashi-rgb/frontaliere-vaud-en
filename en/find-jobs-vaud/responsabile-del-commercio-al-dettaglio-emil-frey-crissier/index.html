What you need to know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family business with more than 100 years of experience, but is also one of the leaders in the automotive sector. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the pillars of the company. Its main activities are the import of world brand vehicles, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare parts and accessories and the provision of financial services. Emil Frey SA Crissier is a trusted partner for a wide range of automotive brands. It also offers a high-performance bodywork service as well as a complete spare parts shop ensuring an optimal customer experience.
