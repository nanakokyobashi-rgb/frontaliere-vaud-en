Your role

As an electrician headman, you will supervise and coordinate all electrical works in different yards, both new construction and renovation. Ensure the correct execution of electrical installations in compliance with current designs, standards, deadlines, safety instructions and quality requirements.

Your main responsibilities

  • Analyze, interpret and validate electrical projects and technical documentation
  • Plan, organize and coordinate the interventions of electricians and subcontractors
  • Supervision of the construction of electrical installations in the yard
  • Coordinate the installation of electrical panels, channels, pipes, technical sheaths and automated systems
  • Organize laying of ducts, channels, supports and other elements necessary for the correct execution of the works
  • Ensure the monitoring of the progress of the yard and ensure compliance with deadlines
  • Check the quality of the work performed and compliance with technical specifications
  • Participating in controls, testing and commissioning of electrical installations
  • Identifying the technical problems encountered on the field and proposing appropriate solutions
  • Ensure strict compliance with safety standards
  • Keep the yard clean, neat and safe
  • Ensure effective communication with the customer, the technical office, the management of work and other professional figures
  • Ensure good coordination between the different subjects involved in the construction site

And now we're talking about you? You have a federal capacity certificate (CFC) as an electrical installer or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland. You have a proven experience in the management of electric yards, both in the field of new buildings and in that of renovations. Master technical facilities, current regulations and field management. Organized, responsive and oriented to solutions, you can make decisions, anticipate the problems of the yard and ensure accurate, reliable and compliant execution of the requirements of the craft.

Your profile

  • Federal Capacity Certificate (AFC) as electrical installer or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland
  • Experience acquired as headman or team manager in the electrical industry
  • Great mastery of wiring, electrical paintings and technical facilities
  • Good knowledge of current Swiss standards
  • Excellent readability and interpretation of electrical plans and schemes
  • Natural leadership and ability to lead a team
  • Sense of organization, priorities and responsibilities
  • Rigour, autonomy and decision-making spirit
  • Good ability to collaborate with the different subjects involved in the construction site
  • Good physical form, adapted to the needs of field work
  • Valid driving licence
  • Personal vehicle: an appreciated advantage

You are a reliable, structured and solution oriented person, able to ensure the proper running of the yard while ensuring a clean, safe and consistent job. Interested? Do you love and master your profession and want to put your skills in the service of projects that really matter? Then this offer is for you. From us, your know-how makes the difference and directly contributes to the success of the yards.
