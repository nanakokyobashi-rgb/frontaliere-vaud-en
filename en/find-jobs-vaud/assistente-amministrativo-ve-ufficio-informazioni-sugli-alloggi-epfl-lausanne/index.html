Full description

Mission

The Student Life Experience (VPH-SLE) field supports the EPFL course and contributes to excellence, promoting integration, academic success and empowerment. It is aimed at students, doctoral students and EPFL staff members in charge of accompanying them.

The housing team provides information on the various housing options in the Lausanne region and on the various steps to be taken.

We are currently looking for an Administrative Assistant.ve to welcome, advise and guide EPFL community in housing search in order to strengthen the current team.

Main tasks and responsibilities

  • Welcome, advise and guide EPFL students, families and in-house services in housing search.
  • Collaborate closely with the units offering services to students, with the bodies in charge of the taking of leases as well as all the partners for the housing of EPFL.
  • Moderating the UNIL-EPFL housing portal, advising tenants in renting their property.
  • Maintain and develop linkages with partner stewardship and governance.

Profile

  • Mastery of French and English spoken and written (minimum C1).
  • You are comfortable with office tools (Word, Excel and Power Point.)
  • Team spirit, are rigorous and precise.
  • You can organize and plan your work and meet the deadlines.
  • You have a spirit of analysis and synthesis.
  • You know how to build good relationships with internal and external interlocutors.
