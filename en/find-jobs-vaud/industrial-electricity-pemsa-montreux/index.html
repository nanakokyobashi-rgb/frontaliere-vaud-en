Your role

As an industrial electrician, you will operate on industrial sites, technical plants and sensitive environments (heavy, chemical, pharmaceutical, energy or nuclear industry). You will take care of the installation, maintenance and repair of electrical equipment in compliance with safety standards and production requirements.

  • Perform assembly, wiring and commissioning of industrial electrical installations.
  • Installation of cables, connection of electrical panels, control panels, engines, sensors and actuators.
  • Perform preventive and corrective maintenance operations on industrial plants and equipment.
  • Diagnose electrical failures and intervene quickly to limit production stops.
  • Read and interpret electrical schematics, planes and technical documentation.
  • Participate in the modification, improvement and modernization of electrical installations.
  • Perform the tests, controls and commissioning of the equipment ensuring compliance.
  • Strictly respect HSE procedures and specific security protocols of industrial sites and high-risk environments.
  • Collaborate with maintenance, production and technical managers to ensure the proper functioning of the plants.

And now we're talking about you?

  • You have an AFC as an electrician, assembly electrician, automation technician or an equivalent training.
  • You have proven experience in the industrial electricity sector, ideally developed in the sectors of heavy, chemical, petrochemical, pharmaceutical, energy or nuclear industries.
  • Master industrial wiring, connection of electric paintings, low voltage plants, as well as maintenance and repair operations.
  • You can read and interpret electrical patterns, technical drawings and industrial documentation.
  • An experience on sites subject to stringent constraints (SEVESO, nuclear, white, central, production facilities or industrial platforms) is a real advantage.
  • You are recognized for your rigour, your autonomy and your ability to intervene effectively in demanding technical environments.
  • Strictly apply HSE procedures, blocking and labelling procedures (LOTO) and applicable safety standards.
  • Appreciate teamwork while being able to manage its operations independently.
  • Owner of the B licence, is available to move in order to intervene in different industrial sites in roman Switzerland.
  • A rapid availability and experience in industrial or nuclear maintenance will be particularly appreciated.
