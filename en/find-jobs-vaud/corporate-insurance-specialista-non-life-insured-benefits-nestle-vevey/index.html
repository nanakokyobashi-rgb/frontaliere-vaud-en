As a Corporate Insurance Specialist – Non-Life & Insured Benefits at Nestlé, your mission is to support the effective renewal and administration of global insurance programs that protect our people, operations and assets. You’ll make a difference by collaborating closely with Group Risk Services colleagues, Finance, Human Resources, Legal, markets, insurance brokers and insurers. This is your opportunity to take ownership, grow your expertise, and deliver meaningful results that make a difference at scale.

How you’ll make an impact as a Corporate Insurance Specialist – Non-Life & Insured Benefits

  • Supporting timely, accurate renewals of global non-life and insured employee benefits programs.
  • Coordinating insurance data, submissions and documentation to strengthen coverage decisions across markets.
  • Ensuring policies, endorsements, premium allocations and payments are accurate and completed on time.
  • Managing smaller insurance programs from renewal administration through payment and claims follow-up.
  • Contributing to fair, effective selection processes for global and local insurers and brokers.
  • Building trusted relationships that improve service quality and support consistent insurance delivery worldwide.

What will make you successful

  • Bringing practical knowledge of insurance gained with an insurer, broker or corporate risk management team.
  • Applying strong attention to detail across renewals, data collection, policy reviews, premium allocation and record keeping.
  • Using sound knowledge of non-life insurance and insured employee benefits to support global programs confidently.
  • Collaborating effectively across teams while building productive relationships with insurers, brokers and internal partners.
  • Communicating fluently in English
  • French and German are an advantage.
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.

What to expect from our hiring process
