  • Lead and drive the deployment of large-scale SAP S4 initiatives, owning key areas such as Asset Accounting, CAPEX, and Lease Accounting while ensuring successful project delivery and integration.
  • Execute and continuously enhance end-to-end business process management (E2E BPM) strategies, delivering integrated solutions across processes, technology, and analytics while enabling global business adoption.

  • You demonstrate a strong methodological foundation (BPM, Six Sigma, Lean, Scrum, PDCA, Kaizen) combined with an agile, customer-centric mindset and high attention to detail and quality.
