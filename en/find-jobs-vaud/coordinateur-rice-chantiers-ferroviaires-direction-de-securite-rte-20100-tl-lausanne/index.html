Coordinator - Directorate of security TEN 20100 Your challenge

You guarantee daily safety during work that impacts our railway lines (m1/m2, tram) and thus ensure the smooth running of operations.

In your capacity as an expert in the field, you establish and monitor safety devices near the lanes according to the regulations in force. You validate applications for site authorization and ensure regular follow-up with the various partners in order to minimize inconvenience to the operation.

You are also responsible for the direction of railway safety and, as such, ensure that safety personnel are instructed and that clearances are followed.

The nature of the position requires perfect control of railway safety regulations (RTE 20100 / 20600) and office automation tools (MS Office).

You

  • Technical diploma ideally supplemented by higher education
  • Experience of minimum 2 years as Chief Security TEN 20100.
  • If possible experience of Directorate of Safety TEN 20100, and/or experience planning/realization of railway yards.
  • Sense of analysis, decision-making capacity, timeliness
  • Excellent sense of service, relational ease
