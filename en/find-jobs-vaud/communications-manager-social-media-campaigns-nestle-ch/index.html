Be a force for good and accelerate change that improves lives and the planet

As a Communications Manager – Social Media & Digital Campaigns at Nestlé, your mission is to lead our global corporate social media channels and digital campaigns, turning corporate priorities and reputation objectives into meaningful audience engagement and measurable outcomes. You will make a difference by working across content, corporate affairs units, markets, analytics, agencies and platform partners. This is your opportunity to take ownership, grow your expertise, and deliver meaningful results that make a difference at scale.

How you’ll make an impact as a Communications Manager – Social Media & Digital Campaigns • Shaping and implementing global channel, audience and content distribution strategies that strengthen engagement across Nestlé’s corporate social media presence. • Leading integrated digital campaigns and paid media activation that advance corporate priorities and reputation objectives. • Optimising platform, search and campaign performance through audience insights, experimentation and setting up a clear measurement framework, including KPIs and reporting standards. • Building scalable governance, guidance and toolkits that enable consistent, effective campaign activation across regions and markets. • Strengthening community management and issue response through clear standards, timely escalation and audience-focused communication. • Developing a high-performing team and growing digital communication capability across the Corporate Affairs network.

What will make you successful • Bringing substantial experience in leading global social media and multi-market digital campaigns and channels. • Demonstrating deep expertise in paid media strategy, audience targeting and campaign optimisation. • Applying strong analytical thinking to turn performance, audience and social listening data into clear decisions. • Applying expertise across search, social platforms and content formats to increase visibility, engagement and campaign effectiveness. • Collaborating and influencing across complex global organisations while leading teams, agencies and partner relationships. • Communicating fluently in English.
