Full description

Amstein + Walthert is an engineering company with approximately 1100 employees. As engineers, planners and consultants, we deal with all aspects of building technology and infrastructure throughout their life cycle. Our solutions are innovative, elegant and durable. Your missions: Do you want to give a new orientation to your construction journey? We propose you to join an experienced team and gradually train you as a fire protection specialist. You take part in various projects, from design to site monitoring, with structured support towards obtaining the title of AEAI specialist. Your missions:

  • Contribute to the development of fire protection concepts, with the support of experienced specialists.
  • Analyse architectural and technical plans to identify applicable fire protection requirements.
  • Complete and maintain fire protection plans in PDF and DWG formats.
  • Develop variants and propose technical solutions adapted to the challenges of each project.
  • Collaborate with architects, contractors and technical representatives throughout the projects.
  • Participate in on-site execution checks alongside the project team.
  • Identify non-conformities in fire protection and contribute to the monitoring of their resolution.
  • Prepare quality assurance and compliance files with specialist support.
  • Prepare and participate in technical and coordination sessions.
  • Assist specialists in their client advisory activities.
  • Participate in discussions with authorities and stakeholders.
  • Gradually develop your skills to obtain the AEAI fire protection specialist. Your skills and qualifications:
  • Training or professional experience in construction.
  • Strong motivation to train and evolve into a fire protection specialist.
  • Practical mind, rigour, organizational sense and good analytical skills.
  • Great ease in reading and understanding technical plans, as well as good spatial representation.
  • Good skills in technical drawing and mastering common software.
  • Significant interest in the technical, normative and regulatory issues of construction.
  • Ability to work independently while fostering collaboration within a multidisciplinary team.
  • Experience in dealing with clients, agents and various stakeholders in the project.
  • Very good editorial skills and ease of speech in French.
  • SWC of draftsman: an expert in architecture, civil engineering or technical CVCSE, or academic training in construction.
