Full description

At the heart of the integrated management of surface waters and lakes and streams, in the Water Resources and Hydraulic Economics Division (WED-Water), Watercourse and Lakeshore Developments Section, in the Natural Resources and Heritage Directorate, as Project Manager, you have the following tasks:

  • Carrying out projects to develop streams and lake banks both for flood protection and revitalization
  • Managing large river correction enterprises (FTEs) for large one or more watershed scale projects
  • Support the hydraulic power manager for the environmental remediation of hydroelectric concessions
  • Advise files related to the Public Domain Police Act (PDPA) for planning and building permit applications In the DGE-EAU, you will be attached to the Water and Lakeside Development Section, composed of 7 dynamic collaborators, for which you will participate in the tasks and activities entrusted to it. For further information, the specifications are available by clicking on this link. Any questions? Mr. Joël Varidel, Head of the Rivers and Lake Shores section, kindly informs you at 021 316 75 13. We look forward to receiving your file and please send it to us in a single PDF file to facilitate processing.
  • Diploma in engineering EPFL or HES in the fields of civil engineering, river hydraulics, environmental science, nature management or equivalent, supplemented by a minimum of 5 years' professional experience in the field of stream development and project monitoring.
  • Very good writing and communication skills, both orally and in writing.
  • Organizational skills, interpersonal skills, stress resistance, ability to adapt and listen.
  • A sense of public service responsibilities and ethics, a spirit of mutual assistance and collaboration.
  • Interest in environmental issues.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
