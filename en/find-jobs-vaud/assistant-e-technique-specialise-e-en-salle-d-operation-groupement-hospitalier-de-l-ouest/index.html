Full description

Your missions

We propose to continue your professional development by engaging you in new missions and participating in the development of the operating unit. Thus, in your new duties, your missions have an impact on the quality of patient care.

Your role will include

  • Participate in patient reception and facility
  • Work closely with all operating unit, anesthesia and sterilization personnel
  • Optimally ensure the operation of surgical procedures in accordance with aseptic and hospital hygiene rules
  • Provide and handle the technical equipment required for each type of intervention

Profile required

We are confident of your rapid success in your future functions through your:

  • Significant experience in the operating room
  • Dynamics and capacity for anticipation
  • Rigor
  • Physical resistance and ability to manage stress
  • Ability to learn and question each other
  • Flexibility
  • Attendance in oral and written communication in French

Level of training

The ATSSO Certificate would be a real asset

Benefits

  • Possibility of rapid career development with management of the training of ATSSO at Espace Competencies after 1 year in the operating unit
  • Exercise within a block providing various specialties (visceral, bariatric, traumatological, orthopaedic, urological, gynaecological, obstetrical, ENT and plastic surgery)
  • Elective and emergency activities
  • Hours of 8h30 and 10h – weekend guard and nights in 12h
  • Specific in-service training for block staff is organised every last Monday of the month
  • Payment of a specialisation bonus for employees with ATSSO certification
