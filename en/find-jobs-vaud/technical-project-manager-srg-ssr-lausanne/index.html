Your Mission

  • Design the technical solutions of the broadcast and IT infrastructure projects (studios, production and distribution authorities, technical premises, mobile units), in pairs with the project manager who carries the steering, budget and planning.
  • Develop design files: functional synoptics, principle diagrams, low current and strong current wiring plans, space layout plans and bay face views.
  • Establish electrical and thermal load balances, size power supplies, protections, redundancy and cooling requirements of technical premises.
  • Define and track the racking: installation of equipment, paths, tracking, mixing, mass management and access.
  • Coordinate technical specialists (IT, network, audio and video broadcast, security) to ensure overall consistency of the solution, and interface with third-party companies (electricity, CVC, second work) as well as with project agents.
  • Follow-up on-site implementation: compliance check, receipt of work, commissioning, withdrawal of reserves.
  • Produce and maintain technical documentation throughout the life cycle: implementation records, plan updates, records of completed works, equipment inventories.
  • Participate in technology watch. Knowledge requirements:
  • Mastery of design and technical documentation tools (AutoCAD, Visio or equivalent).
  • Knowledge of broadcast infrastructures, IP networks and professional audiovisual environments.
  • Experience in structured copper and fibre optic wiring and integration into technical bays.
  • Electricity knowledge applied to technical infrastructure (power supply, redundancy, cooling).
  • Good understanding of professional audio and video protocols (SDI, ST 2110, MADI, AES67, PTP).
  • Project management concepts and experience in provider coordination.
  • Knowledge of Agile and ITIL methods. Your profile
  • Technical training of type ES (graduate technician) or Bachelor HES in electricity, industrial systems, telecommunications or audiovisual. An CFC in a technical field supplemented by significant experience in design or construction is also considered.
  • Professional experience of at least 4 years in the design or construction of technical infrastructures, ideally in the broadcast environment, datacenter or control room.
  • Ability to work in pairs with a project manager and to play the role of technical referent to trade specialists.
  • Documentary rigor and sense of detail
  • Initiative, autonomy and management of priorities, availability and strength of proposals.
  • Ability to co-ordinate on-site and dialogue with Broadcast and non-broadcast trades.
  • Availability for travel to RTS sites.
  • Personal interest in new technologies, innovation and teamwork.
  • English essential. What we offer you Human relations The Operations area takes over the central strategic direction of the SSR's technological orientation and production. The Personnel Management Department Operations ensures, through clear roles, competency-based planning and regional monitoring by language, that employees develop in a targeted manner and are appropriately assigned. It also creates transparency in the use of resources and stable structures for efficient exploitation. In order to strengthen its teams, the RTS is looking for two technical projects for a fixed period of two years. You contribute to the design and implementation of the technical infrastructure necessary to carry out the projects.
