Full description

Job overview

As part of strengthening our Sales Service & Renovation service, we are looking for an Order Coordinator – Maintenance & Modernization Contracts. In this capacity, you will be responsible for preparing and monitoring maintenance and modernization offers, as well as the administrative and commercial management of orders after their assignment by the client, whether service contracts or modernization projects.

What you will do

Customer management

  • Identification of the operator of newly commissioned facilities.
  • Creation and updating of customer data in the CRM for operators of new facilities (end customers).
  • Management and updating of installed fleet data (change of operator, contracting authority, building manager, etc.).

Contract management

  • Development of maintenance contract offers and follow-up of customer follow-ups.
  • Creation and processing of contracts following client validation.
  • Document management and archiving of signed contracts in the CRM.
  • Administration of contracts throughout their lifecycle (modifications, renewals, and terminations).

Modernization order management

  • Preparation of modernization offers in collaboration with the Service Account Manager and the Service Project Manager.
  • Offer tracking
  • Creation of modernization orders in case of contract award.
  • Checking equipment availability and placing the order with the Service Project Manager.

What we ask for

  • Commercial or technical-commercial training (e.g., Technical Sales Agent).
  • Experience in the automatic door sector is an asset.
  • Experience using CRM systems and proficiency in common IT tools.
  • Strong organizational skills.
  • Ease in interpersonal skills and good communication skills with clients and internal contacts.
  • Customer service skills and ability to conduct business transactions.
  • Ability to manage multiple files simultaneously and work efficiently under pressure.
  • Good command of German, both spoken and written.

We offer

  • Attractive employment conditions combined with competitive advantages.
  • Possibilities for partial teleworking.
  • An innovative and exciting technological environment.
  • The opportunity to join an international group with a family spirit.
  • Support that promotes personal and professional development.
  • A collaborative workspace offering autonomy and responsibility.
