Full description

Aubonne Hospital has served its community since 1895. Ideally located near the vineyards and the village, it is one of the three founding hospitals of the EHC. It brings together all the EHC's missions in one place: first-recourse medicine with its medical centre, acute care with its recognized beds of internal medicine and its palliative care unit, the multi-site treatment and rehabilitation centre or long-term care with its EMS and its Temporary Reception Centre (CAT).
