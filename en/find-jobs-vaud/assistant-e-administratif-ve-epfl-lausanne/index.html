Administrative Assistant — EPFL

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Mission

The Infrastructure, Security and Operations Domain (SSD) ensures the operation, maintenance, safety and health of the EPFL campus. It comprises three departments (Operation, Infrastructure, Safety and Health) and nearly 170 collaborators.

To strengthen its team, the DSE is looking for an administrative assistant who will join the administrative pool of the field. You will support all departments and management in their daily administrative tasks, in close collaboration with the other four pool members.

This position is intended for a versatile, organized and comfortable person in a varied technical environment, who wishes to participate in the establishment of common processes within a recently reorganized team.

Main tasks and responsibilities

  • Provide daily administrative support for the domain: processing of requests received by the ticket system, mail, filing, writing of documents and letters
  • Manage orders, invoice and commitment tracking, linked to the Finance Department
  • Organize meetings and events in the domain (convocations, reservations, logistics) and write minutes of them
  • Maintain the agenda of one or more executives in the field, depending on the distribution within the pool
  • Welcome, inform and guide staff, internal and external contacts
  • Supporting projects in the field: monitoring deadlines, preparation of documents, coordination among stakeholders
  • Assure the replacement of colleagues in the administrative pool

Profile

  • SWC or training deemed equivalent
  • At least 3 years experience in a similar administrative function
  • Very good mastery of office tools (Microsoft 365: Outlook, Word, Excel, Teams, OneNote)
  • Excellent writing in French
  • Good knowledge of English (level B2)
  • Organizational sense, rigour and autonomy in the management of priorities
  • Team spirit, discretion and ease in contacts
  • Adaptability and interest in process improvement

Your assets

  • Experience in project management assistance (planning monitoring, coordination, documentation)
  • Experience in public or parastatal administration
  • Practice of a ticket system or application management tool
  • Access with layout of documents and presentations
  • Interest in new technologies, including artificial intelligence, and ability to use them critically and responsibly, while respecting data confidentiality

We offer

  • A varied position at the heart of the operation of a campus of nearly 20,000 people
  • A dynamic working environment, with the possibility of teleworking according to EPFL regulations
  • Conditions of engagement according to the provisions of the EPF and a wide range of services (continuous training, restaurants, sport, mobility)

Information
