This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Within the Brand Creative Studio, and attached to the Brand Creative Studio Manager, you will give life to the brand's graphic expression. You will translate the creative territories defined by the Studio into visual identities, graphic systems and communication media, designed to be deployed consistently across all contact points. Refer to branding issues, develop guidelines, accompany teams in their application and ensure that brand codes are respected. With a visual culture and sharp graphic design, you will play a key role in the graphic and visual development of projects.

Your main activities

  • Design and implement print and digital communication media (publications, newsletters, presentations, events, signage, brochures, etc.).
  • Ensure unimpaired delivery of deliverables within constraints, deadlines and quality standards.
  • Adapt graphic creations to different formats, uses and channels.
  • Create and develop project visual identities (logos, branding, graphic elements and recognition codes).
  • Decline visual identities in a consistent way across the brand's media and universe.
  • Build and formalize clear, durable and easily deployable graphics and guidelines systems.
  • Ensure the role of reference branding by ensuring respect for and consistency of brand codes.
  • Pilot several projects simultaneously and support stakeholders by challenging graphic proposals to maintain a high level of quality.
  • Work closely with internal teams and subsidiaries, while contributing to the evolution of the studio thanks to an active watch in design, branding and visual culture.
  • You have a graduate degree in graphic design, creation, applied arts or equivalent, you justify an experience of at least 5 years in a similar function, in creative studio, agency or luxury home
  • You have a strong graphic and visual culture, rigor and a strong sense of detail, a strong sensitivity to the universe of luxury, design, art and watchmaking
  • You have a very good knowledge of communication media as well as production and manufacturing processes print (printing, finishing...) and ability to dialogue with technical partners
  • You have experience in creating graphic guidelines
  • You have excellent mastery of design software such as Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign), webdesign software (Sketch, Figma)
  • You are comfortable working in a team and managing several projects at the same time and you know how to manage stressful and unexpected situations
  • Your appreciation or mastery of complementary disciplines (motion design, illustration, knowledge in UX/UI, IA...) are real plus
  • You have perfect command of French as well as English in oral and written language
