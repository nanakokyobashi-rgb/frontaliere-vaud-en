Industrial Maintenance Technician (M/F) back to list In order to strengthen our teams, we are looking for an industrial maintenance technician (M/F). Job description

Your mission

  • Ensure preventive and corrective maintenance of production facilities
  • Diagnosis of mechanical, electrical, pneumatic and automated failures
  • Perform troubleshooting interventions in accordance with safety and quality requirements
  • Actively participate in continuous improvement of equipment and maintenance processes
  • Perform facility settings, commissioning and validations
  • Documenting the interventions carried out
  • Working closely with production, quality and methods departments
  • Participate in industrial performance optimization projects

Profile

Profile desired

  • Holder of an CFC of automatic, polymeric, maintenance mechanic or equivalent technical training
  • Confirmed experience in industrial maintenance, ideally in an automated production environment
  • Good knowledge in mechanics, industrial electricity, pneumatics and automation
  • Mastering the reading of electrical and technical diagrams
  • Knowledge of programmable controllers (Siemens, Beckhoff, Schneider or equivalent), an asset
  • Analytical Spirit and Good Diagnostic Capabilities
  • Independent, rigorous and oriented solutions
  • Availability for teamwork or picket services according to business needs
