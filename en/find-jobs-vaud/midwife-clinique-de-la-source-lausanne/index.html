Full description

La Source Clinic is a unique, multidisciplinary acute care facility. It belongs to the La Source Foundation, an independent, private, not-for-profit institution that also oversees the La Source Institute and High School of Health. The independence conferred on him by this status allows him to invest continuously in human and technological terms in order to guarantee his patients the best possible care. We are looking for a Midwife or Nurse 80%

Key Position Information

Reference : CLS/RH/2026.791 / Type of contract : Contract with Indeterminate duration 80% / Hours : Monday to Sunday according to schedule in 12 hours / Date of entry : as soon as we find the right person / Location : in the heart of Lausanne (M2 stop « Riponne » or Bus 2, 3 or 21 stop « Beaulieu-Jomini »)

Why do we need you?

Following the departure of one of our collaborators, we are looking for a midwife or midwife nurse to join our team and participate in the care of patients throughout their journey, in an environment where quality of care, listening and collaboration are central.

What will you do?

  • Accompany future mothers, newborns and their families at each stage, from pregnancy to postpartum
  • Ensure independent, personalized and secure care, in collaboration with doctors and the multidisciplinary team
  • Monitor the evolution of pregnancy, work and postpartum, and intervene with discernment when the situation requires it
  • Monitoring of independent work in the delivery room, in close connection with independent doctors responsible for medical care
  • Support parents in their choices and pass on useful tips around birth, breastfeeding and the first days with baby
  • Support with listening and benevolence specific situations, including during more difficult times
  • To contribute to the diversity of the activities offered within maternity by drawing on its skills and interests, particularly in the context of birth preparation courses and complementary consultations (hypnosis, baby massage, prenatal yoga, etc.)
  • Participate in the supervision, training and evaluation of students and in the development of skills within the team
  • Contribute to continuous improvement in quality of care, working groups and research projects in obstetric care

Who are you going to work with?

The maternity team consists of 26 caregivers, mostly midwives. In a family atmosphere, relaxed, caring and very happy, we accompany families before and after the arrival of their child. Self-help in maternity is one of our values. We can always count on a colleague, and that is really valuable.

You will be in direct contact with Emily Wieland, Nurse Sage Woman Head of Maternity Care Unit. With strong experience in various acute obstetric care units, her priority is to ensure the well-being of her team so that she can provide high quality care. It ensures the smooth functioning of the service on a daily basis according to the common objectives of the service and the Clinic.

What do we expect from you?

The indispensables

  • To benefit from a HES diploma from a midwife nurse or a midwife or a Swiss Red Cross recognized title
  • Justify a recent experience in the delivery room
  • Have experience in consultation and post-partum support
  • Mastering mother and newborn surveillance and quickly identifying a situation at risk
  • Demonstrate autonomy, rigour and discernment in its practice
  • Great relational ease, ability to manage stress and sense of responsibility
  • Putting the patient's listening, respect and needs at the heart of her support

Your assets

  • Be comfortable with breastfeeding and birth preparation
  • Additional diplomas or certifications in the field of perinatality
  • Investing in knowledge transfer and student support
  • Demonstrate interest in continuing training and improving the quality of care

Why choose The Source Clinic?

  • Join the largest private clinic in the canton of Vaud and evolve in a dynamic environment, recognized for its expertise and know-how
  • Offer personalized care and a wide choice of offers before and after birth for families
  • Regularly develop its skills through a rich offer of in-house continuing training, including emergency management and neonatal resuscitation
  • Benefit from a human-sized work environment, while benefiting from the wealth and diversity of activities of a future-oriented health institution
  • Benefit from attractive terms of engagement and advantageous social benefits (reception and integration, LaMal participation, advantageous LPP, discount, etc.)
  • Reconciling work and private life, through opportunities for part-time work, up to more than 7 weeks of vacation, unpaid leave, compensated holidays and valuing exchange time
  • Sharing similar values: a Unique, Respectful, Innovative and Responsible Clinic
