What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Join the first universal bank in the canton of Vaud, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Retail Division, we are looking for one or more:

Digital Marketing Specialist Your missions

  • define and manage the commercial growth strategy across the funnel (acquisition, activation, retention) to translate business objectives into action plans,
  • identify and deploy new sales levers (up-sell, cross-sell) and optimize onboarding and subscription routes in order to develop the customer relationship,
  • planning, targeting and monitoring the performance of campaigns on internal channels (BCV Mobile, BCV-net), in line with the overall strategy,
  • measure the multichannel impact (KPIs, tracking) and optimize the ecosystem of marketing tools to guide decisions,
  • coordinate stakeholders (IT, Legal, Security) and providers to ensure the feasibility and compliance of projects in the banking environment,
  • ensure developments in the areas concerned and implement appropriate adaptations,
  • develop employees' digital skills and design devices to encourage customers to adopt digital channels. Your profile:
  • 5 to 7 years in the digital ecosystem (digital marketing, e-commerce, web performance, SaaS, marketplace),
  • CAS in digital marketing or equivalent training,
  • confirmed experience on AEM (content management, landing pages, forms), Adobe Target (testing, customization) and Adobe Analytics (performance tracking and tracking),
  • excellent understanding of the whole tunnel (acquisition, conversion, retention, recovery) and implementation of digital campaigns (newsletters, forms, SEO/GEO),
  • ability to collaborate effectively with multidisciplinary teams (communication, marketing, IT, providers) and to communicate with non-technical interlocutors;
  • great autonomy, proactivity, rigour of execution and strong sense of responsibility,
  • analytical mindset and ability to prioritize,
  • digital curiosity and a strong sense of internal service,
  • very good knowledge of English, the element is an asset.

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let us write together three stories, one of your career, one of your bank, one of our region.
