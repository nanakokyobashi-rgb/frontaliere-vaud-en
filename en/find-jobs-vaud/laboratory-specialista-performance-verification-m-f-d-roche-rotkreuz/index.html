Main responsibilities

  • Key Responsibilities and Contributions
  • Study Execution & Documentation: Meticulously plan, execute, and report on verification and development studies.
  • Document all findings thoroughly using electronic lab notebooks (eLN).
  • Sample Preparation & Biochemistry: Prepare, contrive, and manipulate whole blood samples to achieve target analyte concentrations for testing.
  • Equipment Management and Ordering: Support equipment qualification and ordering of chemicals and consumables.
  • Safety & Compliance: Work safely and routinely with potentially infectious human blood samples in accordance with biosafety guidelines.

  • Industry Experience: Proven experience in the In-Vitro Diagnostics (IVD) or medical device industry, specifically in system integration, verification, or validation.
