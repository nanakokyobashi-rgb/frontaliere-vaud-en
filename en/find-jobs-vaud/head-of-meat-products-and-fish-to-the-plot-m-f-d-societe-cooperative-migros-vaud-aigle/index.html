Full description

Passionate about the art of butchering, you put your expertise and know-how at the service of our customers. You're ready to coordinate a store and unite a team around ambitious goals. A true ambassador of the brand, you contribute to the performance of your store, to the satisfaction of our customers and to the development of your team.
