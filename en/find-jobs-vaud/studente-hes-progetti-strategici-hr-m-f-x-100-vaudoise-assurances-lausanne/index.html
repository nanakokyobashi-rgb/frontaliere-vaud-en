Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. Active in vocational training, it promotes the development of young talent, with every year more than 120 learners trained in our departments. We are looking for a motivated intern for a straight start or to agree, for a period of 6 months, to join the HR Strategic Projects team and participate in projects serving the entire Human Resources department. This internship offers concrete immersion in the management of cross-cutting HR projects, in collaboration with multiple stakeholders. As an HR Project Intern, you will help build confidence in information, identify trends and focus points, and then provide clear results to inform leadership and decision-making on cross-cutting HR issues. You will also support the coordination of initiatives, the consolidation of information and the design of appropriate monitoring and communication materials for different stakeholders. You will also focus on analysing data from different sources and producing cross-sectional reports and synthesises. The missions that await you:

  • Support for HR project coordination: Support planning and monitoring of cross-cutting projects and topics, prepare coordination points, maintain milestones, actions, responsibilities and next steps
  • HR Portfolio Monitoring Assistance: Contribute to the consolidation of initiatives, timelines, dependencies, risks and issues requiring special attention.
  • Data analysis and reporting: Collect, structure, control and analyse information from different sources, then produce cross-sectional synthesis and reporting
  • Preparation of project deliverables: Design and format presentations, summary notes, follow-up tables, framing documents, FAQs and other materials adapted to different stakeholders
  • Governance and Communication: Contribute to the preparation of governance sessions and forums, document decisions and ensure clear follow-up of agreed actions
  • Cross-cutting collaboration: Interact with HR teams and, depending on projects, with other corporate functions to collect information, coordinate contributions and facilitate consistency
  • Continuous improvement: Propose simple methods, models and tools to make monitoring reliable, improve the readability of information and enhance collective effectiveness Your profile:
  • You are in the course of academic studies (Bachelor or Master) in a field closely related to human resources, economy, management, project management, business analysis, data analysis or equivalent domain
  • Strong interest in HR issues, data analysis and project management
  • Excellent mastery of Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint; a knowledge of Power BI or other visualization tools is an asset
  • An analytical, structured and solutions-oriented mindset, with particular attention to the quality and reliability of information
  • The ability to synthesize complex topics and produce clear, professional and audience-friendly materials
  • Excellent mastery of French; good knowledge of German and/or English is an asset You are also autonomous, curious, proactive and have an excellent sense of collaboration. We offer you:
  • A healthy working environment in a mutual enterprise
  • A diverse experience at the heart of HR department strategic and cross-cutting projects and topics
  • Exposure to different HR expertise and corporate stakeholders
  • The possibility to develop your skills in project coordination, analysis, reporting and communication in practice
  • Proximity support and progressive autonomy on deliverables with real added value The HR team is looking forward to receiving your complete application package (CV, cover letter and 3 final transcripts).
