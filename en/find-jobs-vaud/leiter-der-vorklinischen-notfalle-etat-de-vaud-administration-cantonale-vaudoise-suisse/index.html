Full description

As the head of the pre-hospital emergency field, you are responsible within the Emergency and Crisis Preparedness Directorate for managing the pre-hospital emergency area, managing its human resources and maintaining relationships and collaborations with key partners.

  • Supervise the dossiers carried out by the collaborators of the field, in order to have an overview and provide them with support where necessary
  • Ensure, in collaboration with the Community Emergency Officer, the synergy and coherence of work on pre-hospital and community emergency arrangements
  • Supervise the activities of the BUSAMA. Coordinate continuous improvement work on events
  • Supervising contractual relations and monitoring of institutions with the State
  • Engage and unite its team in the implementation of the strategic plan. Set and monitor the objectives of employees. Promoting a feedback culture
  • Monitor the delivery of services in terms of time, costs and quality. Ensuring the application of standards and the flow of information within its entity and the implementation of management decisions
  • Representing the field with partners and fostering dialogue in a climate of trust-based exchange
  • Provide special expertise in relation to pre-hospital emergency management on behalf of its hierarchy. For more information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact us by email at recruitment.dgs@vd.ch . In order to have all the necessary information to analyze your file, we would like to thank you for sending us, in addition to your CV, a cover letter and all your work certificates and diplomas.
  • Master in a field useful to the function
  • Title or diploma in a field of care (ES, Bachelor, Master, Doctorate)
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in management and project management and 3 years in public administration
  • Leadership
  • Very good personal organization and time management
  • Excellent analytical and synthesis capacity

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
