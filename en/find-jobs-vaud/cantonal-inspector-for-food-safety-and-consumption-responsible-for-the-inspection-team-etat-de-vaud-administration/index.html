Full description

As a cantonal inspector for food security and consumption, you hold a key role in the Office of Consumer Affairs (OFCO), attached to the Department of Agriculture, Sustainability and Climate and Digital Affairs. A field manager and community manager, you coordinate and accompany the team of inspectors in carrying out official controls, inspections and monitoring of risks related to food safety and consumer protection. In close cooperation with the cantonal chemist and the alternate cantonal chemist, you contribute to the implementation of the Office's strategic guidelines and to the development of benefits. You guarantee a coherent, harmonised and demanding application of control practices, placing consumer safety at the heart of every action. A dynamic person, a federator and a power of proposal, you support teams in daily challenges and actively contribute to continuous service improvement.

  • Direct, manage and animate the corps of food and objects inspectors and staff of the cantonal office of weights and measures (BCPM)
  • Coordinate and ensure the technical and scientific activities for the protection of consumers of the inspection of foodstuffs and usual objects throughout the cantonal territory
  • Anticipate and organize the continuing training of personnel of the bodies of food inspectors and common objects and staff of the cantonal office of weights and measures (BCPM)
  • Represent the Inspectorate of Food and Common Objects and ensure external relations and information For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking HERE or contact Lucie Marie Catiau, Head of Office and Cantonal chemist by email at lucie.catiau@vd.ch or by telephone at 021 316 41.80.
  • Master's degree in food science or in a field related to food security or equivalent
  • Federal Diploma in Official Food Control (DCAL) and Federal Diploma in Official Food Control (DDCAL) - possibility of completing these trainings during the first two years of employment
  • Field experience and in-depth knowledge in the area of food security for at least 5 years
  • Recognized management training and/or experience
  • Public service responsibilities and ethics
  • Global vision and perspective
  • Organization and time management
  • Conflict management
  • Leadership
  • Driving licence and provision of private vehicle for business travel
  • Conduct spot inspections during the night, early morning, Saturday and Sunday

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
