Full description

In order to respond to the increase in the number of mandates entrusted to the service and within the framework of the new organization of the Administrative and Financial Support (SAF), we are looking for a person to drive a unit of benefits related to the insurance of persons under curate. In this capacity as Head of Insurance Unit, within the Administrative and Financial Support of the Service (SAF), you drive about ten employees and actively support professional guardians in their adult or child protection mandates. By choosing this mission that carries meaning and responsibility, you contribute to a consistent and diligent application of benefits that people under curate are entitled to. You thus participate in the prospect of a dignified and autonomous life for the most vulnerable.

  • Ensuring the operational conduct of Unit 2 of the benefit sector Insurance
  • Provide guidance, support and support to unit staff
  • Organize, coordinate and supervise the activities related to the management of insurance of persons under the care of the wards.
  • Guarantee health insurance coverage and treatment of complex social insurance situations.
  • Monitor the process of payment and reimbursement of medical and accident invoices.
  • Provide expertise and advice to professional curators and managers.
  • Develop and maintain relationships with internal and external partners. For further information, please consult the Specifications or contact Cédric Monbaron, Head of the Insurance Services Sector at 021 316 67 30.
  • SWC trade employee or equivalent.
  • Federal patent of generalist in social insurance.
  • Training and 3 years experience in team driving.
  • 6-year professional experience in health insurance or social insurance.
  • Advanced knowledge of health and social insurance.
  • Analytical capacity, sense of responsibility and ability to collaborate with multiple partners.
  • Administrative rigor, organizational sense, timeliness and procedures.
  • Proactivity and approach solution. Disclosure of any criminal history and an excerpt from the Prosecution Office is required for this function. Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
