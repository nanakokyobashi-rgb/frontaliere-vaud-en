Location: Vevey, Switzerland, with regular travel to Orbe and other Nestlé R&D sites Work model: Full-time, 100% Contract type / duration: Permanent Closing Date for Applications: 15th September 2026

Be a force for good and grow through experiences that push you further

As a Patent Counsel in Digital & AI, your mission is to enable and protect Nestlé’s digital, AI-driven and electronics-centric innovations through pragmatic legal services and business-driven IP protection. You will act as the go-to Legal R&D partner for Digital & AI topics across the Nestlé R&D ecosystem, collaborating closely with R&D teams, Patent Counsels, R&D Counsels and IP Analysts. This is your opportunity to shape and grow the IP strategy for one of Nestlé’s most strategic innovation frontiers.

How you’ll make an impact as a Patent Counsel – Digital & AI

  • Develop and deliver the IP strategy for digital, AI and electronics-driven innovations, in close partnership with R&D teams, the patent team, R&D Counsels and IP Analysts.
  • Lead business-driven patent filing and prosecution, Freedom-to-Operate assessments, oppositions of third-party patents and defense against third-party oppositions.
  • Manage your patent portfolio with strong business alignment and rigorous cost management.
  • Advise on IP matters in R&D collaborations, including Digital & AI partnerships, licensing and joint developments.
  • Support Coffee & Systems and other categories on connected systems, digital platforms and AI-based innovations, in collaboration with category Patent Counsels.
  • Partner with Nestlé Research, the Nestlé Deep Tech Center, NPTC – Nestlé Professional and other R&D centers to protect digital and AI innovations across categories.
  • Promote passion for IP through awareness sessions, training and communication, while contributing to the awareness and enforcement of Nestlé patents in R&D and in the markets.

What will make you successful

  • Fully qualified patent attorney, European Patent Attorney (EQE) and/or national qualification in a major jurisdiction such as the USA, with a background in Electronics, Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, AI or a related engineering discipline.
  • Proven experience in digital, AI and/or electronics-related IP projects, ideally including software-implemented inventions, AI/ML, connected devices and computer-implemented business methods.
  • More than 3 years of post-qualification experience with patent drafting, prosecution and Freedom-to-Operate assessments in the digital, electronics or AI space.
  • Experience developing business-driven IP strategies across multiple jurisdictions, with a solid understanding of software and AI patenting specificities such as EPO, USPTO and CNIPA.
  • Service-oriented, pragmatic, rigorous, hands-on and collaborative, with the ability to engage confidently with R&D partners, category teams and stakeholders at different levels of seniority.
  • Fluent in English
  • French is a plus.
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning.
