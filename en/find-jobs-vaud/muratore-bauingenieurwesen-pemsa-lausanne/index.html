Your assignment

  • Perform several works of civil engineering and masonry on site;
  • Participating in the construction of formworks, armor and concrete castings;
  • Build and manufacture concrete and reinforced concrete works;
  • Work on foundations, walls, floors, ropes and civil engineering works;
  • Perform traditional masonry and construction works;
  • Participate in the preparation, assembly and disassembly of the formworks;
  • Pose and arrange the armor according to the projects and directions of the yard;
  • Run the jet, vibration and finish of concrete;
  • Read and interpret technical projects and documents;
  • Perform finishing work and ensure the quality of the realized works;
  • Use the tools and equipment necessary for work properly;
  • Respect safety standards and construction procedures;
  • Working in collaboration with the team leader and the various operators present at the construction site;
  • Keep your workplace and equipment clean and in good condition.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Experience gained as a mason in civil engineering;
  • Experience in the construction of concrete, reinforced concrete, formwork and armour;
  • Good knowledge of masonry and civil engineering techniques;
  • Ability to read and understand construction projects and cashout plans;
  • Mastery of tools and equipment commonly used in civil engineering yards;
  • A formation or a federal certificate of capacity (AFC) as a bricklayer is an advantage;
  • Good physical form and attitude to outdoor work;
  • Autonomous, rigorous, precise and versatile profile;
  • Ability to work effectively on team;
  • Strong respect for safety standards;
  • Sericity, reliability and punctuality;
  • Driving licence, advantage;
  • Immediate availability.
