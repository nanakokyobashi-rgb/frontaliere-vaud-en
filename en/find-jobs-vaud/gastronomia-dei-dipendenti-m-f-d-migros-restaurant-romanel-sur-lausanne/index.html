Employé Gastronomie (H/F/D) — Migros Restaurant

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Driven by service and customer satisfaction, you offer each customer a quick, warm and personalized experience. As an employee in gastronomy, you welcome, advise and cash, watch out for the presentation of buffets and showcases, support the preparation of the dishes, guarantee the cleanliness of the places and carry out the receipts rigorously.

Luogo di Lavoro: Migros Restaurant, Romanel Restaurant, En Fézelin, 1032 Romanel-sur-Lausanne

Important remarks

All applications are welcome, but we can only consider online applications. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Welcome, advise and serve clients in a warm and fast way to ensure a quality experience and desire to come back.
  • Prepare, present and follow buffets and showcases (mounting, restocking, animations, display of products and prices, filling of machines), as well as make/prepare sandwiches, hot dishes and desserts according to recipes.
  • Receive deliveries, monitor product quality and quantity, apply hygiene guidelines (HACCP), maintain equipment, showcases, machinery and premises.
  • Ensure the installation, cleanliness and order of the room throughout the day, including handling of goods and ad hoc assistance to colleagues, including dishes (dive)
  • Perform cash transactions (cash, currency preparation, cash count) with rigour and reliability.

Requirements

  • Professional experience in customer service: An asset
  • You have an experience in gastronomy, ideally in fast food or self-service.
  • Customer-oriented and selling, you are smiling, sociable, dynamic and present well
  • You know how to advise, inform, and retain customers, even in times of heavy influx, while keeping your calm.
  • Organised, fast and versatile, you work in a clean and orderly manner and strictly apply hygiene, safety and internal directives.
  • You master French (read and written) and are comfortable with basic computing as well as with money manipulation.
  • No initial/continuous training required
  • French (Very good knowledge)
