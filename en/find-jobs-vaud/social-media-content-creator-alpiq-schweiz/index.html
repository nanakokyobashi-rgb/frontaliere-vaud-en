Full description

As a social media content creator (m/f/d), you actively shape Alpiq’s digital presence. Your mission is to identify strong stories, bring them to life visually across all channels, and create content that makes a real impact, not just output. You combine creative storytelling with efficient implementation and a clear understanding of target groups, platform logics and performance.

Your main responsibility

  • You design and produce digital content with a focus on social media for all digital channels, such as videos, animations and new formats
  • You are responsible for productions from A to Z – from ideation to shooting and editing to publication
  • You are superuser: in for our digital channels (social media, website, intranet)
  • You bring your own story ideas and implement them independently, creatively and in line with the target group
  • You observe social media and video trends and translate them into new formats and approaches
  • You analyze the performance of your content and derive optimizations from it
  • You accompany and supervise a:n Mediamatics learner:n and actively pass on your knowledge
  • You take on an active role in the Alpiq Corporate Newsroom
  • Together with a cross-functional team, you will continue to develop our digital presence
  • You bring “a different kind of energy”

Your impact With your work you make the brand promise of Alpiq tangible. You ensure high-quality, consistent and target-group-oriented content on all relevant channels. Through data-based optimization, a strong sense of trends and creative implementation, you make a significant contribution to the further development of digital communication and content strategy – and thus strengthen the positioning of Alpiq.
