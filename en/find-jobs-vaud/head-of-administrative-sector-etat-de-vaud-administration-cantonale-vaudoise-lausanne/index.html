A superior judicial authority in the Canton of Vaud, the cantonal court assumes the dual role of judicial authority and of direction of the Vaud judicial order. It consists of 45 cantonal judges and alternate judges elected for the term of the legislature, as well as a registry for its various courts. The cantonal court, which employs nearly 200 magistrates and collaborators, is seeking a person responsible for the administrative sector attached to the management of the Registry of the cantonal court.

  • To conduct and supervise the Head of Chancery of the Registry of the Cantonal Court.
  • To assist the management of the Office, in particular the First Registrar, the First Deputy and the Executive Registrar in the day-to-day management of their administrative tasks, and to participate actively in the management and development of the administration of the Office.
  • To assume responsibility for and coordinate the activities of the executive secretariat.
  • Supervise the administrative management of staff and ensure the recruitment process of administrative staff.
