Full description

Thanks to its 800 collaborators, the Broye Intercantonal Hospital (HIB) provides acute care and rehabilitation. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the broyard population. To meet the many challenges ahead, HIB is looking for:

Trainee Administration Pre-HES within the 100% HR Service

Your Mission

  • Establishment and verification of the employee's administrative file prior to salary establishment.
  • Drafting of employment contracts and associated contractual annexes.
  • Managing the process for signing contractual and administrative documents.
  • Data entry and administrative management of new employees in the Adequasys system.
  • Updating, tracking and monitoring the HR tracking table.
  • Managing e-mails and phone calls.

Your assets

  • High interest in administrative management and human resources
  • Attendance in oral and written communication
  • Organizational sense, rigour and discretion
  • Good computer literacy

Entry into office: 01.07.2027

Duration: 1 year.
