Full description

Thanks to its 800 employees, the Intercantonal Hospital of Broye (HIB) provides an acute care and rehabilitation mission. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative health institution striving for excellence for the benefit of the Broyard population. To meet the many challenges that await it, HIB is looking for a: Pre-HES administration intern within the HR department 100% Your mission Assist the HR team in the administrative management and monitoring of employee files by carrying out various tasks of classification, document processing, reception and support for current administrative processes. Your main responsibilities:

  • Filing and archiving of employee files
  • Updating HR databases
  • Digitization and management of administrative documents
  • Writing standard letters or e-mails
  • Receiving and processing HR e-mails
  • Managing calls and redirecting to the relevant contacts
  • Reception of employees for their administrative procedures
  • Administrative management of employee entry and exit Specific participation:
  • Contribute to an IT project linked to time management within the HR department (tests, monitoring, implementation of new processes, documentation) Your strengths
  • Strong interest in administrative management and human resources
  • Ease in oral and written communication
  • Sense of organization, rigor and discretion
  • Good IT knowledge and attraction to digital tools
  • Motivation to participate in a transversal IT project Starting position: immediately or to be agreed. Duration of the internship: 1 year. We thank you for sending us your complete application file by applying online.
