Main responsibilities

  • Key responsibilities
  • Build and implement sustainable market access and pricing strategies for Roche’s products and portfolio from pre-launch through life-cycle management.
  • Identify the primary focus of high medical value products to guide future new product planning efforts.
  • Drive ecosystem and policy-shaping activities to support and expand broader patient access opportunities.
  • Lead successful NHIA reimbursement submissions and negotiations and oversee the end-to-end process.
  • Formulate and execute pharmacoeconomics and HTA strategies.
  • Ensure robust, payer-relevant evidence is generated (via clinical trials, health economic modeling, real-world evidence, and meta-analyses) to comprehensively demonstrate product value.
  • Identify patient access barriers or opportunities and develop best-in-class access solutions for the self-pay market and areas beyond standard NHIA coverage.
  • Collaborate with global teams, commercial teams, and cross-functional groups to align and execute overall access strategies.
  • Develop and execute engagement plans with KOLs, patient advocacy groups, and diverse external stakeholders.
