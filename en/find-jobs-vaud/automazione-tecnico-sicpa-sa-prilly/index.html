Full description

To strengthen our Facilities & Services department at SICPA, we are looking for a:

Maintenance Technician

General mission

The Maintenance Technician provides autonomous and proactive operation, maintenance, troubleshooting and monitoring of the technical infrastructure of the Florissant site, including facilities serving laboratories, the Data Center and the general infrastructure of the site. It is responsible for technical installations from their production to their distribution or connection point to user equipment, in accordance with the safety rules, standards and requirements in force.

Technical areas concerned

Electricity

  • Electrical production and distribution of the site.
  • Maintenance and troubleshooting of electrical installations to the connection point.
  • Intervention according to the required competencies and authorizations.

Sanitary and heating

  • Production and distribution of heat and water.
  • Maintenance of networks, pumps, valves and associated facilities.
  • Diagnosis and troubleshooting of installations.

Cold production and distribution

  • Cold production and associated facilities.
  • Distribution networks and circuits.
  • Pumps, valves and peripheral equipment.
  • Monitoring of operating and troubleshooting parameters.

Breakdown

  • Operation and maintenance of ventilation and air extraction facilities.
  • Breakdown of premises, including laboratories and technical areas.
  • Monitoring of flow rates, temperatures, pressures and operating parameters.

Compressed air

  • Production and distribution of compressed air.
  • Maintenance of compressors and distribution networks.
  • Monitoring of installations and searching for anomalies or leaks.

Automation and supervision

  • Operation and monitoring of systems for automation and supervision of technical infrastructure.
  • Monitoring of alarms and installation parameters.
  • Diagnosis of defects and interventions within the limits of competence and authorisations.

Autonomy and organization

The Maintenance Technician works independently and organizes his daily activity according to the priorities, emergencies and needs of the site.

It monitors interventions, identifies anomalies and offers suitable solutions. It works with external companies or specialists when the necessary skills exceed its scope of action.

Picket service

Participation in the picket service about once a month, when the team is fully staffed, to ensure the continuity of the technical infrastructure of the three sites: Florissant, Campus and Bussigny.

Primary responsibility

The Maintenance Technician contributes directly to the availability, reliability, safety and sustainability of the technical infrastructure, in its area of competence and to the distribution or connection points.

PROFILE

Training and experience

  • Technical training in maintenance, electricity, CVC or equivalent training.
  • Experience in maintenance of infrastructure and technical installations.
  • Good knowledge in several technical fields: electricity, ventilation, heating, sanitary, cold, compressed air and automation.
  • Holder of an authorization of electrician according to Art. 13 ITTO. Failing this, holder of a minimum authorisation according to Art. 15 ITTO or meeting the conditions for obtaining it.
  • Experience in a laboratory, industrial or Data Center environment is an advantage.

Technical expertise

  • Ability to diagnose and resolve faults independently.
  • Good understanding of technical installations and their interactions.
  • Ability to intervene on complex installations and to analyse the causes of malfunction.
  • Knowledge of automation and supervisory systems.
  • Ability to perform preventive, corrective and curative interventions.
  • Ability to work with external companies and specialists when necessary.

Personal suitability

  • Autonomy and sense of responsibility.
  • Ability to organize and manage priorities.
  • Spirit of analysis and orientation solutions.
  • Practical spirit and ability to take initiatives.
  • Rigor and reliability.
  • Good ability to work alone as a team.
  • Good communication with users, colleagues, suppliers and external companies.
  • High sensitivity to safety, quality and continuity of service.
  • Availability to participate in picket service.
