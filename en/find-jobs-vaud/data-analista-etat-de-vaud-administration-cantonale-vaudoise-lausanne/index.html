Full description

As a Data Analyst within the Vaudese Cantonal Police (VCP) IT Sector, you support and coordinate migration, exchange and integration of data with police databases, Odyssey and partner systems (study credit to continue the Odyssey program). In addition, you coordinate the PCV geomatics solutions and services and provide specialized support to users. By joining the CPV, you contribute to a public service committed to serving its population, its security and the maintenance of social cohesion.

  • Coordinate with the Digital and Information Systems Branch (DNSB) external providers and user entities of geomatics applications, incidents, developments and needs.
  • Coordinate police data initiatives and support their development in the information system.
  • Collect and analyse operational requirements and contribute to their translation into functional and technical requirements. Prepare business test plans.
  • Coordinate with the Governance of Operational Data (GDO), with the program for renewal of the Vaud police information system (Odyssey), with the DGNSI and with the trades and entities concerned the initiatives relating to the creation, structuring, use and evolution of data.
  • Contribute to the definition and application of structures, formats, repositories and metadata necessary for consistent use of data.
  • Participate in the migration and recovery of data within the framework of Odyssey and developments in the police information system.
  • Coordinate with trades, NSB and suppliers the preparation, testing and validation of data migrations. Identify anomalies, inconsistencies or missing data and coordinate their processing with the actors concerned.
  • Contribute to the definition of data exchange between partner applications and systems. Participate in the integration of exchange standards, including eCH-0051, and ensure their relevance to the needs and data models of the CPV.
  • Coordinate the needs for forms and data collection mechanisms with trades, ODM, Odyssey and NSIB.
  • Advise project managers and trades on data structuring, integration, quality and security.
  • To act as an internal consultant and expert on PCV activities (projects, studies, etc.) with a significant data-related component. Analyze needs and identify relevant data, sources, flows, interfaces, repositories and dependencies. For further information, please consult the Terms of Reference by clicking here or contact Mr CHERPILLOD, Chief IT Officer, at 079 934 10 16 or by e-mail: guillaume.cherpillod@vd.ch . Further information on the activities of the Cantonal Police can also be found at www.police.ch
  • Bachelor of Computer Science, Management Computer Science, Information Systems or Training deemed equivalent (required).
  • Training or certifications in the fields of data management, databases, data integration or information system (subject matter).
  • Training or knowledge in geomatics (souhaité).
  • Experience in managing, evolving or coordinating computer solutions involving data (5 years).
  • Experience in data integration, migration, interfacing or transformation projects (5 years).
  • Knowledge of data models, databases, exchange formats and integration mechanisms (required).
  • Knowledge of quality issues, repositories, metadata and data migration.
  • Knowledge of exchange standards and interfacing technologies (API, XML/KSON, eCH standards, etc.)
  • Ability to analyze and document data sources, flows, structures and rules.
  • It may be necessary to take part in special operations, migration, commissioning or operations outside the standard timetable.
  • It may be necessary to use its private vehicle for service purposes.
  • Housing radius: maximum 45 minutes from the workplace. Our terms and conditions of employment require that you be exempt from prosecution or default.
