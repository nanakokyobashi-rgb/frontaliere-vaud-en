What awaits you.

As a locomotive pilot, you are doing great things: by working at SBB Voyageurs, you carry around 482 million travellers each year. Once the second 15-month training has been completed, you travel the most beautiful railway lines in Switzerland. You bring our travelers to destination with punctuality, thus contributing to a positive travel experience with the SBB. Your professional daily also offers you many opportunities for evolution as well as attractive conditions for all life situations. At home, night and weekend work is entitled to additional remuneration (e.g. 16 francs per hour for Sunday work). Time increases of between 10 and 30% are also granted for night work. This means that you have more free time than the staff who do not work at night or on weekends. In addition, at the end of the locomotive pilot training, you will be required to work in the Payerne or Vallorbe or Lausanne depots. What you can move.

  • As a locomotive pilot, you not only take the lead on the train, but also take responsibility for safety and punctuality on a busy rail network.
  • During the journey, great attention must be paid to react quickly and correctly to signals, speed instructions and unpredictable events.
  • You are self-employed while at the same time maintaining contact with operating centres and management positions.
  • In addition to driving and manoeuvring trains, your tasks also include checking the technical condition before and after taking over the vehicle.

Get more information about this business profile by attending the next information session. What you bring.

  • You have completed a three-year apprenticeship or matured. Your motivation matters more to us than the branch you come from.
  • You approach challenges calmly, you make thoughtful decisions even in stressful situations and you like to work by team.
  • You can communicate in French (level C2) and have knowledge in a second national language in German (level A1).
  • Your view of the colours is perfect (no prot anomaly or deutera) and there is no entry in your criminal record or in the prosecution registry.
  • During the intensive and demanding training period, you must demonstrate a high level of personal responsibility and motivation, as well as your investment in different learning environments, from the face-to-face phase to digital self-learning.

Your salary and benefits.

Classification during the second training is based on function and age. You will find more information on the concrete salary for this role under Professional conversion.
