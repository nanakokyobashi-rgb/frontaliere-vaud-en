Full description

What awaits you

As a Legal Officer at Protekta Legal Protection Insurance SA, a company of the Mobilière Group, you are part of our legal service Romandie, a team of lawyers responsible for dealing with and settling claims in many areas of law.

What you can achieve with us

Your main task is to independently resolve legal protection cases within a dynamic team.

With your social skills and a strong sense of service, you advise the insured (in both oral and written terms), give them legal information and defend their interests against the opposing parties and legal representatives.

You maintain contact with lawyers outside our company responsible for defending the interests of our insured persons in court, as well as with our clients in order to ensure optimal processing of cases.

What you bring

  • Master in Swiss law, preferably with a lawyer's patent
  • Strong legal experience in the areas of contract law, insurance, civil liability and/or road traffic
  • A communicative, empathic and pragmatic personality
  • Excellent mastery of French, mastery of German and/or Italian is an asset

Working with us

We offer you modern conditions of engagement and a pleasant working environment to choose from between Nyon and Bern.

The contract is of fixed duration at a rate of occupancy which can be negotiated between 80% and 100%, from 1 December 2026 to 30 June 2027. You will learn more about our commitment as an employer below.
