Full description

In a context of development in Switzerland, your missions will be as follows: As Store Director, you are fully responsible for your store, your team and its performance. Inspiring leader and talent developer

You build your team around a common vision and create a positive dynamic on the ground. You support employees in their development, encourage autonomy and accountability, and demonstrate managerial courage when necessary. 🛍️ Commerçant(e) passionné(e)

You place the customer at the heart of the priorities and make a shopping experience simple, enjoyable and efficient. You know your local environment, identify business opportunities and take your team into a true customer culture. Performance driver

You analyze and pilot your store's economic indicators, identify growth levers and put in place the necessary action plans. You also guarantee the proper management of the store as well as compliance with safety rules and standards.

For this position, we are looking first and foremost for an experienced store manager who can quickly be operational and autonomous in his/her role.

  • You have a significant and successful experience in store management and animation, allowing you to drive performance, animate teams and master all the operational issues of a point of sale.
  • You know how to drive a team and a business activity with autonomy.
  • You're comfortable with performance indicators and can turn them into concrete action plans.
  • You are a field leader, close to your team, able to federate but also to make decisions.
  • You show pragmatism, agility and managerial courage.
  • You want to invest fully in a recent store and raise a real human, commercial and managerial challenge.
  • A good knowledge of the Swiss market is a real asset.

You're looking for a job where you can really impact, take over a store and write the rest of his story with your team? The post shall be filled as soon as possible. If you are available quickly and this challenge speaks to you, we look forward to meeting you!
