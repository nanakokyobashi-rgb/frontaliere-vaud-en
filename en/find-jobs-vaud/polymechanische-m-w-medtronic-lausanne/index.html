Careers that change live start here. Medtronic is a global leader in healthcare technology with a Mission to alleviate bread, restore health, and extend life. Our 95,000 employees work across more than 150 countries to put patients first — developing innovative medical technologies that improve the lives of 72+ million patients each year. Your unique talents will help shape the future of healthcare while building a career grounded in purpose, growth, and impact. A Day in the Life Relieving pain, restoring health and prolonging life is guided by this mission that Medtronic continues to benefit millions of patients from its innovative therapies. Today, you are given the opportunity to integrate the largest medical device company in the world and participate in this daily challenge. By joining our teams, you will be at the heart of our values!

Our Tolochenaz site (400 people) is a global centre for development and production in the field of implantable medical devices. We assemble 500,000 pacemakers per year while ensuring the industrialization of new products.

As part of the expansion of activities to support European and Asian platform plants, we are looking for a prototyping mechanic.

The precision mechanic, prototypist, is responsible for the programming, setting of the machine, machining and control of unique or small series parts for tools for the manufacture of medical devices. He is a member of a team of designers and mechanics with a state-of-the-art workshop.

This position is carried over to the engineering manager of the Mechanical Service team within the development department. Schedule : flexible, to be defined Responsibilities may include the following activities and other activities may be assigned

  • FAO programming 3/5 axes
  • Machine adjustment and machining of mechanical parts of unit or very small precision on conventional machine s and CNC 3 and 5 axis machining centre.
  • Control of dimensions according to planes and tolerances
  • Assembly, adjustment and adjustment of assemblies
  • Follow requests for ongoing mechanical work
  • Meet established quality standards
  • Contribute to and participate in safe working rules, report any risks and EHS events (Environment, Health and Safety). Understanding and implementing all health and safety policies, procedures and regulations
  • Be proactive to meet client needs
  • Participate in the life of the workshop, order, maintenance, repair
  • Create and track external commands

Knowledge and experience required

  • CFC Polymechanic or equivalent specialized in machining
  • Several years of experience in an equivalent position
  • CNC programming - Knowledge Gibbs CAM a plus
  • Knowledge of milling shooting – Fehlmann , Bumotec a plus
  • Establish process and operational plans
  • Dimensional measurement , with basic tool and measuring column - self-control
  • Mastery of the iso language on order H eidenhain , Fanuc a plus
  • Microsoft Office Pack: Excel, Word, Powerpoint

Wished

  • Dimensional inspection on three-dimensional measuring machine
  • Concept of GMAO , computer-assisted maintenance management
  • ISO Metric Standard, US ASME Standard , EC Regulation
  • Electrical and pneumatic (assembly)
  • Easy communication
  • Quality and result oriented
  • Active, autonomous and collaborative
  • Spirit of continuous improvement and cost reduction
  • Basic English (A2) level is a plus
