Full description

More than a dozen EHC medical centres run along the coast. The Simplon Medical Center offers specialized medical and family consultations next to Renens Station, in modern and bright spaces.

We are looking for one for the Simplon Medical Center

Medical Assistant (M/F) at 60%

CDI, starting on 01/11/2026, or to be agreed

WE OFFER YOU

  • 5 weeks of annual vacation, 6 weeks from the age of 50. Possibility of redeeming an additional week of vacation, as well as 10 holidays offered.
  • Several proposals providing flexibility (unpaid leave, part-time leave, paid exchange time, etc.).
  • Remuneration based on the valuation of your previous years of professional experience, as well as seniority bonuses within the EHC.
  • Opportunities for internal change, according to your desire for development.
  • Many accessible in-house or certifying training.
  • Fixed hours.

YOU MAY

  • A medical assistant CFC.
  • Prior experience in a medical practice and infusion skills.
  • A natural lover and open and have at heart to offer a welcome and care for the patient based on benevolence and respect.
  • Flexibility and versatility to provide physicians with quality support in the execution of routine and exceptional activities.
  • Rigor and good management of priorities allowing you to cope with an ever-changing activity.
  • An excellent team spirit and the desire to evolve in an environment with collegiality at heart.

YOUR OBJECTIVES

  • Provide care and guidance for patients in general medicine, ORL and urology.
  • Ensure a patient experience of excellence on a daily basis.
  • Manage hotline and message take.
  • Manage administrative tasks related to a medical centre.
  • Provide care, provide clinical supervision of patients and assist physicians in specific interventional and non-interventional care.
  • Perform common diagnostic services: laboratory, ECG, Remler, Apnealink, infusion, etc.
  • Accomplishments and small urology interventions.

WE ARE

  • An institution consisting of acute care and rehabilitation institutions, a private clinic, EMS, medical centres and permanences located in the region of La Côte and the first clinical hypnosis school dedicated to the care community in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A network of care allowing many opportunities for internal change according to the aspirations and development projects of employees.
  • More than 2,000 employees taking care of patients and residents of the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment conducive to the realization of projects.
  • A training company engaged in succession training, with about 80 apprentices in 16 different trades, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.
