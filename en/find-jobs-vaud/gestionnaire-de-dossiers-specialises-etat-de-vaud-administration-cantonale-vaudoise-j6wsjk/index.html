Specialist File Manager — État de Vaud (Administration cantonale vaudoise)

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

The Vaudois Office for Health Insurance (OVAM) attached to the Directorate of Social Assistance and Insurance (DIRAAS) within the Directorate-General for Social Cohesion (DGCS) is responsible for granting and paying subsidies for compulsory health insurance premiums. As a specialist file manager you calculate and order the payment of the subsidies to the various health insurers in the canton.

  • Establish the calculation of the subsidy for both first applications and requests for review;
  • To investigate and process the various applications for health insurance subsidies;
  • Ensure that the subsidy decision is communicated to the beneficiary and its insurer;
  • Ensure the processing of claims filed as a result of decisions rendered;
  • Manage contacts with users and different partners. For further information, please consult the terms of reference by clicking here or contact Ms Myriam Corfù, Head of Group Support at OVAM, at the email address: dgcs.recruitment@vd.ch

The appointment will be in salary class 6 or 8 depending on the candidate's experience.

  • Holder of a trade employee CFC or equivalent.
  • Generalist certificate in social insurance required. In the absence of the diploma, it must be obtained after the appointment.
  • Federal social insurance patent, an asset.
  • Work experience of at least 5 years in a similar activity in a field providing financial benefits.
  • In-depth knowledge of the various social plans, laws, regulations and procedures that underpin the allocation of health insurance subsidies.
  • Interest in training apprentices employed in commerce, a strong asset.
  • Excellent knowledge of the Vaud social system.
  • Mastery of numerical reasoning and developed analytical and synthesis spirit.
  • Aisance with computer tools.
  • Adaptability and flexibility.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
