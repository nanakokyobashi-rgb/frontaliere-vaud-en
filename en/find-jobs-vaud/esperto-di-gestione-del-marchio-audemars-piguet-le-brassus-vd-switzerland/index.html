Expert Brand Management — Audemars Piguet

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Under the responsibility of Brand Director and as part of a job creation, you will put your expertise at the service of Audemars Piguet by contributing to the development and evolution of strategies aimed at enhancing the desire, visibility and long-term value of the company. As a guarantor of this strategic vision, you will help shape and spread the identity of Audemars Piguet through coherent and meaningful experiences, in close collaboration with cross-cutting teams and international markets.

In this context, your main responsibilities will be

  • Contribute to the development and deployment of strategies to enhance Audemars Piguet's long-term desirability, visibility and value, consistent with its vision and strategic objectives
  • Translate strategic directions into integrated marketing plans, briefs and marketing strategies.
  • Ensure brand consistency through different functions, initiatives and customer contact points.
  • Work closely with Brand Operations, Product, Retail, Finance, Merchandising and Regional Marketing teams to ensure integrated implementation of strategies.
  • Support markets in the deployment of global plans while promoting their local relevance.
  • Collect and integrate market needs to enrich marketing plans and activations.
  • Representing the customer's voice through analysis of consumer insights, market trends and the competitive environment.
  • Power creative and narrative reflections to build impacting branding campaigns and experiences.
  • Define and monitor key performance indicators to measure the effectiveness of initiatives and identify opportunities for optimization.
  • University training in Marketing, Management, Business Administration or equivalent.
  • Experience from 5 to 8 years in Brand Management or Strategic Marketing.
  • Significant experience in the world of luxury, watchmaking, fashion or a premium environment.
  • Strong experience in developing and deploying brand strategies and integrated marketing plans.
  • Good understanding of the dynamics of product marketing, collection management and launching of international initiatives.
  • Mastery of market analysis methods, consumer insights and marketing performance indicators.
  • Excellent skills in managing complex projects involving multiple stakeholders.
  • Demonstrated ability to influence, unite and build consensus in a matrix and international environment.
  • Excellent analytical capabilities combined with strong creative sensitivity and strategic vision.
  • Common French and English.
