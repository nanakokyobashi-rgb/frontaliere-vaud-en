Full description

Your mission Team coordination, graphical and technical monitoring Information (60%)

  • Receive needs from the news editor and challenge requests.
  • Distribute the workload on a daily basis and ensure the operational monitoring of graphic creations (TV, digital).
  • Ensure that RTS visual identity is respected on all platforms and the event graphic charters.
  • Prepare and make available the graphic elements necessary for events to facilitate exchanges.
  • Collaborate with the graphical integration team for the creation and evolution of templates.
  • Conduct technical and organizational monitoring to anticipate needs and optimize processes.
  • Support the transformation of productions into digital in collaboration with the artistic director.
  • Find autonomous solutions to unforeseen events. Macro planning (10%)
  • Establish medium-term planning (quarterly), with a view to anticipating needs and taking into account major upcoming events from a service matrix.
  • Manage daily production planning by ensuring workload.
  • Ensure the proper functioning of weekly planning in close collaboration with the planner.
  • Orient the planner if necessary to replace an absent graphic designer. Your profile
  • Minimum 5 years experience in graphic production and/or project management, agency or media.
  • In-depth knowledge of the audiovisual professions, post-production processes and production methods of a TV show.
  • Technical knowledge related to studio, set and management
  • Sense of priorities and good stress resistance.
  • Ability to coordinate several projects simultaneously with rigour.
  • Team spirit affirmed and great relational ease.
  • Adaptability and openness to change.
  • Autonomy, proactivity
  • Mastery of graphic tools (adobe, cinema4d, blender, figma...)
  • Mastering graphic production tools (pixotop, unreal, watchout) an asset. What we offer you
  • The possibility of integrating a creative, dynamic and motivated team into an environment where innovation and collaboration are at the heart of projects.
  • The chance to participate in ambitious projects that contribute to the transformation of public service audiovisual media.
  • A stimulating work environment where you can enrich your skills. About the recruitment process Human relations The RTS puts in the competition three positions of Graphic workshop manager for the productions of the Information RTS within its department Artistic Directorate. Your mission is to ensure the operational follow-up with the writing of the Information RTS, while organizing the life of the graphic workshop responsible for producing visual elements for TV and digital content. You are actively contributing to strengthening collaboration between different stakeholders and ensuring the proper application of the visual identity of the RTS on all its platforms.
