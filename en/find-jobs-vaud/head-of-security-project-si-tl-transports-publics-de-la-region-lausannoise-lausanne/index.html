Full description

As a reference player in public transport, they transport each customer by making the heart of the Lausanne region beat by a commitment to simple, innovative and sustainable mobility.

To strengthen our teams, and also contribute to this experience, join us.

Become our future

Head of security project SI

Your Mission

Attached to the SI Security Manager, within the Information Systems entity, you contribute to the development and implementation of the SI Safety Roadmap.

In a context of digital transformation and enhanced cybersecurity, you are piloting structuring projects to protect IT/OT environments, manage risks, and strengthen compliance, resilience and business continuity.

You frame the projects entrusted, define objectives, deliverables, milestones and follow-up indicators, and then coordinate IS teams, trades, suppliers and external partners to ensure their advancement. As such, you bring your security expertise to projects, advise stakeholders on data protection, continuity and business resumption requirements, and accompany the associated changes.

You also contribute to the evolution of IS standards, standards and security processes and ensure active monitoring of threats, vulnerabilities, technologies and regulatory requirements in order to strengthen long-term risk control, resilience and security posture of the company.

Your profile

  • You have a higher education in computer science, information systems, networks, cybersecurity or an equivalent field.
  • You justify at least 5 years of professional experience in the security of information systems, with significant experience in the conduct of SI projects, cybersecurity, infrastructure, networks or risk management
  • You have mastered project management, planning, risk tracking, budget, reporting and documentation practices
  • You have good knowledge of cybersecurity principles, IS architectures, IT/OT infrastructure, vulnerability management, incidents, identities and access, as well as issues of continuity and resilience
  • You know how to unite transversal teams and dialogue with technical, trade and managerial interlocutors.
  • You know how to advise, assist, alert and popularise IS safety issues among trades, technical teams and pilotage bodies
  • Autonomous, structured and results-oriented, you know how to step back and move decisions forward.
  • Further training in project management, change management or piloting of SI projects, as well as certification in information security – ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27005, CISSP, CISM, Security+ or equivalent – are assets
  • You are fluent in English in oral and written language and have good knowledge of technical English

We offer you

A transversal and visible role, at the heart of the protection of tl information systems, with:

  • Concrete and structuring projects for the resilience of a public transport network;
  • Close collaboration with SI teams, trades and IT/OT specialists;
  • Self-reliance, a real ability to influence and the ability to make lasting progress in security practices.
