Full description

Under the responsibility of the Domain Lead IT Brand, your main mission will be to play a key role in the design, evolution and performance of our digital platforms. As a technical reference, you will guide development teams in the realization of robust, innovative and scalable solutions, while actively contributing to our digital roadmap. At the interface between business teams, technology partners and IT teams, you will participate in the definition of technical standards, the reduction of technical debt, the continuous improvement of our practices and the success of strategic projects that have a direct impact on customer experience.

In this context, your main responsibilities will be

  • Ensure that solutions developed meet business needs and technical standards
  • In-depth analysis of the specifications in order to have a clear understanding of the objectives, needs, and expected deliverables
  • Contribute to the definition of a technical architecture and be qualified as a technical reference for issues related to digital platforms for customers.
  • Collaborate with our developers teams and provide expertise on the development and integration of our digital customer solutions.
  • Work with developers to solve complex issues
  • Make clear and concise presentations of technical solutions
  • Write and distribute detailed release notes in order to synthesize the novelties, corrections and improvements made to each version and ensure that the technical documentation is maintained.
  • Ensure the quality of code and deliverables through regular code reviews and automated testing
  • Advanced computer, engineering or equivalent training
  • Minimum 8 years experience confirmed in the development and role of Technical Lead
  • Controlling Java/J2EE programming languages, JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3
  • Experience with front-end frameworks Vue.js. React, Angular is a plus
  • Good understanding of IC/CD concepts and automated testing
  • Knowledge of cloud architectures (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) is desirable
  • Ability to work in teams and coordinate teams and understand business needs
  • Strong ability to solve complex technical problems
  • You are curious, autonomous, open to change and a force for proposal
  • You are fluent in French and have very good knowledge of English
