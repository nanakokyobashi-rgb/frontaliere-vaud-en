Full description

Description of work EMIL EGGER SA is one of the leading Swiss logistics companies and offers its customers a full range of services in the fields of general freight, heavy transport and warehouse logistics. About 800 employees currently work in 11 locations for renowned customers in industry, crafts and commerce. Our heavy logistics department includes gruting services, special transport, industrial relocations and the rental of lifting platforms. In order to strengthen our freight department, we are looking for an experienced truck driver, who holds the C/E licence, to transport goods throughout Switzerland. Are you a road professional, self-sufficient, reliable and committed to the quality of work? Want to join a dynamic company in which your experience and commitment are valued? Then we would be delighted to welcome you to our team!

Tasks

  • Provide domestic transportation of various goods by means of a trailer truck, in accordance with the deadlines and requirements of our customers.
  • Take charge of loading and unloading operations, ensuring proper handling and securing of goods.
  • Control the distribution and securing of the load in order to ensure safe transport in accordance with the requirements in force.
  • Plan and organize your tours according to instructions and operational constraints.
  • Ensure proper functioning, cleanliness and routine maintenance of your vehicle.
  • Perform normal vehicle checks before, during and after transport.
  • Respect strictly the legal requirements for traffic, driving time and rest, as well as safety instructions.
  • Ensure professional and courteous contact with clients, partners and internal staff.

Your profile

  • Professional experience confirmed in Switzerland in the transport of goods.
  • Driver's licence valid C/E.
  • CAPO up to date.
  • ADR permit (an asset).
  • Good knowledge of the Swiss road network.
  • Independent, organized and responsible person.
  • A developed sense of customer service and teamwork.
  • Flexible, dynamic and able to manage unforeseen events.
  • Good stress resistance and excellent professional awareness.

Assets

  • Various and stimulating activities within a recognized company in the transport and logistics sector.
  • A dynamic and professional working environment.
  • Attractive conditions of engagement and first-rate social benefits.
  • A family business culture based on trust and respect.
  • A salary in line with your experience and responsibilities.
