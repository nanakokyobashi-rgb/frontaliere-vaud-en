Full description

You love contact with customers and you have to heart to offer a warm and neat service in our coffee area. As ambassador of the service, you welcome, advise and serve clients while contributing every day to a pleasant experience.

Luogo di lavoro: Migros Restaurant, Café 75 Romanel, En Félezin, 1032 Romanel-sur-Lausanne

Important remarks

All applications are welcome, but only online applications can be considered. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • Welcome and loyalty to customers through personalized and warm service
  • Prepare and serve hot and cold drinks and snacks (snacks)
  • Ensure an attractive presentation of space. Unload, clean and prepare tables for future customers
  • Cashing purchases and managing the cashier
  • Participate in the reception and storage of goods.

Required

  • Professional experience in customer service: an asset
  • You have a sense of service and make every effort to offer a warm and quality customer experience.
  • You appreciate human contact, advise clients with pleasure and contribute to a welcoming atmosphere.
  • You work rigorously, quickly and efficiently, even when the activity is sustained.
  • You rigorously apply the standards of hygiene, quality and safety specific to the restoration.
  • No initial/continuous training required
  • French (Very good knowledge)
