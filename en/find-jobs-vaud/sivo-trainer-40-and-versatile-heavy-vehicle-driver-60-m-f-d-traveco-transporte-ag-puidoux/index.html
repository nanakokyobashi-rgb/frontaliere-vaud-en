To complement our team in French-speaking Switzerland, we are looking for an SIVO trainer (40%) and multi-purpose driver (60%).

Nathalie Haymoz HR Business Partner +41 58 433 70 44

Exciting tasks

  • You plan, organize and animate SIVO trainings, individually in the vehicle and in small groups (Planning and writing independent reports)
  • You pass on safety rules and legislation, and you show concretely how to drive safely and proactively
  • You observe driving behavior and give a clear, fair and situation-appropriate feedback
  • In addition to driving behaviour, topics such as loading securing, managing customer relations, etc. are also analysed.
  • You analyze recurring disasters, anomalies and risks, and deduct the priority training axes
  • You document coaching sessions and training, and you actively follow the evolution of drivers
  • You work closely with management, planning and services
  • Samsara reports: Thanks to a continuous video analysis, you write monthly reports that you send to the driving staff.
  • Delivery of goods on pallets, bulk and fuel in Western Switzerland with 4 axle vehicles or trailer truck
  • Rotating driver
  • Technical inspection of the vehicle and its cleanliness
  • Compliance with hygiene guidelines.

The profile sought

  • Strong professional experience in road transport, EC category (at least 10 years of driving experience)
  • Exemplary, prudent and proactive behaviour
  • Excellent relational skills and taste for human contacts
  • Attitude respectful, even in difficult situations
  • Willingness to learn and openness to new regulations, technologies and internal standards
  • Good IT knowledge (Microsoft 365) for e-mail management, simple analysis and documentation
  • Ability to work independently, properly organized and rigorously maintained files
  • Mastery of French and German.
  • Driver's licence cat. C/E and valid OACP training
  • Updated CSD/ADR permit, mandatory tank
  • Good knowledge of French-speaking Switzerland
  • Flexibility and endurance, good physical condition
  • Good presentation, friendly and helpful
  • Caregivers in the handling of vehicles and auxiliary means
  • Honest and loyal behaviour
  • Self-governing and responsible.

Benefits to be discovered

  • Initial and continuing training: We invest in the professional development of our employees through numerous in-house training and support for external training.
  • Interesting Holiday Conditions: Depending on our vacation conditions, all staff are entitled to a minimum of 5 weeks of vacation, or 6 weeks from 50 years and 7 weeks from 60 years. It is also possible to buy additional vacation days.
  • Parental leave: Mothers receive 100 per cent of their insured earnings during the 18 weeks of maternity leave. The other parent has 3 weeks of parental leave with full pay.
  • Insurance benefits: We provide our employees with above average social and welfare benefits and offer special conditions on the supplementary insurance of different sickness funds for the whole family.
  • Benefits and reductions: Our employees benefit from discounts on purchases and fuel, REKA money at preferential rates and mobile subscriptions at reduced prices for them and their families.
