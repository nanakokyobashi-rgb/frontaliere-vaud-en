Full description

Want to join a dynamic team and play a key role in key moments in the life of citizens? We are looking for a motivated and committed person to join our team and actively participate in our missions. You will make your skills and sense of service available to citizens. You will accompany them in their efforts and ensure that they have a quality service. You will evolve in a stimulating environment, where collaboration, rigour and human contact occupy an essential place. Join us to meet new challenges and invest you in a useful, rewarding and meaningful mission.

  • Welcome Administers (window/rendez-vous)
  • Issue civil status documents in accordance with the legal basis for data protection and disclosure
  • Ensure registration of events related to civil status
  • Conduct wedding ceremonies and update the Federal Register For more information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Mr André Sá, co-responsible of the Civil Registry Office at +41 21 557 07 28. In order to give you a concrete idea of the activity of civil registrar, we invite you to watch this video.
  • SWC or equivalent
  • Federal civil status officer's patent or undertaking to obtain it
  • Professional experience of at least 5 years in case management with analysis and decision-making
  • Interest in Swiss and international socio-political news
  • Rigor, a spirit of methodological analysis, a spirit of mutual aid and collaboration
  • Vif interest in family law, private international law and civil registration provisions
  • Interest in societal change in civil status
  • Ability to speak in public, sense of courtesy and discretion
  • Basic terms in German and/or English: an asset The conditions of employment require you to be in possession of an CFC and of Swiss nationality or holder of a permit C. You will be required to work on Saturdays for wedding ceremonies at the frequency of 1-2 Saturdays a month. The certificate of civil status officer, which is necessary for the performance of the office, is a minimum of 2 years with a class 8 promotion as soon as it is obtained.
