Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. True to its cooperative nature and values, it encourages the creation of a healthy, inclusive and inspiring work environment, ideal for developing your talent. Join Sales and Distribution as a Senior Business Analyst dedicated to our Internal & External Portal solution. He will be a key player between the Product Owner (which carries the vision of the product) and the Solutions Manager (which guarantees the delivery). The missions that await you:

  • Collect, analyse and formalize business needs in order to translate them into appropriate technical-functional solutions, identifying the necessary impacts, dependencies and evolutions.
  • Contribute to the design, configuration and governance of deployed solutions and features, in close collaboration with Dynamics 365 teams.
  • Optimize business processes and support their evolution by continuously monitoring new technological features and innovations.
  • Pilot end-to-end testing activities, from definition of acceptance criteria to final validation and coordination of resolution of anomalies.
  • Support users in the adoption of changes, in coordination with support teams, training, change management and trade referents.
  • Provide level 2 support, monitor the use and adoption of solutions through dashboards and reports, and contribute to the enrichment of the knowledge base. Your profile:
  • A higher education in IT type HES, Bachelor, Master, EPFL, ...
  • A strong functional technical expertise in web solutions and technology
  • 5 years minimum experience on Atlassian technologies in IT missions/projects, business projects with a strong technological and transformation dimension
  • A developed analytical capacity, a sense of sharp relational, editorial skills
  • An excellent mastery of agile methodologies, ITIL processes
  • A sensitivity to change management
  • A perfect mastery of the French language: oral and written and a mastery of English: minimum B2 level. German is a plus We offer you:
  • A healthy work environment within a mutual company certified Great Place to Work
  • Attractive conditions and competitive social benefits
  • Flexibility in work organisation
  • A stimulating framework for skills development and continuous improvement We look forward to receiving your complete application package digitally (with curriculum vitae, cover letter, copies of work certificates and diplomas).
