Full description

Shape Nestlé’s global approach to leadership coaching, mentoring and team effectiveness.

Location: Barcelona, Spain or Lisbon, Portugal Work model: Hybrid / Full time Contract type / duration: Permanent

Be a force for good and accelerate change that improves lives and the planet

As a Leadership Development & Team Effectiveness Manager at Nestlé, your mission is to bring clarity and consistency to our global mentoring, coaching and team effectiveness landscape by defining practical frameworks, prioritizing the right solutions and making them easy to activate across the organization. You’ll make a difference by collaborating closely with Talent, Leadership Development, Human Resources Business Partners, markets and business leaders to understand evolving needs, build internal capability and connect teams with the right internal or external support. This is your opportunity to take ownership, grow your expertise, and deliver meaningful results that make a difference at scale.

How you’ll make an impact as a Leadership Development & Team Effectiveness Manager • Leading a clear, scalable mentoring framework that strengthens matching, development relationships, knowledge sharing and professional networks. • Driving an integrated coaching portfolio across internal and external coaches, digital platforms, executive coaching and leadership transitions. • Building mentoring and coaching capability among leaders and the HR community so they can confidently activate the right solutions. • Defining practical team effectiveness frameworks, methods and tools that help teams improve collaboration and collective performance. • Designing, facilitating and guiding team interventions that support leadership teams through transformation, change and critical transitions. • Using business needs, insights, feedbacks and relevant measures to improve the Team Effectiveness offer and its impact.

What will make you successful • Bringing strong expertise in leadership development, mentoring, executive coaching, team coaching, or organizational development. • Experience in designing and facilitating team effectiveness interventions, ideally with senior leadership teams. • Strong understanding of team dynamics, organizational behaviour, high-performing team methods and recognized coaching practices and/or having a professional coaching qualification. • Ability to translate research and market insight into simple, scalable solutions while influencing stakeholders and managing capability portfolios and external partners. • Communicating fluently in English. • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.
