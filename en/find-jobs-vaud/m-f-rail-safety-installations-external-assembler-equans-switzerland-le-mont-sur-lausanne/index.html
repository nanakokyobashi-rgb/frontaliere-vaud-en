Full description

To support our growth and participate in projects essential for tomorrow's rail mobility, we are looking for an external assembler to strengthen our team in the Rail Safety Facilities department.

Your Mission

At the heart of the railway projects, you contribute to the realization of new installations and the maintenance of signalling systems, level crossings or remote control:

  • Participate in the installation, assembly, commissioning and maintenance of indoor and outdoor security facilities
  • Ensure quality, compliance, timeliness and traceability of the work carried out
  • Strictly apply existing safety regulations and standards, rules and guidelines
  • Assume, where necessary, the function of Chief Security Officer by implementing established security arrangements
  • Actively contribute to continuous improvement by proposing measures to enhance the safety, quality and productivity of interventions
  • Working closely with the various teams in the department
  • Ensure proper maintenance of vehicles, tools and equipment made available
  • Perform regular night interventions throughout French-speaking Switzerland, occasionally in Ticino, German Switzerland or France

Your Profile

  • Electrician installer CFC or equivalent training.
  • Good command of French in both oral and written terms
  • Ability to work at night (medical certificate required)
  • Chief of Safety Certification according to TEN 20100 or will obtain it
  • Very good reading skills for electrical diagrams and execution plans
  • A rigorous, reliable and quality-oriented method of work
  • Independent, proactive, pragmatic and solutions-oriented personality
  • Strong team spirit and sense of customer service
  • Driver's licence category B essential

Your Opportunity

  • Concrete and visible projects that contribute to the development of safe, efficient and sustainable mobility
  • A stimulating working environment within a committed and passionate team
  • Various missions and real autonomy in your activity
  • Opportunities for continuing training and professional development
  • Prospects for future developments
  • The opportunity to participate actively in the growth and innovation of our department

Ready to meet the challenge? Join a team that contributes daily to the safety and availability of the regional rail network, and participates in the construction of tomorrow's mobility.
