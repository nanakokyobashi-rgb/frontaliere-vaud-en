Your assignment

For one of our customers specializing in the sale, maintenance and repair of agricultural machinery and equipment for green, we look for a multipurpose electromechanical. Working mainly in the workshop, you will take care of the diagnosis, maintenance and repair of different types of machines, from small equipment to professional equipment.

Your responsibilities

  • Diagnosing mechanical, electrical and electromechanical faults
  • Perform repairs and preventive maintenance
  • Intervention on lawnmowers, decelers, hedge cutters, light agricultural machinery, municipal equipment and electric commercial vehicles
  • Occasionally perform welding jobs
  • Working independently, accurately and accurately

And now we're talking about you? You have obtained a federal capacity certificate (AFC) as a mechanic of agricultural machinery, automotive maintenance mechanic, polymechanical, electromechanical or equivalent training. A proven experience in repairing agricultural machinery, green equipment or similar machinery is a serious advantage. At ease with mechanics and electricity, you can diagnose faults with method and autonomy. Versatile, reliable and solutions-oriented, you want to engage in a stable company for a long term.

Your profile

  • Good knowledge of mechanics and electricity
  • Experience in repairing similar machines or equipment
  • Autonomy, versatility and sense of responsibility
  • Structured, precise and conscientious work
  • Ideally located residence in the Cossonay–Crissier region or at a reasonable distance
