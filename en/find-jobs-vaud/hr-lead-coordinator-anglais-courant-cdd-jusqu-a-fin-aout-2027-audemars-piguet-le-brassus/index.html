Full description

In this role, you will be attached to HR Services Manager. You play a key role in providing an exceptional collaborative experience and manager, while ensuring operational excellence throughout the employee life cycle. In close collaboration with HR Business Partners, CoE Centres, managers and local managers, you provide HR services and high-quality operational support aligned with Audemars Piguet's culture, values and strategic objectives. As a member of HR Service Delivery, you contribute to effective workforce management and continuous improvement of practices through efficient processes, rigorous data management, digital HR solutions and a collaborative experience-based approach. As such, you ensure that HR services comply with regulatory, scalable, simple-to-use requirements and are able to respond agilely to the evolving needs of the company and its employees.

Your main activities

  • Acting as a trusted interlocutor for employees and managers on all HR processes, internal policies and employee life cycle topics
  • Provide responsive, reliable and solutions-oriented HR support to ensure a high-quality employee and manager experience
  • Administrative management of staff, including the drafting of employment contracts, amendments, certificates, HR letters and all documentation related to the integration and departure of employees
  • To provide operational support to HR Business Partners and the Centres of Expertise in implementing processes related to performance management, compensation and talent development
  • Coordinate HR operations, ensure the quality and accuracy of collaborating data and prepare elements for payroll activities in collaboration with external providers
  • Ensure compliance with Swiss labour law, internal policies, audit requirements and HR governance standards
  • Produce and analyze HR indicators, dashboards and reporting to support decision-making, workforce planning and organizational performance
  • Promote the use of HR systems and self-service tools, contribute to HR digitalization initiatives and identify opportunities for automation and process optimization
  • Actively participate in HR transformation projects, continuous improvement initiatives and local and global HR projects to strengthen collaborative experience, operational excellence and HR service quality
  • You have an EFA, an HR Bachelor/Business Administration or equivalent training, ideally complemented by a Federal HR Patent
  • To the benefit of at least 6 years of experience in HR coordination, administration or management in a complex environment
  • Strong knowledge of Swiss labour law, HR processes and employee life cycle management
  • Experience confirmed in personnel administration, recruitment, payroll and HR information systems, ideally Workday
  • You are comfortable in an international, matrix or luxury industry environment.
  • You have excellent analytical capabilities, including monitoring HR data, indicators and reporting
  • You are recognized for your customer orientation and your ability to develop relationships of trust with various interlocutors
  • You master digital tools and sense of continuous improvement to optimize HR processes
  • You combine rigour, agility and operational excellence, you actively contribute to the organization's performance and transformation
  • You speak English fluently
