Full description

As Executive/HR Secretary within the Chamblandes Gymnase, you ensure the coordination and supervision of the Secretariat's activities, while providing administrative and organizational support to management. You are also responsible for the administrative management of human resources and ensure the application of existing HR processes. Through your sense of organization, initiative and ability to coordinate activities, you contribute to the smooth functioning of the institution and the continuous improvement of administrative services.

  • Organisation, coordination and supervision of the activities of the secretariat, as well as supervision and support of the administrative team
  • Development, implementation and updating of administrative procedures in a process optimization and continuous improvement approach
  • Administrative and organizational support to management, including time management, file preparation and follow-up of decisions
  • Administrative management of human resources, including personnel records, recruitment, absences, salary data and continuing training
  • Advice and support to staff and management on administrative and HR issues, in collaboration with DGEP HR Unit
  • Recruitment, mentoring and training of apprentices and trainees of the Secretariat
  • Coordination and participation in the institution's major operations, including school start-up, promotion and events
  • Management of administrative communications and collaboration with the various internal and external partners of the institution For further information, please consult the terms of reference by clicking here or contact Ms Morgane TORRAS, Executive Secretary & HR at 021 557 46 61 or by e-mail at morgan.torras@vd.ch.
  • Holder of a SWC of commercial employee, supplemented by a Federal Patent of Executive Assistant
  • Certificate of Personnel Management Assistant.
  • Training for CFFE trainer.
  • Further training in desired team conduct.
  • Proven professional experience in a position of executive secretary in a large company or in the public or parapublic sector, minimum 7 years.
  • Team leadership skills, responsibility and decision-making capacity
  • Excellent organizational, analytical and synthesis capabilities, with an overall vision of activities
  • Public service sense, relational ease, discretion and ability to communicate with various interlocutors
  • Autonomy, flexibility, proactivity and ability to manage priorities in a dynamic environment
  • Very good knowledge of computer tools, especially desktop software (Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, FileMaker pro, etc.)

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
