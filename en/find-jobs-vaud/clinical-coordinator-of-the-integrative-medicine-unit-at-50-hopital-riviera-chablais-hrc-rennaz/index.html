Clinical Coordinator of the Integrative Medicine Unit at 50%

The Hospital Riviera-Chablais, Vaud-Valais (HRC), is an independent institution under intercantonal public law. He employs nearly 2,500 employees. Its device consists of the Rennaz Acute Care Hospital, two Geriatric and Rehabilitation Clinics in Monthey and Vevey, as well as two medical clinics in Monthey and Vevey.

The HRC attaches particular importance to the patient's reception and care throughout his or her career. Our teams are committed to putting their skills to the benefit of the patient and his loved ones. As part of the development of our integrative medicine unit, we are looking for a clinical coordinator to develop and coordinate it.

The main tasks of this post are

  • Conduct medical-care consultations and guide patients
  • Linking the different complementary therapies proposed
  • Medical coordination of the Unit of Integrative Medicine
  • Participate in the development and implementation of development projects
  • Ensuring quality and security of care
  • Develop collaboration with the various medical-care services of the establishment
  • Representing unity with internal and external partners

We ask

  • Confirmed clinical experience in general or internal or palliative or integrative medicine
  • Clinical experience in complementary medicine, an asset
  • High interest in integrative medicine and patient-centred approaches
  • Experience in team coordination, project management or medical responsibility, an asset
  • Initiative, organizational sense and ability to work in an interdisciplinary environment

We offer

  • The opportunity to actively participate in the development of an innovative unit
  • An interprofessional working environment conducive to collaboration and innovation
  • Attractive working conditions
  • Opportunities for continuing training and professional development
  • A meaningful activity focused on a comprehensive approach to patient health and well-being

Information can be taken from Prof. Redouane Bouali, Medical Director or Dr Ludovic Melly, Deputy Medical Director at 058 773 48 09 or direction.medicale@hopitalrivierachablais.ch .

Deadline for application: 31 July 2026
