Full description

Job Overview

As an Access Control Systems Software Specialist, you play a key role in the deployment, configuration and support of our access control solutions. You work with our clients to ensure the implementation of software platforms, the integration of associated infrastructures and support for users throughout the project life cycle. You work in close collaboration with project teams, field technicians, our partners and our customers to guarantee reliable, secure and efficient installations.

What you will do

  • Carry out the installation, configuration and migration of our access control software for our clients.
  • Ensure the configuration, integration and commissioning of software solutions in clients' IT infrastructures.
  • Participate in the creation, configuration and administration of the server and network environments necessary for the operation of our systems.
  • Coordinate technical activities with client IT teams, project managers and external partners.
  • Provide level 1 and 2 technical support for software solutions.
  • Support our technicians and partners during the deployment and commissioning phases.
  • Carry out technical analyses, diagnostics and incident resolutions.
  • Train users and client administrators in the use of our solutions.

What we ask

  • Higher education in computer science (CFC, ES/ET, HES or equivalent).
  • Minimum 3 years of experience in a similar function oriented towards software, infrastructure or systems.
  • Excellent knowledge of IT infrastructures and computer networks.
  • Proven experience with Microsoft Windows Server operating systems and virtualized environments.
  • Basic knowledge of SQL and experience with Microsoft SQL Server.
  • Additional skills in implementing and managing backup solutions and deploying and managing IT monitoring solutions will be highly appreciated.
  • Experience in electronic security, access control, video surveillance or identity management systems is an important advantage.
  • Excellent sense of customer service, autonomy and ability to manage several priorities simultaneously.
  • Availability for travel to clients in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • Valid driving license.
  • Good command of French as well as English and/or German.

We offer
