Full description

The cantonal Office for Scholarships and Apprenticeships (OCBE), attached to the Directorate of Social Assistance and Insurance (DIRAAS), is responsible for providing financial assistance for training after compulsory schooling. In your capacity as an Adjunct to the Head of the OCBE, you assist him in determining the entity's conduct strategy and ensure that the accounting, litigation and reception units are managed.

  • To support the Head of Office in determining the entity's conduct strategy and thus help ensure its smooth functioning in synergy with internal and external partners;
  • To manage the accounting, litigation and reception units composed of 10 staff (Specialised case managers and case managers);
  • To assume responsibility for the establishment and proper application of the business procedures necessary to ensure the proper performance of the mission;
  • To organise, coordinate and supervise all day-to-day activities carried out by the Office in the event of the absence of the Head of Office;
  • Represent the entity in projects or working sessions. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Ms Sonia Gomes, Head of the OCBE Office, at: dgcs.recruitment@vd.ch
  • University degree in humanities or economics, Bachelor's degree in management or equivalent.
  • Federal patent of social insurance specialist an asset.
  • Additional training in management, leadership or team leadership.
  • Strong professional experience in team leadership.
  • Excellent analytical and synthesis capabilities.
  • Ability to define and interpret pilotage indicators.
  • Taste pronounced for the numbers.
  • Mastery of computer tools.
  • Aisance in both oral and written communication.
  • Interest in the policy issues surrounding the activities of the Directorate General for Social Cohesion (DGCS) and in particular the OCBE.
  • Proactivity, strong decision-making, flexibility and orientation solutions.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
