Full description

  • Accompany patients to maximum autonomy during rehabilitation.
  • Conducting care and surveillance that contributes to their functional recovery.
  • Support the resumption of daily activities and the return to home.
  • Collaborate with an interdisciplinary team involved in the patient's therapeutic project.
  • Participate in a safe, personalized and patient-centred management.
  • Holder of a Community Health and Care Assistant (CHS) CFC.
  • Recognized for your team spirit, sense of collaboration and versatility.
  • Capable of prioritizing, organizing and effectively coordinating your activities in a dynamic environment.
  • With a strong sense of responsibility, great motivation and a constant commitment to the quality of care.
  • Flexible, autonomous and comfortable in managing demanding situations, with good adaptability.
  • An activity in an exciting field, within a dynamic and expanding clinic
  • A pleasant working environment

We pay great attention to the diversity of life paths of our collaborators. Our positions are open to both women and men.

Since 1905, Clinique Valmont has been welcoming patients looking for peace and personalized care. With more than 115 years of experience and history in the service of health, Clinique Valmont is now a flagship rehabilitation facility, due to its unparalleled therapeutic efficiency, competent teams and state-of-the-art technical platform. At the Valmont Clinic, personal attention is paid to each of our patients. We make every effort to achieve our goal: to restore your health capital.
