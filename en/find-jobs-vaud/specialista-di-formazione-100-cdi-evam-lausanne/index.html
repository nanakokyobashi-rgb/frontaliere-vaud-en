Full description

EVAM (Etablissement vaudois de l'accueil des migrants) is a major player in the voodoo public service. With more than 1,000 employees and an annual budget of 360 million francs, we welcome, support and integrate migrants in the canton of Vaud.

Every day, our teams work with openness and responsibility to address key social issues. Joining EVAM means putting its skills at the service of a human mission and of public utility, in a stimulating, diversified and constantly evolving professional environment.

As part of a job creation, we recruit one: Training Specialist (100%) - CDI

Salary class: 7 (This scale refers to validated prerequisites, failing which the classification is at grade -1)

Place : Lausanne Entry into office : to be negotiated, 1st quarter 2027

What you bring to our mission

You will evolve into a team of 3 people: a training specialist, a training coordinator and a training assistant. You have a solid experience in pedagogical engineering and master the design, deployment and evaluation of training devices.

Your analytical capacity, creativity and organizational sense allow you to offer solutions tailored to the needs of the field. Self-reliant, rigorous and endowed with excellent relational skills, you know how to work with a variety of people, accompany change and actively contribute to the development of skills within the institution.

The word of your future responsible, Aurélie Derguini

"I prefer management based on trust, autonomy and accountability. I attach great importance to freedom of initiative, collaboration and sharing of ideas. I am also very committed to work-life balance, and consider flexible working hours as an essential element of well-being at work. I like working with committed, curious people who dare to propose, question practices and bring their own eyes. My goal: to create an environment where everyone finds their place, develops their skills and enjoys working together.

Your key missions

  • You analyze individual and collective training needs (use of tools such as: interviews, satisfaction surveys, skills analyses)
  • You define the educational objectives for each training
  • You are in charge of Training Engineering: program design based on the objectives and target audience
  • You participate in the development of tailor-made trainings for internal needs
  • You evaluate and select training offers from external partners
  • You measure the quality and effectiveness of training (satisfaction indicators, skills acquired, return on investment)
  • You are in charge of organizational management, in collaboration with our training coordinator and training assistant
  • You collaborate with internal and external services
  • You are in charge of some of the administrative tasks related to the training you manage

Your profile

  • Bachelor's degree useful to the function
  • 3 years experience min. in teaching engineering in a similar position (creation of training pathways)
  • Mastery of learning methods (face-to-face, e-learning, blended learning)
  • Mastery of Swiss legislation on continuing training, financing and certification systems (SEFRI, Training Funds, ESFP...).
  • Knowledge of skills assessment tools
  • Ability to analyse training needs
  • Mastery of the French language and excellent oral expression
  • Mastery of common office tools

What EVAM proposes

  • A committed working environment
  • A policy of continuing training to evolve in your career
  • A telework policy with 2 days of telework per week
  • 5 weeks of holiday (6 from 50 years)
  • Very good social benefits
  • Meaningful projects in a stimulating intercultural context

The 2-step recruitment process

  1. Interview with the Training Specialist and the Head of the Training Centre
  2. Situation

At the end of the process: request for an extract from the criminal record, special extract and certificate from the prosecution office (undocumented documents required).

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Vaud?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Vaud's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
