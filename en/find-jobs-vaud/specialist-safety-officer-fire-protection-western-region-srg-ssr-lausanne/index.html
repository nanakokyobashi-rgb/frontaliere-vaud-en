Full description

In order to strengthen our safety sector, the RTS is putting together a specialist fire safety officer, ideally with complementary skills in MSST/STPS, safety and/or event safety.

Your Mission

Attached to the Western Region Security Manager, you are actively contributing to the prevention of risks and the protection of people and infrastructure at the various sites and productions of the Western Region SRG SSR.

As the Western Region is also a national Champion Safety, you are contributing to the development and sharing of good practices in the fields of fire protection and occupational safety.

Main responsibilities

  • Monitor fire protection and ensure compliance with the AEAI requirements.
  • Monitor fire safety installations and equipment and identify any non-compliances.
  • Develop and maintain instructions, procedures and contingency plans.
  • Organize evacuation and first response exercises and monitor fire, rescue and evacuation devices.
  • Participate in risk analyses and in the implementation of security arrangements for productions and events, with or without public.
  • Participate in your development, transformation or construction projects.
  • Participate in incident management, RETEX and corrective action follow-up.
  • To advise and support trades and partners in the search for solutions adapted to the realities of the field.
  • Contribute to Western Region MSST/STPS and Safety activities and, depending on your profile, to certain security activities.
  • Contribute to the sharing of safety standards and best practices with other SRG SSR regions.

Your profile

  • Federal certificate of security officer required, being obtained or equivalent training.
  • Fire protection training, good knowledge of AEAI requirements and experience in risk prevention or management.
  • A complementary experience in one or more of the following areas is particularly appreciated: MSST/STPS, fire services, safety, technical environment, event security or event organization and management.
  • No one on the ground, pragmatic, versatile and solutions oriented, able to reconcile security, compliance and operational needs.
  • Good analytical and synthesis capacity, with an ability to heighten, structure the issues and develop concepts, standards and recommendations in the safety areas of his expertise.

What we offer you

  • An Interesting Key Role in National Security SRG SSR
  • An environment that promotes diversity, autonomy and social commitment
  • A hybrid work model with the possibility of partial telework
  • A committed team, driven by public service passion
