Fairmont Le Montreux Palace

Located on the shores of Lake Geneva, with magnificent views of the Alps, the Fairmont Le Montreux Palace hotel is a distinguished establishment, a member of the Accor group and the Fairmont brand, and affiliated with Swiss Deluxe Hotels. With an unrivaled worldwide reputation, 236 luxurious rooms and suites, and one of the finest dining experiences, the Fairmont Le Montreux Palace hotel is truly an enchanting place. Its six restaurants and bars offer a wide selection of delights sure to satisfy the most discerning palates. Le Montreux Jazz Café is the must-visit place to experience the rhythm of the Festival 365 days a year in the magical setting of Fairmont Le Montreux Palace, MP’s Bar & Grill is an upscale restaurant with a relaxed atmosphere specializing in grilled dishes, unrivaled in the region, and Funky Claude’s Bar is the ultimate place to head to late into the night to enjoy an incredible lineup of traditional and exclusive cocktails to the rhythm of the best live music scene in town. Fairmont Le Montreux Palace also specializes in hosting conferences, meetings and seminars. The Fairmont Spa is a 2000 m² wellness space dedicated to fitness, relaxation and beauty.

Job description

Reporting to the Banquets & Breakfast Manager, the responsibilities and essential functions of the position include, but are not limited to:

  • Consistently provide professional, friendly and personable service
  • Set up and dismantle rooms according to the Service Orders (SOs)
  • Review the Service Orders (SOs) daily
  • Ensure that the equipment in the rooms is in good condition
  • Respond to guest requests
  • Carefully perform the service tasks requested
  • Recognize the group leader and ensure high-quality service for them
  • Be flexible and cooperative under all circumstances, particularly when required to collaborate with another department on an occasional basis
  • Commit to the quality of their service, their discretion and their complete honesty
  • Maintain a professional attitude, smile and remain calm at all times
  • Respect ergonomic work standards, health & safety regulations, and hygiene and sanitation requirements
  • Respect the department's and the company's policies, procedures and service standards
  • All other tasks as assigned

Credentials

  • Fluency in French
  • Knowledge of English is an asset
