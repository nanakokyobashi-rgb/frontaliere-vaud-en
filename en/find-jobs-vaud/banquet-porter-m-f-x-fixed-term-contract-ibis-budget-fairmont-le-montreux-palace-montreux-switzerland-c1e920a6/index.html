Job description

Reporting to the Banquets & Breakfast Manager, the responsibilities and essential functions of the position include, but are not limited to:

  • Consistently provide professional, friendly and personable service
  • Set up and dismantle rooms according to the Service Orders (SOs)
  • Review the Service Orders (SOs) daily
  • Ensure that the equipment in the rooms is in good condition
  • Respond to guest requests
  • Carefully perform the service tasks requested
  • Recognize the group leader and ensure high-quality service for them
  • Be flexible and cooperative under all circumstances, particularly when required to collaborate with another department on an occasional basis
  • Commit to the quality of their service, their discretion and their complete honesty
  • Maintain a professional attitude, smile and remain calm at all times
  • Respect ergonomic work standards, health & safety regulations, and hygiene and sanitation requirements
  • Respect the department's and the company's policies, procedures and service standards
  • All other tasks as assigned
