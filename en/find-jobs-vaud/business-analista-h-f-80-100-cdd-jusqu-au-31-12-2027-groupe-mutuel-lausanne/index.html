Description

You analyze the needs of the Medical Service and identify opportunities for automation, simplification and value creation through new technologies. A true business partner, you link users, Data Analytics Technology teams and other stakeholders to transform ideas into concrete solutions. You contribute to the development and implementation of innovative projects around artificial intelligence, process automation and business digitization. You pilot the analyses, workshops, feasibility studies, tests and validations needed to integrate new solutions into our business processes. Data exploitation is an integral part of your activity to produce indicators, analyses and recommendations to inform decisions. Thanks to your spirit of synthesis and excellent communication capacity, you prepare and present recommendations for management and accompany the change with users. Groupe Mutuel is one of Switzerland's leading insurance groups, headquartered in Martigny (Valais). The company offers a wide range of insurance and pension products for individuals and businesses across Switzerland.
