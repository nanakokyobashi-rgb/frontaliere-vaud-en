Full description

The Groupement Hospitalier de l'Ouest Lémanique (GHOL) is a publicly recognized health institution serving the population of the region. Through its facilities in Nyon and Rolle, it offers a diverse range of care and ensures quality care in a welcoming, safe and responsive environment. Within our stewardship department, you contribute daily to the quality of reception and well-being of patients, staff and visitors. Through your work, you are involved in maintaining a clean, pleasant environment that respects the hygiene requirements of the hospital environment. In order to ensure better coordination of activities and close monitoring of teams, the role of our managers in stewardship is directed towards more steering of sectors and accompanying employees in their daily lives.

Your mission

  • Ensuring the daily functioning of hotel activities in the field
  • Organize and allocate the tasks of the teams
  • Manage contingencies and oversee sector activities
  • Ensuring first level quality controls
  • Participate in the integration and training of employees
  • Ensure compliance with institutional standards

Your profile

  • Stewardship Manager SWC
  • Minimum 3 years of experience in a health facility
  • Organizational sense, autonomy and team spirit
  • Excellent relational skills and sense of responsibility

We offer

  • A varied activity within a dynamic hospital environment
  • Close cooperation with the various departments of the institution
  • Benefits of the collective agreement for the public health sector (CCT-San)
  • Collaborating advantages and preferential rates with local brands
  • A staff restaurant with meal cost sharing
  • Priority for admission of staff children to the Le Carrousel nursery
  • Institutional events

Do you recognize yourself in this profile and want to join a service offering a stimulating work environment? We look forward to receiving your application.
