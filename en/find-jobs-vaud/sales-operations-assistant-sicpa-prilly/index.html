SALES ASSISTANT OPERATIONS

Role

  • Actively contribute to customer demand management and tracking in collaboration with the Offer and Sales Order Processing and Demand Management teams
  • Provide administrative and operational support related to Sales Operations activities

Key responsibilities

1.Support to the Offer and Sales Order Processing team for customer request management:

  • To register customer requests related to a price request, offer or order
  • Participate in early deployments of customer demand digitization
  1. Support to Customer Demand Management Team

2.1 Demand and Order Management

  • Confirm customer orders in SAP (ATP check)
  • Manage time, priorities and urgent orders in collaboration with the Production Planning team

2.2 Client claims management

  • Record client claims and ensure they are directed to appropriate internal resources
  • Administrative support for claims management
  • Coordinate returns, replacement orders and preparation of credit notes

Profile

  • Trade, administrative or equivalent training in data management, international trade or sales administration is an asset
  • 2-3 years' experience in a valued industrial or international environment
  • Mastery of SAP Order to Cash system and good understanding of Order-to-Cash processes
  • ERP and CRM (SAP, Salesforce or equivalent) is an asset
  • Good mastery of Excel, and other Microsoft Office tools
  • Sense of organization and rigour
  • Strong quality orientation and attention to detail
  • Analysis and problem-solving capability
  • Spirit of collaboration and sense of service
  • Excellent relational and communication skills

Why should we join?

  • Take on a strategic role in a financially strong and innovative business
  • Collaborate with high-level experts and contribute to international projects with high added value
  • Evolve in a multicultural, demanding and integrity-based environment where autonomy and leadership are encouraged
