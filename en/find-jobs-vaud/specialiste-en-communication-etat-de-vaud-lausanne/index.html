Full description

As a communications specialist, you are involved in the information, public relations and external communication activities of the Vaudese Cantonal Police (VCP) and the Savatan Police Academy (PA). You propose and manage communication projects with high added value (recruitment campaigns, prevention, institutional image). You ensure relations with the media and the judicial communication of the Vaudese cantonal police.

  • To act as a contact specialist for the Savatan Police Academy (PA);
  • Carry out outreach and external public relations;
  • Ensure media relations and judicial communication;
  • Carry out internal communications and public relations activities;
