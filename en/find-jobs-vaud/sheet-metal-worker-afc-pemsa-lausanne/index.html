Your assignment

As a lactern, you will intervene in new construction sites, renovation, maintenance and repair of covers.

Your responsibilities

  • Read projects and make reliefs
  • Cut, shape, fold and assemble the sheet
  • Making and laying gutters, rains, fittings and coatings
  • Put metal coatings on roofs and facades
  • Welding, brazing, riveting and glazing work
  • Participating in the complete renovation of roofs
  • Maintenance and repair the skylights, especially the Velux
  • Locate and solve leaks and infiltrations
  • Compliance with the safety standards for work at altitude

And now we're talking about you? You are in possession of a federal capacity certificate (CFC) as a lactonist or equivalent training recognized in Switzerland. You have a proven experience in sheet metal processing, covering or refurbishing roofs. Autonomous and meticulous, master craft techniques and ensure a precise and lasting work.

Your profile

  • Good grip of welding, brazing and assembly techniques
  • Knowledge of materials and cover systems
  • Ability to read technical projects and schemes
  • Precise, rigorous and quality oriented work
  • Good physical form and familiarity with work at altitude
  • Reliability, organization and team spirit

Interested? Do you love and master your job and want to put your skills in the service of projects that really matter? Then this offer is for you. From us, your know-how makes a difference and directly contributes to the success of the yards.
