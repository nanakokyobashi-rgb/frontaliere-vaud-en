Full description

About us

Joining the Groupement Hospitalier de l'Ouest Lémanique (GHOL) is to integrate a regional hospital of human size, recognized for the quality of its care and attention to the well-being of patients. In Nyon, our teams combine professional requirements, proximity and spirit of collaboration. In this context, we are strengthening our brigade and looking for a Deputy Chef de Cuisine, animated by healthy nutrition, quality dietetic cuisine and gastronomy.

Your mission

Within the central kitchen, you integrate a brigade of about 20 employees working in continuous hours. A true link between medical prescriptions and hospital food for our patients, you contribute every day to a safe, adapted and appetizing diet, fully participating in the care journey. As a kitchen assistant, you assist the kitchen manager in organizing, coordinating and monitoring the production and distribution of meals for patients, employees and external clients. You will replace him in his absence in the framework of the delegations granted. In your new function, you will be responsible for the following main tasks:

  • Participate in the development of menus in collaboration with the chef des cuisines et les dieteticiennes.
  • Ensure daily monitoring of hot, cold and dietetic production and control the taste, nutritional and aesthetic quality of preparations.
  • Ensure compliance with technical data sheets, HACCP standards, hygiene and food safety.
  • Participate in orders, monitor receipt of goods and manage inventory.
  • Supervise and coordinate cooks, cooks in dietetics, pastry chefs, kitchen assistants and apprentices, allocate tasks and monitor the quality of the work performed.
  • Support the integration of new employees and practical training of apprentices.
  • Participate in the development of production schedules, absentee management and the planning of special events and services.
  • Ensure the condition, maintenance and optimum use of machinery and equipment, and participate in the maintenance of inventories.
  • Ensure a field presence and intervene in production when necessary.
  • Ensure continuity of kitchen operations in the absence of the kitchen chef
  • To contribute to the budget and to the continuous improvement of benefits by being a proposal force.
  • Participate in symposia, working groups and audits, and relay institutional guidelines to teams.

Profile required

Your success is guaranteed by one/one,

  • Cookery CFC in dietetics or training deemed equivalent; further training in management or team management.
  • Experience confirmed (min. 5 years) in hospital cooking, with team coaching experience.
  • Excellent mastery of modern culinary techniques and knowledge of diets and textures adapted to patients.
  • Mastery of HACCP standards and community food legislation.
  • Stock and order management knowledge.
  • Mastery of common computer tools (Excel, Word, business software like Logimen).
  • Responsibility, organizational, planning and operational decision-making capacity.
  • Rigor, reactivity and sense of anticipation.
  • Team spirit, constructive communication, sense of service, diplomacy and good conflict management.

As a field leader, you like to convey your know-how and unite your brigade around a quality kitchen, serving patients. A true team player, you promote a positive and collaborative working climate and are comfortable relaying instructions as well as raising the needs of your teams.

What we offer

A position of responsibility at the heart of the hospital restaurant, with a role of replacement of the chef des cuisines. A human-sized regional hospital where proximity and spirit of collaboration count. Close collaboration with dieticians and all GHOL services.
