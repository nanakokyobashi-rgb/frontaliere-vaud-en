Your role

As an electric designer, you participate in the design, planning and technical monitoring of electrical projects in the building sector. Propose reliable, effective and consistent solutions to Swiss standards in force, taking into account the needs of customers, technical requirements and site constraints.

Your main responsibilities

  • Develop technical concepts related to electrical construction plants
  • Design solutions for lighting, emergency lighting and safety systems
  • Making plans, schemes and technical documentation necessary for project execution
  • Draft invitations to tender, tender documents and technical documents
  • Prepare documentation according to the needs of the project, current standards and customer requirements
  • Ensure the technical monitoring of the yards and ensure the correct running of the works
  • Working with clients, field teams, suppliers and customers
  • Ensure good coordination between the different subjects involved in the project
  • Participate in cost monitoring and ensure compliance with budgets
  • Analyze technical constraints and propose appropriate solutions
  • Contribute to the quality, safety and correct execution of electrical projects

And now we're talking about you? You are in possession of a Federal Capacity Certificate (CFC) as an electric designer or equivalent training recognized in Switzerland. You have a proven experience in the field of electrical construction plants, ideally matured on the Swiss market. Master technical design and design tools and you can manage your projects by method, precision and priority. Organized, autonomous and oriented to solutions, you are able to analyze the technical needs, anticipate the constraints and propose solutions suitable both to the needs on the field and to those of the customer.

Your profile

  • Eligibility as an electric designer (CFC) or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland
  • Proven experience in electrical construction plants
  • Good knowledge of Swiss standards and practices in force
  • Mastery of technical design and design software, especially AutoCAD
  • Knowledge of WinProgitel: an appreciated advantage
  • Ability to draft plans, schemes, offers and technical documentation
  • Autonomy, rigor and sense of organization
  • Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously
  • Analytical spirit and ability to propose concrete solutions
  • Ability to anticipate needs and good priority management
  • Excellent relational capacity
  • Effective communication with customers, customers and technical teams

You are a structured, reliable and solution-oriented person, able to ensure accurate technical follow-up by actively contributing to the success of electrical projects. Interested? Do you love and master your profession and want to put your skills in the service of projects that really matter? Then this offer is for you. From us, your expertise makes a difference and directly contributes to the success of the yards.
