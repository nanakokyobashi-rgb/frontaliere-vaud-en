Full description

Riviera-Chablais Hospital, Vaud-Valais (HRC), is an intercantonal reference hospital, which brings together more than 2,500 employees. The Rennaz Hospital Center has more than 350 acute care beds. It is complemented by two geriatrics and rehabilitation clinics as well as two medical hotlines. All employees work hard every day to provide quality care to patients, embodying the values ​​of the institution: authenticity, exemplarity and innovation.

To complete the team at our medical clinics, in Monthey and Vevey, open 7 days. on 7 days, from 9 a.m. to 7 p.m., we are advertising for the position of:

Head of clinic at 80%-100%

The main missions of this position are

  • Ensure patient care, in collaboration with senior doctors
  • Participate in the custody tour

We ask

  • A federal medical diploma or a diploma issued by an EU member state and recognized by the federal office of public health (MEBEKO)
  • Advanced training in general internal medicine (FMH title or equivalent)
  • Experience in emergency medicine would be a plus
  • An open and dynamic personality, eager to integrate into the team already in place and to collaborate with senior and approved doctors from other HRC services

We offer

HRC offers great employment and professional development opportunities in all of its fields of activity: medical, nursing, medical-technical, hotel, administrative, technical, logistics. In a desire for continuous improvement, skills development is at the heart of HRC's concerns. The institution offers its employees access to training both internally and externally.

In 2021, HRC was twice ranked among the best employers in French-speaking Switzerland by Bilan magazine and Le Temps newspaper, placing it as a leading institution.
