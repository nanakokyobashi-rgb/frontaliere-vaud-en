Full description

The Riviera-Chablais Hospital, Vaud-Valais (HRC), is a reference intercantonal hospital, which brings together more than 2,500 collaborators. Rennaz Hospital has over 350 acute care beds. It is complemented by two Geriatric and Rehabilitation Clinics and two medical clinics. All employees invest on a daily basis to provide quality care to patients, embodying the values of the institution: authenticity, exemplaryness and innovation.

Our service of Gynecology-Obstetrics provides in particular nearly 2,000 births per year.

In order to create the position, we are looking for an indeterminate contract, one:

Midwife M/F, head of 80% outpatient care unit: gynaecological and obstetric emergencies and outpatient consultations

The main tasks of this post are

  • Provide leadership to his team and clear and transparent positioning
  • To supervise and supervise the staff of the unit, in accordance with the institutional directives
  • Organize work, care activities and guarantee mutual assistance between employees
  • Ensure the quality of patient reception and services provided
  • Participating in staff recruitment
  • Participate in quality projects and research protocols in the unit
  • Ensuring a spirit of openness, flexibility, creativity and innovation

We ask

  • A Bachelor of Midwifery or Midwifery Nurse or equivalent
  • Minimum experience of 5 years in obstetrics and gynaecology
  • Training in community management, or committing to training
  • Team management experience would be an asset, as well as leadership skills
  • Ability to work as a team, a sense of collaboration and organization, management of priorities
  • A good mastery of computer tools

We offer

The HRC offers great job opportunities and career development in all its fields of activity: medical, nursing, medico-technical, hotel, administrative, technical, logistics. In a desire for continuous improvement, skills development is at the heart of the HRC's concerns. The institution offers its employees access to training both internally and externally.

In 2021, the HRC was ranked twice among the best employers in French-speaking Switzerland by the magazine Bilan and the newspaper Le Temps, placing it as a leading institution.
