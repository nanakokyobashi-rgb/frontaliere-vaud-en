This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

In your capacity as project manager within the General Services (SG) of the Cantonal Police (PCV), you ensure the replacement of the project manager for a period of 7 to 9 months in the coordination, monitoring and organisation of portfolios of projects, in particular in the field of the evolution of the Vaudian police landscape (EVOPOL) and in topics internal to the PCV. By joining the CPV, you contribute to a public service committed to serving its population, its security and to maintaining social cohesion.

  • Ensure the replacement of the project leader during her absence in the coordination tasks between all projects under the responsibility of the unit.
  • Manage projects in the area of the evolution of the Vaud police landscape or internal to the cantonal police, including organizational projects.
  • Support, at the methodological level, certain project leaders within the PCV.
  • Conduct and supervise the work of project teams.
  • Develop contacts with project stakeholders to integrate or keep them informed.
  • Perform project tasks in accordance with established methods and standards.
  • To communicate and manage changes in portfolio projects under the auspices of the Service Branch.
  • Ensure the traceability of project information with a view to the transfer upon the licensee's return. For further information, please consult the terms of reference by clicking here or contact Ms. THÉVENAZ, Project Leader, at 021 644 82 66 or by e-mail: celine.thevenaz@vd.ch . Further information on the activities of the Cantonal Police can also be found at www.police.ch
  • Academic (Bachelor or Master) or equivalent (HES or recognized certification).
  • Training in project management required.
  • Training in communication and/or change management.
  • Experience in project management (5 years).
  • Writing and use of computer tools.
  • Analysis and synthesis capacity.
  • Communication and negotiation. Relationship strength.
  • Knowledge of public institutions and strong interest in the police field. Our terms and conditions of employment require that you be exempt from prosecution or default.
