Full description

Thanks to its 800 collaborators, the Broye Intercantonal Hospital (HIB) is responsible for acute care and rehabilitation. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the Broyard population. To strengthen our team in general and orthopaedic surgery, the HIB is looking for the Payerne site of a-e:

Nurse in general and orthopaedic surgery 80% - 100%

Your Mission

  • Ensuring preventive, curative, palliative and educational nursing through clinical evaluation
  • Ensure patient care by providing independent nursing care in a safe, efficient and comfortable manner
  • Perform medical-delegated acts and control the technical actions resulting therefrom
  • Actively participate in the evaluation, analysis of clinical situations and development of care projects
  • Ensure the effectiveness of therapeutic projects by integrating the requirements of interdisciplinary work into your daily practice
  • Ensure good information and quality of reception for patients and their relatives
  • To be a force for proposal and to ensure approaches to improving the efficiency of care
  • Apply and enforce hospital and safety rules in force in the institution

Your assets

  • You benefit from a HES degree in general care (or title deemed equivalent)
  • Hospital experience in surgery service an asset
  • Proactively, your technical, organizational and relational skills allow you to provide quality care
  • Equipped with a common sense of communication, you have a strong interest in multidisciplinary collaboration and demonstrate speed and accuracy in the performance of your tasks
  • For this position, excellent command of French (spoken and written) and computer tools are essential

Entry into office: immediately to be agreed
