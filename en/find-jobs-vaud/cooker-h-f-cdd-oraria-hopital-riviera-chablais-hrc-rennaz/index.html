Full description

Cooker H/F - Hourly CDD The Riviera-Chablais Hospital, Vaud-Valais (HRC), is a reference intercantonal hospital, which brings together more than 2,500 collaborators. Rennaz Hospital has over 350 acute care beds. It is complemented by two Geriatric and Rehabilitation Clinics and two medical clinics. All employees invest on a daily basis to provide quality care to patients, embodying the values of the institution: authenticity, exemplaryness and innovation. Our team is looking for, for a fixed period, a: Cooker H/F The main tasks of this post are: As a cook, you contribute daily to the production of high-quality meals for patients, staff and the hospital's satelist services. You participate in the excellence of hotel management, while respecting the requirements of food safety, hygiene and quality approach.

  • Make meals on a daily basis in accordance with the fact sheets and the planning established
  • Ensure high and constant taste quality of preparations
  • Strictly apply hospital hygiene, food safety and occupational safety rules
  • Participate in the production chain and in the distribution of meals to patients and staff
  • Cleaning the workstation and equipment used
  • Contributing to a good working environment and providing the information necessary for continuity of service
  • To supervise and accompany cooking aids and to participate in the training of apprentices
  • Involved in the quality approach and continuous improvement of services
  • Ensure the proper functioning of the equipment by reporting any defects to the hierarchy
  • Apply the HACCP method rigorously and ensure its traceability We ask
  • A cookery CFC or a title deemed equivalent
  • Excellent knowledge of hygiene, food safety and good HACCP practices
  • A strong sense of organization, rigour and quality
  • An ability to work independently while fostering teamwork
  • A customer-oriented attitude, with concern for the well-being of patients and staff
  • Good internal communication and transmission of information
  • An ability to manage priorities and work in a dynamic environment
  • Flexibility and availability according to service needs (irregular/weekends) We offer The HRC offers great job opportunities and career development in all its fields of activity: medical, nursing, medico-technical, hotel, administrative, technical, logistics. In a desire for continuous improvement, skills development is at the heart of the HRC's concerns. The institution offers its employees access to training both internally and externally. In 2021, the HRC was twice ranked among the best employers in French-speaking Switzerland by the magazine Bilan and the newspaper Le Temps , placing it as a leading institution.
