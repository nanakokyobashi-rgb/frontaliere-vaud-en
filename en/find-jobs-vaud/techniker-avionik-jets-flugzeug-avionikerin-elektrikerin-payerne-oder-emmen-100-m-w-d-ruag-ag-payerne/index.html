Full description

About 3000 employees of RUAG and RUAG Real Estate each day make an essential contribution to the security of Switzerland. Their mission is to ensure that the Swiss Army and other intervention and security organisations can fully assume their duties at all times.

Your area of action

In this role, you will work directly on current and future military aircraft, primarily with the following tasks:

  • Maintenance and modification work: execution of scheduled maintenance operations, including replacement and control of avionics and electrical systems (instruments, electronics, radio and avionics)
  • Repairs and fault finding: analysis of error messages and diagnostic processes, including fault resolution
  • Modification and control missions: visual and functional checks after each flight (aircraft and systems condition)
  • Documentation and quality management
  • Participation in internal avionics safety and quality training as part of maintenance and runway support.

Your contribution

  • Technical or electrotechnical vocational training with CFC (automatician, electronician, diagnostician or related field)
  • Experience in aeronautics and/or automotive electricity desired but not required
  • Excellent technical skills
  • Mastery of English to communicate at B1/B2 level; for Emmen site knowledge in German are also desired
  • Category B driving licence
  • High availability and flexibility (including for possible shift schedules and picket service)
  • Stress-resistant personality with a team spirit and a high sense of responsibility, coupled with a precise and quality-oriented work method
  • Nationality/Citizenship Switzerland (required to perform this function on a military basis)
