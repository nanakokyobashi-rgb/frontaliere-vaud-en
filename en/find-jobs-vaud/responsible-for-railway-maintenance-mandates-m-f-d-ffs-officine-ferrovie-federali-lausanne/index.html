Head of Mandate maintenance track (m/f/d) 60-100%

Move Switzerland with us. Manager of railway maintenance (m/f/d) Lausanne, 01.12.2026 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

An innovative working environment awaits you, in which you can implement major changes and thus make a decisive contribution to the digitisation of SBB. In collaboration with your team colleagues, you are developing and implementing innovative solutions to meet existing challenges. This post also includes picket services, (StöOrg), For the lifting of disturbance, which can also cause you to have to move to places of intervention. What you can move.

  • Take responsibility for mandates and ensure implementation/execution of multidisciplinary maintenance mandates involving small multidisciplinary missions.
  • Perform AVOR1 for calculation processing and AVOR2 for execution planning.
  • Ensure the conduct of work.
  • Advise and provide technical support to mandate holders, mandate managers and team leaders.

What you bring.

  • SSE foreman school completed successfully.
  • Experience/knowledge of construction processes and operating conditions.
  • Directorate of safety according to the TEN 20100 or willing to attend the training.
  • Team spirit, interpersonal and autonomous.
  • Driver's licence category B mandatory.
  • You are flexible and use the form of Work Smart work to also work at home and on the move.

Your salary and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive salary system and we are actively committed to equal pay. This is why additional and detailed information on the SBB salary system can be consulted in full transparency. This position falls within the G level and is eligible for a Level 1 regional allowance. Learn more about salary and benefits.

Here's a good way to start.

The fact that you want to move Switzerland with us is the greatest motivation for us. We therefore waive our request for a cover letter for this position. If you wish, you can write to us in our application tool.
