Project Manager & Expertise Automation Confirmed (M/F) back to list To strengthen our teams, we are looking for a Project Manager & Expertise Automation Confirmed (M/F). Job description

Your mission

  • Technical piloting of automation projects, from needs analysis to installation
  • Coordinate the interventions of integrators, suppliers and external partners during the deployment phases
  • Design and implement new features to optimize equipment performance, availability and ergonomics
  • Participate in continuous improvement and technology renewal projects that have become obsolete
  • Bring your expertise to complex technical investigations and critical failures
  • Support production, maintenance, quality and engineering teams in solving technical problems
  • Ensure the updating of technical documentation and compliance with internal standards
  • Contribute to the dissemination of good practices and the development of technical team skills

Profile

Profile desired

  • Advanced ES, HES or equivalent training in automation, electrotechnical or industrial systems
  • Confirmed experience in a similar function in an automated industrial environment
  • Excellent control of industrial automation programming
  • Good knowledge of industrial data monitoring and acquisition systems (SCADA/HMI)
  • Strong reading skills and understanding electrical diagrams
  • Knowledge of industrial network infrastructure and computer production architecture
  • Experience in instrumentation, industrial vision, MES systems or industrial digitalisation is a real advantage
  • Ability to manage technical projects while maintaining a strong operational presence in the field
  • Excellent communication skills, knowledge sharing and team support
  • Spirit of analysis, autonomy, rigour and strength of proposal
