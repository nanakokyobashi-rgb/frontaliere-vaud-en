Full description

Your future playground You will join the shop in Bussigny opened in 2018, a real XXL format offering a commercial area of 4,500 m2. You will be part of a dynamic team of 80 collaborators. This shop is a real locomotive: it is in the first place in the country in turnover! There are great challenges on a large scale and in the dynamic city of Lausanne! Join an adventure that continues to grow Decathlon in Switzerland, it is a success story that started in 2017 in Neuchâtel. Since then, we have opened around ten stores a year to reach 50 outlets today. And we're not going to stop there! Our expansion continues, especially in German-speaking Switzerland, offering career opportunities throughout the country. Why is this job for you? Are you looking for a job that moves, where you share your passion for sports from morning to night? You want to be proud to advise the right equipment and see your client go away with a smile? Do you like action, terrain and teamwork? Your 3 great benefits to us:

  • You're at the heart of the action. Your daily life is 100% in the field. You're Decathlon's face. Whether it's welcoming a beginner or advising an expert, you share your passion and create the link.
  • You're inventing tomorrow's business. You're not just cashing in. You offer durable solutions: equipment rental, second-life buyback or personal shopper. You're acting for sport and the planet.
  • You are autonomous and responsible: The welcome and the line of caisses is your domain! You're the first and last contact of our athletes. You guarantee the fluidity of the passages, you take initiatives to find solutions and you highlight our services. Your impact is immediately apparent on your customers' final satisfaction. What You Will Do Every Day: Your Missions
  • A top reception and tailor-made advice: You are the first smile at the entrance and the last one at the exit! You welcome, listen to and guide athletes in the store, while advising them on our products, services and innovations (personal shopper, gift cards, etc.).
  • The guarantee of a smooth passage: You take an active part in the receipts and accompany the clients to the automatic caisses with pedagogy. You take the opportunity to propose and animate our loyalty program, all with efficiency and good humour.
  • An impeccable reception area: The entrance and line of boxes are your showcase! You ensure the availability and dispatch of items in your area (pulse purchases, small accessories) to ensure a smooth and pleasant customer experience throughout the year.
  • Management of omnichannel services and circular economy: You are at the heart of customer solutions. You manage the removal of web commands (Click & Collect), returns or exchanges. You are also the ambassador of our new models: you inform our athletes about rental, workshop services and buy-in to offer a second life to the equipment.
  • Specific missions: Depending on your wishes, you will be able to become a referent on a subject (e.g. the circular economy at the reception), trainer for your colleagues, or even help on an ad hoc basis the teams in the department and the overall management of the store. This job isn't for you if...
  • You'd rather work alone in peace. The entrance to a sports store is lively, it moves, and you will be constantly asked!
  • You don't like to go spontaneously to others.
  • You think managing the return of items, maintaining an impeccable cash area, "this is not your problem."
  • You don't want to work on Saturdays.
  • You are looking for fixed and routine schedules: here we live in the rhythm of sport and our schedules adapt to the activity (even if we communicate them to you 6 weeks in advance so that you can organize!).
