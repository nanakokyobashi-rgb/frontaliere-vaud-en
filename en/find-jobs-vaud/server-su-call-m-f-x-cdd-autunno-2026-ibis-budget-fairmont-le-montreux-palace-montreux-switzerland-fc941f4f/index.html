Job description

Reporting to the Banquets Supervisor, the responsibilities and essential functions of the position include, but are not limited to:

  • Consistently provide professional, friendly and engaging service
  • Participate in setting up, serving and clearing the rooms
  • Check the order before serving it to guests and ensure that service is as fast as possible
  • Serve orders to guests discreetly
  • Have thorough knowledge of event menus (food and beverage) and be able to explain their main ingredients
  • Ensure that guests have everything they need
  • Be efficient and able to take initiative, assist the supervisor and act as a liaison between the latter and on-call staff (extras)
  • Learn about events at the hotel and participate in the briefing before events
  • Follow the instructions provided (setup, storage, cleaning), perform assigned tasks to the best of your ability and ensure that on-call staff do the same
  • Inform the supervisor of any equipment malfunction
  • Inform the supervisor of any requests or complaints from guests and, if possible, respond to them directly
  • Participate in weekly meetings
  • Actively participate in service in other departments or at events taking place at the hotel, at the request of superiors
  • Actively participate in inventories in the department
  • Participate in training the new intern and new on-call staff
  • Participate in an evaluation interview with the supervisor once a year
  • Be flexible and cooperative in all circumstances, particularly when required to collaborate with another department on an occasional basis
  • Commit to the quality of service, discretion and complete honesty
  • Have a professional attitude, be cheerful and remain calm at all times
  • Comply with ergonomic work standards in accordance with health & safety regulations and hygiene and sanitary requirements
  • Respect the department's and company's policies, procedures and service standards
  • Any other duties as assigned

Qualifications

  • 6 months of prior experience in service
  • Fluency in French
  • Good knowledge of English
  • Diploma in service an asset
  • Ability to work effectively under pressure in a fast-paced environment
  • Well-presented, neat and conscientious
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team
  • Flexibility in working hours
