Full description

The Finance, Administration and Support Directorate combines strategic, financial, administrative, technical and policy knowledge. As a support entity for the other branches of the Directorate-General for Buildings and Heritage and its Directorate-General, it brings together various cross-cutting activities. In order to strengthen its accounting unit on the Mont-sur-Lausanne site, the Finance, Administration and Support Directorate is looking for an assistant in accounting management.

  • Assume accounting management of supplier and debtor: manage, seize and control accounting documents related to suppliers and customers on the basis of evidence, process supplier recalls, monitor and renew independent certificates
  • Ensure that general and ancillary accounts are kept on a regular basis in accordance with all accounting processes: follow up on outstanding cases, reconcile accounts to be settled, capture actual expenditure, process expense notes
  • To ensure the overall management of financial flows from ordering to invoicing in collaboration with the various actors involved
  • Respond to internal and external requests for accounting information. For further information, please consult the specifications here or contact Mrs. Noémie Kohli at 021 338 45 72.
  • SWC of trade employee or equivalent
  • Professional experience of at least 5 years in accounting
  • Mastery of Excel and all MS Office software as well as SAP software
  • Rigor, adaptability and flexibility, sense of integrity and public service
  • Spirit of self-help, ability to develop positive exchanges, sense of listening and communication.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
