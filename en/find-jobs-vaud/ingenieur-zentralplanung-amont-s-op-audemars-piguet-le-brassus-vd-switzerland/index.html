Full description

Within the Central Planning Up Team, you play a key role in driving the Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP) process. A true partner of Supply Chain, Production, Purchasing, Finance teams, you help to develop tactical plans to align the demand, industrial capabilities and ambitions of the company. Thanks to your overall vision of the value chain, you are a guarantor of the coherence of capacity scenarios and actively participate in decision-making on medium-term planning issues.

In this context, you will be in charge of

  • Pilot and lead the upstream S&OP process on a tactical horizon to ensure consistent and aligned decisions with the company's objectives
  • Ensure the consolidation and reliability of planning data required for load/capacity analysis and operational arbitration
  • Develop macro models of load and capacity projection, build and challenge capacitive plans to anticipate industrial constraints and secure the achievement of production objectives
  • Develop production, supply and capacity development scenarios to support the growth ambitions of the Manufacture
  • Analyze risks, identify opportunities and make recommendations to support decision-making by different governance bodies
  • Contribute to strategic thinking related to Make or Buy choices and the evolution of internal and external industrial capabilities
  • Coordinate discussions with different stakeholders to ensure a common vision of priorities and action plans
  • To be a driving force in the evolution of planning practices, tools and processes to strengthen their robustness, effectiveness and sustainability
  • You have an engineering degree in Supply Chain, or equivalent, you have significant experience in an industrial planning function, S&OP or Supply Chain
  • You master the concepts of PIC/S&OP, capacity planning, load/capacity analysis
  • You have a strong understanding of Supply Chain processes in a complex industrial environment; CPIM APICS certification is an asset
  • You master Excel at an advanced level (Power Query / Pivot) as well as Business Intelligence tools such as Power BI
  • You are comfortable with LES and knowledge of an APS is an advantage
  • With a strong analytical and synthesis spirit, you can turn complex data into pragmatic and actionable recommendations
  • Recognized for your ability to federate and influence diverse interlocutors, you demonstrate excellent relational and communication skills
  • Organized, rigorous and autonomous, you are at ease in demanding environments where operational and strategic issues come together
