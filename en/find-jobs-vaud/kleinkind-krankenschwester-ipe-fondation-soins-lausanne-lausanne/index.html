Full description

Our Medical-Social Centres (CMS) offer the Lausanne population high-quality multidisciplinary services and their interventions are based on values of humanity and fairness. By joining the Lausanne Care Foundation, you are committed in a concrete way to promoting health and well-being in the canton of Vaud, within expert, motivated and enthusiastic teams, in a varied activity rich in human contacts. In the field, you intervene at the place of life or activity of the beneficiaries. You define with customers the solutions best suited to their situations and thus develop a fine knowledge and a privileged relationship of trust with them. As part of a team that is welded and supported, the exercise of these professions allows for a great autonomy but requires great responsibility and flexibility on the part of our employees. In order to complete our teams, La Fondation Soins Lausanne is looking for on 1 January 2027 or to agree one:

Small Child Nurse (IPE)

Activity rates and variable hours (approximately 20%-30% annualised) during the day and week Undetermined fixed-term contract Location : Lausanne and Lausanne crown

Your Mission

  • Promoting a family and social environment conducive to the optimal development of children from birth and pre-school age
  • Preventing psycho-affective disorders, diseases and accidents occurring at the beginning of life according to the IPE framework in the canton of Vaud
  • Provide quality services in accordance with ethical standards and collaborate with other professionals of the Early Childhood Health and Social Network

Your profile and skills

  • Bachelor of Nursing (or equivalent)
  • Demonstrated 3-year experience in pediatrics or early childhood
  • Strong interest in community health
  • Networking capacity
  • Responsibility
  • Ability to organize independently and set priorities
  • High availability in terms of hours, replacements also during school holidays
  • Master's degree in oral and written French
  • Good ease with computer tools and mobile telephony
  • Driving licence and vehicle

Our employer benefits

  • Working conditions and remuneration according to the Collective Labour Convention (CCT) in the public health sector in the Vaudois
  • Plan of integration by occupation, offers of rich and varied continuing training, opportunities for vocational development
  • Five weeks of vacation per year and six weeks from 50 years
  • Gender Equality ( Fair-ON-Pay Certification)
  • An autonomous activity, a multicultural, interdisciplinary and rich in human contacts
  • Soft mobility policy and participation in public transport costs
  • A multicultural context with beautiful encounters in perspective
  • Exciting work for children and families

Ms. Zimmermann, Regional Head Early Childhood Nurse, is at your disposal at 079 540 96 79 for any information. The Human Resources department will be happy to respond as soon as possible.
