Social Reference 70-100%, Lausanne and/or Geneva (m/f/d)

Caritas Switzerland manages a structure that organizes and coordinates foster placements. This organization, Caritas Family Placement, is certified and approved by different cantons, according to the Ordinance on the placement of children. Caritas Family placement is committed to supporting children in care and foster families according to the best professional standards possible.

Your tasks

  • Recruitment of new families, support of the assessment and authorisation process
  • Organization and facilitation of training days for host families in presentation and videoconferencing
  • Coordination of work and project management with the professional or social network surrounding young people in care
  • Support, counselling and coaching for foster families, children and young people in care
  • Administrative work and financial monitoring of budgets of minors and foster families
  • HES or Master's degree in Social Sciences or equivalent
  • Relationship, listening and ability to work with complex situations
  • Ability to act independently while working closely with a multidisciplinary team and the network
  • Minimum work experience of 2 to 3 years in the care of children and/or young people, ideally in the care of minors
  • Knowledge of the social environment in French-speaking Switzerland - Ancestor of sessions (intervision and team conference with host families)
  • Very good command of oral and written French
  • Knowledge of German a plus
  • Mastery of common computer tools
  • Availability a few Saturdays per year
  • Flexibility to intervene outside office hours on an ad hoc basis
  • Court case blank
  • Driving licence, a plus
