In order to strengthen its team of poolists in the Adult Psychiatry and Psychotherapy Service (SPPA), the Nant Foundation is seeking

Variable-rate caregiver THE MISSION The mission of the caretakers is to provide, on call, the care required for each patient in close collaboration with an interdisciplinary team. Called to intervene in adult hospital units, they have the characteristic of being particularly flexible.

WE OFFER

The Nant Foundation is a private institution recognized in the public interest, mandated by the State to organize and provide psychiatric care in the eastern Vaud region, from child psychiatry to psychogeriatry. Working within the foundation is evolving in a dynamic working environment at the heart of a defined institutional project. WE SEEK A nursing student wishing to gain experience in a psychiatric setting or a person holding an SWC of Community Care and Health Assistant (or equivalent). We are looking for a particularly adaptable and flexible person with the ability to be at the service of the patient, his colleagues and the institution. We are also looking for someone who has week and weekend availability. Finally we want to hire a person motivated by the Foundation's mission and loving the work as an interdisciplinary team.
