Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated service provider for the elderly and has about 4000 employees. Ambulatory and stationary offers merge perfectly. While Senevita sites are synonymous with autonomous shelter and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading private service provider of help and home care throughout Switzerland. In order to strengthen our care team on our website Senevita Casa Vaud, Lausanne sector, we are looking for:

One nurse/her HES 80%-100% Your tasks

  • Evaluations, advice and support to different clients
  • Health control nursing care
  • Coordination of interventions with the planning team
  • Hygiene and comfort care
  • Guidance and monitoring of auxiliary staff
  • Transmission of information to different stakeholders, updating of files
  • Interdisciplinary cooperation with health insurance funds, doctors and hospitals
  • Participation in symposia
  • One-time integration into the regional picket service

Your profile

  • HES nursing degree or equivalency with Red Cross recognition and right of practice
  • 2 years minimum experience required in home care, including knowledge of the Vaudois care network
  • Ability to organise independently and flexibly
  • Accountability
  • Experience in staff driving and management
  • Evidence of knowledge in the use of IAR
  • Digital skill (computer and mobile tools)
  • Oral and written knowledge of the French language
  • Holder of a driving licence and residence in the Lausanne region

Our offer

  • Interesting and stimulating activity
  • Collaboration and active support of your manager on a motivated team
  • Flexible working hours
  • A continuing training programme of the Senevita Academy
  • Provision of a function vehicle
  • Access to discounts on various items and services
