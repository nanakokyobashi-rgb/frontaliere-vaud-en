Full description

Assistant physician in general internal medicine, 50%-100% The Riviera-Chablais Hospital, Vaud-Valais (HRC), is a reference intercantonal hospital with over 2,000 collaborators. Rennaz Hospital has over 350 acute care beds. It is complemented by two Geriatric and Rehabilitation Clinics and two medical clinics. All employees invest on a daily basis to provide quality care for patients, embodying the institution's values of authenticity, exemplaryness and innovation. To complete the team of our medical services, in Monthey and Vevey, open 7 days a week, from 9 am to 7 pm, we put the position of: To participate in a team of 10 doctors, in the operation of our medical clinics. Within the permanence, you will work with a team of nurses and medical assistants. On site, a conventional radiology of the latest generation, an ultrasound apparatus, and a medical analysis laboratory will allow you to take initial care of patients in the best conditions. In case of need for additional specialized examinations, the permanence is a unit of the Riviera-Chablais Hospital, which offers the latest medical technologies. The main tasks of this post are:

  • Care for adult patients and sometimes, from 4 years of age, without appointment
  • Participate in the management and development of medical facilities
  • Supervising medical staff (assistant physicians and caregivers) and providing training (ISFM level B recognition) We ask
  • A federal medical diploma or diploma awarded by an EU Member State and recognised by the Federal Office of Public Health
  • An ISFM degree in general internal medicine and/or an emergency medicine AFC or equivalent recognized training (MEBEKO)
  • A wide experience is needed, especially in general ambulatory medicine, ideally in practice or in a medical clinic
  • An experience in a surgical discipline of at least 1 year is desired and an experience in paediatrics would be an asset We offer The HRC offers great job opportunities and career development in all its fields of activity: medical, nursing, medico-technical, hotel, administrative, technical, logistics. In a desire for continuous improvement, skills development is at the heart of the HRC's concerns. The institution offers its employees access to training both internally and externally. In 2021, the HRC was twice ranked among the best employers in French-speaking Switzerland by the magazine Bilan and the newspaper Le Temps , placing it as a leading institution.
