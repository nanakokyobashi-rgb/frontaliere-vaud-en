Full description

Profil

McDonald’s, the world’s largest restaurant chain, is an innovative and people-oriented company backed by a leading multinational brand. Currently, there are 190 McDonald’s restaurants in Switzerland, run by 46 independent entrepreneurs (franchisees). Because our business is growing fast, we are looking for talented people to strengthen our main offices that provide support to all restaurants. To complement its Development department based in Crissier, McDonald’s Switzerland is looking to fill the role of : Asset Management Consultant (80-100%) We are looking for an experienced professional to strengthen our Development department. In this key role, you will contribute to the strategic and operational management of our real estate portfolio in French‑speaking Switzerland. You will work closely with internal departments and external partners in a dynamic, business‑oriented environment. Key

  • Responsibilities

Implement the real estate strategy and actively contribute to its further development. Manage and develop the existing real estate portfolio across Switzerland. Oversee all administrative, operational, and contractual processes related to the properties. Serve as the main point of contact for property owners, management companies, authorities, and external partners. Manage the full lifecycle of leases and land‑use rights, including durations, renewals, rent adjustments, and negotiations. Draft, review, negotiate, and finalize real estate‑related contracts. Conduct tenant search activities and ensure optimal occupancy of available spaces. Prepare market analyses, reporting documentation, and internal presentations. Support the preparation of files requiring legal expertise related to real estate. Provide solid strategic recommendations to assist decision‑making at management level.

What You Need to Be Successful

Recognized education in real estate (Federal Diploma, professional certificate, or equivalent qualification). Several years of successful professional experience in Asset Management or real estate management. Strong knowledge of the Swiss real estate market, ideally with an active network in French‑speaking Switzerland. Languages: Native French speaker with perfect command of English. German is a strong additional asset. Proven ability to negotiate, draft, and review contracts with precision. Strong analytical skills and the ability to manage complex files independently and in a structured manner. Reliable, committed, flexible personality with a hands‑on, solution‑oriented mindset. Ability to thrive in a dynamic environment and manage multiple priorities simultaneously.

With us, you get the following benefits

Flexible working: the ability to work remotely to achieve the best possible balance between work and private life. Home Office is possible. Working hours are flexible in agreement with the leader, taking into account business and individual needs. Mobility benefit associated with this position: company car or SBB season ticket or a travel allowance. Bonus: a variable percentage based on performance and commercial results. Active talent management: targeted engagement in personal learning and skills development, on-site, via e-learning, or on the lively campus of Hamburger University in London, Paris, and Munich. Employee well-being: contributions to wellness activities. Attractive discounts in our restaurants and food for employees. Program packed with exclusive discounts on our partner platform, corporate benefits.
