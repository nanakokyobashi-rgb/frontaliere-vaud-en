Your role

As a drilling assistant, you will be able to use the drilling machine in different civil engineering sites, special foundations, geotechnical or special works. He actively participated in the proper implementation of the interventions, while ensuring compliance with safety standards and quality requirements.

Your main tasks are as follows

  • Assist the driller in the preparation, installation and disassembly of drilling equipment.
  • Participate in assembly, dismantling and ordinary maintenance of machines and tools.
  • Resupply the construction site of the material and consumables necessary for operations.
  • Manipulate rods, pipes, sheaths and other drilling accessories.
  • Collaborate with the construction of perforations according to the instructions of the foreman.
  • Perform the cleaning and maintenance of the equipment and the work area.
  • Participate in loading and unloading of equipment.
  • Strictly comply with safety instructions, internal procedures and applicable rules.
  • Effectively collaborate with the construction teams to ensure the proper performance of the works.
  • Be versatile and provide support in different activities related to special jobs when necessary.
  • And now we're talking about you?
  • You have gained a first positive experience as a drilling assistant, specialized crankcase or special work, civil engineering or foundations.
  • Feel comfortable with physical work and appreciate the environment of the yards.
  • Show motivation, rigour and an excellent team spirit.
  • You can follow the drilling instructions and work in compliance with safety standards.
  • You are able to work outdoors, regardless of weather conditions.
  • You are punctual, reliable and demonstrate a good ability to adapt.
  • You are autonomous in the tasks entrusted to you, while knowing how to collaborate effectively with the different operators of the yard.
  • The B license and a vehicle are highly appreciated.
  • An experience in the field of micropals, anchoring rods, injections, support systems, Berlin walls or other special works is a real advantage.
  • Safety training courses (SUVA, harnesses, altitude work, etc.) are an advantage.

Interested? Do you love and master your job and want to put your skills in the service of projects that really matter? Then this offer is for you. From us, your know-how makes a difference and directly contributes to the success of the yards.
