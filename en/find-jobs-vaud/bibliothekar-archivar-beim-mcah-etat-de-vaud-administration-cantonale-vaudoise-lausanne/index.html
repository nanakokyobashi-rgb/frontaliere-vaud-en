Full description

As a librarian-archivist, you play an essential role in the Cantonal Museum of Archaeology and History (MCAH). Your mission is to ensure the management, development and exploitation of documentary resources, while guaranteeing a quality service to users and partners.

  • Be responsible for the design and implementation of MCAH's Archives Management Policy.
  • Ensure responsibility, operation and service to library users (cataloging, coding, equipment, storage, conservation, inventories).
  • Ensure the development of physical and electronic collections according to the needs identified by scientists and ensure the ordering and receipt of acquisitions and donations.
  • Collaborate in documentary research to facilitate mediation, communication and/or performance of exhibitions.
  • Participate in the supervision of auxiliary/trainee staff, maintain relationships with all partners and represent the library in business sessions and seminars. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Mr Lionel Pernet, director of the Cantonal Museum of Archaeology and History, by tel. at 021 316 34 30.
  • Master in Information Sciences.
  • Further training in archives management.
  • Experience of minimum 5 years as a librarian or archivist, ideally acquired in the cultural field.
  • Good mastery of Alma SIGB, GED software and common office tools.
  • Interest in archaeology, history and ethnography.
  • English level B1 and German level B1, an asset.
  • Decision-making and determination.
  • Organization and time management.
  • Capacity for analysis and synthesis.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
