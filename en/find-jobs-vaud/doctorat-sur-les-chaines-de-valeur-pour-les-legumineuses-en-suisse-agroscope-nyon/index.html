Put to the Point

Legumes have great potential to diversify farming systems, reduce the use of nitrogen fertilisers and promote a more sustainable diet. Despite growing interest, cultivation in Switzerland is still limited. Most of the legumes consumed are imported. Building local value chains faces numerous challenges, from production to processing and marketing. Against this background, the federally supported project “Filigrane” aims to promote the development of Swiss value chains for legumes. It combines the evaluation of varieties adapted to Swiss conditions with the optimisation of cultivation methods, the design of value chain organisation scenarios and the study of processing and consumption factors. The dissertation is part of this project and is jointly supervised by Agroscope and INRAE (UMR Agronomie) under the direction of Marie-Hélène Jeuffroy.

Additional information

The research group Cultivation Technology and Varieties Agriculture develops sustainable production techniques as well as new varieties adapted to climate change for the main crops, which are grown according to different production methods (integrated production, labels or organic farming).
