Full description

As part of our medical team, you will play a key role in the reception and administrative care of our patients:

  • Management of appointments for different medical imaging examinations (radiography, MRI, scanner, ultrasound, mammography...)
  • Administrative preparation and organization of patient records prior to examinations
  • Verification of medical requirements and coordination with radiologists and radiologists
  • Management of patient consents and information on the conduct of examinations
  • Seizure and archiving of imaging results, secure transmission to treating physicians
  • Monitoring of radiology-specific protocols and procedures (safety, radiation protection, confidentiality)
  • Coordination with laboratories and ancillary services for combined examinations
  • Reception and support of patients, including those in need of special support (old or disabled)
  • Diploma of essential medical secretary
  • Confirmed experience of medical secretary, ideally in radiology
  • Required English minimum B1
  • Mastery of medical terminology and computer tools
  • Self-help and collaboration
  • Dynamics, responsiveness, proactivity and speed of execution
  • Meaning of contact
  • Very good stress management and ability to work as a team
  • Flexibility
  • Excellent presentation
  • The opportunity to join a renowned clinic at the cutting edge of development
  • Motivating activity in a pleasant work environment
  • A corporate culture oriented towards excellence and the quality of human relations

We pay great attention to the diversity of life paths of our collaborators. Our positions are open to both women and men.

With strong expertise, the Genolier Clinic is distinguished by its experienced and qualified team and its state-of-the-art medical equipment.

The Genolier Clinic offers a wide range of services covering cardiology, neurology, oncology, orthopaedic surgery and plastic surgery. In addition, the clinic provides its patients with a wide range of prevention and rehabilitation services to promote their health and well-being.

The reputation of the Genolier Clinic is based on its excellent medical infrastructure and cutting-edge technologies. These include a state-of-the-art diagnostic centre, a high-performance intensive care unit and an operating room equipped with the latest generation instruments and devices to ensure optimal quality care.
