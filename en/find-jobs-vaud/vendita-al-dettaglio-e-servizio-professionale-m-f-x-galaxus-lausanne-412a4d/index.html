Full description

For our Lausanne store, we are looking for an energetic, flexible and committed person, either right away or to agree. It is a versatile retail position combining customer advice, sales and operational management. You support customers, manage orders and contribute to the proper functioning of the store, while handling customer requests in back office.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Shop Lausanne, Rue du Grand-Pré 2B, 1007 Lausanne

Important remarks

All applications are welcome, but only online applications can be considered. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • First contact point for our customers: removal of goods, order of products or return of items that are not suitable
  • Seizure and tracking of orders, so that each person leaves with exactly what they expected
  • Customised advice in store or accompaniment on our online shop, up to the perfect product
  • Resupply of shelves, impeccable showroom and transfers of goods on a daily basis – the shop runs thanks to you
  • Back-office processing: behind-the-scenes too, no requests remain unanswered

Required

  • 1 to 3 years: in retail trade (preferably in multimedia)
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC): Retail trade
  • Understanding the workings of online sales and passion for digital shopping
  • Fascination for our assortment, cold blood in rush moments and engagement when needed
  • Actions and decisions aligned with your personal beliefs and values
  • Creativity and initiative to solve problems and achieve your goals
  • French (Very good mastery)
  • English (Very good knowledge)
