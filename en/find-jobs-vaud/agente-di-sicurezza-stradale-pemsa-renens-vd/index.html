Your assignment

  • Ensure the safety and regulation of traffic near the yards and the working areas.
  • Ensure the safety of road users, pedestrians and construction teams.
  • Install and monitor provisional signs in accordance with current directives.
  • Adjust traffic and facilitate the entry and exit of construction vehicles.
  • Address users and ensure smooth and secure circulation.
  • Watching compliance with safety standards on site.

And now we're talking about you?

  • It is essential that you have followed Swiss training as a road safety officer.
  • Ideally, you have already gained a first experience in the road safety sector or in the yards in Switzerland.
  • You know road safety standards and the principles of temporary signage.
  • You are careful, responsive and able to handle unexpected situations.
  • Prove punctuality, reliability and a great sense of responsibility.
  • You like to work outdoors and you can work independently.
  • Master the French and communicate easily with users and yard teams.
