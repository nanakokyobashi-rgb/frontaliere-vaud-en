Full description

The Valais Hospital is looking for the Central Hospital Institute for the ICH Riviera-Chablais laboratory:

Medical secretary with blood training 60%-100%

Mission

  • Perform blood samples from outpatients
  • Ensure the management, treatment and administrative follow-up of laboratory samples
  • Manage pre-analytical and sample compliance
  • Participate in the day-to-day management of the secretariat, including telephone reception and mail processing

Profile

You benefit from the diploma of medical secretary or diploma deemed equivalent as well as from the training of blood taking (mandatory). You are flexible and comfortable with variable working hours during the day, distributed from Monday to Friday according to the needs of the service. You appreciate teamwork while being able to work independently. You are organized, versatile and know how to manage priorities. You have an excellent sense of hospitality, communication and confidentiality.
