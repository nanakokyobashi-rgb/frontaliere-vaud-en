Full description

Vaudoise is one of the leading Swiss insurance companies and an attractive and forward-looking employer. The Vaudoise creates a healthy, inclusive and inspiring working environment in which you can develop. To strengthen our Product Management Precautions department, which offers our customers high-quality services in private pensions (pillar 3a), we are looking for a Product Manager / Product Manager Precaution that actively contributes to the development, optimization and performance of our life insurance solutions and this in a cooperative and innovation-oriented environment. The following exciting tasks await you:

  • Control of the life cycle of precautionary products, from idea to market launch and further development
  • Analysis of portfolio performance (market, customers, competition) and derivation of optimization measures
  • Implementation of product development and development projects in cooperation with specialist departments, IT, marketing and sales
  • Participation in the digitization of products and customer processes
  • Development of documents for the launch, training and communication for internal stakeholders
  • Support for product partners This distinguishes you:
  • They have 5 years of experience in product management and/or project management, ideally in the field of precautionary or life insurance
  • Very good French and German knowledge (orally and in writing at least C1) ideally supplemented by English knowledge
  • Higher education (higher education, university or equivalent) in business, insurance, finance, management or comparable
  • Good understanding of product-related challenges (market, regulation, pricing, profitability) as well as safe handling of cross-departmental methods and tools (e.g. design thinking, Scrum, Jira, Confluence, etc.) They are also analytical, structured and solution-oriented. They like to work in the team, take responsibility and advance projects in a challenging and constantly developing environment. We offer you:
  • A pleasant working environment in a cooperatively shaped company that has been chosen to be the best employer several times
  • Numerous social benefits and employee benefits, including flexibility in the organisation of work
  • Support for your further development and further training
  • A dynamic and passionate team We look forward to your complete application documents electronically (with CV, letter of motivation, copy of the certificates and diplomas).
