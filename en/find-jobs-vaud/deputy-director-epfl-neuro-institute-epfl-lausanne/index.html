Full description

Mission

EPFL Neuro Institute is a new transversal institute bringing together the communities of neuroscience, neuro-engineering and artificial intelligence applied to the brain within EPFL. In order to support its development and support its scientific, strategic and institutional ambitions, we are looking for an assistant to the Director.

A true right-hand arm of the Director, you contribute to strategic leadership, operational and budgetary management and the Institute's outreach. You play a central role in the coordination of structuring projects, the development of academic and institutional partnerships, the search for funding and the facilitation of the scientific community.

Main tasks and responsibilities

  • Support the Director in the strategic and operational management of EPFL Neuro Institute.
  • Contribute to the definition and implementation of the Institute's strategy and coordinate cross-cutting projects and key partnerships.
  • Supervise budgetary management, financial monitoring and participate in funding research activities.
  • Coordinate administrative activities and ensure the proper functioning of the Institute at several sites.
  • Lead the Institute team and promote effective collaboration with central services and internal and external partners.
  • Lead the organization of scientific and institutional events and the Institute's communication activities.
  • Coordinate academic affairs, including monitoring recruitment, affiliations and training programmes.
  • Represent the Institute and the Director to the various stakeholders when necessary

Profile

  • Master's degree in a relevant scientific field
  • Additional management training is an asset.
  • Experience in operating, financial and piloting complex projects.
  • Good understanding of academic environments, research and their funding mechanisms.
  • Leadership, organizational sense and ability to coordinate multiple stakeholders.
  • Excellent relational, communication and institutional representation skills.
  • Ability to work independently on strategic and confidential issues.
  • Mastery of French and English, both oral and written

We offer

At EPFL, we are convinced that everyone can accomplish great things when he or she evolves in a caring, respectful and successful environment. By joining our institution, you will help shape the future of society through education, research and innovation.

We offer an inspiring, international and collaborative work environment where your contribution has a concrete impact and where your ideas are valued.
