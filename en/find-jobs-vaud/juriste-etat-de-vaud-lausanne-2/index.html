Full description

Within the Directorate-General for the Environment (DGE), several units provide coordination, administrative and logistical support. The Legal Affairs Unit (JUR) is responsible for both advisory, expert and legal oversight functions, while representing the department or department in court to defend its interests. It also plays a key role in the strategic and cross-cutting coordination of certain issues. As a lawyer in the DGE-JUR, you have a wide range of legal and technical tasks related to the activities of the Directorate-General for the Environment. The expected main missions are summarized below:

  • Providing advice and resolving legal issues
  • Assume instruction of administrative procedures, coordination of technical aspects with specialists and the drafting of decisions in the areas of activities of the DGE
  • Assume the management and treatment of redress procedures in the areas of activities of the CEO
  • Ensure legal oversight and updating of reference documents (tables, templates, processes, etc.)
  • Participate in and contribute to the development of the EDG-JUR
  • Substitute or replace legal colleagues For more details, you can refer to the specifications by accessing the attached link. For more technical questions, please contact Ms. Carine Chafik Meylan, Head of DGE-JUR, at 021 316 91 13.
  • Master in Swiss law; internship or lawyer's certificate or similar or relevant further training
  • Professional practice confirmed in transplantation, as a lawyer or as a lawyer; good control of public law, environmental law and administrative procedure
  • Master's degree in German and/or Italian, plus
  • Organizational sense, good pressure resistance, rigor and editorial accuracy
  • Adaptability, flexibility, self-help and collaboration
  • Autonomy and sense of initiative, analytical and synthesis capacity
  • Public service and discretion In case of interest, please send us your file in a single PDF document (including cover letter), for reasons of document management. The position is in CSD with extension opportunities.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
