Full description

Location : Orbe, Switzerland

Entity : Nespresso Production Centre Rate : Full time 100%

Reimagin what coffee can be. Imagine what you can become.

As a Senior Automation Specialist, you will pilot large-scale automation projects, from concept to start-up and performance achievement. A reference expert for the automation technologies in your area, you will guarantee the reliability of the installations, upgrade our standards and accompany the increase in skills of team automatians. You will also participate actively in the digitization of the factory.

How will you have an impact as Senior Specialist Automation?

  • Coordinate external suppliers and commissioning on production lines
  • Develop new features on equipment to improve line performance and ergonomics
  • Carrying out continuous improvement and obsolescence management projects
  • Intervention on complex troubleshooting
  • Provide operational support to the production, engineering and quality departments
  • Maintain technical documentation and share good practices

What will make you successful

  • Diploma in automation (or equivalent) and experience confirmed as an automation specialist in the industrial environment
  • Very good control of automatic programming, ideally in the Rockwell/Allen-Bradley environment
  • Very good knowledge of supervisory systems (InTouch, ArchestrA)
  • Easy reading of electrical diagrams and basic knowledge of server and network architectures
  • Knowledge in instrumentation, industrial vision and ESM are assets
  • Ability to convey your knowledge, coach and support change, with rigour and initiative
  • Incarnate WE CAN and The Nestlé Way values on a daily basis: Collaboration rather than Consensus, Courage rather than Comfort, Speed rather than Perfection and Learning beyond Knowledge.

  • Business restaurant on site with menus at subsidized prices
  • Free parking and charging stations for electric cars
  • Many advantages at the Nespresso Club
