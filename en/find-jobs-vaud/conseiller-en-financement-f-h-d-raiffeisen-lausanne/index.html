11.11.2026 Lausanne

Be part of our team!

"We are looking for a personality that embodies our values of trust, audacity, respect, commitment and community spirit, and that wishes to contribute actively to the sustainable development of our bank and its region. Collaborative spirit and leadership are key in the relationships that drive us."

What are you waiting for?

  • a key role at the heart of the customer relationship: you conduct real estate financing interviews, in collaboration with customer advisors and advisors, and support our clients in their projects
  • tailor-made financing solutions: you structure solutions adapted to each client's projects and financial situation in accordance with our credit policy
  • a contribution to commercial development: you identify customer needs beyond financing and offer, with our specialists in investments, foresight and insurance, comprehensive and personalized support
  • long-term support: you ensure proactive monitoring of financing, anticipate deadlines and ensure the satisfaction of our clients
  • various real estate valuations: you make estimates of properties per floor, villas and performance objects
  • Cross-cutting collaboration: you coordinate the processing of applications with the teams involved and ensure smooth, rigorous and effective follow-up.

What are you bringing?

  • relevant banking or higher training (University, High School or equivalent)
  • at least five years of experience in consulting and financing real estate with private clients, with strong customer orientation and sensitivity to business development
  • a comprehensive view of customer needs and the ability to identify opportunities in the areas of provisioning, investment and insurance in collaboration with the specialists concerned
  • real relational ease, a taste for advice and the ability to build lasting relationships of trust
  • an analytical and critical mindset, coupled with a capacity to make clear recommendations and propose solutions tailored to each situation
  • of autonomy, rigour and an excellent sense of organization
  • a strong spirit of collaboration and the pleasure of working on multidisciplinary teams
  • you are mobile and enjoy collaborating with the teams of our different sites
  • a good knowledge of the regional real estate market.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed.
