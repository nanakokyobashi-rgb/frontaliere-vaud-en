Your role

As an electrician installer, it works in new construction and renovation. Participates in the complete realization of the electrical systems, from the preparation of the yard to the controls and commissioning.

Your responsibilities

Making electrical installations according to the projects, technical instructions and established deadlines

  • Pose sheaths, pipes and boxes before the jet of the suns and laying the partition walls
  • Shooting, marking and organizing cables
  • Connect sockets, switches, paintings and electrical equipment
  • Install devices, security systems and control devices
  • Perform controls, tests and commissions
  • Diagnose faults and perform necessary repair operations
  • Participate in preventive and corrective maintenance work
  • Respect applicable standards, especially NIBT, as well as safety rules
  • Effectively collaborate with other professionals present at the construction site

And now we're talking about you? You have a federal capacity certificate (CFC) as an electrical installer or equivalent training recognized in Switzerland. You have at least two years of experience in Switzerland in a similar job. Autonomous, meticulous and organized, you can read the projects and the schematics well and you like to work on the team.

Your profile

  • CFC of electrical installer or equivalent training
  • At least two years of experience in Switzerland
  • Good mastery of new electrical installations and in the process of restructuring
  • Ability to read and interpret projects and schemes
  • Autonomy, rigour and sense of responsibility
  • Good team spirit
  • Statistics with computer tools for monitoring interventions
  • Valid driving licence
