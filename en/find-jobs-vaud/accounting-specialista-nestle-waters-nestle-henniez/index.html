Be a force for good and accelerate change that improves lives and the planet

As an Accounting Specialist at Nestlé Waters, your mission is to manage and analyse financial information, ensure accurate accounting and reporting, and support effective business decision-making. You’ll make a difference by collaborating closely with Finance and Control colleagues, internal business teams and external stakeholders. This is your opportunity to deliver meaningful results that unlock the future of beverages and experiences that elevate everyday living.

How you’ll make an impact as an Accounting Specialist

  • Delivering accurate and timely accounting, financial analysis and reporting across your areas of responsibility.
  • Driving monthly closing activities, journal entries and balance sheet reviews to maintain reliable financial records.
  • Preparing transparent forecasts of the overheads and analysing performance against budgets to support informed business decisions.
  • Ensuring strong governance, internal controls and compliance through audit support and accurate statutory declarations.
  • Managing treasury information, current accounts, asset records and master data to strengthen financial visibility and control.
  • Coaching and supporting the Finance apprentice while collaborating closely with Finance colleagues and cost owners to translate financial data into meaningful business insights.

What will make you successful

  • Bringing very good knowledge of accounting and controlling principles, Swiss accounting standards and month-end closing processes.
  • Applying good knowledge of controlling principles, financial analysis, budgeting and forecasting.
  • Using SAP and Microsoft Office tools to support accounting, reporting and financial performance management activities.
  • Collaborating proactively with internal stakeholders while demonstrating autonomy, accuracy and strong attention to detail.
  • Communicating fluently in French and English
  • German is an advantage.
  • Bringing to life our values of care, grit, connection and ingenuity, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.
