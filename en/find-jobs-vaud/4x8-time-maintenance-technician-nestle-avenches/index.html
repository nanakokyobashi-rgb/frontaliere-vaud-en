Full description

Location : Avenches, Switzerland Entity: Nestlé Nespresso Manufacturing SA Production Centre Rate : Full time 100% Type of contract: Undetermined contract

Reimagin what coffee can be. Reimagin what you can become.

Within our 4x8 maintenance team in Avenches, you contribute directly to the availability, reliability and performance of our production equipment. You perform preventive, corrective and improved maintenance activities to ensure business continuity. Thanks to your technical skills and analytical capacity, you diagnose malfunctions, identify their causes and implement durable solutions. You are also involved in developing the team's knowledge by clearly documenting your interventions, drafting technical instructions and accompanying technical operators in the use and control of equipment.

How will you have an impact as a 4x8 Maintenance Technician?

  • Perform diagnostics and solve failures in various technical fields: mechanical, electrical, pneumatic, automation and instrumentation.
  • Identify the root causes of malfunctions and propose reliable and sustainable technical solutions.
  • To contribute to improving the safety, quality and compliance of facilities.
  • Ensure clear and structured traceability of your interventions and ensure the quality of technical data.
  • Use digital maintenance and intervention management tools including SAP, POKA and TrendMiner.
  • Share your knowledge and support the development of the skills of operators and your colleagues.
  • Identify and implement improvements that contribute to industrial performance, cost reduction and plant sustainability.
  • Work closely with production teams, operators, day maintenance teams and external providers.

What Will Make Your Success

  • You have technical training in the field of industrial maintenance (CFC in the fields of electrotechnical, automation or equivalent training).
  • You have a successful experience in a manufacturing industrial environment.
  • You have practical experience in the diagnosis, troubleshooting and maintenance of mechanical, electrical, pneumatic and automated equipment.
  • You hold ITTO electrical certification (Article 13 or 15), or are willing to obtain it as part of your function.
  • You have a good analytical spirit and a structured approach to problem solving.
  • You are autonomous, organized and able to manage your priorities according to operational needs.
  • You are proactive and powerful.
  • You communicate clearly and adapt your message to your different people.
