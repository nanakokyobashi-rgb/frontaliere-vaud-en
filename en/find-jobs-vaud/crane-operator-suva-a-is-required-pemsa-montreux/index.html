Your assignment

  • Drive and maneuver a clinging crane in the building sites.
  • Safely carry out lifting and moving loads.
  • Ensure the precise handling and positioning of materials and building elements.
  • Strictly collaborate with ground teams, machine operators and leaders.
  • Check machine stability and lifting conditions before each operation.
  • Check the operating status of the vehicle and report any anomalies.
  • Participate in the installation and positioning of the crane according to the needs of the yard.
  • Strictly respect the safety standards and SUVA directives in force.

And now we're talking about you?

  • In possession of the SUVA category A crane license, mandatory and valid in Switzerland.
  • Experienced in the conduction of clinging cranes.
  • Good mastery of lifting and handling operations.
  • Ability to work with precision, autonomy and rigor.
  • Reliable, responsible and punctual person.
  • Good team spirit and strict compliance with safety standards.
  • Driving license and vehicle: an advantage.
  • Immediate availability
