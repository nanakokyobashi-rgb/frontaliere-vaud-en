Full description

Occupancy rate: 80–100%

Helps us shape Switzerland's energy infrastructure. As a Senior Builder, you take responsibility for demanding infrastructure projects and actively bring your experience, sense of quality and commitment to the team.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • You direct and coordinate complex assembly work on high voltage installations (AIS/GIS) in voltage levels from 50 kV to 380 kV.
  • You independently perform mechanical and electrical assembly work and ensure the assembly and commissioning of high voltage equipment in accordance with the rules of art.
  • You support project and construction management thanks to your technical expertise and actively propose solutions and improvements.
  • You accompany the team members, share your knowledge and encourage the transfer of know-how.
  • You ensure strict compliance with safety rules at work and participate in risk assessments and the implementation of safety measures.
  • You work closely with internal and external partners and communicate solutions with different interfaces.

Profile & Requirements

  • You have several years of experience in mounting high voltage electrical installations and a solid technical understanding of AIS and GIS systems.
  • You have a CFC in the electrotechnical field
  • Further training is an asset.
  • You work autonomously, organized and enjoy taking responsibility in demanding technical situations.
  • You support the team members technically and develop solutions.
  • You are willing to travel to Switzerland and work flexibly on different sites.
  • You communicate easily in French, you have knowledge in German and ideally in English.

Even if you do not meet all the requirements, we look forward to receiving your application.
