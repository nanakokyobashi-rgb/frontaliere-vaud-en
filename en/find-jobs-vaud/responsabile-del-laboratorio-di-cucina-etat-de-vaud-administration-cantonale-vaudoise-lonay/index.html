Full description

In order to strengthen the current team, we are looking for a kitchen workshop manager for a fixed period of three months. As a kitchen workshop manager at the Tuilerie Prison, you reinforce the existing team and accompany women in their daily work. You pass on your professional knowledge to them, enabling them to develop new skills and thus help them to reintegrate. Through your commitment, you contribute to the protection of the population by providing supervision and supervision of detainees. You act at all times in accordance with our values: "Loyality, Solidarity, Credibility".

  • Train and mentor detainees in the activities of the cooking workshop in order to acquire new social and professional skills.
  • Ensure the preparation of the two meal services for detainees and employees while ensuring hygiene and safety standards and the maintenance and proper functioning of the equipment.
  • Ensure the security and supervision of detainees working in the kitchen, ensure the maintenance of discipline.
  • Develop behavioural reports and progress of supervised persons.
  • Work with stakeholders in the various sectors of the institution (social, security, professional, etc.) to ensure adequate care for detainees. Three-month fixed-term employment contract, possibly renewable. For further information, please consult the terms of reference by clicking here or contact Mr. Julien Sonney, Head of Workshops in Detention at 021 557 45 78.
