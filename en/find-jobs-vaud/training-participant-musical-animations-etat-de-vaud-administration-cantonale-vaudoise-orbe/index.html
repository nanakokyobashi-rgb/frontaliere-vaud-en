Role overview

Would you like to participate in our mission to reintegrate detainees?

Are you passionate about teaching and teaching?

As an animator of musical activities within the Prison of La Croisée, you will design and conduct music classes with detained persons.

  • Would you like to participate in our mission to reintegrate detainees?
  • Are you passionate about teaching and teaching?

Application process

  • Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.

Contacts

  • For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Mr Sylvain Schlatter, head of the socio-educational sector, at 024 557 67 49.
  • Auxiliary contract per hour (about 16h/week), for one year, potentially renewable.
  • Pay according to hourly schedule, quarterly payment for trips.
  • Experience in the animation of educational musical activities and in the use of musical media enabling people to develop in new constructive projects.
  • Ability to manage individuals with individual and group difficulties in a closed environment.
  • Your open mind, good communication skills and strong emotional mastery allow you to cope with tense situations, and listen, while keeping the distance necessary for your mission.
  • Good mastery of common computer tools and software related to office automation.
  • Knowledge of one or more foreign languages, an asset.
  • Our terms and conditions of employment require you to be a Swiss citizen or holder of a licence C, free of a deed of default of property (an extract from the Office of Prosecutions must be presented during the process), and your criminal record must be blank.
  • There is a right to consult the criminal record under the Federal Criminal Records Act by the engagement authority for this function.
