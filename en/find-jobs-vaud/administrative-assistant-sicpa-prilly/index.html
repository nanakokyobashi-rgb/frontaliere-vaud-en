Full description

For our Commercial Department, we are seeking a highly organized, proactive, and service-oriented:

Administrative Assistant (ful-time, permanent)

ROLE

  • Manage complex international travel arrangements, coordinating itineraries and optimizing schedules in line with colleagues’ agendas
  • Coordinate visa applications and other travel-related administrative requirements, ensuring all necessary documentation is prepared and submitted in a timely manner
  • Coordinate on-site visits for customers and prospects, ensuring seamless logistics, including agendas, meeting rooms, accommodation, transportation, and restaurant bookings
  • Ensure a smooth and professional visitor experience from arrival to departure
  • Manage calendars autonomously, coordinating schedules, prioritizing requests, and anticipating potential conflicts
  • Organize internal and external meetings, conference calls, and workshops
  • Prepare meeting agendas, coordinate required materials, and support follow-up actions
  • Prepare and reconcile travel and expense reports accurately and in a timely manner
  • Provide day-to-day administrative support to multiple teams
  • Manage purchase requests and invoices
  • Prepare documents, presentations, and reports using Microsoft Office tools
  • Maintain accurate records and ensure effective document organization

PROFILE

  • Proven experience as an Administrative Assistant supporting multiple teams or stakeholders, ideally within an international environment
  • Excellent organizational and time-management skills, with strong attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple priorities
  • Proactive and service-oriented, with the ability to anticipate needs, take ownership, and work autonomously
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with a professional demeanor
  • Flexible and adaptable, with the ability to remain calm and effective in a fast-paced environment
  • High level of confidentiality, discretion, and reliability
  • Hands-on, solution-oriented mindset with strong initiative
  • Strong proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Outlook, Excel, Word, and PowerPoint)
  • Experience with SAP is an advantage
  • Knowledge of business etiquette and hospitality best practices is a plus
  • Fluent in French and English; any additional language is an asset
  • Positive attitude and strong team spirit
