Full description

THE PSYCHIATRIAL RESEARCH HNV LIAISON SERVICE

MAIN ACTIVITIES

In hospitals

  • Advise, support, assist liaison psychiatry therapists in complex hospital care around patient orientation in the intra- and extra-hospital care path
  • Work in coherence, in conjunction with and in accordance with the missions of each care provider to prepare the person for departure (stays B, C, other accommodation facilities) towards better adapted care facilities
  • Actively collaborate with BRIO teams
  • Inform and train team members on legislative or social developments and the socio-educational sector

Outpatient

  • Upon request of the therapists in charge of the patient followed in ambulatory context, assist the clinician in developing a social action plan with the person taking into account his resources, respecting the care project and within the limits of institutional constraints

More generally

  • Provide an interface between the person and the Liaison Psychiatry Service, other Hospital Services or different institutions
  • Participate in ongoing quality improvement within the institution, in the development of Service projects
  • Adapt its activity to the changing needs of the Service and the Institution

SKILLS – REQUIREMENTS REQUIRED

  • Bachelor's degree from a recognized social school of type HES (social work profile) or university degree deemed equivalent.
  • Knowledge of the psychiatric environment and the cantonal Vaudian institutional network
  • Ability to work independently
  • Share great flexibility in the organisation of working time (consultations, multidisciplinary and networking work, mobility)
  • Knowledge of common computer tools
  • Strong interest in multidisciplinary team work (psy-psy; psy-med. and networked
  • Compulsory driving licence and vehicle available for travel from one site to another

WE OFFER

  • A varied activity at several sites (eHnv/HIB/EHC)
  • Continuing training opportunities
  • A salary according to the scale of the Vaudian Hospital Federation (FHV)
  • Excellent social benefits

DATE ENTRY: 1 MAY 2027
