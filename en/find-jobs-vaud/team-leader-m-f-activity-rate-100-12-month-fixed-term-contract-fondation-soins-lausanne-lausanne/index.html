Full description

Our Medical-Social Centres (CMS) offer the Lausanne population high quality multidisciplinary services and their interventions are based on values of humanity and fairness.

As part of an absence from illness and in order to complete our team at the CMS of Chailly-Sallaz, The Lausanne Care Foundation is looking for a continuation or to agree, for a fixed period of 12 months, a-e:

Team Leader (M/F) Activity rate: 100% 12-month fixed-term contract

Your main missions

  • Manage, support and support a multi-disciplinary team of about a dozen employees
  • Ensure an operational organisation that allows favourable working conditions and high quality home maintenance in accordance with institutional practices and standards
  • Actively collaborate in developing an agile, positive and caring work environment
  • Contribute to fair and efficient support and services to CMS clients in accordance with the values, practices and programs defined by AVASAD and the Foundation
  • Participate in the development of innovative team projects to respond to the evolution of home care health policy
  • Working closely with the Executive Committee by promoting communication flows and continuous improvement

Your profile and skills

  • Bachelor's degree in nursing (or equivalent), completed with advanced management training ideally
  • Experience in employee management or significant professional experience that demonstrates your ability to assume managerial responsibilities an asset
  • In-depth knowledge of the socio-sanitary field of Vaud
  • Willingness to engage in a developing CMS
  • Authorization to practice or ready to initiate the proceedings - conditional on the undertaking

Your assets

  • A management style oriented towards collaboration and proximity
  • Participatory leadership that helps you motivate your teams
  • Excellent communication capabilities that facilitate the cohesion of your team and its understanding of internal and external issues
  • Public service, commitment and reliability are essential values for you

We offer

  • Working conditions and remuneration according to the Collective Labour Convention (CCT) in the public health sector in the Vaudois
  • Conditions of occupational security which extend beyond the minimum benefits provided by law
  • Five weeks of vacation per year and six weeks from 50 years
  • Modern and efficient work tools
  • Pay equity between women and men ( Fair-ON-Pay certification)

Start-up: as soon as possible or to be agreed Mrs. Charlotte Schröder, Human Resources, is at your disposal at 021 651 30 60 for information. For quality processing of your application, please send a complete application file via Jobup until 6 December 2026. The Human Resources department will be happy to respond as soon as possible.
