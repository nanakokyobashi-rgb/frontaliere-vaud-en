Full description

Due to the size of its collections, the Cantonal and University Library – Lausanne (BCUL) is one of the largest libraries in Switzerland. A public institution with a heritage, cultural and academic vocation, the Cantonal and University Library – Lausanne (BCUL) is dependent on the Directorate-General for Culture (DGC). At the heart of access to culture and information, you play an essential role for users of the Riponne site. In order to guarantee the quality of their reception, facilitate their orientation and ensure the fluidity of our public service, we are looking for professionals who are rigorous and passionate about librarianship.

  • To ensure the reception and registration of users.
  • Manage loan and return of documents and provide other services to users.
  • Provide service in closed stores and free access.
  • Perform various specific tasks.
  • Ensure its own continuing training. For further information, please consult the Terms of Reference by clicking here or contact Charlotte Grisard, Head of Riponne, by telephone at 021 316 78 62.
  • SWC d'Agent·e in documentary information or training deemed equivalent.
  • 3 years' experience in the field of books or public services.
  • Mastery of the loan software and the usual office tools required.
  • Taste, convenience and courtesy in contact with the public
  • Interest in books and new technologies (robots, reading machines, etc.)
  • Oral knowledge of a foreign language required.
  • Ability to work as a team and independently.
  • A spirit of mutual help and collaboration.
  • Accurate, timely and dynamic.
  • Fixed working days to be determined.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
