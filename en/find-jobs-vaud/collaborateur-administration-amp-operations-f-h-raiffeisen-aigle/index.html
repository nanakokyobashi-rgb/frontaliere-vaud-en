Banque Raiffeisen Alpes Riviera Chablais Vaudois, a fast-growing establishment well established at the local level, is looking for an administrative and operational partner (m/f) for its agency in Eagle.

What are you waiting for?

  • Support for estate files and wards.
  • Administrative management of foresight.
  • Trafficking in domestic and international payments, including the processing of SWIFT investigations.
  • Follow-up of contactless and disinherent relationships.
  • Processing and administrative follow-up of various client operations in support of advisors.
  • Participation in the administrative functioning of the service (document management, controls and support tasks).

What are you bringing?

  • Experience confirmed in the areas of succession, foresight or payment traffic. Competencies in one of these areas could be a determining criterion for the position.
  • SWC business employee or training deemed equivalent, ideally minimum 3 years banking experience in Switzerland.
  • Interest in contacts with internal and external clients, strong sense of service and excellent relational skills.
  • Self-reliant, rigorous, structured and conscientious, with a high capacity for learning.
