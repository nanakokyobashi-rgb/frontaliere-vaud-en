Full description

To strengthen our DIGITAL R&I department, we are looking for a:

Senior Cloud Platform Engineer

We are looking for a Senior Cloud Platform Engineer who is passionate about cloud-native technologies, infrastructure automation, and platform engineering. The ideal candidate combines strong technical expertise with a collaborative mindset and enjoys designing, delivering, and operating modern cloud platforms that empower development teams and business solutions.

Your Responsibilities

  • Architect, develop and operate cloud platform capabilities and core infrastructure services.
  • Contribute to the definition and execution of the platform's technical and functional roadmap.
  • Design and implement Infrastructure-as-Code solutions to enable consistent, scalable, and automated deployments across cloud and on-premises environments.
  • Build, maintain and optimize CI/CD platforms, pipelines and automation frameworks supporting software delivery at scale.
  • Design and implement cloud operational best practices, including monitoring, observability, disaster recovery, and operational resilience.
  • Develop and maintain platform services, automation tooling, and operational capabilities using modern software engineering practices.
  • Apply software engineering best practices, including automated testing, peer code reviews, documentation, and continuous improvement.
  • Collaborate closely with software engineers, DevOps engineers, architects, and product teams to improve platform adoption and developer experience.
  • Participate actively in Agile ceremonies, platform planning, technical discussions, and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Support the deployment and operation of cloud-native platforms, data services, and infrastructure components.

Your Profile

  • BS/MS degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related technical field.
  • Proven experience as a Cloud Platform Engineer, Platform Engineer, DevOps Engineer, or equivalent role.
  • Strong experience deploying and operating Kubernetes-based container platforms.
  • Experience with one or more major public cloud providers such as Azure, AWS, or equivalent cloud technologies.
  • Strong programming and automation skills in Python and shell scripting.
  • Ability to understand, troubleshoot and collaborate on solutions developed in languages such as Java, Kotlin, Go, Groovy or similar.
  • Experience building and maintaining container images and Kubernetes ecosystem components, including Helm and Operators.
  • Experience designing and operating CI/CD platforms, workflows, and deployment automation.
  • Strong hands-on experience with Infrastructure-as-Code and configuration management technologies such as Terraform, Terragrunt, CloudFormation, and Ansible.
  • Experience operating cloud-native data and messaging platforms such as Kafka, PostgreSQL, or similar technologies.
  • Solid understanding of Linux systems, networking fundamentals, and distributed systems concepts.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities with the ability to work independently while maintaining strong collaboration across teams.
  • Familiarity with Agile methodologies and modern software delivery practices.
  • Fluent in English
  • French is a plus.
