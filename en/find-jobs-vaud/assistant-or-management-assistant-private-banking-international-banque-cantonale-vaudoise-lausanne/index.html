What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Put your managerial ambitions at the service of a healthy and sustainable performance by joining the canton of Vaud's first universal bank, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Strategy and Organization department, we are looking for one or more:

Assistant or Management Assistant Private Banking International Your main missions:

  • actively support advisors in client portfolio management and development
  • ensuring the correct application of the defined rules for international private customers
  • propose wealth management solutions to optimize the profitability of customer portfolios
  • ensure the proper execution of instructions sent by customers and ensure administrative follow-up
  • offer an optimal reception in the context of telephone contacts with customers
  • coordinate the various administrative aspects related to the optimal functioning of the team Your profile:
  • banking experience in a similar position
  • relational ease, organizational rigour and orientation solutions
  • good organization, sense of responsibility, accuracy and autonomy
  • mastery of common computer tools
  • team spirit, sense of initiative and priorities
  • perfect mastery of French, very good knowledge of English (level B2).

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
