Role overview

Integrated into a three-person team of Internal Audit IT of the Edmond de Rothschild Group's Internal Audit IT, you are responsible for independent audit missions relating to the various IT (Information Technology) environments of the EdR Group's activities (mainly Private Banking and Asset Management) in Switzerland and abroad.

These missions may either be dedicated to the IT domain or involve collaboration with the general IT component.

In this context, you prepare reports identifying the findings, making recommendations and ensuring the proper implementation of the action plans associated with them.
