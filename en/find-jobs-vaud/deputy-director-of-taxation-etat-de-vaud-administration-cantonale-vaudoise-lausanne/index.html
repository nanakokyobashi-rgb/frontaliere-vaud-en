Full description

Missions

  • Lead strategic projects and cantonal tax reforms in partnership with the Director General.
  • Advise the General Management on tax, financial, institutional, and political issues related to ongoing reforms.
  • Contribute to defining the vision and strategic orientations of the DGF, and ensure the substitution of the Director General.
  • Supervise the legislative affairs of the DGF, conduct legislative work in tax matters, and ensure the preparation and follow-up of bills and regulations.
  • Ensure the coherence and legal security of Vaud's tax practice and support DGF units in handling complex, new, or sensitive tax issues.
  • Coordinate responses to parliamentary interventions, ensure follow-up on relations with the Grand Council, and participate in preparing files for the Council of State.
  • Represent the DGF before cantonal and federal tax administrations, participate in the work of the Swiss Tax Conference (CSI), and develop a professional network in relevant fields.
  • Support the transformation and modernization of the tax administration, promoting a process-oriented logic, the digitalization of services, and continuous improvement of operational methods.
  • Profile sought
  • University degree in law, lawyer's certificate, diploma as a tax expert or expert from the Swiss Tax Conference (CSI).
  • LL.M. in taxation or a doctorate in tax law is an asset.
  • Additional training in management or public administration.
  • Proven professional experience in the field of Swiss taxation, including leading or supporting tax policy projects and legislative reforms, within a Swiss tax administration or an institutional environment closely linked to tax authorities.
  • Excellent command of Swiss tax law and its national and international developments.
  • Global and strategic vision, excellent analytical, synthesis, and writing skills, strong decision-making ability, sense of responsibility, and public service ethics.
  • Excellent interpersonal, negotiation, and representation skills, leadership, and a strong sense for cross-functional collaborations.
  • Proficiency in French as well as very good knowledge of German and English.

  • Position within the Vaud Cantonal Administration (State of Vaud), Switzerland.
