Full description

Riviera-Chablais Hospital, Vaud-Valais (HRC), is an intercantonal reference hospital, which brings together more than 2,500 employees. The Rennaz Hospital Center has more than 350 acute care beds. It is complemented by two geriatrics and rehabilitation clinics as well as two medical hotlines. All employees work hard every day to provide quality care to patients, embodying the values ​​of the institution: authenticity, exemplarity and innovation.

In order to complete our physiotherapy team on the Monthey site, we are looking for an indefinite period for a:

Physiotherapist M/F between 60 and 80%, with experience in the field of geriatric rehabilitation.

The main missions of this position are

  • Ensure the quality and continuity of inpatient therapies in geriatrics and rehabilitation clinics

• Actively participate in the organization of work and the smooth running of department activities.

  • Collaborate in the supervision and evaluation of students and new collaborators
  • Contribute to an interdisciplinary approach to care and teamwork
  • Promote and promote physiotherapy within and outside the institution

We ask

  • A Bachelor of Science HES-SO in physiotherapy or a title deemed equivalent
  • Full recognition from the Swiss Red Cross for foreign titles (files without recognition will not be considered).
  • A major interest in geriatric rehabilitation.
  • A modern and evolving vision of physiotherapy in a hospital environment, with an interest in the development of practices.
  • Good interpersonal skills, a collaborative spirit and the desire to work within a multidisciplinary team
  • Good knowledge of IT tools

We offer

  • A pleasant working environment, with recent infrastructure and a varied technical platform, promoting active support.
  • An experienced, dynamic and caring team, conducive to knowledge sharing and mutual assistance.
  • Daily interdisciplinary work, in contact with numerous therapeutic, medical and nursing specialties.
  • An establishment in constant evolution, with numerous projects in development.

Information can be obtained from Mr François Letoucque, Physiotherapist head of unit, 41 79 891 22 34

Only complete files with Swiss Red Cross recognition for foreign diplomas will be processed.

Application deadline: September 30, 2026
