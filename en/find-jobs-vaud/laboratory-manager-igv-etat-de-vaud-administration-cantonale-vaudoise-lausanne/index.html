Role overview

Attached to the Directorate of Veterinary Affairs and Inspectorate, the Galli-Valerio Institute (IGV) is a central role in the fight against epizootic diseases and in the epidemiological surveillance of animal diseases.

Its main tasks include the diagnosis of notifiable animal diseases, the conduct of analyses related to food safety, and diagnostic activities concerning other non-epizootic animal diseases.

As an official laboratory, it works closely with the cantonal and federal authorities, as well as veterinary professionals, to ensure a rigorous and responsive health watch in the face of emerging issues.
