Located in the panorama of the Riviera since 1905, the Valmont Clinic offers personalized rehabilitation stays for people with neurological, orthopaedic and back damage, in an exceptional setting, facing Lake Geneva and the Alps. Today, it has become a flagship rehabilitation facility, due to its unparalleled therapeutic efficiency, competent teams and state-of-the-art technical platform. In fact, doctors in rehabilitation collaborate with a team of multidisciplinary experts, composed of physiotherapists, occupational therapists, neuropsychologists, logopedists, sports masters and dietician. In 2006, the clinic joined Swiss Medical Network, one of the two main Swiss groups of private clinics.
