Head of Algorithmic Trading Governance •

Olten, CH | Lausanne, CH - 100% | Full time

About the Role

Alpiq believes that algorithmic trading and automation will play an increasingly important role in the future of energy trading. While traders remain fully accountable for commercial decisions, risk-taking, and portfolio performance, algorithms are expected to become a core capability supporting execution, optimization, analysis, and decision-making across the trading value chain.

The Head of Algorithmic Trading Governance is responsible for establishing, governing, and scaling Alpiq's algorithmic trading framework across Asset Trading, Merchant Trading, and Structured Energy Management activities.

The role does not manage a proprietary trading book. Instead, it acts as the business owner and governance lead for algorithmic trading initiatives, ensuring consistency, transparency, performance measurement, risk oversight, and adoption across all trading activities.

Initially focused on intraday and day-ahead algorithmic initiatives supporting Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) and Renewable Energy Sources (RES), the mandate will progressively expand to additional portfolios, including hydro assets and future algorithmic trading applications across Alpiq's European trading activities.

The position sits within the Quantitative Analytics team and operates at the intersection of Trading, IT, Risk Management, Compliance, and Data & Analytics.

Your main accountabilities

1.Algorithmic Trading Governance

  • Establish and maintain Alpiq's governance framework for algorithmic trading across all trading value chains.
  • Develop standards, controls, validation processes, approval mechanisms, and documentation requirements for algorithm deployment.
  • Ensure clear accountability principles whereby traders remain responsible for trading decisions and portfolio outcomes while leveraging algorithmic tools.
  • Work closely with Risk and Compliance to develop robust controls around algorithm usage and automated execution activities.
  • Define model governance and review processes covering algorithm development, testing, approval, monitoring, modification, and retirement.
  1. Portfolio-Wide Algo Coordination & Standardization
  • Create a consolidated overview of all existing algorithmic initiatives across Trading.
  • Assess and document desk-level developments currently operating within Intraday, Day-Ahead, BESS, RES, and other Gas Merchant trading activities for Long term.
  • Identify duplication, inconsistencies, and improvement opportunities.
  • Drive harmonization of algorithmic approaches across portfolios while preserving local optimization needs.
  • Support the extension of successful approaches to additional portfolios, including Hydro and future flexibility assets.
  1. Algo Performance Management
  • Develop a framework of KPIs and performance metrics for algorithm assessment.
  • Monitor algorithm effectiveness under different market environments and liquidity conditions.
  • Conduct regular performance reviews and recommend improvements, recalibration, suspension, or retirement of underperforming algorithms.
  • Capture and document lessons learned to support future strategy development and knowledge sharing.
  • Establish transparency on value creation and operational performance of algorithmic initiatives.
  1. Trading Community Enablement
  • Lead the adoption of algorithmic trading capabilities across the Trading organization.
  • Develop training programs, best practices, user documentation, and onboarding materials.
  • Promote a culture where traders understand, monitor, challenge, and effectively utilize algorithmic trading solutions.
  • Build an algorithm-enabled trading organization where algorithmic tools become part of every trader’s standard toolkit. The ambition is that within two years, all Alpiq traders are proficient in the use, supervision, and interpretation of algorithmic trading solutions, while retaining full accountability for trading decisions, risk-taking, and portfolio performance.
  • Establish a community of practice that fosters knowledge sharing between traders, quants, analysts, developers, Risk, and IT.
  • Drive change management and user engagement to accelerate the adoption of automation across Trading's value chains.
  1. Strategic Development of Trading Automation
  • Define the long-term roadmap for algorithmic trading and automation across Alpiq.
  • Evaluate opportunities to expand the use of algorithms beyond execution into analytics, opportunity identification, decision support, forecasting, and idea generation.
  • Assess internal versus external development opportunities and support the integration of third-party solutions where appropriate.
  • Maintain oversight of external providers and strategic partnerships supporting algorithmic capabilities.
  1. Product Ownership and Business Sponsorship
  • Act as “Business Sponsor” for strategic algorithmic trading initiatives and transformation projects (4PP).
  • Partner closely with Business Technology Transformation and IT including Data teams to ensure developments align and build on Alpiq's broader data transformation roadmap.
  • Define business priorities and oversee implementation roadmaps for algorithmic trading platforms and supporting infrastructure.
  • Ensure investment decisions and budgets are aligned with business value creation and strategic objectives.
  • Support the launch and governance of Alpiq's dedicated algorithmic trading program starting in 2027.
  1. Cross-Functional Collaboration
  • Maintain strong engagement with trading teams in Lausanne and Olten. Algo team shall sit among Traders.
  • Work closely with Quantitative Analytics, Risk Management, Compliance, IT, Data Engineering, and Trading leadership.
  • Facilitate alignment between business needs, technical capabilities, and governance requirements.
  • Promote consistency of algorithmic trading practices across all functions and locations.
