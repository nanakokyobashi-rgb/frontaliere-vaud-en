Full description

Thanks to its 800 collaborators, the Broye Intercantonal Hospital (HIB) is responsible for acute care and rehabilitation. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the broyard population. In order to meet the many challenges ahead, HIB is putting the position of:

Nurse Head of Care Units for the Medical Service at 80 to 100%

Your Mission

The medical service has 40 hospital beds and is organized in two sectors. Its mission is to provide clinical care for all adult patients in the Broye region who require in-house medical care. The service works closely with intensive care, emergency care and the RTC of Estavayer-le-Lac. The healthcare team is composed of about 40 epts under the supervision of two ICUS.

Your responsibilities will include

  • Ensure proper care for patients in the units for which you are responsible, using a patient-family-centred approach
  • Leadership of staff
  • Lead and support the team to promote its development and reflexive practice
  • Planning, organizing, coordinating and monitoring the implementation of units' activities
  • Provide team coaching focused on clinical support by ensuring good practice monitoring
  • Promote and practice interprofessional collaboration
  • Participate in the application and control of care and security measures
  • Managing and running the various symposia
  • Planning and conducting staff appraisal interviews
  • Ensure good transmission of up, down and horizontal information with all partners
  • Initiate and conduct practice analyses in the unit to improve the quality of care, patient care and work processes.

Your assets

With a HES degree in nursing, you have a minimum of 3 years' experience in hospital care. An experience in surgery and a proven experience in management are assets. You also have Level 1 management training or are ready to take it. With a spirit of initiative and common sense of responsibility, you have excellent analytical, synthesis, adaptation and communication capabilities. Of an open nature, you are strong for proposals and have a strong interest in collaborating in a multidisciplinary way and with the health network. Excellent command of French (spoken and written), as well as computer tools are essential. Entry into office: 1 September 2026 or to be agreed
