Full description

Senevita is the largest integrated service provider for the elderly and has about 4000 employees. Ambulatory and stationary offers merge perfectly. While Senevita sites are synonymous with autonomous shelter and comprehensive care, Senevita Casa is the leading private service provider of help and home care throughout Switzerland. To strengthen our Senevita Casa Vaud team, we are looking for several: CRS health auxiliaries in hourly contract Regions: La Riviera, La Côte et Lausanne Your tasks

  • Relieving and supporting our customers in their daily lives and in the domestic economy
  • Provide basic care according to the individual needs of our clients
  • Accompany clients to the doctor, for shopping or for walks
  • Ensure nighttime stays and watches
  • Contribute to respectful, caring and secure care

Your profile

  • Training of the Swiss Red Cross as a caregiver
  • Professional experience in long-term care or home care (an asset)
  • Stress resistance, autonomy and flexibility
  • Good ability to use digital tools (tablet, mobile phone)
  • You live in canton Vaud
  • Having a driving licence and a car is an advantage

Our offer

  • A varied activity adaptable to your occupancy rate (%) and availability
  • The possibility of working in a very autonomous way, while enjoying the support of an experienced and caring team
  • Free access to the extensive continuing training programme of the Senevita Academy
  • Various discounts (gasoline, supplementary insurance, fitness, pharmacy, etc.)
