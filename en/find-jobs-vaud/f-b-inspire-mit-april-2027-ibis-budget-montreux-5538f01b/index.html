Hotel overview: Fairmont Le Montreux Palace

Located on the shores of Lake Geneva and overlooking the magnificent Alps, Fairmont Le Montreux Palace is a distinguished hotel establishment, a member of the Accor Group, part of the Fairmont brand and affiliated with Swiss Deluxe Hotels. Boasting an incomparable worldwide reputation, 236 luxurious rooms and suites, and one of the finest dining experiences, Fairmont Le Montreux Palace is truly an enchanting place. Its six restaurants and bars offer a wide range of delights to satisfy even the most discerning palates. Le Montreux Jazz Café is the must-visit destination for experiencing the Festival's rhythm 365 days a year in the enchanting setting of Fairmont Le Montreux Palace, Lemantine by Alan Geaam, featuring the cuisine of the French-Lebanese Michelin-starred chef who has created the menu for our new pop-up restaurant for the summer season, and Funky Claude’s Bar is the undisputed place to end up late into the night to enjoy an incredible line-up of traditional and exclusive cocktails to the rhythm of the best live music scene in town. Fairmont Le Montreux Palace also specializes in hosting conferences, meetings and seminars. Fairmont Spa is a 2000 m² wellness space dedicated to fitness, relaxation and beauty.

Job description

INSPIRE F&B - MANAGEMENT TRAINING 18 months M/F – Spring 2027

The Inspire programme is designed for promising hospitality talent, offering a unique opportunity to immerse yourself in the heart of hotel operations and train alongside the industry's top professionals. As an Inspire F&B, you will benefit from an immersive and diverse experience within our iconic restaurants, bars and banquets, discovering all facets of Food & Beverage in a prestigious setting.

This programme will enable you to develop your skills, gain a global perspective on luxury industry standards and actively contribute to delivering special and unique experiences to our guests.

If you aspire to an exceptional career in luxury hospitality, the Inspire programme is the stepping stone you need!

General responsibilities

  • Continuously provide professional, friendly and engaging service
  • Establish and maintain effective relationships with employees, monitor staffing levels and schedules
  • Organize employee schedules
  • Implement all necessary actions to deliver high-quality, upscale service
  • Be fully conversant with the menu offering, its garnishes, ingredients and preparation methods, and master its promotion
  • Handle guest complaints and report them to your immediate supervisor
  • Lead pre-service briefings and maintain close ties with the Head of Department
  • Monitor inventory for daily use in restaurants and bars to ensure service levels
  • Lead by example and apply LQA technical and emotional standards
  • Be committed to the quality of your service, discretion and complete honesty
  • Rigorously comply with hygiene and sanitary requirements
  • Comply with ergonomic work standards in accordance with health and safety regulations
  • Follow the department's policies, procedures and service standards
  • Follow all safety policies
  • All other tasks as assigned

Specific responsibilities

F&B rotation through all food and beverage outlets

Banquet Supervisor

  • Supervise the service team in collaboration with the Banquet Supervisors
  • Personally attend to guests and their requests during the organization of banquets, breakfast and brunch
  • Participate in all required administrative tasks, including, but not limited to: scheduling, opening/closing tasks, cleaning and maintenance checklists
  • Coordinate staff training
  • Be present on the floor and lead by example as a warm and professional host

Montreux Jazz Café Supervisor

  • Supervise the service team in collaboration with the Assistant Manager of Montreux Jazz Café
  • Personally attend to guests (reservations, seating, taking orders, cashier duties if necessary) and ensure that colleagues under your supervision serve them efficiently
  • Participate in all required administrative tasks, including, but not limited to: scheduling, opening/closing tasks, cleaning and maintenance checklists, employee action plans, employee evaluations, recruitment
  • Coordinate staff training (responsible for role-playing exercises)
  • Be present on the floor and lead by example as a warm and professional host

Funky Claude's Bar Supervisor

  • Supervise the service team in collaboration with the Funky Claude’s Bar Supervisor
  • Organize the service plan and station plans
  • Participate in beverage inventory
  • Participate in all required administrative tasks, including, but not limited to: scheduling, opening/closing tasks, cleaning and maintenance checklists, employee action plans, employee evaluations, recruitment
  • Coordinate staff training
  • Participate in regular cleaning inspections with the manager of the Funky Claude's Bar to ensure compliance with health and safety standards, as well as hotel cleanliness

In Room Dining & Pureaty Café Supervisor

  • Supervise the In Room Dining team in collaboration with the In Room Dining Supervisor
  • Participate in department management and be responsible for it in the supervisor's absence
  • Be familiar with the operation of room service, VIP equipment and Pureaty Café
  • Ensure cleanliness and hygiene measures are maintained for the trays, trolleys, equipment and premises of IRD and Pureaty Café
  • Be fully conversant with the menu offering, its garnishes, ingredients and preparation methods, and master its promotion

Terrasse du Petit Palais - Supervisor

  • Personally attend to guests (reservations, seating, taking orders, farewells, cashier duties if necessary) and ensure that colleagues under your supervision serve guests efficiently and promptly
  • Participate in beverage inventory
  • Participate in all required administrative tasks, including, but not limited to: scheduling, opening/closing tasks, cleaning and maintenance checklists, employee action plans, employee evaluations, recruitment
  • Coordinate staff training
  • Participate in regular cleaning inspections with the manager of the Terrasse du Petit Palais to ensure compliance with health and safety standards, as well as hotel cleanliness

Observation and support in F&B administration by the F&B Coordinator / F&B Director.

  • Be introduced to the various budgets associated with the F&B department
  • Manage pressure and urgent requests from other departments regarding inventory and budget management
  • Gain exposure to the logistics and organization of the various F&B departments
  • Be in contact with suppliers and service providers
  • Understand the department's dynamics and schedule colleagues effectively
  • Effectively plan and prepare the hotel and department for future audits.

Qualifications

  • Graduated from a recognized hospitality school or university within the last two years
  • Minimum of 1 year of experience in food and beverage service
  • Minimum of 6 months' experience in a supervisory role
  • Experience in a 4or 5 hotel is an asset
  • Fluent in spoken and written French and English
  • Flexibility, adaptability and ability to work under stress
  • Very good communication skills
  • Flexible and available for various schedules
  • Team-oriented
  • Customer-service-oriented
  • Attention to detail
  • Organized
  • Comfortable with presentations and training
  • Leadership
