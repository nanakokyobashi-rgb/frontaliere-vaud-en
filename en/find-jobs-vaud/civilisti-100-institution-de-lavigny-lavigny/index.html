Full description

Deployed across several sites, the Institution de Lavigny consists of a neurological hospital with outpatient consultations on two sites, a housing department for adults with intellectual disabilities or physical problems, a special school and socio-professional workshops. We are looking for: Civil Servants, 100% Job Description We welcome several civil servants each year and are happy to contribute to the development of new skills as well as enriching experiences.   • Kitchen and Cafeteria Assistant

  • Technical Assistant
  • Cleaning Services Assistant
  • Support to the Socio-Professional Master
  • Adapted Physical Activity Intervenor

  • Socio-Cultural Animation / Day Care Assistant
