Full description

Your missions, under the direct responsibility of the operational director:

  • Coordinate the maintenance team in charge of scientific equipment, infrastructure and IT equipment.
  • Organize maintenance and updates and ensure daily monitoring for air conditioning, media distribution and recovery.
  • Provide expert expertise in troubleshooting, maintenance, updating and construction.
  • Establish, train and monitor procedures to ensure the safety of persons and property. Main tasks and responsibilities
  • Be responsible for the proper functioning of the platform equipment by ensuring its maintenance, as well as repairs and troubleshooting as soon as possible.
  • Establish a coherent platform development plan with the appropriate installation of the equipment taking into account the specific specifications of each equipment.
  • Coordinate and participate in the interventions, including with the support of the engineers responsible for the equipment, with the various central services of EPFL and with the external companies involved in the site.
  • Implement the necessary tools to assess the performance of equipment and infrastructure.
  • Follow-up of periodic inspections of infrastructure, equipment and clean rooms.
  • Participate in the evaluation of new equipment.
  • Define specifications when creating new premises.
  • Plan in detail and actively manage the installation of new equipment and the dismantling of old equipment.
  • Provide technical support to other EPFL laboratories. Profile
  • To benefit from industrial experience in semiconductors and controlled clean rooms.
  • Demonstrate capacity to manage a team and coordinate contractors.
  • Have good knowledge of mechanics, electronics and optics (draping).
  • Have computer skills and communication interfaces.
  • Be able to manage the use of chemicals and their waste.
  • Have skills in vacuum techniques.
  • Experience in handling active gas cylinders.
  • Meaning of dialogue and collaboration within multidisciplinary teams.
  • Good knowledge in English (oral and written).
  • A good understanding of the processes used in MicroNanoManufacturing would be an advantage.
  • Rigor, accuracy and organization. We offer
  • A friendly and stimulating working atmosphere.
  • A quality working environment.
  • Continuing training.
  • Work on industrial equipment in an evolving ISO5 clase room. Information Start date: to be agreed Occupancy rate 100% Type of contract: CSD Duration: 1 year renewable Further information: https://cmi.epfl.ch/
