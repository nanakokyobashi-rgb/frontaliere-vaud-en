Your assignment

  • Perform several traditional masonry works in construction and renovation sites;
  • Building walls, partitions, facades and masonry works;
  • Place bricks, concrete blocks, concrete blocks and other building materials;
  • Perform mortar, sealing, sealing and finishing;
  • Participate in the realization of foundations, floors, masses and small concrete works;
  • Perform concrete-cutting, armor and casting works according to the requirements of the construction site;
  • Realize renovation, transformation and restoration of existing works;
  • Perform the restoration work of the masonry and the necessary repairs;
  • Read and interpret technical projects and documents;
  • Perform finishing work in order to ensure a clean and quality result;
  • Use the tools and equipment necessary for work properly;
  • Respect safety standards and construction procedures;
  • Working in collaboration with the team leader and the various operators;
  • Keep the material and the working area clean and in good condition.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Consolidated experience as a traditional bricklayer;
  • Good mastery of traditional masonry techniques;
  • Experience in laying bricks, concrete blocks, agglomerated blocks and other building materials;
  • Good knowledge of crates, armor and concrete casting;
  • Experience in new constructions, renovations or transformations;
  • Ability to read and understand building projects;
  • Mastery of tools and equipment commonly used in yards;
  • A formation or a federal certificate of capacity (AFC) as a bricklayer is an advantage;
  • Good physical form and attitude to outdoor work;
  • Autonomous, meticulous, precise and versatile profile;
  • Ability to work effectively both alone and on team;
  • Strong respect for safety standards;
  • Sericity, reliability and punctuality;
  • Driving licence, advantage;
  • Immediate availability.
