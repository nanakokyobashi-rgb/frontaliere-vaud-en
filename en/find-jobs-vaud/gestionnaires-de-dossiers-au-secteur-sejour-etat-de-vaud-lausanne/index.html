Full description

Are you rigorous, organized, autonomous and have a sense of public service? Join the area of residence as a case manager to ensure the administrative follow-up of asylum seekers, provisionally admitted and refugees assigned to the canton of Vaud. You contribute to the smooth running of their administrative journey by ensuring that files are kept strictly and that the information is reliable, in accordance with the legal framework in force. In direct contact with the people concerned, you welcome them, inform them and accompany them with professionalism, clarity and respect.

  • Processing the files of asylum-seekers, provisionally admitted persons, refugees and persons in temporary protection on an autonomous basis by classifying and managing the monitoring of documents relating to them in accordance with the legislation on residence and the right of asylum
  • Process key administrative procedures related to permits, including grants, address changes and other changes in status
  • Processing related claims and benefits, including travel documents, emergency assistance, birth registration, readmission and income-related claims
  • Contributing to the operational functioning of the sector, including reception at the counter, telephone centre, opening and monitoring of cases and processing of the mailbox
  • Updating data on assigned files in data management information systems
  • At the request of the Division's management, perform specific tasks related to the proper functioning of the sector. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Diego Stöcklin, Sector Manager at 021/316.46.30.
  • SWC or equivalent
  • Professional experience of 5 years min. in administrative and case management
  • Knowledge of asylum and foreign law, good general culture and interest in monitoring global political and economic developments
  • Good control of computer tools
  • Intergent and diplomacy, autonomy and responsibility
  • Stress resistance, ability to work under stress
  • Ability to communicate in English, other languages welcome Fixed-term contract of one year, renewable. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- You're attracted to this description? After a first selection on file, the first interviews will take place on 9, 13 and 16 October.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
