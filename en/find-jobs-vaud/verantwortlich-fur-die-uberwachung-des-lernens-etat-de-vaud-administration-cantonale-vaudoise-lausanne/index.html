Full description

The Directorate-General for Post-compulsory Education (DGEP) is responsible for training between the end of compulsory education and higher education. It offers training in 16 gymnasiums and 13 vocational schools and has more than 3,400 employees. In order to strengthen the Office for Vocational and Continuing Training (OFPC), we are looking for a person responsible for monitoring apprenticeship.

  • Manage the procedure for issuing and renewing apprentice training permits (standards and individuals).
  • Respond to partners (Professional Commissioners, Apprentices Advisors, Learning Contract Parties) on topics related to learning monitoring.
  • Collaborate in the training of procedures in the event of withdrawal/refusal of authorization to train apprentices.
  • Promote learning to non-training enterprises.
  • Work as a professional commissioner-replacement. For further information, you can consult the specifications here or call Ms Fabienne Dorthe, head of pole at 021 316 63 72.
  • SWC or equivalent.
  • Training related to vocational training (training courses for trainers in enterprises, training modules of the Federal Patent of Specialist in Vocational Training or Adult Trainer or title deemed useful equivalent to the activity).
  • Driver's license and car.
  • Prior professional experience in a similar field of 3 years.
  • Good knowledge of vocational training and related legal provisions.
  • Excellent sense of organization, ability to manage multiple activities in parallel, autonomy.
  • Excellent office knowledge.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
