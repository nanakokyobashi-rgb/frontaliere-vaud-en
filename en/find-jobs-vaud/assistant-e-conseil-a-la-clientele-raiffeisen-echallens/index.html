21.10.2026 Echallens

Be part of our team!

Join a bank that places the human at the heart of its values! Strongly established in Gros-de-Vaud, the Banque Raiffeisen du Gros-de-Vaud continues its development through its agencies in Echallens, Thierrens, Le Mont-sur-Lausanne and Prilly. In order to strengthen its team, we are looking for a customer advisory assistant

What are you waiting for?

At the heart of the advisory activity, you support customer advisors in their activities and help offer a quality experience to our clients.

Your main missions

  • Provide various monitoring and advisory activities and various administrative tasks for a range of clients.
  • Provide administrative and organizational support to client advisors in their day-to-day operations.
  • Support advisors in preparing and following up on their appointments.
  • Prepare and monitor contractual and regulatory documentation.
  • Ensure quality and compliance of records.
  • Process customer requests efficiently and professionally.
  • Identify business opportunities and pass them on to relevant specialists.
  • Work closely with the bank's various teams to ensure efficient and smooth processing of requests.

What are you bringing?

  • An EFA for a bank business employee or equivalent training.
  • An experience in the Swiss banking sector.
  • Good knowledge of banking products and regulatory requirements.
  • An ease with the usual computer tools (Microsoft Office).
  • An excellent sense of organization and rigour.
  • A positive mindset, oriented solutions and service.
  • The taste of teamwork and collaboration.
  • The desire to invest in a bank close to its customers and its region.

We work in a friendly environment

Co-operative values are our pillars. Together. For each other. They shape how we work together and behave toward each other. The culture of mentoring promotes personal proximity and exchanges on an equal footing at all levels of function.

We live in diversity and equal opportunities

We encourage diversity of perspectives, experiences and skills within our teams. This diversity makes us rich and makes a decisive contribution to our success.

We encourage personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is an integral part of our culture. We create a space for the professional and personal development of each individual and support our employees in a targeted manner, through a wide range of in-house training and participation in external development costs.
