Full description

The LEB (Lausanne–Echallens–Bercher) is a key player in the mobility of Gros-de-Vaud, which links the countryside and the city in a sustainable way and is evolving at the pace of a developing region.

At the dawn of a new stage with integration in Movodis as early as January 2027, new perspectives opened up: ambitious projects, a changing environment and many opportunities for change. We are looking for talents ready to engage in this adventure, within a human, demanding and moving enterprise.

Want to move the lines with us?

Become our future

Your Mission

You accompany the LEB project leaders in the day-to-day management of their activities.

You take care of the administrative tasks related to the projects: mail processing, verbatim reporting, billing monitoring, support for contractual management and coordination of information among the various stakeholders.

Self-employed and organized, you also provide one-time support to unit assistants.

You also ensure the administrative follow-up of works notices and support the technical management.

To carry out these missions, you master Microsoft Office tools and ideally have SAP knowledge as well as a strong interest in technical and rail environments.

Your profile

You have an SWC of a commercial employee and have more than three years of experience in assisting a technical directorate, ideally in a study office, an architectural office or a similar environment.

You master Microsoft Office tools.

You can organize your priorities and carry out several tasks at the same time.

Your relationship and sense of service make you a reliable and appreciated partner.
