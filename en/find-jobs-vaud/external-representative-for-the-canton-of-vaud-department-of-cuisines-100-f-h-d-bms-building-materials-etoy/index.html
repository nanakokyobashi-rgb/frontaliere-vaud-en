Full description

BMS Building Materials Switzerland is a wholesaler of construction materials for the large and second work. Our group of companies includes renowned specialists such as Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz, Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced specialists advise, support and inspire with great commitment the customers of the construction sector and private customers at 114 sites throughout Switzerland. Close to the daily construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Fast, simple and competent.

To strengthen our team for the canton of Vaud, we are looking for a person who loves the sale from 1st October 2026

Missions

  • Monitor real estate clients and proactively acquire new clients

  • Analyze and implement offers
  • Follow its construction sites, ensure professional appointments (measurement)
  • Analyze results and market in order to put in place action plans for sales development
  • Participate in various Events and events in the region to develop its network

  • Mastering Kitchen Drawing Software (Winner)
  • Good mastery of English required
  • Creativity, curiosity, dynamism and business excellence are among your assets

We offer
