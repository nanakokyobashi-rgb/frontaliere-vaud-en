Full description

Encourage reading and training students at the school to seek information by implementing educational activities in the school library, in conjunction with the PER (Plan d'études romand).

  • Planning and management of activities necessary for the proper functioning of the Villeneuve Haut-Lac school library.
  • Establishment and implementation of educational activities for students from 1H to 11H in relation to the PER defined with the school management and within the framework of the Library Commission, according to the recommendations and standards for school libraries.
  • Promotion of literacy skills of students.
  • Training students in literature research at the library, online catalogue and on the Internet.
  • Management of the documentary policy according to the School's missions, in collaboration with the school management and the Library Commission.
  • Documentary acquisitions, promotion and animation of the library, in consultation with the Library Commission.
  • Management and monitoring of tasks delegated to the AID. Special condition: Specific organization of working time over school periods. For more information, please consult the specifications by clicking HERE . You have questions, Ms. Muriel Ansermoz, Director of Villeneuve Haut-Lac Primary and Secondary School, 026 557 35 00.
  • Bachelor HES specialist in documentary information.
  • Further training in the teaching field: CAS librarian in school.
  • Experience in contact with students aged 4 to 16.
  • 4 year professional practice as a librarian-documentalist.
  • Mastery of library techniques, standards and tools.
  • Mastery of computer tools and document management systems.
  • Knowledge of ICTs and their use in documentary methodology.
  • Excellent general culture, intellectual curiosity, knowledge and interest in youth literature.
  • Interest in teaching and working with the teaching staff.
