Host Manager (100%) – CDI

Location : Vevey Commencement : As soon as possible

What you bring to our mission

Within the administrative branch located in Vevey, you direct, respond, filter, the requests of the beneficiaries as well as those of internal/external partners by telephone, physically at reception and by e-mail.

The word of your future manager, Francesca

"In our team, commitment and solidarity are not just values: they guide each of our actions in the service of the beneficiaries we accompany. I am proud to work alongside collaborators invested, welded and fully autonomous in their responsibilities. Joining our team is integrating an environment where self-help is natural, where everyone finds their place and can rely on a dynamic and caring working atmosphere.

Your key missions

  • Receive and direct requests from recipients, internal and external collaborators and partners at reception.
  • Management of the caisse du site.
  • Process applications for basic transportation benefits.
  • Management of access badges and site economy.
  • Processing of incoming and outgoing mail.

Your profile

  • SWC or equivalent.
  • 3 years experience min. in the administrative field, ideally in a similar function.
  • Mastery of French, oral and written, English an asset.
  • Mastery of common office tools.
  • Service.
  • Adaptability and stress management.
  • Relationship, listening and collaboration.
  • Interest in migration and social action.

What EVAM proposes

  • A work environment engaged
  • A continuous training policy to evolve in your career
  • 5 weeks of holiday (6 from 50 years)
  • From very good social benefits
  • Meaningful projects in a stimulating intercultural context
