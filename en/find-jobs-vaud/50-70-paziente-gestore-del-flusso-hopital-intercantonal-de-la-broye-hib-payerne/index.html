Full description

Thanks to its 800 collaborators, the Broye Intercantonal Hospital (HIB) is responsible for acute care and rehabilitation. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the broyard population. In order to meet the many challenges ahead, and to strengthen and optimize the organization and coordination of patient trajectories and health pathways, the management of care is putting two positions in the competition:

50 %–70 % patient flow manager Nurse

Your Mission

On delegation of the care management

  • Identify and monitor daily, with nursing staff, the availability of beds and occupancy rates of care units
  • Organize, pilot and prioritize, in consultation with health care professionals, the management of hospital beds (missions A – B and C)
  • Ensure patient flow optimization and participate actively in regulating the tensions and saturations of the internal and/or external system
  • Organize, coordinate and monitor the internal management of patient trajectories between acute units (A) and RTC (B); processes related to the application for admission (AD)
  • Regulate and coordinate referral of patients from emergencies, day hospital or operating room
  • Identify, track and coordinate patient trajectories waiting for placement beds with external partners
  • Provide liaison and coordination activities for Fribourg patients, in partnership with external bodies, for return home with care and/or placement in EMS
  • Ensure the proper flow of information between the various trades involved in patient flow management (ICUS, service nurses, MCU and doctors, as well as all institutional partners)
  • Document, monitor and analyse data on patient flows, length of stay, bottlenecks and propose process improvements
  • Ensure and participate in the safety and quality of care
  • Providing support to different health care teams
  • Participate in the implementation of the institutional strategy and working groups

Your assets

You benefit from a HES diploma, ideally complemented by specialization, expertise in management, project management, or care coordination. You justify a hospital experience of at least 3 years. Proactive in nature, your strong professional, organizational and relational skills allow you to understand demanding and complex environments. With a high level of stress resistance and anticipation, your leadership and relationship ease underpin a strong interest in quality care, multidisciplinary collaboration, care coordination and system efficiency. For this position, excellent command of French (spoken and written), as well as computer tools are essential. Entry into office: 1 January 2027
