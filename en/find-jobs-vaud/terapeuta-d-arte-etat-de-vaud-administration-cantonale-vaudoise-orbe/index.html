Full description

Do you want to contribute to the reintegration of detainees through creativity, self-expression and personal development? Are you passionate about art, human accompaniment and the development of everyone's resources? As an art therapist (M/F) in the Crusader's prison, you will have the task of animate creative workshops adapted to the detainees, offering them a space of expression, listening and reflection. Through different artistic practices, you will accompany them in exploring their emotions, strengthening self-esteem and developing their capacity for expression and resilience. By integrating our service, you will be actively involved in the proper functioning of the Crusader Prison and will contribute to the appropriate and enriching care of detained persons. You act at all times in accordance with our values: "Loyality, Solidarity, Credibility".

  • Design and conduct art therapy workshops tailored to the needs and capacities of inmates, promoting self-expression through various artistic mediations.
  • Support participants in a kind creative process, conducive to the expression of emotions, the development of self-esteem and the strengthening of personal resources.
  • Organize and supervise sessions by ensuring compliance with safety rules, equipment made available, establishment instructions and the collective living environment.
  • Observe the evolutions of participants, adapt artistic mediations and workshop objectives to their needs, capabilities and evolution.
  • Manage, prepare and maintain the artistic materials required for the workshops, in conjunction with the institution's technical teams.
  • Work in coordination with other stakeholders and institutional staff to ensure consistent and appropriate care. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Mr Schlater – Head of the socio-educational sector in detention at 024 557 67 49.
  • Bachelor level training useful to the function.
  • A recognized diploma or certification in art therapy, or equivalent training in an artistic discipline supplemented by a specialization in art therapy.
  • Professional experience of at least 3 years in animation or teaching supervision of adult groups.
  • Oral and written mastery of French; knowledge of other foreign languages is an asset.
  • Strong pedagogical ability to transmit techniques in a progressive, accessible and motivating way.
  • Ability to work in a supervised environment and adapt to a specific audience.
  • A sense of communication and listening, with a benevolent and structured approach. This is a 12-month CSD, possibly renewable. This activity is about 8pm/week. Our terms and conditions of employment require you to be a Swiss or holder of a licence C, free of a deed of defect of property (an extract of the OP must be presented during the process), and your criminal record must be blank. There is a right to consult the criminal record under the Federal Criminal Records Act by the engagement authority for this function.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
