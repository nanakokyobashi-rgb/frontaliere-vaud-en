Description of post On behalf of one of our customers, a well-known and well-established company in French-speaking Switzerland, we are looking for an electrical project manager to strengthen his team.

Your Mission

You take charge of the complete management of large-scale electrical projects, from their preparation to their reception. A true conductor, you ensure the coordination of the teams, the technical, financial and administrative monitoring of the yards, while guaranteeing the quality of the services and the respect of deadlines. The sector of activity covers the region between Coppet and Morges, with a strong presence on various projects of great importance.

Your responsibilities

  • Pilot medium- and large-scale electrical projects.
  • Planning, organizing and coordinating human and material resources.
  • Ensure technical, financial and administrative monitoring of mandates.
  • Supervise site teams and coordinate subcontractors.
  • Maintain relations with contractors, architects, technical offices and partners.
  • Ensure compliance with standards, deadlines, budgets and quality requirements.
  • Participate in business development and customer loyalty.

Your profile

  • Ideally holder of the Federal Project Leader Patent in Installation and Security or equivalent training.
  • In the absence of a CFC, installer-electrician with significant and successful experience in a similar function.
  • Strong experience in project management and team management in Switzerland.
  • Excellent organizational, anticipation and communication skills.
  • Driving licence required.
