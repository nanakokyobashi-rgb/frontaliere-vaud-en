Location : Lausanne, Switzerland Work Model: On Site / Full Time Type of contract / duration : Permanent (CDI) Shape the future through scientific breakthroughs and innovation. As a Senior Pilot Plant Technician – Dairy Technologies and Food Processes at Nestlé Research, your mission is to transform scientific concepts into robust and industrializable food processes, by building our capacities in dairy and biotechnological processes and in innovative packaging testing with vertical lensacher. You will make a difference by working closely with scientists, project managers, engineering and maintenance teams, quality, and safety. It is an opportunity to deliver sustainable and innovative prototypes and processes for people, animals and the planet. How will you have an impact as Senior Pilot Plant Technician – Dairy Technologies and Food Processes

  • Prepare, conduct and close complex pilot trials on dairy processes (UHT) and associated food processes as well as vertical siding.
  • Technical support for projects: equipment selection, settings, testing strategy and problem solving.
  • Collaborate with scientists, engineers and project teams to move from ideas to practical pilot plant testing.
  • To contribute to the scaling up of processes and the continuous improvement of equipment and working methods.
  • Work in accordance with quality, hygiene, food safety, safety and environment standards. Which will allow you to succeed
  • Technical training in food, biotechnology or processes (CFC, Federal Master's, Bachelor's or equivalent).
  • Significant R&D experience Pilot Plant or industrial environment.
  • Knowledge of dairy technologies and processes and packaging lines.
  • Autonomy, practical mind, analytical ability and problem solving.
  • Good team spirit, curiosity and desire to learn.
