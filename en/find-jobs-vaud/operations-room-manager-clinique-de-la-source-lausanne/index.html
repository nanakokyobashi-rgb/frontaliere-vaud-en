Operations Room Manager — Clinique de La Source

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

La Source Clinic is a unique, multidisciplinary acute care facility. It belongs to the La Source Foundation, an independent, private, not-for-profit institution that also oversees the La Source Institute and High School of Health. The independence conferred on him by this status allows him to invest continuously in human and technological terms in order to guarantee his patients the best possible care. We are looking for a 100% operating room manager

Key Position Information

Reference : CLS/RH/2026.775 / Type of contract : Contract with indefinite duration 100% / Date of entry : as soon as we find the right person / Location : in the heart of Lausanne (M2 stop « Riponne » or Bus 2, 3 or 21 stop « Beaulieu-Jomini »)

Why do we need you?

As part of a new dynamic within our Nursing Directorate, we are looking for our future Operations Room Manager, following the departure of the incumbent. A key player in the operation of the operating unit, you will be at the heart of the organisation of the activities and the support of the teams. Your expertise in surgical instrumentation and your ability to federate will directly contribute to the quality, safety and performance of our activity, in an environment where collaboration and patient are at the centre.

What will you do?

  • Pilot the operation rooms and ensure a smooth and efficient organisation of the operating programme
  • Coordinate medical, nursing and partner services to ensure continuity and quality of activities
  • Build and support a multidisciplinary team (approximately 42 EFAs) by fostering collaboration, skills development and a climate of trust
  • Supervise the application of best practices and specific instrumentation protocols to interventions, in collaboration with physicians
  • Developing a relationship of trust with surgeons, anaesthetists and the various partners of the bloc
  • Ensuring the quality and safety of care, as well as compliance with hygiene, aseptic, traceability and safety protocols
  • Ensure availability and compliance of medical devices, equipment and resources for interventions, in collaboration with relevant services
  • Analyze performance indicators and implement actions necessary for continuous improvement of activities
  • Contribute to institutional projects and the development of operational activity
  • Managing your sector's resources and budget in line with the Institute's objectives and cost control strategy

Who are you going to work with?

The operating unit brings together a multidisciplinary team of about 60 professionals (Instrumentists, ATSSO, Room Helpers, Equipment Managers, Household Workers). Together, we are a united and versatile group committed to working together to provide a safe quality of care and a harmonious working environment.

What do we expect from you?

The indispensables

  • To benefit from a diploma in operating room or operating room technician, supplemented by a higher management training or equivalent training
  • Justify at least 5 years of specialized practice, including 2 years in a position of responsibility
  • Demonstrate strong organizational, planning and coordination skills
  • Be able to unite multidisciplinary teams around common objectives by fostering trust and accountability
  • Have excellent communication, collaboration and negotiation capabilities with partners with diverse profiles
  • Having a good spirit of analysis, initiative and decision-making
  • Mastering current IT and management tools

Your assets

  • Experience in operating environment and collaboration with medical teams
  • Demonstrate diplomacy, discernment and relational ease
  • Demonstrate excellent relational ease and real ability to build relationships of trust
  • Know how to combine listening, assertiveness and collective sense
  • Demonstrate good adaptability and contingency management

  • The ambiance of a human-scale clinic with the challenges of a health institution with varied future-oriented activities
