Full description

Does your passion for cooking go beyond the plate?

You are convinced that in EMS, each meal is much more than just a moment at the table: a pleasure, a time of sharing and an essential contribution to the health and well-being of residents? Do you want to put your know-how at the service of a quality restaurant and participate in the creation of a culinary offer fully integrated into the life of the establishment?

Then you're at the right address!

For our new Tertianum Le Rolliet establishment in Plan-les-Ouates (GE), which will open in April 2026, we are looking for a

Chef / Chef 100%

Your role

You are responsible for the kitchen and your team. You ensure that you offer a tasty, balanced diet that is tailored to the needs of the elderly and that respects the requirements of food security.

You make the cuisine a true actor in the quality of life of residents: the pleasure of eating, habits, tastes, textures, nutritional needs and conviviality find their place in your approach.

Your main responsibilities

  • Build and structure service processes within the framework of opening, then monitor and continuously improve them.
  • Develop a varied, tasty, balanced food supply adapted to the geriatric needs and the special situations of the residents.
  • Design and develop menus taking into account nutritional needs, textures, seasons, preferences, presentation and costs.
  • Cultivate a "home-made" approach while optimizing processes, ensuring compliance with group directives and budget.
  • Work interdisciplinaryly with care and other sectors to ensure that food is fully integrated into the resident's care.
  • Organize the operation of the kitchen, purchases, stocks, inventories and budget.
  • Ensure compliance with HACCP requirements, safety and hygiene legislation and directives.
  • Manager, support and develop the team, including apprentices, by valuing skills and transmission.
  • Organize and plan work in compliance with labour law, the CMS Geneva CTC and Tertianum guidelines.
  • Represent the kitchen within the establishment and actively contribute to the interdisciplinary dynamic.

Your profile

  • Cookery CFC, ideally complemented by management or management training
  • Experience confirmed as a chef, ideally in EMS, community hotels or system restoration.
  • Solid mastery of the kitchen in production and requirements related to different diets and textures.
  • Natural leadership, ability to federate and fun to transmit.
  • Good knowledge of labour legislation and the CLC EMS Geneva.
  • Training a trainer in company an asset
  • Special sensitivity to the place of food in the quality of life and health of the elderly.
  • A sense of organization, planning and budget management.
  • Taste for construction, continuous improvement and networking.
  • Good command of French and ease with computer tools.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed.
