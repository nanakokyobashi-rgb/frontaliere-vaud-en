Full description

The Directorate-General for Post-compulsory Education (DGEP) is responsible for training between the end of compulsory education and higher education. It offers training in 15 gymnasiums and 13 vocational schools and has more than 3,400 employees. In order to strengthen the Information Systems Unit (ISU), we are looking for an IT Fleet Manager.

  • Manage the computer fleet of institutions.
  • Consider and track incidents (PC and MAC).
  • Train and assist users on equipment (PC and MAC).
  • Collect the needs of users and transmit them to decision-making bodies (PC and MAC)
  • Coordinate and participate in local projects. For more information, you can consult the specifications here or call Mr José Castineira, Head of Entity at 021 316 77 28.
  • Information technology CFC or equivalent.
  • Diploma ES or Bachelor in Computer Science, one more.
  • Professional practice in the computer field of 5 years.
  • Technical English.
  • Mastering the usual computer tools (Microsoft Office),
  • Knowledge of several operating systems (MAC and PC),
  • Knowledge of network infrastructure, development and operation of solutions, business needs analysis.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
