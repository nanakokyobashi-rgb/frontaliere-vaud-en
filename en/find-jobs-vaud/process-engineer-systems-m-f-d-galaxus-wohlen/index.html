Full description

In this role, you are the bridge between software and company development and supply chain management. Together with IT, development and logistics, you will continuously improve our processes and systems while always keeping an eye on the big picture. With your hands-on art, you are also regularly directly in the logistics on site.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Hauptlager Wohlen, Industriestrasse 21, 5610 Wohlen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Analysis and further development of existing processes in the logistics and system environment (WMS, ERP) as well as conception of new, software-based process solutions based on business requirements
  • Translation of technical requirements into technical concepts for IT and development and vice versa
  • Evaluation, introduction and commissioning of new control logic, system connections and logistics facilities including expansion and integration into the existing system landscape
  • Data-based analysis of processes using process mining, SQL and AI-powered tools to detect deviations, patterns and dynamic bottlenecks along the value chain
  • Development of sustainable solutions across process and system boundaries instead of short-term individual measures
  • Collaboration or management in projects on process automation, system integration and change management, from planning to implementation
  • Evaluation of new ideas together with stakeholders, qualitatively and quantitatively, and support until successful implementation
  • Documentation and training of new processes as well as regular presence in operational logistics so that they are correctly applied in everyday life

Requirements

  • min. 3 years of experience: in process or system development in an industrial or logistical environment
  • Completed studies (uni/ETH, FH, HF): in business engineering, business informatics or similar
  • University degree (FH, Uni, ETH) in Business Engineering, Business Informatics or similar as well as min. 3 years of experience in process or system development in an industrial or logistical environment
  • In-depth experience with automated storage and logistics systems, their control systems and the connection to WMS/ERP as well as secure handling of large amounts of data, SQL, process mining and AI-supported analyses
  • Structured, analytical and solution-oriented way of working, networked thinking, hands-on mentality and joy in mediating between business and IT
  • Very good German and English skills
  • The willingness to question “as it has always been done” and propose more efficient, automated or effective solutions
  • Using creativity and initiative to solve problems and achieve goals
  • German (flowing)
  • English (very good knowledge)
