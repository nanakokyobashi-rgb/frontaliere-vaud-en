Lead Cox Social Media — Audemars Piguet

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Connected to the Manager Media, you help define and deploy the brand's social media strategy, while supporting the development and performance of its various platforms. You are involved in the design, coordination and monitoring of digital communications campaigns on social networks (organic and pay), as well as on emailing channels and landing pages, in order to enhance the visibility, commitment and image of the House.

Your main missions

  • Contribute to the definition of the Social Media strategy, the development of guidelines and the construction of the editorial calendar
  • Coordinate the production, publication and monitoring of content on all the social platforms of the House.
  • Manage Audemars Piguet's proprietary social networks and act as a preferred point of contact for markets
  • Piloting the deployment of communication campaigns on social networks, whether they be global campaigns, event coverage or content related to watchmaking know-how and brand heritage.
  • Ensure the adaptation of messages to the different audiences and the specificities of each channel, while ensuring the consistency of digital experience through newsletters and landing pages
  • Collaborate with Paid Media teams for the implementation of paid social media campaigns and other digital activations
  • Analyze the performance of campaigns and content to identify opportunities for continuous optimization and feed into future recommendations
  • Develop and oversee social media processes, tools and good practices, including through platforms such as Sprinklr, in collaboration with various internal stakeholders
  • Ensuring continuous monitoring of emerging trends, platforms and technologies, managing exchanges with partner agencies and identifying new innovative opportunities to strengthen the digital presence of Audemars Piguet
  • You have a Bachelor or Master in Business School, Marketing, Communication, Digital Media or equivalent
  • You justify a significant experience in social media management, digital marketing or brand communication, ideally in the world of luxury and ideally in watchmaking
  • You have mastered the main social platforms (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Pinterest, WeChat, Weibo, etc.) and their codes and best practices
  • You have a solid experience in coordinating integrated digital campaigns, including organic, pay media, newsletters and landing pages
  • You are comfortable with content management and publishing tools such as Sprinklr, as well as with performance analysis and reporting tools
  • You have excellent editorial skills and a keen sense of storytelling, with a high sensitivity to brand issues and user experience
  • You have an analytical mind to interpret the data, measure the performance of the actions taken and make relevant recommendations.
  • You are known for your sense of organisation and coordination, you know how to manage several projects simultaneously and collaborate with many internal and external interlocutors
  • You are curious, proactive and looking for digital trends, you take an innovative approach and are constantly looking for new opportunities to strengthen the presence and visibility of the brand
  • You speak English and French fluently and you are able to write posts in English

What we offer

  • Health insurance (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual holiday weeks and 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • A contribution to your sports or cultural activities
  • The ability to work remotely depending on the function
  • A business restaurant
  • An advantageous PBL
