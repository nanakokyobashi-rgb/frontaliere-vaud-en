Head of Customer Project Management

Key Responsibilities

  • Define and deploy the global Customer Project Management strategy and operating model aligned with business priorities.
  • Lead and develop a high-performing global organization, fostering talent growth, coaching, and succession planning.
  • Oversee the worldwide customer project portfolio, ensuring delivery against contractual commitments for scope, schedule, cost, quality, profitability, and customer satisfaction.
  • Establish and maintain global project governance, methodologies, standards, tools, KPIs, and reporting practices.
  • Drive portfolio reviews and provide executive-level visibility on project performance, risks, opportunities, claims, and resource requirements.
  • Act as executive sponsor for strategic or critical customer projects and lead recovery plans when required.
  • Strengthen collaboration across functions and regions to improve end-to-end project execution.
  • Ensure rigorous management of project profitability, cash flow, forecasting, change management, and risk mitigation.
  • Build strong executive-level customer relationships and support key business objectives.
  • Lead continuous improvement and digitalization initiatives to enhance project management capabilities and decision-making.
  • Ensure compliance with contractual requirements, internal policies, quality standards, and health & safety regulations

Profile

  • Master's degree in Engineering, Business Administration, Project Management, or a related field. MBA is a plus.
  • Extensive leadership experience in global customer project management within industrial equipment, engineered-to-order, or capital goods environments.
  • Strong track record of leading large international and multicultural teams.
  • Demonstrated experience managing complex global project portfolios with accountability for revenue, margins, cash flow, customer satisfaction, and risk.
  • Deep expertise in project governance, contract management, financial management, risk management, and project recovery.
  • Strong business and financial acumen with the ability to balance strategic and operational priorities.
  • Executive presence with excellent stakeholder management and influencing skills.
  • Proven ability to drive organizational transformation and continuous improvement.
  • Excellent communication, negotiation, and leadership capabilities.
  • Fluent in English
  • French and/or German are considered strong assets.
  • Willingness to travel internationally.
