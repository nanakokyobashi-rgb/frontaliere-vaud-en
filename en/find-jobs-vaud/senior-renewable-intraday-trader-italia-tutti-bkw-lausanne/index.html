Full description

We are excited to scale our short-term renewable power trading activities in Italy. This role fits with someone who has minimum 5 years of renewables trading experience in Italy and is motivated to relocate to Lausanne.

Your role

  • Good understanding of the regulatory framework for short term power trading in Italy

Your expertise

  • Proven track record of renewables short term trading in Italy

  • Team player
