Full description

The Ensemble Hospitalier de la Côte - EHC is a healthcare network that brings together structures with complementary missions. Part of this network are over thirty medical centers, as well as 4 medical presence sites along the coast. These centers and presence sites receive every day, in modern and colorful spaces, our clientele for consultations with general practitioners and specialists, checks, or even non-vital adult emergencies. These proximity structures prioritize quality care, a relationship of trust with patients, and a convivial and benevolent working atmosphere.
