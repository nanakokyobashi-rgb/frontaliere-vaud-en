Full description

The Riviera-Chablais Hospital, Vaud-Valais (HRC), is a reference intercantonal hospital, which brings together more than 2,500 collaborators. Rennaz Hospital has over 350 acute care beds. It is complemented by two Geriatric and Rehabilitation Clinics and two medical clinics. All employees invest on a daily basis to provide quality care to patients, embodying the values of the institution: authenticity, exemplaryness and innovation.

The Hygiene and Environment (UPHE) Unit, attached to the Facility Management department, is responsible for hospital cleaning, lingerie and waste management.

The main tasks of this post are

As a multi-purpose operating agent, you intervene in a number of areas essential to the proper functioning of the

Hospital

  • Cleaning and maintenance: Mechanized cleaning of corridors, elevators and stairs, technical cleaning and refurbishing of floors.
  • Bioncleaning: clean and disinfect care equipment and equipment according to hospital hygiene procedures and sanitary requirements.
  • Waste and linen management: sorting, collection, transport and disposal of waste, as well as laundry management, in accordance with current procedures.
  • Maintenance of equipment: clean and maintain autowashers, carts and cleaning spaces, and ensure resupply of products and consumables.
  • Logistical services: participate in relocations, installation of equipment and various activities necessary for the operation of the hospital.
  • Collaboration and versatility: working as a team and collaborating with different services to ensure quality services.
