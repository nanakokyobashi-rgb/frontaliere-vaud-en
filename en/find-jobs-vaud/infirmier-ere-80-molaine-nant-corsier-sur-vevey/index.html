Full description

In order to complete its team of the Hospital Unit of Psychiatry of the Elderly (Molaine), the Foundation of Nant places a position of

Nurse at 80%

MISSION Nurses in the elderly psychiatry hospital unit are responsible for providing the required care for each patient in close collaboration with an interdisciplinary team.

WE OFFER

The psychiatry and psychotherapy service for the elderly is one of the three services of the Nant Foundation. Its mission is to provide care for people over the age of 65 in the Vaud area. The hospital unit of this service, Molaine, is intended to provide urgent, semi-urgent or scheduled hospitalizations for people over 65 years of age. Being a nurse in this service is evolving within a dynamic working environment at the heart of an interdisciplinary team.

WE SEEK A nurse with experience in psychiatry, holder of a Bachelor of Science HES-SO in Nursing with Red Cross Recognition (or title deemed equivalent). Moreover, having the capacity to be at the service of the patient, his colleagues and the institution is important to work as a nurse in Molaine. Finally we want to hire a person motivated by the Foundation's mission and loving the work as an interdisciplinary team.
