Full description

What lies ahead

In the Prévoyance team, you and your colleagues help develop and improve the analysis, reporting and piloting processes that support our services and decisions. Thanks to your understanding of business data and needs, you are participating in the continuous evolution of our foresight environment.

What you can do with us

You analyze the needs of foresight professions and translate them into appropriate indicators, reports and decision-making solutions. In close collaboration with specialized teams, you help improve the quality, availability and understanding of data.

You are involved in the development and optimization of management reports, dashboards and ad hoc analyses. Your structured approach allows you to identify trends, highlight opportunities for improvement and support decision-making processes.

In interdisciplinary teams, you support projects related to data transformation and digitization of foresight processes. You encourage collaboration between professional specialists and technical teams to develop useful and sustainable solutions.

What you bring

  • Higher education in business economy, management information technology, data analytics or training deemed equivalent
  • Experience in a Business Analyst, Data Analyst or Reporting Analyst function
  • High interest in forecasting, data and process improvement
  • Interest in developing data roadmap
  • Knowledge of desired tools (Power BI, SQL, SAP BO)
  • Excellent analytical capabilities, structured approach and synthesis
  • Collaborative, autonomous and solutions-oriented personality
  • Current mastery of French and/or German with excellent English skills

Working at home

You join a team of foresight specialists, analysts and project managers who cultivate an atmosphere of committed, communicative and solutions-oriented work. Together, we develop services that bring real added value to our clients.

Your occupancy rate is negotiable from 80% with access to flexible working time models. Below you will learn more about our commitment as an employer.

What we offer

Balance of working life - privacy

We apply flexible work models and allow 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave. We also offer above-average family allowances and a sustainable pension fund.

Continuing training and development

We offer a wide range of in-house courses and allow you to participate in possible programs abroad. We contribute generously to external continuing training related to work.

Benefits

We offer up to 25% discount on premiums, half-rate subscription, discounts on mobile phones, free flat-rate mobile subscriptions and discounts on cultural events.
