Full description

You have the motivation to give the best of yourself and help us do even better. For our part, we offer you a solid framework so that you can fully focus on your work: an equal corporate culture, flexible working hours, the possibility of working at home and many development opportunities.

Plunge into the exciting universe of analysis of profitability and the fight against insurance abuses. You will actively help protect our clients from unjustified, abusive or even fraudulent services. Are you interested? We look forward to meeting you!

Your mission

  • At the heart of your business: you examine and investigate complex benefit and claim files with evidence of unjustified, abusive or fraudulent claims
  • To identify suspicious cases, you perform in-depth data analysis
  • Intensive negotiations are not always easy. Thanks to your psychological ease, you implement targeted measures, whether in dialogue with clients or with providers
  • You write a demanding correspondence and record each step accurately and comprehensively
  • You are the specialist reference person for all internal and external interfaces, including our legal department, and give them your support
  • Your expertise is very appreciated! You actively participate in in-house specialized training, in-service training and awareness-raising, and advise the interfaces of all sectors of the group

What you bring

  • You have professional experience in the field of health insurance or personal insurance, ideally complemented by legal knowledge and experience in the fight against abuse (LFA). Further training in social insurance or health insurance is an asset
  • You can analyze complex situations and numerical data, then present your findings in a structured way. You have excellent knowledge of Excel or other analytical tools
  • You approach demanding negotiations with skill and perseverance, and convince by your professional and engaging attitude
  • You speak very well in French, oral and written. The mastery of German, Italian or English is a more
  • You appreciate teamwork and the true spirit of collaboration, while being comfortable working independently in your field
  • You have strong medical knowledge and know how to use it to analyze and evaluate complex files
  • Work flexibly: Adapt your work every year to your lifestyle, with part-time opportunities and other flexible models such as telework, to name but one.
  • Enthusiasm your purse: Benefits such as bonuses, Reka cheques, preferential health insurance premiums, etc., which can quickly reach several thousand francs per year.
  • Choose your holiday model: Five or six weeks of vacation? You choose. You work 41 or 42 hours a week, respectively.
  • Keep training: We want you to continue training internally or externally and generously support you with time and money.
  • Keep the form: ergonomic work places, promotion of psychological well-being and many other offers related to health.
  • Engage in social matters: Is life good for you? So make others enjoy it and broaden your horizons, alone or as a team.
