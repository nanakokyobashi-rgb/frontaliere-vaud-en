Full description

Located in Nyon since 2021, Dr Bloch is a specialist in haematology. As part of her collaborator's retirement, we are looking for her future medical secretary to ensure the continuity of the firm's operations. You will actively contribute to the quality of patient care. Description of the post: In your new role, your tasks will include:

  • Provide physical and telephone reception for the patient
  • Manage medical agenda and appointment making
  • Build and maintain medical records
  • Organize additional examinations and external appointments
  • Ensuring medical and administrative correspondence
  • Review and complete referrals of consultation reports and medical correspondence
  • Invoicing and administrative tracking of files
  • Provide exchanges with insurance, treating physicians and other health partners Profile required: We are convinced of your rapid success in your future functions thanks to your:
  • Excellent relational skills and hospitality
  • Organizational sense, autonomy, rigour and ability to manage priorities
  • High capacity for empathy and ability to manage complex situations with tact and professionalism
  • First successful work experience
  • Excellent mastery of oral and written French and medical terminology
  • Good command of English to communicate with an English patient
  • Good mastery of computer tools and medical secretarial software
  • Knowledge of Swiss medical billing, an asset
  • Ability to build trust with patients
  • Discretion, professionalism and strict respect for confidentiality Passionate about your profession, your motivation guides your actions towards excellence. Benevolent with patients, your empathy is recognized by your previous employers. You have an excellent sense of organization and the necessary experience to independently manage a medical secretariat. Skilled with IT tools, you are open to change, the introduction of new IT tools for the entire profession does not scare you. Finally, your emotional stability allows you to evolve serenely in a specialty faced with sometimes delicate situations. Level of training:
  • Diploma of medical secretary Benefits: You are responsible for the administrative management of the office of Dr. Bloch, a specialist in haematology. Your office is located within the Nyon Hospital, in a valuable working environment and you collaborate daily with the teams of other private offices and with the nursing team. Finally, by joining Dr Bloch's private medical office, you will benefit from the benefits of the collective agreement for the parapublic health sector (CCT-San), as well as from the benefits offered to the employees of the GHOL: staff restaurant, institutional events, discounts to various providers in the region etc... You recognize yourself in this profile and want to join a human-sized firm offering a stimulating work environment? We look forward to receiving your application.
