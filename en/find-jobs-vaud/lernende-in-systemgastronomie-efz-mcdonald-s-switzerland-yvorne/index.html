Apprentices in system gastronomy EFZ

Our goal: to train young people!

We are looking for an apprentice for our restaurant as a system catering specialist EFZ.

The EFZ training is a three-year training aimed at people who are motivated to gain a holistic view of the profession, to gradually assume responsibility and to develop in the long term in system catering.

Profile

  • This teaching is made for you if:
  • You have completed secondary school
  • You have basic knowledge of German (in word and writing)
  • You like practical and manual work
  • You enjoy working in a team and having customer contact
  • You are reliable, punctual and willing to learn
  • You like to follow clear guidelines and procedures
  • You are stress resistant and feel comfortable in a dynamic environment
  • Order, cleanliness and hygiene are among your priorities

Your future profession

As a system gastronomy EFZ learner, you will receive a comprehensive and varied education that combines practical practice with in-depth theoretical knowledge.

Among other things, you will learn

  • Preparation of products and customer service according to high quality standards
  • Organize work and work together effectively in a team
  • Understanding and applying restaurant work processes
  • Quality assurance, hygiene and safety
  • Gradually take on more responsibility in different positions

As your education progresses, you will also be introduced to

  • Work planning and team organisation
  • Cost control and resource management
  • The Basics of Marketing and Customer Satisfaction
  • Staff management principles

Your education

EFZ training lasts 3 years and includes

  • Practical training directly in the restaurant
  • Courses in vocational school for deepening theoretical knowledge
  • Intercompany courses for linking theory and practice

Your Federal Certificate of Competence (EFZ) is a solid foundation for your professional future at McDonald's or in catering.

Your development prospects

After your EFZ, you have many possibilities

  • Direct entry into the labour market
  • Development towards leadership positions (management)
  • Internal or external training
  • Development opportunities in restaurants, the franchise organisation or in the Head Office in Crissier

What we offer you

  • A recognised, demanding and valuable education
  • A dynamic, structured and caring working environment
  • Professional support and personal support throughout the learning process
  • Experienced and dedicated trainers
  • Real long-term development prospects
