Full description

BMS Building Materials Switzerland is a wholesaler of construction materials for the large and second work. Our group of companies includes renowned specialists such as Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz, Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced specialists advise, support and inspire with great commitment the customers of the construction sector and private customers at 114 sites throughout Switzerland. Close to the daily construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Fast, simple and competent.

To strengthen our team based in Yverdon-les-Bains, we are looking for an entrepreneurial and motivated personality.

Missions

  • Welcome and advise our clients in a friendly and competent manner
  • Providing technical advice in the field of second work (building & sanitation technology)
  • Preparation of delivery slips and invoices
  • Cash, goods and inventory management
  • Arrivals, dispatch of goods, preparation of orders
  • Manage and process merchandise returns
  • Work closely with back office (update, request for tender)
  • Notify customers of arrival of their order

Your Profile

  • Owning a CFC Retail Manager or CFC in the field of building technology or sanitary installer
  • Taste pronounced for sale
  • Have ease in customer contacts
  • Mastering common IT tools
  • Know how to work independently while easily integrating into a team
  • Safety at work is a priority for you

We offer

  • A stimulating, versatile and interesting activity in a good working environment, where active collaboration is valued while encouraging autonomy.
  • A comprehensive integration programme; our new colleagues also benefit from in-house training on products and systems
  • Support for the professional and personal development of our employees
  • Collaborating discounts, various discounts such as fleet discounts, discounts in selected fitness centres and numerous offers to promote their mental and physical health
  • An attractive wage package combined with a strong pension fund, with flexible savings contributions and above average employer share
  • 5 weeks of holidays (6 weeks from 50 years) and the possibility to buy up to 10 additional days of holidays, as well as additional benefits such as a highly funded independent pension fund and discounts on our entire product range as well as external premium benefits
