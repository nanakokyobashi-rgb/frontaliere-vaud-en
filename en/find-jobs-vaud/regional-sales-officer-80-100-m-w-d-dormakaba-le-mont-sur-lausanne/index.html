Full description

As Regional Sales Manager, you are in charge of commercial development in Western Switzerland for access control solutions and door technology. You manage your region's business performance with your team, following the business strategy defined by headquarters, and implementing a structured, proactive and results-oriented approach.

You also ensure synergies and cross-selling with the automatic door sales department, and develop B2B, B2C and partner relationships.

THIS OPENS THE DOOR OF YOUR FUTURE: YOUR TIME

  • Achieving the defined sales objectives (budget and KPI) in your region
  • Develop and implement structured business planning
  • Manage and develop the team of sales advisors
  • Support teams in the presentation and advice of solutions to end customers, contractors, general contractors, architects, planners and engineering offices
  • Develop and acquire new customers and projects
  • Monitoring and retention of existing clients
  • Actively participate in promotional activities and business events
  • Support the development of safety concepts and high value-added offers
  • Ensure good sales (technical, commercial and administrative)
  • Conduct regular client and partner visits
  • Ensure monthly reporting (results, activities, market, competition)
  • Document and track business activities in CRM (Salesforce)

THAT'S WHY WE'RE CONFIDENCE: YOUR SKILLS

  • Training in the field of sales required, additional technical training being considered an asset
  • Experience confirmed in sales B2B and B2C, ideally complemented by experience in collaboration with architects, general enterprises (EG), contractors and engineering offices
  • Experience confirmed in business team management
  • Excellent negotiation skills, with strong results orientation
  • Structured, autonomous and customer-oriented method of work
  • Excellent relationships with technical and decision-making partners
  • Ability to anticipate and exploit the skills of the future dormakaba, including: digital skills, problem solving, transfer of know-how, adaptability, thinking design and agile change management
  • Good command of English and/or German, both oral and written
  • Valid driving licence
