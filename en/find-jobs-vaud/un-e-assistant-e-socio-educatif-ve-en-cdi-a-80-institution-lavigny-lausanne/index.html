Full description

A socio-educational assistant in CDI at 80%, 80% The Département Plein Soleil de l'Institution de Lavigny, located in Lausanne, is dedicated to people with progressive neurological diseases or brain damage, and includes a 68-bed EMS, a 10-seat CAT, 12 sheltered accommodation and an outpatient therapy centre. It is a unique place of life and care in the Canton of Vaud, which offers personalized accompaniment, with care services and therapies, educational and socio-cultural activities, workshops for personal development and well-being, spiritual support and a service dedicated to the relatives of the people welcomed. Do you have an interest in the field of neurology and want to work in an environment of strong meaning and values in a pleasant setting where residents are at the centre of our concerns? For our sociocultural service, we are looking for a sociocultural sector: A socio-educational assistant in CDI at 80% In particular, you will be responsible for:

  • Contribute to the development and implementation of personal support projects for residents through interdisciplinary work
  • Design, organize and organize group or individual activities to meet the needs of residents
  • Provide support to residents in everyday activities and provide medical-delegated care under the responsibility of the responsible nurse
  • Providing "well-being" benefits that contribute to residents' quality of life
  • Assume the role of accompanying reference and promote integration of the resident into his network
  • Collaborate closely with the teacher responsible for the residence and the animation sector Your profile:
  • SWC socio-educational assistant or equivalent
  • Experience in accompanying people with disabilities
  • Good ability to work in interdisciplinarity
  • Flexibility in working hours (weekends, evenings, public holidays)
  • Driver's licence B required.
  • EEF training an asset We offer you :
  • A pleasant working environment, an atmosphere that enhances exchange, proximity and teamwork,
  • Active participation in institutional projects within an interdisciplinary framework,
  • The opportunity to develop your skills through internal and external training,
  • The social benefits of a large institution,
  • Working conditions according to the CLC of the parapublic health sector Vaudois.
