Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. True to its cooperative nature and values, it encourages the creation of a healthy, inclusive and inspiring work environment, ideal for developing your talent. In order to support the happy development of our general agency La Côte, we are looking for, for a start on 1 January 2027, a Chief or Chief Agency directly related to the general agent, whose mission is to lead a team of advisers from the Nyon agency and to develop the business of the Nyon region. The missions that await you:

  • Train, accompany and regularly follow the advisers to the external service
  • Participate in the Agency's strategy
  • Support a client portfolio and contribute to its development; identify customer needs and submit appropriate and differentiated solutions
  • Propose process improvements related to the development of tools
  • Know market trends; keep up to date with their evolution
  • Participate in and contribute to the economic and social life of its perimeter; create contact opportunities, develop networks of indicators and business providers and actively influence them Your profile:
  • Intermediate certificate of compulsory AFA insurance, IAF diploma, federal patent or equivalent title
  • Successful years of experience in insurance consulting and managing a sales team
  • Human-oriented leader and performance culture
  • Strong entrepreneurial spirit
  • Financial and analytical expertise Well integrated into the economic and social environment of the region, you love contact and develop relationships. You also have natural leadership and know how to make decisions. Organized, invested and of a positive character, you are a force for proposals and willingly take initiatives. Your customer orientation and your ability to support your colleagues and work as a team bring you real added value. In addition, you are open to training young people. We offer you :
  • A healthy working environment in a mutualist company certified "Great Place to Work"
  • Multiple social benefits, including flexibility in work organisation
  • Team management and accountability
  • Human values focused on the well-being of employees We look forward to receiving your complete application package digitally (with curriculum vitae, cover letter, copies of work certificates and diplomas).
