Full description

What lies ahead

As a Business Analyst, you are part of an interdisciplinary team that supports the Mobilière in its development in the foresight market through ambitious projects and agile methods.

What you can do with us

You are contributing to the modernization of our business processes and the evolution of our contract and task management platform. With your understanding of the end-to-end processes and the opportunities offered by our platforms, you identify opportunities for improvement and design solutions with high added value for users and the company.

Within our SAFe organization, you guarantee to Scrum teams the representation of the needs collected and you fully participate in the definition of the variants of solutions with these teams and the managers of the project portfolio.

You also participate in the receipt of deliverables and the exchange management activities.

What you bring

  • University training in business economy, management information technology or equivalent
  • Mastering Business Analysis methods, tools and good practices with experience in analysis and optimization of end-to-end processes
  • Significant experience in managing, evolving or implementing a major business platform.
  • System vision, ability to understand interactions between processes, systems, data and users
  • Outcome-oriented solution and pragmatic approach and value creation with the ability to bring together stakeholders, influence decisions and build commitment to proposed solutions
  • Current mastery of French and/or German with excellent English skills

Working at home

The Private Business Transformation Service is responsible for the conduct and implementation of projects in the field of Planning. It is composed of dynamic, diverse teams from Business Analysts and project managers working together in a collegial atmosphere.

Your occupancy rate is negotiable from 80% with access to flexible working time models. Below you will learn more about our commitment as an employer.

What we offer

Balance of working life - privacy

We apply flexible work models and allow 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave. We also offer above-average family allowances and a sustainable pension fund.

Continuing training and development

We offer a wide range of in-house courses and allow you to participate in possible programs abroad. We contribute generously to external continuing training related to work.

Benefits

We offer up to 25% discount on premiums, half-rate subscription, discounts on mobile phones, free flat-rate mobile subscriptions and discounts on cultural events.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Vaud?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Vaud's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
