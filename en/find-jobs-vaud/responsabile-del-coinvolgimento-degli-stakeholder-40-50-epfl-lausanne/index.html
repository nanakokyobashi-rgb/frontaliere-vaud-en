Full description

Mission

We are seeking a proactive Stakeholder Engagement Manager to strengthen the valorisation and impact of the SWICE project by connecting research outcomes with the organisations, institutions and decision-makers who can benefit from and act upon them.

The successful candidate will identify and engage potential beneficiaries across the public and private sectors, ensuring that SWICE findings, recommendations and solutions reach relevant audiences and are taken into consideration in policy development, institutional strategies and professional practice. This includes building relationships with politicians and public institutions at municipal, cantonal and federal levels, as well as professional associations, construction-sector organisations and private-sector actors.

Working closely with SWICE researchers and external partners, the Stakeholder Engagement Manager will develop and coordinate targeted engagement activities to promote the uptake and practical application of project results.

About SWICE

SWICE (Sustainable Well-being for the Individual and the Collectivity in the Energy transition) is a collaborative project led by EPFL and supported by the Swiss Federal Office of Energy, aimed at accelerating the Swiss energy transition by developing innovative solutions that combine environmental sustainability with social well-being and adaptive behaviour. SWICE involves a large consortium of researchers, policymakers, and stakeholders from diverse disciplines and sectors across Switzerland, working together to address the pressing climate and energy challenges of our time.

Main duties and responsibilities

• Identification of beneficiaries: Identify and map potential beneficiaries of SWICE research outcomes across the public and private sectors. • Stakeholder engagement: Proactively establish and maintain relationships with relevant decision-makers and organisations, including politicians, municipal, cantonal and federal administrations, professional associations and construction-sector organisations. • Outreach and valorisation of research outcomes: Support the development and coordination of targeted outreach activities to ensure that SWICE findings, recommendations and solutions reach relevant audiences. Identify opportunities to bring research outcomes into policy discussions, institutional processes and professional initiatives. • Knowledge transfer and uptake: Facilitate direct exchanges between SWICE researchers and potential beneficiaries, supporting the translation of scientific findings into actionable knowledge and fostering their uptake in public policies and private-sector practices. • Monitoring and impact assessment: Monitor stakeholder engagement, collect feedback and document opportunities and progress towards the uptake and practical application of SWICE research outcomes.

Profile

• Master or PhD in political science or a related discipline, preferably with experience in public affairs, science policy or stakeholder engagement; alternatively, a Master or PhD in a scientific or technical field, combined with professional experience in public affairs, science policy, stakeholder engagement or knowledge transfer. • Professional experience in engaging with public institutions, policymakers, professional associations or private-sector organisations. • Excellent understanding of the Swiss institutional and political landscape, including its municipal, cantonal and federal levels. • Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English, French and German. • Strong interpersonal, networking and relationship-building skills, with the ability to engage effectively with diverse audiences, from researchers and policymakers to industry representatives. • Ability to understand complex scientific and technical topics and translate research findings into relevant messages and actionable insights for non-academic audiences. • Proactive and results-oriented approach, with the ability to identify opportunities, initiate contacts and independently develop and coordinate engagement activities. • Excellent organisational skills and ability to work in a multidisciplinary environment.
