Accounting Manager — Audemars Piguet

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

In this role, you will refer to the Director of Finance Operations and your main mission will be to guarantee the excellence of the financial and accounting operations of the Holding as well as the companies of the headquarters. You will ensure the reliability of financial closures, accounting and regulatory compliance, while ensuring compliance with deadlines and internal controls. You will also oversee supplier and customer accounting processes to ensure effective management of financial flows, high quality of service and rigorous monitoring of receivables and commitments.

Your main activities

  • Pilot and coordinate monthly, quarterly and annual financial closures ensuring compliance with deadlines, internal controls and regulatory requirements
  • Oversee external audits, VAT declarations and all accounting and reporting obligations to the competent authorities
  • Ensure the reliability, accuracy and completeness of the general accounting and financial statements
  • Manage complex accounting records, provisions, accruals and balance sheet reconciliations in accordance with the Group's standards and procedures
  • Coordinate accounting activities with Finance teams (Controlling, Consolidation, Treasury and Taxation) and resolve issues affecting closures
  • Supervise the entire supplier process, from receipt of invoices to payment, ensuring efficiency, compliance and data quality
  • Optimize Payable Accounts processes through standardization, automation and rigorous tracking of accounts payable
  • Ensure rigorous management of client accounting, including invoicing, cash collection, collection and tracking of receivables
  • Manager and develop Accounting, Payable Accounts and Receivable Accounts teams by defining priorities, efficiently allocating workload and ensuring business continuity
  • You have a higher education in Accounting, Finance, Economy or an equivalent degree, ideally supplemented by a Federal Patent of Specialist in Finance and Accounting, an expert degree in Finance and Controlling or a comparable certification
  • You have at least 10 years of experience in financial accounting, including several years in a team management role
  • You have proven experience in managing financial closures, statutory audits and tax and regulatory obligations
  • You have mastered Swiss accounting principles, internal controls and best practices in financial governance
  • You have strong expertise in general accounting, suppliers and customers, in complex and demanding environments
  • You have an excellent knowledge of Swiss GAAP RPC standards and their application in a Swiss business context
  • You master the financial ERP, ideally M3, as well as the reporting tools and Microsoft Office suite
  • You are known for your leadership, your ability to develop talent and to unite teams around common goals.
  • You communicate with ease in French and English and show an excellent sense of organisation, a developed spirit of analysis and a strong focus on results
