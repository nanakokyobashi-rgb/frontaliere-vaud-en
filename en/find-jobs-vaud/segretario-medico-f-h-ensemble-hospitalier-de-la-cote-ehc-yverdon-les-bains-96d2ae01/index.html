The Ensemble Hospitalier de la Côte — EHC is a health network that brings together institutions with complementary missions. More than 30 medical centres and 4 medical clinics along the coast are part of this network. These centers and permanences, receive every day, in modern and colourful spaces, our patient for consultations of general practitioners and specialists, checks, or even non-vital adult emergencies. These proximity structures favour quality management, a relationship of trust with patients and a friendly and caring working environment.
