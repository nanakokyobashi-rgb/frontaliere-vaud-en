Full description

Would you like to exercise a creative, manual and varied function at the heart of the Château de Morges's activities? Join our team and put your technical and artisanal know-how at the service of the presentation of the cantonal heritage. In your capacity as Head of the scenographic workshop of the Château de Morges, you design, manufacture and install the scenographic furniture and devices of the permanent and temporary exhibitions of the institution. In connection with management, conservation/mediation teams and external scenographers, you transform museum intentions into concrete achievements, taking into account the constraints of a classified building. You manage the workshop independently and participate, with the stewardship section, in the maintenance of the site and the logistics of the events of the Castle. A versatile position that requires precision, practicality and a taste for teamwork.

  • Participate in exhibition design meetings with management and external scenographers, assess technical feasibility and propose suitable alternatives.
  • Translating scenographic projects into concrete projects: sketches, technical plans, 3D models, cost and time estimates.
  • Design and manufacture scenographic furniture and display devices (sockets, windows, supports, frames, decorations, signage, non-electronic interactive devices) of wood, metal, fabrics, resins, plexiglas, etc.
  • Make prototypes and test the stability, strength and compatibility of materials in connection with conservation teams and in compliance with museum standards.
  • Design reusable furniture and help optimize storage spaces for museum and event equipment.
  • Coordinate and monitor outsourced services (carpenters, painters, electricians) and ensure the aesthetic consistency of internal and external devices.
  • Ensure transport, assembly and disassembly of scripts, collaborate in hanging works and manage the storage, recycling or disposal of items.
  • Manage the workshop independently, including planning, budget, orders, inventory, equipment maintenance, security procedures and technical watch.
  • Participate in the maintenance work of the Castle, the monitoring of the technical facilities of the exhibition rooms and the coordination of the providers during the events.
