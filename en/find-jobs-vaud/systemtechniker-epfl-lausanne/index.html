Full description

Mission

EPFL's IC Faculty is at the heart of technological developments in informatics and communication, areas essential for innovation and scientific progress. As a IT support team, we play a crucial role in enabling the integration of advanced technologies into educational and research practices. We work closely with teachers and researchers to facilitate technological innovation and encourage the emergence of new ideas, thus helping to keep EPFL at the forefront of global academic excellence.

Main tasks and responsibilities

Technical solutions

  • Administer, maintain and evolve the Kubernetes clusters under Rancher to ensure their availability, safety and performance.
  • Deploy, configure and maintain applications on Kubernetes using Helm and Argo CD charts, applying GitOps principles and ensuring their full life cycle.
  • Administer and maintain GitLab rest, CI/CD pipelines and Harbor container records necessary for the development, integration and deployment of applications.
  • Design, deploy and maintain the necessary infrastructure for research services and scientific applications.
  • Install, operate and maintain research platforms, including Solr clusters, REDCap and associated services.
  • Ensure availability, performance, system security (especially via Uyuni) and supervision of hosted platforms and services.
  • Participate in deployment automation and continuous infrastructure improvement in accordance with the principles of Infrastructure as Code, GitOps and CI/CD.
  • Produce, maintain and develop technical documentation of infrastructure, procedures and architecture.
  • Participate in infrastructure development projects, from design to production and operation.
  • Provide IT technical support, ensure the full life cycle of solutions and provide outreach support to research laboratories, teaching and administrative units.
  • Ensure the integrity of the information system under the responsibility of IC-IT.
  • Ensure performance, availability and security of data.
  • Monitor and maintain backup infrastructures, as well as proper backup execution.
  • Support tickets, resolve incidents and requests in the field of activity, and ensure administrative follow-up.
  • Take into account budgetary and financial constraints when designing and implementing solutions.
  • Participate in projects, from needs analysis to implementation, production and monitoring.
  • Perform other ad hoc tasks.

Technical watch and watch IC IT service

  • Follow technical developments and propose improvements or new services.
  • Propose process improvement and automate tasks that need to be done.
  • Replace co-workers within the department.
  • Develop professional and personal skills.
  • Participate in the training of apprentices and trainees.
