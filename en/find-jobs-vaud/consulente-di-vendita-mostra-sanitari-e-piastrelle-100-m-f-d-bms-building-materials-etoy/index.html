Full description

BMS Building Materials Switzerland is a wholesaler of construction materials for the large and second work. Our group of companies includes renowned specialists such as Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz-Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced specialists advise, support and inspire with great commitment construction customers and private customers on 110 sites throughout Switzerland. Close to the daily construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Fast, simple and competent.

To strengthen our Etoy-based team, we are looking for a dynamic personality that loves sales.

Missions

  • Welcoming and advising our clients in a friendly and competent manner
  • Make choices and offers with our customers
  • Administrative monitoring and recovery

Your Profile

  • CFC Manager Retail trade with a minimum of 3 years of experience in sales (knowledge in tile, parquet and/or sanitary fields)
  • Creativity and taste for decoration
  • Having ease in contacts, good oral expression and loving customer relationship
  • Very good presentation and strong team spirit
  • Mastering common IT tools
  • Knowledge of German and/or English as an asset

We offer

  • A stimulating, versatile and interesting activity in a good working environment, where active collaboration is valued while encouraging autonomy.
  • A comprehensive integration programme; our new colleagues also benefit from in-house training on products and systems
  • Support for the professional and personal development of our employees
  • Collaborating discounts, various discounts such as fleet discounts, discounts in selected fitness centres and numerous offers to promote their mental and physical health
  • An attractive wage package combined with a solid pension fund, with flexible savings contributions and above average employer share
  • 5 weeks of holidays (6 weeks from 50 years) and the possibility to buy up to 10 additional days of vacation, as well as additional benefits such as a highly funded autonomous pension fund and discounts on our entire product range as well as external premium benefits
