Full description

As a Sovereign Data Program Intern, you will be fully immersed in the delivery of innovative digital solutions for government and institutional customers. Working alongside business stakeholders and technical teams, you will contribute to the development of sovereign data solutions across the project lifecycle. You will support multidisciplinary teams in transforming complex requirements and data into operational capabilities and actionable insights.

ROLE

  • Contribute to all phases of the delivery lifecycle, from solution design and prototyping to deployment for government and institutional clients.
  • Work with various data sources to collect, enrich, and analyze information, enabling informed decision-making.
  • Translate complex, heterogeneous datasets into meaningful insights through data visualization and analytical dashboards.
  • Support the development of sovereign AI capabilities, contributing to solutions designed to keep data, models, and infrastructure under our clients' control.
  • Build and maintain data pipelines (ingestion, cleaning, enrichment) on modern data stacks, in collaboration with the data and infrastructure team.

PROFILE

  • Master student in Engineering, Data Science, Business Information Technology, or equivalent.
  • Exposure to Data Science, Business Intelligence, Analytics, or Statistics.
  • Ability to organize your work and manage priorities.
  • Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
  • Strong analytical skills, curious and able to work autonomously.
  • Team player able to thrive in a multicultural environment while working independently when needed.
  • Fluent in English and French.

WHY JOIN US?

  • Join a global leader in trust technologies with a mission that matters.
  • Be at the strategic heart of a financially sound and innovation-driven company.
  • Collaborate with high-level experts and work on impactful international projects.
  • Operate in a multi-cultural, high-integrity environment where autonomy and ownership are encouraged.
