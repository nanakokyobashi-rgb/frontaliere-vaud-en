20.10.2026 Nyon

Be part of our team!

The Bank Raiffeisen Nyon-La Vallée, firmly established in the region thanks to its three agencies and its proximity to its customers, is looking for its future Head of the Investment Department

What are you waiting for?

As a real investment and foresight expert, you are involved, alongside the Directorate, in the development and implementation of the Bank's strategy in this area, while ensuring the management and development of the investment advisory activity. As a member of the executive, you embody the driving culture promoted within our institution, ensure the continued development of your area of expertise and optimize the processes in your sector. You are the head of the Placement department, composed of five employees, both technical and human, and support your team in achieving its objectives by ensuring that our clients have an experience of consulting excellence. In addition to your managerial responsibilities, you manage and develop a portfolio of private clients by providing them with personalized, high-value support. You advise them in all aspects of their property situation, including investments, global financial planning, foresight, taxation, transfer of assets and preparation for retirement. Thanks to your knowledge of the regional market and your network, you are actively contributing to the acquisition of new customers and to the development of the Bank's wealth management activity. Ambassador Raiffeisen, you are an active participant in our expertise in heritage advice and wealth management. You represent our Bank at local and regional events and, together with your team, implement actions and events aimed at increasing our visibility and valuing our skills with our customers and partners.

What are you bringing?

  • Higher banking or economic training, ideally complemented by a CWMA SAQ certification.
  • Experience confirmed in investment consultancy, wealth management and/or foresight at a Swiss bank.
  • Successful experience in driving, developing and accompanying a team.
  • Excellent control over investment, heritage management, foresight and overall financial planning.
  • Strong network in the Nyon and La Côte region, combined with a real capacity for commercial development and acquisition of new customers.
  • Strong customer orientation, excellent relationship and communication skills.
  • Entrepreneurial spirit, strategic vision and a strong sense of business.
  • Dynamic, autonomous and proactive personality, able to unite teams around ambitious goals.

Take over from a strategic department and actively contribute to the development of our wealth management activity. In a regional bank known for its proximity to its customers, you will have real autonomy to grow your team, develop your network and leave your footprint.
