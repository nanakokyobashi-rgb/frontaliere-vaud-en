Full description

Do you want the freedom of temporary work and the safety of permanent employment? For 30 years we have been joining hundreds of companies in the technical sectors of construction, construction and industry throughout Switzerland, proposing them the best talents. However, we do not hide that we are demanding. So, if you are a motivated and extremely enthusiastic person, join our team for the position of M6 Machine Operator.

Your assignment

  • Drive and maneuver a compressor roller in the asphalt and pavement yards.
  • Ensure the compaction of the asphalt according to the quality requirements and the instructions of the yard.
  • Adapt the number of steps and speed according to the laying conditions.
  • Work in coordination with the finisher, the adjuster and the foreman.
  • Check the quality of the compaction and report any irregularities.
  • Ensure the ordinary maintenance and cleaning of the vehicle.
  • Respect the safety standards and the SUVA directives.

And now we talk about you?

  • Experience proven as a finishing operator in asphalt yards.
  • Owner of the M6 operator license, indispensable.
  • Good knowledge of road works and civil engineering.
  • Self-appointed, precise and with good team spirit.
  • Good physical form and sense of responsibility.
  • Available and motivated for the season and various yards.

Do you care? Love and master your profession and want to put your skills at the service of projects that really matter? Then this offer is just for you. From us, your expertise makes a difference and contributes directly to the success of the construction sites.

What we offer: Your contact person for this location is Valentin COLIN. He will be able to accompany you, advise you and answer all your position questions. We will only answer applications whose profile matches the description you have requested.
