Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. True to its cooperative nature and values, it encourages the creation of a healthy, inclusive and inspiring work environment, ideal for developing your talent. In order to strengthen our Finance & Projects department in the Sales & Marketing department that supports Distribution in managing its commercial and financial performance, we are looking for an Expert Data Analytics & BI Performances. You will play a key role in transforming our data into reliable, forward-looking and directly useful analysis. The missions that await you: Develop and evolve our BI & Data Analytics solutions

  • Design and optimize dashboards, data models and indicators, notably under Power BI
  • Crossing and valuing data from different sources to provide a reliable and integrated vision of the activity
  • Automation of processing, controls and reporting to increase efficiency and reliability
  • Documenting data models, indicators and key calculation rules Strengthening performance management
  • Develop analyses and indicators to understand the levers of business and financial performance and identify trends, risks and opportunities
  • Design simulation tools and prospective analyses to anticipate impacts and inform decisions
  • Develop piloting tools, particularly related to markets, products, distribution channels and distribution remuneration
  • Moving reporting towards more forward-looking analyses, focusing on action levers and value creation Explore the potential of AI and new technologies
  • Identify and develop concrete cases of AI use to enrich or automate analyses, controls and reporting
  • Exploring its potential for forecasting, detection of anomalies and gap analysis, in collaboration with specialized teams
  • Promote the adoption and sharing of good practices around these new tools Ensure data reliability and support trades
  • Understanding and mastering the main data streams feeding reporting and piloting tools
  • Collect and challenge business needs to translate them into relevant Data, BI and Analytics solutions
  • To disseminate analyses and give back results in a clear, synthetic and decision-oriented way, while contributing to the development of a data culture What makes you unique:
  • Confirmed experience demonstrating Power BI mastery, including Power Query, DAX and data modelling
  • Very good knowledge of SQL and the exploitation of multiple data sources
  • Excellent mastery of Excel and analytical tools
  • High interest in artificial intelligence, automation and their business applications
  • Knowledge of Python, Microsoft Fabric, Databricks, Anaplan or similar technologies is an asset
  • Excellent mastery of French, knowledge of German is an asset You are also endowed with an analytical mindset, comfortable with structuring, solving complex problems and understanding financial and commercial issues in order to translate them into analytical solutions to unlock leverages of actions, so you are the person we need! We offer you:
  • A healthy working environment in a mutual company certified "Great Place to Work"
  • Multiple social benefits, including flexibility in work organisation
  • Working in a dynamic and passionate team
  • Human values focused on the well-being of employees We look forward to receiving your complete application package digitally (with curriculum vitae, cover letter, copies of work certificates and diplomas).
