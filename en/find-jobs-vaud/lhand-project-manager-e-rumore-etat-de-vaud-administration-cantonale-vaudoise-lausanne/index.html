Full description

The Directorate-General for Mobility and Roads DGMR is seeking, in connection with the compliance under the Law on Equality for Persons with Disabilities (LHand) of bus stops and compliance with the Ordinance on Noise Protection (OPB), two project leaders specialists LHand and Noise, for its infrastructure division.

  • Within the noise section of the infrastructure division, planning and piloting projects related to LHand compliance of bus stops and compliance with the OPB Noise Protection Ordinance.
  • Ensure compliance with applicable orders, laws, regulations, directives, standards or procedures, in the context of the implementation of the directions chosen by management.
  • Advise the municipal projects and support the communes when drawing up their LHand projects.
  • Develop processes and reference documents for the activities of the field concerned. Please submit a complete application file (CV, cover letter, diplomas, work certificates, etc.). For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Mr Cyril Durussel, Head of the Noise Section, at 021 316 72 19.
  • Diploma of civil engineer EPF or HES, or equivalent, in a field useful for the job.
  • Experience of at least 5 years in a civil engineering branch (technical offices, companies) for project or work management services.
  • Organizational and planning faculties; analytical and synthesis capabilities.
  • Knowledge of laws, regulations and directives in all areas in which the specialist LHand can be appointed as the task manager.
  • Driver's licence (category B) required.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
