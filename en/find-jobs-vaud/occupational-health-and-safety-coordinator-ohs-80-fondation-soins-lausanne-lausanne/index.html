Role overview

The aim of the Medical-Social Centres is to help people of all ages who are dependent or affected in their health to stay in their living environment as long as possible and to support caregivers.

The Fondation Soins Lausanne , with its 8 CMS and 790 employees , takes care of 6'200 clients at home annually and serves the City of Lausanne.

Our Medical-Social Centres (CMS) offer the Lausanne population high-quality multidisciplinary services and their interventions are based on values of humanity and equity.
