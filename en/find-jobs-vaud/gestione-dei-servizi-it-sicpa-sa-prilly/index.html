Role overview

IT Service Management Manager

We are looking for an experienced IT Service Management Manager to define, operate and manage IT service delivery for SICPA's Private Cloud Operations environment using ITIL service management as the foundation.

In this role, you will embed service management capabilities and ownership within Cloud Operations teams, drive transparency and reporting across operational activities, and contribute to the delivery of governance practices covering change management, disaster recovery, security, business continuity and audit.
