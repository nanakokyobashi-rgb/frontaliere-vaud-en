Full description

We deliver! Migros Logistics brings together Migros Verteilbetrieb AG (MVB) and Migros Logistics Romandie SA (MLR) and provides daily supply to Switzerland. Thanks to modern technologies and highly automated processes, we shape the logistics of tomorrow. Join us to build the future of logistics.

Mansioni

  • Participation in quarterly and annual closure work
  • Participation in capital asset management, including monitoring and accounting of investments
  • Participation in the management of accounts payable, accounts receivable and general accounts
  • Participation in internal financial projects in the areas of digitalisation, automation and process optimization (design, documentation and implementation)
  • Development of custom statistics and analyses
  • Replacement of colleagues on the team in case of absence

Requirements

  • 2-5 years: Finance and accounting
  • No initial/continuous training required: Finance and accounting
  • SWC Business with Advanced Training in Accounting/Finance (Federal Financial and Accounting Specialist)
  • Experience confirmed in a similar function, ideally within a logistics or production company
  • Excellent oral and written communication skills in German and French
  • Mastery of SAP S/4HANA and current Microsoft Office suite applications
  • Flexible and committed personality, with a strong focus on quality and timeliness
  • Proposition strength and proactive attitude to identify opportunities for continuous improvement in your area of responsibility and achieve the objectives set.
  • French (Very good knowledge)
  • German (Very good mastery)

  • Sport and fitness offers: We support regular health promotion and maintenance activities.
