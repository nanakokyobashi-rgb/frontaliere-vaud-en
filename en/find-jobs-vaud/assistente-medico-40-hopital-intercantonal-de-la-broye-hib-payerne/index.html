Full description

Thanks to its 800 collaborators, the Broye Intercantonal Hospital (HIB) is responsible for acute care and rehabilitation. Its ambition is to be a dynamic, innovative and excellence-oriented health institution for the benefit of the broyard population. To meet the many challenges ahead, the HIB is looking for:

Medical Assistant - 40%

Your Mission

  • Welcoming and accompanying patients and their loved ones, ensuring professional and caring care
  • Organize appointments via Ultragenda software and follow admission requests for interventions via DA software
  • Prepare patient files prior to consultations: collection and verification of information, document completeness and digitization.
  • Perform medical examinations such as: ECG, blood tests, blood pressure, Frottis VRE/MRSA
  • Prepare and maintain the consultation room, including replacement of protective paper, disinfection of surfaces and medical equipment, storage and organization of space
  • Collaborate with the OP Planning Manager to ensure rigorous and effective follow-up of interventions
  • Ensure the billing of medical assistant services and monitor the billing of medical consultations.
  • Managing the various administrative tasks related to consultations: correspondence, correspondence, e-mails and medical documents
  • Manage and update the watch list and update availability

Your assets

  • SWC medical assistant, SWC or medical secretary degree, supplemented by blood-taking training
  • At least one year of experience in the field of medical consultation
  • Very good mastery of computer tools, including Outlook and Word. Knowledge of Opale, Soarian and Ultragenda software is an asset.
  • You are organized, autonomous and rigorous, with a common sense of priorities
  • You know how to handle stress situations calmly and professionally
  • Your team spirit, flexibility and sense of service allow you to adapt to a dynamic environment.

Entry into office: 1 January 2027

Working days: Wednesday and Friday
