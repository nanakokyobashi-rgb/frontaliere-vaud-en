Full description

As a referent paediatrician within the Directorate-General for Health, attached to the Sector Policy and Health Programmes (PPPS), you contribute to the proper functioning of the medical system of the School Health and Prevention Unit (UPSPS), to ensuring the quality of medical services provided by school doctors, and to the implementation of the health promotion and prevention strategy for children and young people, particularly for school health.

  • To participate in the coordination of the school doctor system
  • Contribute to the management of individual or collective health situations in schools
  • Supporting physicians in their activities and advising them on complex situations
  • In the absence of a school doctor, respond to requests from school nurses and school administrators (compulsory, post-compulsory and specialized pedagogy)
  • Draft framework documents necessary for the proper functioning of the medical sector, ensure respect for the working processes and carry out optimisation measures to ensure the efficiency of the sector
  • Defining the operational objectives and priorities of the sector, as well as guidelines for stakeholders in collaboration with the doctor responsible for school doctors and the PPPS Centre
  • To contribute, in coordination with the head of the school nurse sector, the doctor responsible for school doctors and the PPPS, to the implementation of the Health Promotion and Prevention Strategy for Children and Youth (PSPEJ)
  • To be a force for proposals to improve and develop the benefits and the functioning of the School Health Promotion and Prevention Unit (PSPS)
  • Contribute to the definition of the community health approach and serve as a reference to the medical questions of members of the PSPS Unit and school directorates
  • Contribute to the development of the medico-nursery sector, in particular by participating in the reflection on their activities For further information, please consult the Terms of Reference by clicking here or contact Cristina Firorini Bernasconi, school health doctor, by email at cristina.fiorini-bernasconi@vd.ch.
  • Federal degree of doctor or equivalent, ideally supplemented by a MAS in public health
  • Title of FMH specialist in paediatrics
  • Professional experience in paediatrics of minimum 5 years
  • Practical experience of medical follow-up of children and/or adolescents and their families at least 3 years of age
  • Knowledge of the health and school system of the canton of Vaud
  • Ability to work in interdisciplinarity
  • Good knowledge of child and/or adolescent development and psychology, as well as neurodevelopmental disorders
  • Good analytical and synthesis capabilities
  • Decision-making and determination, responsibility and ethics of the public service
  • Good listening and communication, self-help and collaboration
  • Adaptability and flexibility Remuneration: Associated physician in accordance with the CHUV Executive Medical Officer Compensation Regulations. In order to have all the necessary information to analyse your file, we would be grateful if you would send us, in addition to your CV, a cover letter and your work certificates and diplomas.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
