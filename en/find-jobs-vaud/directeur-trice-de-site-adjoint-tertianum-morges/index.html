Directeur/trice de site adjoint — Tertianum

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Your responsibilities

Business continuity and direction

  • Ensure continuity of site management in the absence of the Director.
  • Make current human resource decisions: planning, absences, day-to-day team management.
  • Engage current expenditures in the budget.
  • Be the interlocutor or contact point for residents and families, including in sensitive situations.

Pilotage and performance

  • Conduct the monthly review of La Gottaz, service by service: budget, variances, indicators.
  • Arrive with proposals for correction, not only observations.
  • Prepare the weekly point with the Director.

Trade development

  • Receive families, tour, drive admissions with customer service.
  • Maintain relationships with external partners, hospitals and networks that guide residents.
  • Valorize La Gottaz's premium offer and contribute to its tariff positioning.

Management and quality

  • Accompany service managers, with listening and requirement.
  • Contribute to the recruitment, integration and development of employees.
  • Ensuring respect for the legal and treaty framework

Projects and collaboration between sites

  • Relay in La Gottaz the life projects of the residents.
  • Work within the Tertianum Group to harmonize practices and share what works.

Your profile

  • You want to grow: a first successful management experience, and the desire to move towards the direction of establishment.
  • Analytics: comfortable with numbers, indicators and piloting tools, especially Excel.
  • Responsible and reliable: you keep the site in confidence and report clearly.
  • Autonomous: you decide at your level and raise a problem with a solution.
  • Organized and methodical: you prioritize, structure your work and follow your files to the end.
  • Commercial sense: you like to welcome, convince, develop a network and promote a quality offer.
  • Service and relationship: field presence, listening to residents, families and teams.
  • Training: management, health, hospitality, HR or close field. Training in institutional direction, completed or ongoing, is an asset.
  • Languages: current French
  • German or English are an asset.

How We Work

  • Decisions based on facts and figures.
  • One weekly point with the director, one monthly review per service.
  • Wide autonomy, with clear delegations.
  • Topics followed until their resolution.

What We Offer

  • A position of full responsibility, in a residence recognized for its quality of care and for accompanying residents.
  • Direct support by the multi-site director: a real opportunity to learn leadership.
  • A leading group in Switzerland, with prospects for evolution.

Application

CV and cover letter . The files are treated confidentially.
