Full description

The Hospitaller of the Coast — EHC has been a health network at your service for over 20 years. It brings together institutions with complementary missions of acute care, rehabilitation, accommodation in EMS and first resort medicine. We deliver more than 40 medical disciplines in order to take care of each patient and resident, thanks to our 2'050 professionals working on a daily basis to provide them with quality care.

We are looking for for the Hospital of Morges

100% IT Apprentice (M/F)

From 1 August 2027.

WE OFFER YOU

  • Various missions within a small, very dynamic working team.
  • The opportunity to discover the world of work in an evolving institution whose mission is to take care of all our patients and residents.
  • The opportunity to benefit from a close follow-up during your learning, by involved and experienced trainers, who will accompany you towards the success of your SWC.
  • 6 weeks of annual holidays, as well as 10 holidays offered.
  • A mobility plan including participation in your trip by bike, basketball or public transport.
  • Free pilates courses, financial participation in sporting events such as the 20km of Lausanne or Bike to Work, a catalogue of advantages offering discounts in the fields of sport, well-being, leisure, mobility, insurance and finance.
  • Hot drinks offered in the morning and meals at CHF 8.50 in our cafeterias.
  • A "Pâquis Plage" business drink to share moments of conviviality during the beautiful days.
  • The possibility of having a fixed time at the workplace to prepare your final exams.
  • Professional opportunities within the institution after your apprenticeship.

YOUR POSITIONS

  • A compulsory school leaving certificate or you will soon finish your education and look forward to discovering the professional world.
  • A first experience in the field of computer science thanks to an observation course that allowed you to confirm your professional choice.
  • A passion for new technologies and computing.
  • A real motivation to involve you for 4 years in a demanding apprenticeship, requiring an excellent level in mathematics and French, as well as good linguistic knowledge in English.
  • A spirit of analysis and the ability to solve problems.
  • Self-reliance, a sense of communication and teamwork..

OBJECTIVES

  • Install, commission and maintain computer systems and applications.
  • Maintain computers, computer systems and support for users.
  • Manage personal computer hardware and software, as well as network integration.
  • Support data entry and ticketing systems.
  • Conduct and participate in the hardware order process.

WE ARE

  • A training company engaged in succession training, with about 80 apprentices in 16 different trades, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.
  • An institution consisting of acute care and rehabilitation institutions, a private clinic, EMS, medical centres and permanences located in the region of La Côte and the first clinical hypnosis school dedicated to the care community in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A network of care allowing many opportunities for internal change according to the aspirations and development projects of employees.
  • More than 2,000 employees taking care of patients and residents of the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment conducive to the realization of projects.
