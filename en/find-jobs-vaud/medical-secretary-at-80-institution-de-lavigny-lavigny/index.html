Full description

Medical secretary at 80%, 80% Integrated into the heart of a large recognized institution, the Hospital of the Institution of Lavigny is one of the reference Romanesque centers for patients with brain damage requiring neurological rehabilitation, or with difficult-to-treat epilepsy. Open since 1 July 2024, the University Neurorehabilitation Service (SUN) is an innovative structure linking two institutions, CHUV and Hôpital de Lavigny. With 75 beds and nearly 200 collaborators, the SUN provides highly specialized and interdisciplinary treatment in academic neurorehabilitation, for more than 500 in-patient patients and nearly 2000 outpatients per year. It is in this context that the hospital department develops, while keeping alive the values dear to it: Benevolence, fairness, respect and responsibility in a collaborative managerial approach. We offer you the opportunity to discover an exciting work in neuro-reeducation, at the heart of interdisciplinarity, in a partnership with patients and their loved ones. Within the team of the medical secretariat of the Hospital Department of Lavigny, we are looking for a:

Medical secretary at 80%

Your mission

Within our medical secretariat, you contribute to the smooth administrative and organisational functioning of hospital activities, providing quality support for patients, residents and different healthcare professionals.

Your main responsibilities

  • To register and monitor applications for hospital admission;
  • Manage, monitor and monitor insurance financial guarantees and requests for extension of stay;
  • Organize and coordinate internal and external medical transport, including patient and resident appointments;
  • Manage medical schedules and schedule appointments;
  • Ensure the secretariat's hotline in collaboration with the team;
  • To welcome and guide patients, residents, their relatives and the various interlocutors;
  • Strike and format medical reports, mail and other medical documents;
  • Monitor billing processes in different IT tools;
  • Maintaining the statistics and administrative procedures of the secretariat;
  • Work closely with physicians, health care teams and various internal and external partners.

Your profile

  • Medical secretary training or commercial/administrative training, ideally complemented by experience in the field of health;
  • Professional experience in a medical secretariat or in a hospital environment, an asset;
  • An excellent mastery of French, both oral and written;
  • Good ease with computer tools and administrative software;
  • A developed sense of organisation and priorities;
  • Recognized for your rigour, autonomy and reliability in managing administrative tasks;
  • Excellent relational abilities and great sense of welcome;
  • A capacity to work as a team and collaborate with interlocutors with diverse functions;
  • Discretion and respect for the confidentiality inherent in the medical field.

We offer

  • A stimulating and exciting work within an institution belonging to the Vaudian neuro-rehabilitation sector, recognised as regards the quality and diversity of its services, which despite its large-scale mission remains human-sized
  • The possibility of being able to highlight its professional skills in an interdisciplinary way where each competence is valued and taken into account
  • Membership in a committed and motivated team and being able to contribute to projects to improve the quality of care in an environment or human being still has its place.
  • The opportunity to meet many exciting challenges in a context of expansion of the structure, in close collaboration with an involved, committed and caring coaching team.
  • A magnificent working environment in new premises where you can take care of your hip therapy, on a climbing wall or in a modern therapeutic pool, all with an implemented robotic park
  • Working conditions according to CTC standards in the public health sector in Vaud
  • Social benefits of a large institution, cafeteria, free parking, public transport provided from Morges station.
