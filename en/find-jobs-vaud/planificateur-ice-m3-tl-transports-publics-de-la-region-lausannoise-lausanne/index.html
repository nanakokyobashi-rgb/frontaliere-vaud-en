Planner.ice m3

Your Mission

Integrated into the operational PMO division of the m3 project, you are responsible for the development, consolidation and monitoring of project planning, from studies to work, testing and commissioning.

As such, you will be led to

  • Develop, structure and maintain the master planning of the m3 project and the synthesis planning for project and programme management.
  • Consolidate the planning of the different poles/domains, agents, companies and partners and ensure their consistency with the objectives and milestones of the project and programme.
  • Produce and consolidate Gantt-type planning and time-space planning, known as "railway", adapted to linear work and different phases of production.
  • Plan and coordinate study phases, procedures, consultations, calls for tenders, works (civil engineering, equipment, systems), testing and commissioning operations.
  • Integrate interfaces with the m2-m3 programme, the future infrastructure manager and operator, as well as with related authorities, partners and projects.
  • Analyze critical pathways, margins, dependencies and calendar risks, and propose corrective measures or catch-up scenarios necessary to ensure reliability and timeliness.
  • Take a critical look at the schedules of agents and companies and formulate proposals for optimisation of phasages, work sequences and interfaces.
  • Produce the indicators, dashboards and analyses necessary for project and program reporting and management decision-making.
  • Ensure the transmission of planning information to the operational PMO m3 pole manager, participate in the animation of dedicated sessions.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement of planning methods and tools.

Your profile

You have advanced training (HES, EPF, ES, Federal Patent or equivalent), ideally in engineering, construction or project management.

You benefit from at least 5 years of experience in planning complex construction or infrastructure projects, ideally in several phases ranging from studies to implementation.

You master planning methods, critical path analysis, identification of risks or optimisation opportunities, tracking milestones, margins and deviations, and the development of catch-up scenarios.

You have excellent analytical and synthesis capabilities and know how to present complex information in a clear and appropriate way at different levels of governance.

You have a good mastery of MS Project and a spatial-temporal planning tool such as TILOS, or equivalents.

You have a good understanding of the construction methods, the work phase and ideally the interfaces between civil engineering, equipment and systems. You show autonomy, rigour and a strong sense of organization.

You are a power of proposal, able to help resolve identified anomalies and anticipate project needs.

You appreciate the cross-cutting work and know how to coordinate many interlocutors in a demanding partnership environment.

Experience in rail, metro or public transport is an asset.
