Role overview

Mission

EPFL's Science Promotion Service (SPS) develops programs and activities for schools, young people, families and the general public to generate interest in science and technology and to strengthen the dialogue between science and society.

We are looking for a communications officer to coordinate and produce the communications activities of the service.

  • Mission
  • EPFL's Science Promotion Service (SPS) develops programs and activities for schools, young people, families and the general public to generate interest in science and technology and to strengthen the dialogue between science and society.
  • Main tasks and responsibilities
  • Implement the SPS's communication actions to promote its activities among the various target audiences.

Main responsibilities

  • Main tasks and responsibilities
  • Implement the SPS's communication actions to promote its activities among the various target audiences.
  • Design, produce and coordinate content for digital and print media.
  • Manage and animate websites and social networks.
  • Ensure consistency of messages and respect for EPFL's visual and editorial identity.
  • Design practical models, guides and tools to help teams produce coherent communication media that are consistent with EPFL's identity.
  • Contribute to the monitoring and improvement of communication activities. Profile
  • You are an organized, creative and autonomous person with excellent editorial skills and a strong interest in scientific communication and promotion of science.
  • You like to produce quality content, work as a team and coordinate projects with multiple stakeholders.
  • Bachelor or Master in communication, marketing, public relations or training deemed equivalent.
