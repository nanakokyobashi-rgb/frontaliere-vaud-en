Full description

Your Mission

Within our Cybersecurity team, you are responsible for protecting industrial environments and operational technologies (OT). You help to strengthen the safety, resilience and standardization of our production infrastructure while also supporting the evolution of practices within our manufacturers. You manage activities related to the assessment of industrial cyber risks, the security of access and identities, and the implementation of the security controls necessary to protect our equipment and operations.

Your Responsibilities

  • Define and deploy industrial cybersecurity policies, standards and controls.
  • Evaluate cyber risks and implement appropriate mitigation measures.
  • Ensure the safety of industrial equipment, OT applications and access to critical systems.
  • Ensure consistency of security architectures and protection solutions deployed.
  • Supervise tools for the detection, monitoring and traceability of industrial environments.
  • Coordinate safety assessments when integrating new manufacturing technologies.
  • Contribute to risk management of external partners and providers.
  • Develop security indicators and support the continuous improvement of cyber maturity.
  • Participate in business continuity and recovery initiatives to ensure business resilience.
  • Advanced training in computer science, cybersecurity or engineering.
  • Confirmed experience in industrial cybersecurity (OT/ICS).
  • Strong knowledge of security architectures, identity and access management, and cyber risk management.
  • Experience in complex industrial or manufacturing environments.
  • Ability to coordinate multiple stakeholders and drive change.
  • Spirit of analysis, pragmatism and orientation results.
