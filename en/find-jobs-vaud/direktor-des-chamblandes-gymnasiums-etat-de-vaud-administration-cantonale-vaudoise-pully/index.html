Full description

At the head of this school of more than 700 students, you pilot all the educational, administrative, technical and financial activities in order to provide a quality and caring learning environment. You federate your teams around the success of the students, while ensuring their professional development. Representing the institution, you are committed to promoting its reputation to partners and the school community, strengthening and developing relationships that are useful to the fulfilment of its missions.

  • Implement cantonal and/or federal education policies within the institution.
  • To conduct and coordinate all its activities and services by ensuring a smooth and structured internal organisation and optimal use of resources.
  • Ensure the quality of education and supervise its distribution, ensuring the monitoring of pupils and compliance with existing programmes and legislation.
  • Ensure that the institution's benefits are adjusted and that a culture of establishment is maintained.
  • Recruit, accompany and unite teaching, administrative and technical teams; encourage the development of their competences within the framework set.
  • Ensure the dissemination of information to ensure clear communication and ongoing dialogue.
  • Develop and monitor the budget, define priorities and ensure reliable accounting management.
  • Ensure the proper use, maintenance and renewal of equipment and infrastructure.
  • Promote the image of the institution and develop its integration into its economic, social and academic environment, including by participating in various conferences, commissions and working groups.
  • University master's degree or equivalent in a field taught at the post-compulsory level, supplemented by a teaching title for secondary level II (or equivalent experience).
  • Multi-year confirmed managerial experience in the field of education.
  • Experienced managerial skills, excellent communication and ease in conflict management, analytical and synthesis capacity, openness to change and innovative projects, capacity to develop a culture of settlement.
  • The CAS in administration and management of training institutions (FORDIF) is desired and will be required if it has not been followed before taking up office. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Mr Lionel Eperon, Director-General of the Directorate-General for Post-Required Education at 021 316 63 22. Disclosure of criminal history is required to perform the function.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
