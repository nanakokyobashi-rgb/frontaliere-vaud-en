Full description

You've been in the fitness sector for many years and you want to set the tone for yourself as a manager? Do you care about the excellence of the customer experience? Do you post a responsible and motivated leadership personality? Then you're in the right place!

Luogo di lavoro: ACTIV FITNESS, Montreux, Grand Rue 3, 1820 Montreux

Important remarks

All applications are welcome, but only online applications can be considered. Applications sent by mail will be eliminated for data protection reasons and will not be returned.

Mansioni

  • You supervise and accompany our clients throughout the training process, you develop training programs and motivate our clients as a provider of excellence.
  • You are the guarantor of the implementation and respect of the operational processes, you manage your employees and apprentices and you actively participate in their evolution
  • You ensure cleanliness and hygiene in the studio
  • You take care of various administrative tasks, preparation of contracts and active contacts with clientses
  • You implement the corporate strategy and represent the company internally and externally

Required

  • As well as experience in a similar position: Many years of management experience
  • Completed initial or continuing training: Fitness instructor training (accredited in Switzerland - CFC or similar) with valid BLS/AED certification
  • You have a very good knowledge of the sector, a good network and very good sales skills
  • You have a higher than average sense of service and a good persuasion force
  • You are flexible and willing to work in the evening as well as on weekends and holidays
  • You are loyal and a model for your employees and our clients
  • French (Very good knowledge)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Centralised workplace: Our studios are located in a central location and easily accessible
  • Mobile subscription: Flat Abo national from CHF 10.– / month (Swisscom /digitec connect)
  • Migros Bank: Free private/investment account with preferential interest
  • Offers for employees: Miscellaneous discounts in the fields of holidays and insurance (Hertz up to 20%, Generali 25%, Axa Winterthur up to 15%, SWISSPEAK Resorts 30%)
  • Tuition: We work with respect for others and equal

Contatto

Candidature and contact Nicole Lambert HR Business Partner

Recruitment process

  • Pre-selection of files: We constantly review the applications received, pre-screen and forward them to the manager. The decision to pursue or eliminate the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Professional interview: During the professional interview, the manager and/or other specialized person, or even the team, asks specific questions about the knowledge to accomplish the work. If possible, then there is more time to get to know the workstation and team in a broken stick discussion. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Test work: A day or half-day on the test allows you to become familiar with the future working environment and team members. Conversely, we have the opportunity to see how compatible the candidates are with the team. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Offer and establishment of contract: The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then initiated by mutual agreement before being sent by post. Return time: up to 3 weeks

Grado di occupazione: 90-100%
