Do you live in the Lausanne area? Do you want to invest in a part-time professional activity with mainly customer contact? You're comfortable on the phone, you have a strong sense of service and customer relationship and a strong sales and trading capacity?

What's waiting for you

  • You trigger the interest of our customers / prospects by phone

  • You suggest marketing and distance business development ideas, you are a powerful proposition and bring dynamism and creativity to your ideas

What you bring

  • Your experience in the field of distance trading (sale, telemarketing, callcenter)
  • Your knowledge of topics related to Planning or rating according to the needs of the position
  • Your open, positive and committed personality
  • Your relationship ease
  • Your listening and your proposal strength both for our clients and within our Agency
  • You master French in both oral and written terms, your knowledge of German is a complementary asset.
