Full description

The General Secretariat is looking for a specialist in document management. You are leading the documentary reorganization of the Department's general secretariat by making the diagnosis of the network disk and designing a new classification plan for the entire service. You ensure the migration of content in accordance with legal requirements.

  • Analysis of the current storage server structure (network disk), including tree, volumetric, content and usage.
  • Identification of duplicates, obsolete documents and classification inconsistencies.
  • Mapping of groups, existing access rights and current document management practices.
  • Collection of team needs and constraints through interviews and workshops.
  • Definition of the guiding principles of the future classification plan, based on the standards and recommendations of the cantonal archives of Vaud.
  • Design of a classification plan for the entire service and support of its validation by COPIL, in line with the recommendations of the cantonal archives of Vaudises.
  • Accompanying documentary migration, including reshaping groups and access rights.
  • Tests, adjustments and consolidation of the classification plan based on feedback from users.
  • Formalizing associated user rules, including managing access rights by roles.
  • Definition of the migration strategy for the server's folders, as well as the quality control arrangements ensuring that the documentary device is kept operational.
  • Development of a migration plan specifying steps, responsibilities and timelines.
  • Team support in the sorting, preparation and migration of documentary content. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Marta Pinto, Head of Innovation and Transverse Files at 021/316.55.67
  • Bachelor HES in documentary information or training deemed equivalent.
  • CAS in information governance and desired digital transformation.
  • Certification of quality management systems.
  • Minimum 3 years of experience in conducting documentary migration projects and implementing classification plans and social conservation rules.
  • Flexibility and organizational sense.
  • Excellent analytical and synthesis capabilities.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
