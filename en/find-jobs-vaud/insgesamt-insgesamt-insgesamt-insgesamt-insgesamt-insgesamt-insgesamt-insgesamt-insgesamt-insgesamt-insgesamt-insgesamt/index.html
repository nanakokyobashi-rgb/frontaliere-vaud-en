Full description

WHO ARE WE?

Champittet College, a reference school in the German region since 1903, offers excellent training to generations of Swiss and foreign students. Its unique spirit revolves around academic performance, multilingualism and values such as respect and trust. The College is part of the North Anglia Education family, the world leader in international schools with 88 schools in 32 countries.

In this dynamic environment, both local and international, we are looking for a passionate and committed philosophy teacher to join and complete our academic team:

MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Teaching to 14th grade students in the Maturity section (in English) and the French Bac section;
  • Use any pedagogical approach to deliver the programme, including ICT, and prepare students for their final exam;
  • Act as a learning coach by setting goals that inspire, motivate and stimulate students;
  • Adapts education to the strengths and needs of each student, regardless of their level or special educational needs;
  • Gives students regular and constructive feedback, both orally and by pre-cise notation.

PROFILE AND SKILLS

  • Master's degree in philosophy and pedagogical training (HEP or equivalent);
  • Confirmed and successful teaching experience with at least 5 years of experience, ideally in an international school environment;
  • Excellent communication skills, flexibility and teamwork;
  • Bilingual French and English;
  • Court cases blank;
  • Swiss or EU nationality with a valid Swiss work permit.

If you are passionate about the field of education and want to join our team, we invite you to submit your application directly online via our website.
