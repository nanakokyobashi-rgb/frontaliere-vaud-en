Your assignment

  • Perform masonry work related to infrastructure and civil engineering works.
  • Lay strings, pavements and prefabricated elements.
  • Perform concreteworks, armor and casting.
  • Participate in laying pipelines, pipes and the realization of underground networks.
  • Build rooms, wells and various concrete works.
  • Perform excavation, leveling and surface preparation.
  • Read and interpret executive drawings.
  • Working with the engineers and other workers on the construction site.
  • Ensure the quality of the work performed and comply with safety standards.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Experience in masonry for civil engineering works.
  • Good mastery of casserole, armor and concrete casting techniques.
  • Experience in laying conduits, cords and nets: an advantage.
  • Ability to read and understand construction projects.
  • Autonomous, versatile, precise and organized person.
  • Good physical form and attitude to outdoor work.
  • Team spirit, reliability and punctuality.
  • Federal Capacity Certificate (AFC) as a mason or equivalent formation: an advantage.
  • Driving license and vehicle: an advantage.
  • Immediate availability
