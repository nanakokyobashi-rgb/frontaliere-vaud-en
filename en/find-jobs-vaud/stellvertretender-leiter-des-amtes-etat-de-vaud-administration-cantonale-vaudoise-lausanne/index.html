Full description

The Lausanne Regional Office for the Protection of Minors is seeking her new assistant to the Chief of Staff to complete the management team.

  • Participate in the conduct of the Regional Office for the Protection of Minors;
  • Ensure the hierarchical conduct of its group;
  • Provide guidance to social workers for the protection of minors in the performance of their duties, ensuring respect for the legal framework and the frame of reference of the service;
  • To monitor situations at risk, support social assistance for the protection of minors in dealing with heavy, complex or conflict situations and manage the processing of complaints;
  • To help promote collaboration with relevant communities and the local network on socio-educational action and child protection. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Ms Fabienne Lombardet, Head of the Lausanne Regional Office for the Protection of Minors, by email at fabienne.lombardet@vd.ch or 021 316 53 71.
  • Diploma of Social Assistant or Social Worker (Bachelor) or equivalent;
  • Minimum 5 years of professional experience, including at least 3 years with minors at risk in their development;
  • Training and experience in team management, desired;
  • Knowledge of family law, child development and family dynamics;
  • Leadership, decision-making and determination;
  • Good capacity for analysis, synthesis and delegation. Disclosure of any criminal history is required for this position. A private vehicle allowance is paid monthly.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
