Summary of the post

As a 5x8 production operator at Nescafé, your mission is to ensure the proper functioning of complex facilities dedicated to the manufacture of soluble coffee in compliance with safety, quality and performance standards. You will make a difference by working closely with production teams, maintenance, quality and other support departments. In this role, your agility, rigor and curiosity will be key to meeting operational expectations. The courage to raise points, make constructive feedback and act on risks or quality or safety gaps will also be essential.

Your impact as a 5x8 production operator

  • Monitor and control soluble coffee manufacturing facilities from the control room, adjusting parameters based on manufacturing recipes and processes.
  • Perform regular tours of facilities to monitor their condition, visually or auditoryly detect anomalies and anticipate failures.
  • Identify failures, implement first-level actions and work in collaboration with maintenance for interventions.
  • Applying good hygiene, food safety and quality practices, you contribute to the consistency of our products and to the confidence of our consumers.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement and safety, change practices, environment and facility performance. Being a driving force in digitalization in production.
  • Collaborate effectively when changing teams, ensure clear transmission of information and business continuity.

What will make your success in the role

  • Technical training or interest in mechanics, automation or electricity.
  • Providing a solid understanding of complex production facilities, you make reliable decisions to maintain line performance.
  • Applying rigorously the requirements of safety, quality, hygiene and food safety, you contribute to an exemplary production environment.
  • Using your digital ease and your sense of analysis, you follow the production parameters, document the controls and resolve the discrepancies with method.
  • Working with responsiveness and autonomy, you support the team, facilitate interventions and contribute to business continuity.
  • Communicates fluently in French.
  • Show courage, collaboration, responsiveness and curiosity.
