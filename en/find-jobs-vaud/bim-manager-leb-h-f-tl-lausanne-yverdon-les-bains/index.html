Full description

The LEB links Lausanne to Gros-de-Vaud and supports the development of a growing region. By 2035, he plans to introduce a 10-minute pace between Lausanne and Echhallens in order to offer ever more attractive and sustainable mobility.

This development is based on several major infrastructure projects, including the underground installation of the line under the village of Etagnières and the renewal of technical installations between Assens and Etagnières. To fully integrate environmental issues into these transformations, we are looking for a person who is ready to put his expertise at the service of concrete and sustainable projects.

BIM Manager - LEB (m/f)

Your Mission

You lead the LEB BIM projects, under the responsibility of the Strategic Programme Manager. You define the BIM framework for projects, structure deliverables, estimate expenses, plan activities and track their progress.

You coordinate the various agents and disciplines, ensure compliance with BIM requirements, BEP, internal standards and ISO 19650 principles.

You oversee the production and coordination of models, organize quality controls, follow the interfaces between disciplines, and ensure that expected deliverables are compliant.

You support project teams in the use of BIM methods, conduct the necessary coordination sessions and monitor actions, risks, decisions and open points, and ensure data quality, model compliance and compliance. You bring together stakeholders, prepare communication and training actions, capitalize on good practices and provide analysis, recommendations and reporting useful for decision-making.

Your profile

You have a higher education in civil engineering, architecture, construction engineering or BIM.

You justify 3 to 5 years of experience in conducting BIM environmental projects, ideally in infrastructure or public transport.

You master the principles of collaboration, multidisciplinary coordination, model monitoring, information structuring in around IFC and data governance, as well as ISO 19650 requirements.

You can translate the needs of the project into technical requirements and adapted deliverables, while taking into account future operational issues. At ease with new technologies, you propose concrete improvements and accompany their adoption with pragmatism.

Your functional leadership, analytical spirit and relationship qualities allow you to coordinate multidisciplinary teams and unite stakeholders.
