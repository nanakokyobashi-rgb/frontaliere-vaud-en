Full description

Outpatient clinic, emergencies, outpatient care, hospitalizations: from birth to old age, Morges Hospital offers a wide range of care services. The establishment has 240 acute care beds, a maternity ward, with around 1,500 births per year, and nearly 30,000 emergency consultations. In all, more than 40 medical disciplines have been practiced within its walls for more than 150 years. Surrounded by vineyards, with an incredible view of the mountains and the lake, the Morges Hospital welcomes you in a warm and dynamic setting.

We are looking for a

Nurse in doctor (M/F) at 80-100%

Start of work as soon as possible or to be agreed.

The medicine department on the 1st floor has 40 beds, including 6 dedicated to palliative care, and provides interdisciplinary care for patients requiring general and acute palliative care.

The medicine department on the 2nd floor includes 43 beds, half of which are dedicated to acute care for seniors, with interdisciplinary care in a suitable environment aimed at promoting and maintaining autonomy in daily life activities.
