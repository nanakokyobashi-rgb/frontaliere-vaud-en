Full description

As a reference player in public transport, each customer is transported by making the heart of the Lausanne region beat by a commitment to simple, innovative and sustainable mobility.

In an environment marked by increasing legal and regulatory challenges , the complexity of infrastructure projects and the need to control risks , they want to further strengthen their legal and insurance expertise and strengthen their cross-cutting support of the company's activities. In this context, we are looking for our future within the Directorate for the Development of Offers and Major Projects:

Responsible for legal advice and insurance

Your role and responsibilities

You manage the legal and insurance activities of the Tl and accompany the governing bodies in mastering the legal issues and risks of the company. As such, you and your team provide pragmatic advice in public and private law, helping to secure company decisions and projects.

Responsible for a multidisciplinary team of seven collaborators, you set priorities, coordinate activities and support skills development. In addition to your role as manager, you supervise the files handled by your team and intervene directly on the most complex and sensitive cases, including contracts, construction, public procurement and litigation. In this context, you represent and defend the interests of Tl in the courts and ensure that legal risks are identified, assessed and controlled.

On the insurance side, you define and direct the company's insurance policy and strategy, ensure that the coverage is appropriate to the identified risks and supervise, in collaboration with your specialists, the management of claims.

Finally, in a highly cross-sectional function, you advise and accompany the different directions in their activities and projects. You are also contributing to the strengthening of the legal certainty of the company, in particular with regard to compliance, data protection, legislative oversight and document management, while also accompanying the evolution of the regulatory framework applicable to tl.

Your journey and your assets

  • You have a university degree in law, supplemented by a lawyer's certificate, and a solid knowledge of Swiss law.
  • You benefit from an experience confirmed in a legal function in a company, ideally preceded by an experience in law studies. Experience in the public or para-public sector is an asset.
  • You master the main areas of public and private law and can analyze complex situations, anticipate risks and propose pragmatic solutions. Knowledge of public procurement is appreciated.
  • With unifying leadership, you show interest in managing and developing your team's skills.
  • Recognized for your relational qualities, your ability to convince and your sense of collaboration, you combine cross-sectional vision, discernment and proximity with the operational challenges of the company.

Do you want to put your expertise at the service of large-scale mobility projects and contribute to the development of legal advice for young people? We look forward to receiving your application.
