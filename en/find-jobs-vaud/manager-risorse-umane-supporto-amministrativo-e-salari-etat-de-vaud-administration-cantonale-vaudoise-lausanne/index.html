Full description

Do you want to put your skills in salary management and human resources administration at the service of the state of Vaud? Join the Human Resources Branch as part of a parental leave replacement to contribute to the smooth functioning of the Salary Sector and to provide essential administrative support to management activities. You are also involved in the follow-up of requests for temporary assistance. This position offers you the opportunity to perform a varied function combining salary expertise, administrative rigor and contacts with many interlocutors. For more information, please consult the specifications here or contact Mr Julien Buard, Head of the Wages Sector, at 021 316 29 21 or julien.buard@vd.ch.

  • Monitor monthly wage controls, carry out the necessary stimulus and maintain the steering tool.
  • Control the daily allowances embedded in the payroll, make necessary corrections and additional seizures.
  • Ensure administrative follow-up of insurance certificate applications for new employees.
  • Managing management's day-to-day administrative activities: mail processing, telephone call management, e-mail and distribution boxes.
  • Respond to requests for information from employees of the State of Vaud and external partners, seeking the necessary information in the available tools and archives.
  • Write minutes of meetings, in particular as part of the coordination of paying offices.
  • Analyze and monitor the compliance of requests for temporary staff from the various services, ensure their transmission to employment agencies and provide information on the conditions of employment. Monitor and track invoices related to general temporary assistance.
  • Trade employee's CFC or title deemed equivalent (required).
  • Confirmed experience of at least two years in salary management and human resources management.
  • Mastery of Microsoft tools including Outlook, Word and Excel.
  • Mastering one or more pay software, an asset.
  • Good organizational and priority management capacity.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
