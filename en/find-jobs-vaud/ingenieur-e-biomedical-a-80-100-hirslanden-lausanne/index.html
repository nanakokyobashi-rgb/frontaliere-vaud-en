Full description

Place of work: Hirslanden Clinique Bois-Cerf Permanent

Reference Number: 71764

The Hirslanden Group is Switzerland's largest medical network. We offer all individuals, regardless of their situation, high-level individual medical care to improve their quality of life - from birth to old age and from prevention to cure. As part of our corporate strategy Together We Care, our ultimate goal is to improve the quality of life of people who trust us.

MAIN TASKS

Within the Bois-Cerf and Cecil clinics, your missions will mainly be:

  • Biomedical Park Management: Facilities Monitoring, Maintenance, Contract Management (HERMED), and Technical Documents Update – Warewave Software Mastery, an asset.
  • Management of acquisition projects: Development of specifications, coordination with procurement, technical and logistics services for the acquisition of biomedical equipment, and management of commissioning.
  • Technological and regulatory monitoring: Anticipation of technological developments, integration of innovations, monitoring of current standards (Swissmedic), and preparation of audits and inspections.
  • Regulatory compliance: Ensure compliance with legal and normative requirements, management of ISO audits, Swissmedic inspections and risk analysis.
  • Financial and budgetary monitoring: Budget development, estimates, invoicing, decisions on the cost-effectiveness of repairs, and management of purchase costs.
  • Material and security: Incident handling, follow-up of FSN/FSCA, alert management and training of staff on good security practices.
  • Multidisciplinary interface: Coordination with the various services (technical, IT, procurement), management of relations with the corporate office, and information in the management system (Waveware).
  • Training and support: Training of users on medical devices, materials and the use of management tools (ticketing).

YOUR PROFILE

  • Bachelor or Master in Biomedical Engineering or equivalent.
  • 2 years experience in a similar position, ideally in a hospital environment.
  • Knowledge of Swiss public procurement, hospital infrastructure and medical device regulations.
  • Excellent skills in planning, negotiating and conducting projects.
  • Spirit of analysis and ability to work as a team.
  • Mastery of GMAO software and knowledge of MS Office office automation tools are required.
  • Project management training is an asset.
  • Mastery of French (oral and written) and technical English.

WE OFFER YOU

In addition to an attractive workstation in the heart of Lausanne, you will integrate a dynamic team that is looking forward to welcoming you into a human-sized structure.

You will benefit from attractive conditions of employment with, among other things, opportunities for continuing training and development throughout your career with us, as well as social benefits and other interesting benefits.

For an optimal balance between work and private life, we offer our employees various contractual models, including part-time and FLEXible contract. There is also the possibility to enjoy up to 7 weeks of holiday thanks to our offer "Congé-Plus".

In addition, employees working in uniform shall be granted 4 days of additional annual leave in proportion to the time of attendance.
