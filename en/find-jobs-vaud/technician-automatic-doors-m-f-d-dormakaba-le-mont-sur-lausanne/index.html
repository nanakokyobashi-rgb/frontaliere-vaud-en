Full description

Job overview

For the Automatic Door Systems department, we are looking for a Field Installer for the Vaud, Fribourg and Neuchâtel region.

What you'll do

  • Install automatic door systems.
  • Commission and adjust the installations.
  • Hand over the installations to clients and train them in their use.
  • Analyze and clarify the technical requirements of projects, both in advance and on site.
  • Provide support to service technicians as needed.
  • Take part in the on-call rotation, approximately once every 6 to 8 weeks

What we're looking for

  • A basic qualification with a Federal VET Diploma (CFC) as a metalworker, mechanic, assembly electrician, installation electrician, or equivalent technical training.
  • Experience in the construction industry.
  • Good command of standard computer tools (MS Office).
  • Excellent presentation and ease in communicating with customers.
  • Residence in French-speaking Switzerland, in one of the regions mentioned in the advertisement.
  • A good team spirit and the ability to manage priorities and workload effectively.
  • An autonomous, organized, structured, and customer-oriented personality.
  • A valid driving license.
  • Knowledge of German and/or English is an asset.

We offer

  • We offer attractive employment conditions with various benefits
  • Opportunities for partial remote work
  • We operate in an exciting technological environment
  • We are an international group with a family atmosphere
  • We attach great importance to promoting personal and professional development
