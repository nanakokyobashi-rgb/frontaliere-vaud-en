Production team leader 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Make Switzerland move with us. Production team leader Yverdon-les-Bains, 01.01.2027 or to be agreed, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you. We are the backbone of Switzerland and master the rail. With its employees, the Passenger Production Rolling Stock Maintenance business unit ensures the maintenance of trains and their components. The team, an integral part of this unit, actively participates in the repair and overhaul of mechanical components. As a team leader, you manage around twenty people and, with your team, you contribute directly to punctuality and customer satisfaction. In this stimulating role, you have the opportunity to make major changes and develop your production team. Working closely with your colleagues, you develop innovative solutions to address existing challenges and implement continuous improvements. To find out about social benefits, such as free GA and discounted travel abroad (also for the family), go to our website under Social benefits

What you can make move.

  • At the helm of production operations, you supervise and coordinate a mechanical team of around twenty people, guaranteeing quality and safety.
  • A strong team culture takes shape under your leadership and promotes collaboration and the involvement of each person.
  • Skills development is based on individualized training plans that stimulate autonomy, commitment and performance.
  • Working closely with internal and external partners, you deliver components on time and maintain high quality standards.
  • Continuous improvement is progressing through the application of lean management and the KAIZEN and 5S principles as well as the conduct of quality safety and ergonomics audits. What you bring.
  • Inclusive leadership and excellent communication skills help inspire, motivate and unite teams, while relying on an analytical mind, a solution-oriented approach and a sharp critical eye.
  • Strong team leadership skills are essential, with an orientation towards both achieving results and developing employees.
  • Mastery of MS Office and SAP tools, combined with good knowledge of production processes and a solid KAIZEN culture, constitutes an important prerequisite for this position.
  • Proven experience of several years in team leading in an industrial production environment is expected.
  • A four-year apprenticeship in the field of mechanics is required, ideally supplemented by training as a Swissmem team leader or equivalent. An ES process technician diploma is an additional asset.
  • Excellent command of oral and written French (level C2) is required, with ideally knowledge of oral German, at least at level A2.
