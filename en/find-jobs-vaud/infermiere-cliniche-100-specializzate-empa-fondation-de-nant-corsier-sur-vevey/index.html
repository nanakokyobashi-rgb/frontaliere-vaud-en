Full description

In order to support the development of clinical nursing expertise and to strengthen the quality of psychiatric care, you will join a team of ICLS already established within the Nant Foundation, which will compete for a post of

100% specialised clinical nurse (EMPA)

WE OFFER

The Nant Foundation is a private institution recognized in the public interest, mandated by the State to organize and provide psychiatric care in the Vaud region. Working within the Foundation is evolving in a stimulating environment and a dynamic working environment.

The Mobile Senior Psychiatry Team (EMPA) operates on a mandate from treating physicians, upon request from patients, health care teams or relatives for persons over 65 years of age in the Eastern Vaud area suffering from psychiatric and/or cognitive disorders, in order to facilitate access to care. To be a clinician nurse specialised in this division is to develop in a framework of work that motivates an interdisciplinary team. THE MISSION OF ICLS The mission of the specialist clinician nurse is to strengthen the quality, safety and excellence of nursing by integrating evidence and clinical expertise into practice. It supports the evolution of nursing practices by promoting the integration of new knowledge, innovation and links with academic circles and it supports interprofessional collaboration while contributing to the continuous improvement of practices for the benefit of patients, teams and the institution. The activity will be divided as follows: 40% will be devoted to direct clinical care of patients, while the remaining 60% will be dedicated to other tasks assigned to ICLS, as described above. WE SEEK A nurse who holds a Bachelor's degree in nursing with Red Cross recognition (or equivalent) and a Master's degree in nursing and has a solid clinical experience, ideally in psychiatry.

We are looking for a person with a spirit of analysis and scientific rigour, who will be able to accompany and grow the teams with pragmatism and benevolence. Finally, we want to engage a mission motivated by the Foundation's mission and love the work as a multidisciplinary team.
