Full description

The Riviera-Chablais Hospital, Vaud-Valais (HRC), is a reference intercantonal hospital, which brings together more than 2,500 collaborators. Rennaz Hospital has over 350 acute care beds. It is complemented by two Geriatric and Rehabilitation Clinics and two medical clinics. All employees invest on a daily basis to provide quality care for patients, embodying the institution's values of authenticity, exemplaryness and innovation.

In order to strengthen its Invoicing unit within the Patient Administrative Management department, we are looking for one/one:

80-100% billing specialist

The main tasks of this post are

  • Check and enter benefits, edit invoices
  • Ensure quality support for billing returns
  • Participate in invoicing unit business processes
  • Propose any measure of improvement useful to the organisation of the unit
  • Contribute to the release/control of certain billing sectors
  • Produce ad hoc analyses on the billing scope
  • To be a trade reference for specific questions related to the field
  • Ensure good collaboration with insurers and other guarantors

We ask

  • SWC training of trade employee, or similar
  • An experience of at least 5 years in hospital billing
  • Excellent knowledge of the tariff systems required (Tardoc, SwissDRG, ST-Reha,..)
  • Mastery of standard office automation tools (MS Office) and mandatory Opal knowledge
  • A good spirit of analysis, reflection and methodology
  • A dynamic personality with a good integration capacity in a team

We offer

The HRC offers great job opportunities and career development in all its fields of activity: medical, nursing, medico-technical, hotel, administrative, technical, logistics. In a desire for continuous improvement, skills development is at the heart of the HRC's concerns. The institution offers its employees access to training both internally and externally.

In 2021, the HRC was twice ranked among the best employers in French-speaking Switzerland by the magazine Bilan and the newspaper Le Temps , placing it as a leading institution.
