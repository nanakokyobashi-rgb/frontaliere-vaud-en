Full description

To strengthen our Digital Solutions department, we are looking for a:

Business Analyst intern (6 months, IDEAL START DATE: JANUARY 2027)

YOUR ROLE

As Business Analyst Intern you work day to day with the Product Lead Digital and the product owners, as an embedded member of the team.

You give the portfolio a consistent analytical layer so that product owners can focus on prioritisation and customer engagement.

  • Gather, structure and document functional and non functional requirements with customers and internal stakeholders.
  • Run gap analyses between customer specifications, tender documents and the existing products.
  • Map business processes and user journeys for public sector use cases (payments, subsidies, oversight, enforcement).
  • Support backlog preparation: user stories, acceptance criteria, prioritisation inputs.
  • Carry out market, competitor and regulatory research to feed product strategy.
  • Contribute to customer workshops, demos and product documentation.
  • Help reimagine how the team works with AI: identify where AI agents and tooling can take over repetitive analysis, documentation and reporting work, then design and test the new workflows.

YOUR PROFILE

  • Master's student or recent graduate in business, engineering, information systems, public policy or similar.
  • Structured, analytical and curious, able to turn ambiguous input into clear written analysis.
  • Comfortable with digital products, basic data handling and AI tools; agile or product management exposure is a plus.
  • Fluent in English
  • French is an asset.
  • Discretion and sound judgement when handling confidential information.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

  • Sovereignty in practice. How complex, multi stakeholder public sector systems are designed, sold and delivered.
  • Daily access to decision makers. Direct exposure to product decisions, customer requirement discussions and prioritisation.
  • Scale up rhythm. Fast moving team inside an established international company, with real ownership of your deliverables.
  • AI native ways of working. Hands on experience redesigning product and delivery processes around AI agents and tooling, a skill set in high demand.
  • Confidentiality and stakeholder management. Working with governments on sensitive projects builds a level of professional maturity few internships offer.
