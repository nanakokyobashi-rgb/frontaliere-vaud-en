Location: Vevey, Switzerland Work model: Hybrid / Full time Contract type / duration: Permanent

Be a force for good and accelerate change that improves lives and the planet As a Head of Swiss Tax at Nestle, your mission is to lead Nestlé’s Swiss Tax function and shape a sustainable tax strategy that supports our Swiss operations and global business objectives. You’ll make a difference by collaborating closely with senior business leaders, the Global Tax leadership team, Finance, Legal, Treasury and key stakeholders across Nestlé. This is your opportunity to take ownership, grow your expertise, and deliver meaningful results that make a difference at scale.

How you’ll make an impact as a Head of Swiss Tax

  • Leading Nestlé’s Swiss Tax function and shaping a forward-looking tax strategy that balances business objectives, regulatory requirements and effective risk management.
  • Driving strategic tax advice for major transactions, acquisitions, restructurings, financing arrangements and business transformations, ensuring Swiss tax considerations are embedded in key decisions.
  • Shaping Nestlé’s position on Swiss and international tax developments, including OECD Pillar Two and Swiss minimum taxation, while representing the company with tax authorities, policymakers and industry associations.
  • Managing Nestlé Group’s Swiss tax position by anticipating the impact of business developments, transactions and regulatory changes on effective tax rates, cash taxes, reporting and compliance.
  • Building trusted relationships across senior leadership and key corporate functions, translating complex tax matters into clear business implications, options and recommendations.
  • Developing a high-performing Swiss Tax team through coaching, empowerment and capability building, including support towards the Swiss Certified Tax Expert qualification.

What will make you successful

  • Bringing extensive, senior-level experience and a proven track record in Swiss corporate income tax, withholding tax, stamp duties, international tax, tax treaties, transfer pricing and tax accounting, gained in a multinational group or leading tax advisory firm, and ideally supported by the Swiss Certified Tax Expert qualification or an equivalent credential.
  • Applying deep knowledge of OECD Pillar Two and global minimum taxation, including QDMTT, GloBE, Safe Harbour and deferred tax considerations, and translating regulatory developments into effective tax rate, cash tax and compliance implications.
  • Leading complex Swiss tax planning, M&A, restructuring, financing and transformation matters, combining strong commercial judgement with effective tax risk management and cross-functional project leadership.
  • Influencing senior executives, tax authorities, policymakers, business associations and international bodies through credible technical expertise, strong negotiation and advocacy skills, and clear, executive-ready recommendations.
  • Communicating fluently in English and French, with German strongly preferred, and explaining complex tax matters clearly to both specialist and non-specialist audiences.
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.
