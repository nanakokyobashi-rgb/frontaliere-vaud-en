Your Mission Within the team, you will participate in the production and distribution of audiovisual and digital content. As such, you will be led to: Realize the de-rushing, editing, post-production and captioning of different video formats. Ensure the taking of images and sound during shootings. Participate in editorial design by developing moodboards and performing visual research. Contribute to the preparation and smooth running of the shoots by providing technical and logistical support.
