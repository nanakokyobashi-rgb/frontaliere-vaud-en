Full description

As a trainee in the Community Health Directorate, you will help manage the entity's partnership with external community health providers. You will be able to assist a mission manager in his or her activities, collaborate in the follow-up of key files related to partners and support the field of the first line of care in the conduct of projects.

  • Contribute to the operational coordination and monitoring of partner mandates
  • Participate in the development of multi-year benefit contracts and annual amendments
  • Monitor and contribute to the periodic evaluation of contracts
  • Contribute to the implementation of the specific mandates related to the independent OSADs and nurses
  • Contribute to the implementation of the DGS strategy on front line care actors
  • Conduct or follow specific analyses on request (organizational, financial, strategic)
  • Support the preparation, organization and follow-up of coordination sessions between DGS and partners For further information, please consult the Terms of Reference by clicking here or contact Katrin Wirsching, Deputy Director, Community Health Directorate, by email at katrin.wirsching@vd.ch .

Profile sought: The course is intended for a person in the course of the Master's or Post Master's degree, in one of the following areas:

  • Public health
  • Management or public administration (Public management, Management of health institutions, Management of social and medico-social organizations)
  • Political science (ideally with specialization in public policy, social policy or health)
  • Social science oriented public policy or health systems
  • Health Economics (Optional) In order to have all the elements necessary to analyze your file, we would like to thank you for sending us, in addition to your CV, a letter of motivation, your diplomas, as well as your certificates of employment if necessary. In case of interest, please send us your file in one pdf.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
