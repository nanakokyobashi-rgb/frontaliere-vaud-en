Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Put your managerial ambitions at the service of a healthy and sustainable performance by joining the canton of Vaud's first universal bank, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Internal Audit department, we are looking for one or more:

Announcement Responsible ICT & Cyber Auditor - Head of Sector Your main missions

  • ensure the management of a sector composed of auditors managers, ICT & cyber risk management experts and senior data analysts, ensuring their development of skills and achieving their professional goals
  • planning, conducting and overseeing the execution of audit and advisory missions dealing with ICT and cyber risk management, business continuity and operational resilience of the BCV Group, as well as overseeing the teams involved in these missions
  • ensuring the presence of ICT & cyber skills in cross-cutting audits
  • to provide assurance to the High Courts on ICT and cyber risk management in the implementation and reporting of audit missions
  • contribute to the continuous modernisation of audit techniques (data analytics, artificial intelligence, continuous auditing)
  • contribute to regulatory, methodological and technological monitoring, as well as to the monitoring of ICT & cyber projects and major developments within the Group, and assess their impact on the Audit Universe and the multi-annual audit plan
  • represent the Internal Audit with internal stakeholders, as well as with the branch's umbrella bodies. Your profile
  • advanced computer and/or information security training EPFL/HES, CISSP certification(s), CCSP, CISA and/or CISM an asset
  • experience of at least 10 years in the areas of ICT & Cyber banking audit and/or in ICT & cyber business risk management
  • excellent knowledge of applicable regulatory requirements, as well as banking applications and systems supporting them
  • proven practice in data analytics techniques
  • experience in line management
  • ability to prioritize in a demanding environment
  • a spirit of synthesis and analysis in a complex and constantly evolving environment
  • rigor, courage, sense of judgment and ability to challenge constructively
  • relational ease, excellent oral and written communication skills, perfect mastery of French.

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
