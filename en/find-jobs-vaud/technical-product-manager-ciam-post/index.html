Full description

Are you passionate about developing secure, scalable, and customer-centric solutions for digital identities? Do you enjoy working at the interface of business, technology, security, and customer experience? As a Technical Product Manager for Customer Identity & Access Management (CIAM), you will play a decisive role in shaping the future of customer authentication, authorization, and digital identity services in our organization.

Together with you, we will create the foundation for seamless customer access, trustworthy digital interactions, and a secure digital ecosystem that supports our strategic business objectives.

What you can achieve

  • As Technical Product Manager for the Customer Identity & Access Management platform, you will be responsible for defining and driving the vision, roadmap, and further development of our CIAM services.
  • You ensure that customers can access our digital services securely, seamlessly, and efficiently by continuously improving the functions for authentication, authorization, registration, and the Identity Lifecycle.
  • Together with the Product Owners on the business side, you will act as the central point of contact for business stakeholders, architects, security specialists, development teams, and external partners on all CIAM-related topics.
  • You define and manage product requirements, translate business needs into technical solutions, and formulate epics, user stories, acceptance criteria, and functional specifications.
  • As Epic Owner, you manage CIAM-related initiatives from ideation and business analysis through implementation and testing to successful rollout.
  • You consistently align the CIAM solutions with the company-wide security strategies and customer experience goals.
  • You actively manage the Product Backlog, prioritize features based on business value and risk, and ensure transparency regarding roadmap progress and delivery commitments.

What you bring

  • You have in-depth IT knowledge and several years of professional experience in the fields of Identity & Access Management (IAM) and Customer Identity & Access Management (CIAM).
  • You have demonstrable experience as a Technical Product Manager, Product Owner, Business Analyst, or in a comparable role in complex corporate environments.
  • A university or university of applied sciences degree in computer science, information technology, Cybersecurity, business informatics or a comparable field, as well as relevant certifications or further training, complete your profile.
  • You successfully apply a solid understanding of CIAM architectures, identity governance, authentication and authorization mechanisms, identity lifecycle processes and access control concepts in practice.
  • You distinguish yourself through personal responsibility, customer orientation and a proactive approach to solving problems and promoting innovation.
  • You impress in stakeholder management with your strong communication and coordination skills.
  • You communicate well orally and in writing in one national language, have a good command of a second national language and good English skills.

To promote gender diversity, we particularly welcome applications from women.
