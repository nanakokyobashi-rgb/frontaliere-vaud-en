100%, Lausanne / Smart Working

Shape your future with us!

You want to use your construction expertise from a new perspective? With us, you assess the risks associated with construction projects, help design appropriate insurance solutions, and support our partners throughout the process. To monitor the market in French-speaking Switzerland, we are looking for a person with solid building knowledge, who likes to take responsibility and wants to develop his professional skills. Even if you don't yet have experience in building insurance, we are accompanying you in this area to enable you to develop in a dynamic and responsible environment.

In the Property & Casualty sector, you offer our private and professional customers more freedom, thanks to innovative products, irreproachable quality services, and simple digital processes. We offer our customers a wide range of standard and individual insurances, legal protection solutions as well as financial products in the field of credit and guarantee.

What's waiting for you

  • You establish offers for construction insurance and third-party liability insurance of the contractor
  • You are assessing the risks for buildings and civil engineering works in Switzerland
  • You monitor the market in French-speaking Switzerland and support our distribution partners thanks to your expertise in construction insurance
  • You intervene during internal and external events or trainings to share your know-how
  • You use your technical expertise in construction, for example to answer technical questions from our disaster service
  • You participate in product development and bring new ideas

What you bring

  • You ideally have a university degree in architecture or civil engineering. Other technical training in the field of construction - for example as construction manager, construction manager or ES technician - is also welcome.
  • You have at least three years of relevant professional experience and solid practical knowledge in construction
  • You have perfect command of French and good knowledge of German
  • You work reliably and autonomously, making well-founded and convincing decisions with your sense of negotiation and confidence.
  • You prefer a customer-oriented approach, solution and sales, and you enjoy designing tailored insurance solutions
  • Knowledge in the insurance sector is an asset, but it is not essential. Experience in subscription or claims management, ideally in the field of construction insurance, is particularly appreciated

The right balance at work: free health check-ups, health weeks every year, varied physical activity offers (on-line and on-site), massage offers, rest rooms, Employee Care, online health portal, various seminars and events around health prevention and mental health

Development: three days to train individually, wide learning and development offer, free coaching, financial participation in continuing training, promotion of internal mobility, offers of talent development, seminars for the over 55 years (AXA Horizon), engagement in communities on different themes, participation in innovative projects (Innoday, AXA Hackathon, UnConference), two days dedicated to volunteer missions (environment and social)

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed.
