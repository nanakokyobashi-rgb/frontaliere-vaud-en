Role overview

Mission

The Program Director / Scientific Programme Lead is responsible for the scientific coordination, implementation and day-to-day management of the Genome of Switzerland (GoS) programme.

Main responsibilities

  • Main duties and responsibilities
  • Coordinate the scientific and operational implementation, future development and national scale-up of the Genome of Switzerland programme.
  • Support the PI/Academic Director(s) in defining scientific priorities and programme strategy.
  • Coordinate activities across participating institutions and project stakeholders.
  • Oversee the generation, quality control and analysis of genomic datasets.
  • Supervise project bioinformaticians and data analysts.
  • Coordinate interactions with national and international initiatives, including Genome of Europe and related genomic data-sharing infrastructures.
  • Contribute to data governance, ethics, regulatory and data-sharing activities.
  • Prepare scientific reports, presentations and project deliverables.

Application process

  • PhD in genetics, genomics, bioinformatics, molecular biology or a related field.
  • Proven experience in large-scale human genomics and population genomics projects.

  • Experience coordinating multidisciplinary projects involving academic, clinical, technical and institutional stakeholders.
  • Knowledge of genomic data governance, ethics, regulatory frameworks and controlled-access data sharing.
  • Experience with national and international precision medicine initiatives and genomic infrastructures.
