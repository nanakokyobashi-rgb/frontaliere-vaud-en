Full description

The cooperative ZFV-Unternehmungen (ZFV) creates, in collaboration with its partners, inspiring meeting places that are enthusiastic about their hospitality and authentic experiences. Founded in 1894 by visionary Zuricho bourgeois women, the ZFV still attaches particular importance to innovation and sustainability. In more than 200 establishments in Switzerland, ZFV employees meet the needs of customers and guests. Areas of activity include restaurants, refectories, staff restaurants, hotels, mobile catering solutions, catering, bakeries and crèches. The cooperative actively contributes to shaping society and takes a stand for the environment, equal opportunities and inclusion. She lives hospitality for a society in which we all want to live. Unisanté in Epalinges, managed by ZFV since October 2025, is open five days a week, from 08:00 to 17:00. This property has 160 seating positions in the restaurant, serves about 180 meals a day and is composed of a team of 4 people. There are two different and varied menus each day, as well as a buffet of salads, desserts and snacks. A catering service for events is also available upon request.
