Full description

VZ VermögensZentrum is Switzerland's leading independent financial services provider and the leading financial advisor. That is what makes our success and strong growth. In order to strengthen our advisory services for our clients, we are looking for a committed personality for our Lausanne branch.

  • You team up with the training specialist and the assist on a daily basis in his missions.
  • In a small team, you contribute to the organization and administration of our VZ Academy in Western Switzerland. This includes the integration days of new collaborators, the Trainee program for training advisors, communication and leadership workshops, and other training.
  • You are in constant contact with our employees and work closely with line managers and stakeholders.
  • You respond to routine administrative requests and take over during absences from the training specialist.
  • You manage registrations, confirmations, changes and lists of participants, update calendars and send invitations and reminders.
  • You help ensure the quality of our eduQua certified training.
  • You update and classify validated media, reread documents, check the consistency of dates, references, information and versions and perform the first translations.
  • SWC or equivalent training and administrative or organizational experience. Experience in administering training or events is an asset.
  • Good mastery of Microsoft 365 tools, including Outlook, Excel, Word and Teams, ease with digital platforms and interest in artificial intelligence tools, including Copilot.
  • Good knowledge of German for internal communication.
  • Excellent organisational capabilities and high autonomy, rigour and reliability in the monitoring of several deadlines.
  • Proactive spirit and pragmatic approach.
  • Pleasure to exchange with others and common sense of contact.
  • We are a motivated team, we support each other and maintain relationships based on respect and esteem.
  • We are proud of our dynamic corporate culture with short decision-making processes.
  • You work in an informal and open environment in downtown Lausanne.
  • Our satisfaction surveys regularly show that our employees regard VZ as a particularly positive working environment, in which they can fully flourish. Join us now!
