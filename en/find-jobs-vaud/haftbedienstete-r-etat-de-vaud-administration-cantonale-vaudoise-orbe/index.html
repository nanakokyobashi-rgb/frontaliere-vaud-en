Full description

In your capacity as a detention officer in one of our six cantonal establishments, you are operating in a demanding but meaningful environment with a strong team spirit. Your activity contributes to the implementation of the two missions entrusted to the prison service: to provide supervision and supervision of detainees and to promote their reintegration while at the same time acting in accordance with our values, namely "Loyality, Solidarity, Credibility". Through your commitment, you participate in protecting the population. You will develop your skills through, among other things, on-the-job training to obtain the Federal Certificate of Detention Officer.

  • Day and night surveillance of detainees
  • Accompaniment of detained persons in their daily activities
  • Listening, support and information to detained persons
  • Securing places and persons
  • Applying discipline, regulations and hygiene standards while ensuring respect for human dignity For more information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact our recruitment sector at 021 338 44 17.
  • Have completed 24 years of age
  • Be eligible for an SWC of at least 3 years of age or equivalent
  • Federal patent of detention officer or undertake to obtain it by following on-the-job training
  • Mastering of the usual computer tools
  • Mastering of the French language both orally and in writing. Knowledge of foreign languages, an asset
  • At ease in human contacts, empathy, sense of communication and negotiation
  • Good mental and physical skills allowing you to adapt to irregular schedules and to manage sometimes difficult situations or conflicts while keeping a distance adapted to your function
  • Ability to manage stress. Sense of observation, analysis and synthesis.
