Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Join the first universal bank in the canton of Vaud, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Services division, we are looking for one or more:

Announcement Specialist Manager Values and Securities Transfers Your main missions:

  • carry out all stages of transactions related to the area of securities, deposits and transfers of securities free of payment. Analyze and control the management of data related to values, especially the course of values.
  • ensure the treatment of complex and unusual operations in its field of activity. Managing suspense and research with counterparties,
  • propose solutions and respond to requests from internal and external clients,
  • participate in the implementation of computer adaptations (in particular through trade tests),
  • participate in the development of new processes (including expressions of need) and in the development and updating of working procedures. Your profile:
  • banking training,
  • experience in the field of securities transfer and free payment,
  • excellent control of interbank messages (SWIFT) and SIX ID portal,
  • sense of organisation, adaptability and team spirit,
  • precision, rigour, proactivity, sense of detail,
  • mastery of French, good knowledge of English (level B2). German is an asset.

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write together three stories, that of your career, that of your bank, that of our region.
