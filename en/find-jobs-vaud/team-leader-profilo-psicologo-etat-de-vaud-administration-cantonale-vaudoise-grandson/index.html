Full description

You have an interest in team leadership, are sensitive to the challenges in the field of specialized pedagogy and want to help children and young people in difficulty in the Vaudian training system. Join us to strengthen our Grandson-Ste-Croix team!

  • Ensure the responsibility of a team, develop the skills of collaborators and promote the multidisciplinary work of the team. Participate in piloting the region and enforce decisions.
  • Support the school's training mission by providing specific skills and knowledge within the school. Provide specific assistance to school children and adolescents who have emotional, relational, learning, behavioural or integration difficulties with a view to their development and social, educational and vocational integration.
  • Collaborate with parents, school stakeholders and other stakeholders and provide specific knowledge in relational, learning, behaviour or development that allows actions or adjustments of the school context for the benefit of the child or group of children in difficulty. Possible disclosure of criminal history is required for this function. Please send us a complete application package in a single PDF consisting of diplomas and certificates. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Emmanuelle Camci-Aeschlimann, Regional Manager, who is happy to be at your disposal at 024 557 79 93.
  • Master's degree in psychology and post-graduate in psychology required (specialization of children and adolescents, psychotherapy, clinical psychology or neuropsychology).
  • Additional training certified in psychology (min.15 ECTS credits) or additional training not certified in the field concerned (of an extent equivalent to the certifying training) and validated by the service or training title in a related field (education, psychology, sociology, etc.)
  • Work experience of at least 5 years, interest in multidisciplinary work and group work. Training in desired team driving.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
