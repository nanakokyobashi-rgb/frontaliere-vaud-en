Full description

Are you motivated to take on a new challenge in an interdisciplinary team? Do you have a special interest in the young adult population, psychotherapy and community care? So you are the person we need to complete our Youth Adult Program (YAP) team for a year as a

Psychologist assistant at 40% THE MISSION The Young Adults Program psychologists provide outpatient psychological and psychotherapeutic services to the young adult population in the Vaud area. You will participate in the prevention and early management of emerging psychiatric disorders, as well as psychotherapeutic follow-up of patients. You will have to work closely with the different professionals in the care network and intervene in the community.

WE OFFER

The Young Adults Programme (YAP) is an outpatient care facility for the population aged 18 to 30 years and is an integral part of the Community Facilities Unit (CD) of the Nant Foundation. It consists of a component for the prevention and early intervention of psychosis, a longer-term psychotherapeutic component and a short intervention component. It promotes alternatives to hospitalization and community care. Working within this team is a diverse clinical activity within a dynamic framework of interdisciplinarity and collaboration with the care network. WE SEEK A psychologist who holds a Master's degree in Psychology (or an equivalent title recognized by PsyCo or OFSP), engaged or in the process of becoming involved in post-graduate training. In addition, good relational, communication, professional ethics and adaptation skills are essential to work in our institution as a psychologist. We want to hire a person motivated by the mission of the Young Adults Program and loving the work as an interdisciplinary team within an institutional framework.
