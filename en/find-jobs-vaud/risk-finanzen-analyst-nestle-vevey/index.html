Be a force for good and accelerate change that improves lives and the planet

As a Risk Finance Analyst, your mission is to support Intercona Re in strengthening financial reporting, risk finance processes and data-driven insights across insurance activities. You’ll make a difference by collaborating closely with Group Risk Services, finance partners and risk management teams. This is your opportunity to take ownership, grow your expertise, and deliver meaningful results that make a difference at scale.

How you’ll make an impact as a Risk Finance Analyst

  • Applying quantitative analysis to underwriting assessments of insurance programs and actuarial reviews.
  • Strengthening the accuracy and reliability of quarterly and annual financial accounts by reviewing actuarial reserving assumptions.
  • Supporting budgeting, forecasting and financial planning for internal reinsurance and global insurance activities.
  • Delivering clear financial analysis that helps teams understand performance, costs and business trends.
  • Building meaningful dashboards and reporting tools that improve visibility and decision-making.
  • Contributing to quantitative insurance and risk assessments with accurate data, analysis and insights.

What will make you successful

  • Bringing strong financial analysis skills and the ability to translate data into practical insights.
  • Applying solid finance, accounting or actuarial knowledge to support accurate reporting and planning.
  • Using digital tools such as Excel, PowerPoint and Power BI to simplify reporting and improve visibility.
  • Collaborating with curiosity, flexibility and a solution-focused mindset across teams and topics.
  • Communicating fluently in English.
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.
