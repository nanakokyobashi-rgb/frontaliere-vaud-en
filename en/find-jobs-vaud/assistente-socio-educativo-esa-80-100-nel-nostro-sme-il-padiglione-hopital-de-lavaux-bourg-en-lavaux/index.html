Full description

The Lavaux Hospital is a private foundation recognized in the public interest, a member of the Vaudian Hospital Federation. Located in Cully (Bourg-en-Lavaux), in the heart of the vineyards and close to public transport, school and Lavaux day care. An expansion project is planned for 2027, with services being moved to new premises currently under construction at the hospital site. This project will, in particular, double the capacity of our EMS and open up good prospects for development. Our EMS is a real place of life, where every resident can find his place, maintain his social ties and enjoy a daily life full of meetings, exchanges and activities. To support this vision, all hospital services work hand in hand, in a complementary and interdisciplinary approach, for the well-being and quality of life of residents. In order to strengthen our teams, we are looking for a socio-educational assistant, available from February 2027. Mission

  • Encourage and develop activities tailored to the needs of residents and in collaboration with them.
  • Encourage residents to participate in the social and cultural life of the institution.
  • Distribution and accompaniment of residents during meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack) in a friendly and attentive atmosphere.
  • Work with the interdisciplinary team.
  • To act by keeping in mind the principle of the promotion of autonomy, decision-making power and independence of everyone.
  • Promote a safe, stimulating and caring environment for all residents.

Your profile Technical expertise

  • SWC of socio-educational assistant (ESA) or title deemed equivalent.
  • Experience with the elderly, ideally in EMS.
  • Interest in the animation and accompaniment of elderly people.
  • Experience in collaboration with interdisciplinary teams.
  • Good control of common computer tools.
  • Driver's license B, an asset.

Human skills

  • Excellent relational qualities and listening.
  • Ability to create a relationship of trust with residents and their relatives.
  • Creativity, dynamism and ability to offer activities tailored to each individual's needs.
  • Initiative and responsibility.
  • Flexibility and adaptability to different situations.
  • A sense of teamwork and interprofessional collaboration.
  • Adherence to the values of respect, benevolence and promotion of autonomy.

We offer

  • A stimulating working environment within a human-sized institution.
  • A committed team that focuses on collaboration, proximity and quality of support.
  • The opportunity to contribute actively to the life and well-being of residents.
  • The opportunity to participate in the development of our EMS as part of its future expansion.
  • Opportunities for continuing training and professional development.
  • Working conditions governed by the collective labour agreement for the public health sector (CCT San Vaud).

Start date: February 2027
