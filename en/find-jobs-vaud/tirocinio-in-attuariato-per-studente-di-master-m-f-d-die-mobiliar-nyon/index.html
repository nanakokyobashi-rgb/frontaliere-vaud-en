Full description

What lies ahead

Do you want to practice your knowledge of actuarial science in a specialized and collaborative team? Join our Risk Management & Reporting team at Prévoyance and take advantage of this 5-month internship to write your master's degree in company.

What you can do with us

Within the team, you are involved in the analysis and development of actuarial models used in our life insurance products. You are contributing in practice to the actuarial modeling of disability cases pending payment as part of your master's work.

You analyze data, document actuarial processes and contribute to the continuous improvement of the tools and methods used by the team. Depending on your profile, you also have the opportunity to develop Python solutions or other quantitative analysis tools.

In collaboration with actuaries, product specialists and business teams, you contribute to concrete projects and develop a thorough understanding of the operation of life insurance and private insurance.

What you bring

  • Master's degree in Actuarial Sciences, Mathematics, Quantitative Finance or Training deemed to be equivalent in progress
  • Interest in life insurance, foresight and quantitative analysis
  • Good programming knowledge, ideally Python or R
  • Analytical capacity, rigour and synthesis
  • Curious, collaborative and proactive personality
  • Excellent command of French; knowledge in German is an asset

Working at home

The Actuariat Risk Management & Reporting team is composed of actuaries and specialists who collaborate closely in a professional and open atmosphere. Regular exchanges and knowledge sharing are an integral part of how we work.

Your activity rate is negotiable between 80% and 100%. The workplace is in Nyon and you benefit from a flexible work model as well as telework opportunities when the activity allows.

We offer you an environment conducive to learning, responsibilities adapted to your level of experience and the opportunity to develop your skills alongside professionals and professionals in the actuarial field. This mission is an excellent bridge between study and practice.
