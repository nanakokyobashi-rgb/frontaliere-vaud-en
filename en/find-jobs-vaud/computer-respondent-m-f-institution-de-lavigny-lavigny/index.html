Full description

IT respondent (M/F), 100% Deployed at several sites, the Lavigny Institution consists of a neurological hospital with outpatient consultations at two sites, a housing department for adults with disabilities, intellectual impairment or physical problems, a specialised school and a workshop and vocational training sector. In order to strengthen our IT team, we are looking for a versatile IT answer, with a common sense of service and a strong interest in user support and IT infrastructure management. At the heart of our institution's activities, you ensure the proper functioning of IT tools, support employees in their daily use and contribute to the evolution of our IT environment, in close collaboration with our technical partners, including FHVI.

IT respondent (M/F)

Your missions As a local computer reference person, you have the following responsibilities: User support and support

  • Provide first-level computer support and support staff in resolving incidents and difficulties encountered on a daily basis
  • Manage user accounts, accesses (Active Directory) and computer profiles
  • Advise users in the use of standard and specific Windows software, Microsoft 365 and common and specialized office automation tools (Word, Excel, Outlook, Opale), as well as in the management and storage of files
  • Participate in the computer training of new employees and support users when introducing new tools
  • Identify recurring problems and encourage the consolidation of similar applications to optimize interventions

Management of IT and equipment

  • Monitoring the computer inventory in collaboration with FHVI
  • Coordinate acquisitions of computer equipment within allocated budgets
  • Ensure replacement of obsolete equipment and software
  • Participate in managing access rights to directories and print media
  • Updating internal directories and telephone lists

Infrastructure and technical environment

  • Participate in the diagnosis, monitoring and improvement of LAN and WLAN network infrastructure
  • Contribute to the installation and setting of network equipment (switches, Wi-Fi terminals, etc.)
  • Participate in the installation, configuration and maintenance of systems (Windows Server, Linux) and IT servers
  • Contribute to backup monitoring and restoration operations
  • Participate in documentation of technical facilities, configurations and procedures
  • Coordinate external technicians and relay with FHVI

Cross-cutting projects and activities

  • Participate in the monitoring of communications technology projects, installations and developments
  • To provide, on a mandate, support for the institution's multimedia activities
  • Ensure compliance with institutional guidelines and IT procedures

Your profile

  • SWC with "Operation and infrastructure" orientation, or training deemed equivalent
  • Good control of computer support and user environment
  • Interest in network infrastructure, computer systems and equipment
  • Ability to diagnose incidents and coordinate technical interventions
  • Organizational sense, autonomy and initiative
  • Relationship strength and ability to communicate with people with different profiles
  • Service, patience and pedagogy guidance in user support
  • Team spirit and ability to collaborate with external IT partners

We offer you

  • A versatile and varied function, based on the collaboration and values of the Institution
  • A role of proximity and contact with all services
  • Diverse responsibilities, combining user support, IT management and technical infrastructure
  • The social benefits of a large institution (staff restaurant, cafeteria and employee prices, free parking, public transport costs, etc.)
  • A salary and social benefits in line with the function and standards of the CTC of the public health sector in the Vaud region.

Entry into office: 1 February 2026 or to be agreed Activity rate: 100% Place of work: Lavigny
