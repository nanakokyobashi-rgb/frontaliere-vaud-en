Do you have a heart for the elderly? Is the profession of caregiver a true vocation? Do you want to put your skills and experience to use in a meaningful mission and contribute concretely to the well-being of our guests? Then you are the person we are looking for!

For Tertianum Le Bourg in the Chablais Vaudois region, we are looking for immediately or to be agreed an

Community Health Care Assistant ASSC 80-100%

Your tasks

  • Supervision and support of assigned guests
  • Support for personal care, movements, and other daily activities. Organization and proposal of daily activities for our guests.
  • Handling tasks as a reference person for accommodation on instructions
  • Participation in care reports and transmission of important observations
  • Writing care notes in our CareCoach care documentation system (possible on the go with the CareCoach smartphone)
  • Help and participation in the organization of logistical and household activities

Your profile

  • Completed training as a Community Health Care Assistant ASSC
  • Several years of professional experience in care is an advantage
  • Warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoyment of tackling various challenges
  • Good knowledge of spoken and written French

  • Internal and external continuing education opportunities
  • An additional day off during the birthday month

  • Various fleet discounts
