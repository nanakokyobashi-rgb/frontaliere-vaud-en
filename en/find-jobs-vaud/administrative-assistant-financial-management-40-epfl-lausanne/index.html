Full description

Mission

As an Administrative Assistant, you provide administrative and financial support to the SV-IN Infrastructure Team. You contribute to the administrative and financial management of the unit, including the preparation and monitoring of the budget, the preparation of financial reports, the monitoring of accounts and the management of the credit card.

You will join the SV-IN Infrastructure team, made up of about 30 collaborators, in a dynamic and multidisciplinary environment.

Main tasks and responsibilities

  • Preparation and monitoring of the unit budget: budget preparation, expenditure monitoring, landing forecasts and annual closure work.
  • Development and monitoring of financial reporting and key indicators (KPIs).
  • Monitoring of accounts: monitoring and processing of vendor invoices and monitoring of service fund planning.
  • Credit card management and monthly allocation of expenses.
  • Administrative support for the team and communication with users, in French and English.
  • Seizing and tracking scientific orders.

Profile

  • SWC of trade employee or training deemed equivalent, supplemented by several years of experience in a similar function.
  • Strong experience in financial and budgetary management.
  • Very good command of French and English.
  • Excellent sense of organization, rigour and attention to detail.
  • Autonomy, reliability and ease in collaboration with different interlocutors.
  • An experience in an academic environment or a knowledge of EPFL is an asset.
