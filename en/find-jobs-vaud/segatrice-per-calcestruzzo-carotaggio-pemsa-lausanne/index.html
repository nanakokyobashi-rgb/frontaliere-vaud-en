Your assignment

As a step-by-step cutter/cartator, you will work in construction sites, renovation, civil engineering and demolition. Real specialist in cutting and drilling, perform high-precision interventions in compliance with projects, safety standards and quality requirements.

Your main tasks are as follows

  • Perform wall cutting works, floor cutting, cable cutting and carroting on reinforced concrete, masonry and other materials.
  • Perform the necessary perforations for the passage of technical networks (heating, ventilation, hydraulic systems, electricity, etc.).
  • Install, adjust and use the different cutting and caring machines.
  • Read and interpret executive designs to ensure precise interventions.
  • Ensure ordinary maintenance and care of the equipment used.
  • Respect the safety instructions and SUVA standards in force.
  • Working in collaboration with the different professional figures present at the construction site.
  • Ensure accurate, accurate and customer-friendly work.

And now we're talking about you?

  • You have gained a proven experience as a concrete segator, carrot or in a similar job.
  • Master the different techniques of cutting and caring of reinforced concrete.
  • You can read the projects and work with precision and autonomy.
  • You are familiar with the use and maintenance of specific machines of the craft.
  • Demonstration, meticulousness and an excellent sense of organization.
  • You appreciate teamwork while being able to operate independently.
  • You have category B driving license (reliable).
  • A safety training or certification (SUVA) is a real advantage.
  • You are motivated, punctual and attentive to providing quality work.

Interested? Do you love and master your profession and want to put your skills in the service of projects that really matter? Then this offer is for you. From us, your expertise makes a difference and directly contributes to the success of the yards.
