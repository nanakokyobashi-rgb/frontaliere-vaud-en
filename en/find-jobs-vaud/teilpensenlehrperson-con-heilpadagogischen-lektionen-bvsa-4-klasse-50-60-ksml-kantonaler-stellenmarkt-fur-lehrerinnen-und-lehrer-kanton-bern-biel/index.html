Full description

Portrait Biel, the city on the beautiful lake, is looking for you as a teacher. Find a lively job at Bieler schools in our bilingual city!

Our school unit consists of the two locations Bözingen and Champagne. Bözingen-Champagne is a city quarter with various recreational areas (forest, zoo, pigeon hole gorge, shooting island). Both locations are easily accessible by public transport as well as by car.

At our school unit we lead 12 primary classes in Bözingen and 6 primary classes in Champagne. We also have 6 kindergartens.

Tasks

They teach 5 lessons in 4th grade (Math, Franz, Sport, BG, NMG), 3 Lect. eu, 3 Lect. IF & 5 lessons BVSA. Working days: Tuesday, Wednesday morning 08:15 - 11:45, Friday morning They support the class teacher.

Requirements

  • You have a primary level teaching qualification (Bachelor of Arts in Primary Education or equivalent) or are currently in training.
  • They love working with children and like to respond to them! They have teaching experience at this school level or corresponding life experience and enjoy working in a team. For additional tasks related to school life such as meetings, parent talks, excursions, you are committed.

We offer

We offer variety and challenge at a lively school. You will be supported by the college, the school management as well as the school social work, curative education and day school. Cooperation is close and cordial. Individual further education as well as an interdisciplinary exchange are part of this with us. We also offer:

  • Well mixed college.
  • Good accessibility with public transport
  • Velo-friendly terrain
  • A diverse cultural and sporting offer of the city of Biel
