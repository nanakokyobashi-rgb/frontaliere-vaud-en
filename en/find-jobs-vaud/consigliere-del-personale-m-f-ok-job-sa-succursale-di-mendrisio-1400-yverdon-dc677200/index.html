Personnel Advisor (M/F) back to list As part of the development of our agency in Yverdon-les-Bains, we are looking for a Personnel Advisor to strengthen our presence in all sectors of activity in the Vaudois North. This function combines recruitment, commercial development and business support Job description

Your mission

  • Develop and retain a portfolio of companies in the northern Vaud region
  • Actively prospect new clients and identify recruitment needs
  • Identify investment opportunities in industry, watchmaking, logistics, administration, construction, technology and services
  • Full management of recruitment mandates for fixed and temporary posts
  • Search, select and evaluate candidates, including through in-depth interviews
  • Present best profiles to our clients and track recruitment
  • Accompanying temporary staff throughout their missions
  • Advise clients on labour market realities, wages and appropriate recruitment solutions
  • Negotiate trade conditions and develop sustainable relationships with partner companies
  • Actively participate in the Agency's development and visibility

Profile

Profile desired

  • Experience in recruitment, placement of staff, sale of services or similar function
  • Excellent relational sense and great interest in human contacts
  • Real business temperament, results oriented and business development
  • Excellent communication, negotiation and persuasion capabilities
  • Good knowledge of the economic fabric of the Vaudois North
  • Ability to evolve in a dynamic environment and manage multiple mandates simultaneously
  • Perfect command of French, any other language constituting an asset
  • Use with common computer tools
  • Independent, proactive, organized and persevering personality
  • Essential driving licence
