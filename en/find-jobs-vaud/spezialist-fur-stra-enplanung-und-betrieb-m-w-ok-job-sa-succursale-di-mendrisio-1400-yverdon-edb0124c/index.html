Specialist in road planning and operation (M/F) back to list To strengthen our teams, we are looking for a (M/F) road planning and operation specialist. Job description

Your mission

  • Ensure the planning and distribution of rolling stock in order to ensure optimum coverage of operational needs.
  • Develop schedules and service plans taking into account transport supply, operational constraints, existing legislation (LDT/OLDT) and applicable collective agreements
  • Coordinate the impact of roadworks in collaboration with the authorities, public services and the various partners concerned
  • Ensure continuity and reliability of transport services by anticipating operating constraints and implementing the necessary measures
  • Analyze customer needs and offer suitable and efficient transport solutions
  • Actively participate in continuous improvement projects and working groups related to road operations
  • Identifying risks, anticipating unforeseen events and providing pragmatic solutions to optimize the organization and quality of services

Profile

Profile desired

  • Experience in planning personnel and operations in a road transport or public transport environment
  • Excellent control of legislation on working and rest time (LDT/OLDT)
  • Good understanding of operational issues related to the operation of a transmission system and the management of disturbances
  • Driver's licence category D, a real asset
  • Excellent relational and communication skills, allowing you to interact effectively with various interlocutors
  • Good negotiating and coordinating with internal and external partners
  • Independent, organized and rigorous person able to manage multiple priorities simultaneously
  • Spirit of analysis, sense of anticipation and ability to make relevant decisions in a demanding environment
  • Ability to maintain a global vision of operation while providing concrete and field-oriented solutions
