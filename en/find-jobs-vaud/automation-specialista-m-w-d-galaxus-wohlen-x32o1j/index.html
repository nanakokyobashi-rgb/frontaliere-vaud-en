Full description

In the role of Industrial Automation Specialist, you develop and implement technical frameworks and standards for optimizing production processes. You work in a dynamic environment at the interface between IT and OT and contribute your expertise to drive automation solutions forward.

Luogo di lavoro: Galaxus, DG Hauptlager Wohlen, Industriestrasse 21, 5610 Wohlen

Important information

We welcome every application, but can only consider online applications. Applications by post are disposed of for data protection reasons and not returned.

Mansioni

  • Development and implementation of PLC programs (Siemens S7, TIA Portal, Beckhoff TwinCAT3, Allen Bradley Studio 5000) according to technical specifications
  • Configuration and troubleshooting of industrial networks (Profinet, CAN, EtherCAT, Ethernet IP, ProfiSafe, ASi) to ensure efficient operations
  • Support for IT network integration (LAN, WLAN, IP rules, NAT, VPN) for stable interfaces in daily business
  • Implementation of OT cybersecurity measures to protect critical systems
  • Integration of IT/OT interfaces via TCP/IP, OPC UA and REST API
  • Development of HMI applications (WinCC, Factory Talk) and integration of IoT sensors (IO-Link, MQTT)
  • Data analysis with tools such as Grafana or Kibana to monitor KPIs such as OEE, MTTR and MTBF
  • Independent work with a high degree of proactivity and technological interest
  • Project management according to agile methods incl. Risk assessment and compliance with safety standards

Requirements

  • 1-2 years of experience: in industrial manufacturing
  • Student (Uni/FH/HF): Electrical engineering, automation technology or similar
  • Completed degree in electrical engineering, automation technology or comparable as well as experience in industrial production
  • In-depth knowledge in PLC programming, HMI development, industrial networks, IT/OT interfaces (TCP/IP, OPC UA, REST API) and programming (e.g. Python, JavaScript)
  • Experience in electrical planning with EPLAN as well as working with IoT protocols and dashboard tools (e.g. Grafana, Kibana)
  • Independent, solution-oriented working method with high personal responsibility and teamwork
  • Very good German and good English skills in word and writing
  • Identify potential for improvement and implement changes to increase productivity and effectiveness
  • Have the courage to try things without planning them down to the smallest detail
  • German (very good knowledge)
  • English (good knowledge)
