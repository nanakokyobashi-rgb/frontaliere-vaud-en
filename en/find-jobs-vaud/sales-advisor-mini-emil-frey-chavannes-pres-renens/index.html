What you need to know about us The Emil Frey Group is not only a Swiss family business with more than 100 years of experience, but is also one of the leaders in the automotive sector. Quality, tradition, innovation and customer orientation are the pillars of the company. Its main activities are the import of world brand vehicles, the operation of garages, the logistics of spare parts and accessories and the provision of financial services. Emil Frey SA Chavannes is a BMW sales partner, Mini as well as Ford and also has a spare parts store. In order to complement our team, we are looking for our future: Sales advisor MINI Your Responsibilities
