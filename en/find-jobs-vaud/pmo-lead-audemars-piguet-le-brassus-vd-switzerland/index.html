Role overview

Your Mission

Within Digital IT, the Project Management Officer (PMO) Lead is responsible for structuring, facilitating and continuously improving the governance of projects, programmes and portfolios.

A true partner of the Directorate, he/she ensures the consistency of steering practices, the quality of management data, the consolidation of portfolio visibility and the development of team maturity.
