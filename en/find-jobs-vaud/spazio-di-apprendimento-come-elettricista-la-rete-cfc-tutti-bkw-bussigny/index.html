Full description

We make spaces where it is good to live.

Bussigny (VD)

At Duvoisin-Groux network electricians build and maintain electricity distribution facilities. A varied and challenging daily work awaits you on well-educated teams that actively support you during your three years of training.

Your tasks

As a specialist in all electrical network work, you are responsible for the construction and maintenance of cables and overhead lines, coupling and processing stations, distribution booths, domestic connecting boxes and public lighting. Whether in the city or in the country, you are on a daily basis outside and in any season. In order to minimize the risks associated with your demanding activity, you learn how to use tools and machines safely, both in-house and in-house. Whether in the trenches or on the towers of the airlines, as a network electrician, you work exclusively in groups. Good cooperation and mutual trust are therefore essential. In our home, you can also do a second apprenticeship in CFC power/network.

Who are we?

Founded in 1947, Duvoisin-Groux SA is active in French-speaking Switzerland and employs about 100 qualified and experienced people. Our four areas of activity are: energy, lighting, mobility and telecommunications. The company, headquartered in Bussigny, offers services such as planning, project management, construction, maintenance and maintenance of network infrastructure. Since 2021, Duvoisin-Groux SA has been a subsidiary of Arnold Group. Within the BKW Group, we are cultivating an inclusive welcome culture, open to all.
