Shape the future through scientific breakthroughs and innovation

As a Technician for In Vitro Digestion Platform at Nestlé Research, your mission is to plan, execute, and follow up laboratory experiments that advance research projects through high-quality, traceable data. You’ll make a difference by collaborating closely with technicians, research assistants, scientists, project leaders, and external research partners. This is your opportunity to turn ideas into sustainable and innovative solutions for people, pets, and the planet.

How you’ll make an impact as a Technician for In Vitro Digestion Platform

  • Executing in vitro digestion experiments that generate reliable evidence for research projects.
  • Preparing reagents and samples with accuracy to support consistent, high-quality results.
  • Analyzing data and communicating clear findings that help project teams make informed decisions.
  • Ensuring sample and data traceability while maintaining safe, compliant laboratory practices.
  • Improving laboratory methods by identifying opportunities, troubleshooting issues, and implementing practical solutions.
  • Building scientific knowledge and external connections to strengthen the platform’s future capabilities.

What will make you successful

  • Bringing hands-on laboratory skills in biology and a rigorous approach to experimental procedures.
  • Applying quality standards to sample handling, data integrity, traceability, and laboratory safety.
  • Using spreadsheet and presentation tools to manage data and communicate scientific results clearly.
  • Collaborating across diverse teams, learning new techniques quickly, and contributing to effective troubleshooting.
  • Communicating fluently in English.
  • Bringing collaboration, fresh thinking, bold questions, respect, and a positive, wellbeing-oriented mindset, to create shared value.
