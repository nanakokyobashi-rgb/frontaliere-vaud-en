Description

Be a force for good and That change thatIows lives and the planet.

At Nestlé Professional, we support foodservice professionals with leading coffee and culinary solutions, enabling gastronomy enthusiasts throughout Switzerland to enjoy high-quality products through iconic brands such as STARBUCKS®, NESCAFÉ®, THOMY®, MAGGI®, CAILLER® and CHEF®.

As a Regional Key Account Manager Food & Beverages, you will join an experienced and committed team. You will play a key role in developing strategic accounts in eastern French-speaking Switzerland, identifying new business opportunities, developing growth strategies and creating lasting partnerships with your clients.

As a true trusted partner, you will be your clients’ preferred point of contact and actively contribute to developing your portfolio by balancing new account acquisition, retention of existing clients and the sustainable achievement of business objectives.

Your impact as a Regional Key Account Manager Food & Beverages

  • Develop and maintain strong partnership-based relationships with clients, acting as their preferred point of contact and ensuring their long-term satisfaction.
  • Identify and convert opportunities to acquire new clients, developing Food & Beverages offers tailored to their needs.
  • Lead contract negotiations through to the conclusion of agreements, ensuring that commercial objectives are met.
  • Roll out and implement the national sales strategy at regional level, adapting it to the local context and the specific characteristics of key clients.
  • Monitor, analyze and manage client performance using key indicators, and define structured action plans to achieve or even exceed the objectives set.
  • Work closely with the various internal teams.

What will make you successful

  • Successful sales experience in the HORECA sector, ideally in coffee, beverages or culinary products.
  • Excellent negotiation skills and strong results orientation.
  • Fluency in French and German is essential
  • English is an asset.
  • Autonomy, initiative, flexibility and strong customer orientation.

At Nestlé Professional, people are at the heart of everything we do.

We do everything we can to provide a work environment that supports your professional development, well-being and work-life balance:

  • Flexible working arrangements tailored to the requirements of the position.
  • More vacation leave than legally required.
  • Numerous training and professional development opportunities.
  • A variable compensation system linked to performance.
  • A contribution toward healthcare costs as well as an employee assistance program.
  • An attractive contribution to your occupational pension plan.
  • Generous parental leave and family support solutions.
  • Attractive discounts on a wide range of products and services.

You will work in an inclusive and stimulating environment, offering numerous opportunities for development and collaboration across Nestlé's different activities.
