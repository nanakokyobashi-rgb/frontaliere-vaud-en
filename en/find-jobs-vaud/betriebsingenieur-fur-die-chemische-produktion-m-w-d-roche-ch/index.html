  • Taking on the role You are responsible for ensuring the functionality and technical availability of process and building facilities.
  • In addition, you will take on the following exciting tasks:
  • Plant availability: Through efficient maintenance measures and continuous optimization, you ensure smooth operation and the cost-effectiveness of the technical plants.
  • Strategic Asset Management: You develop modern service concepts and maintenance strategies based on reliability studies and asset monitoring and maintain them in the SAP system.
