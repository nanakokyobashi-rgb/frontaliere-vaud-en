02.10.2026 Cheseaux-sur-Lausanne

Be part of our team!

A flourishing and dynamic establishment, Banque Raiffeisen d'Assens covers a wide area of activity and enjoys a strong local anchor thanks to its agencies located in Goumoens-la-Ville, Cheseaux, Crissier and Renens, thus offering it many opportunities for the deployment of its activities. To complete our team, we are looking for a customer advisor in basic services for our Cheseaux-sur-Lausanne point of sale, for entry into service on 1 December 2026 (or to be agreed).

What are you waiting for?

  • Qualitatively advise clients at the counter.
  • Monitoring and active management of the agency's client portfolio, prospecting, appointment making.
  • Defining and exploiting the potential of customers, proposing solutions and products that meet their expectations.
  • Management of cash boxes, ticket automatons and safe compartments.
  • Active cross-selling and transmission of technical questions to the appropriate adviser.

What are you bringing?

Do you benefit from Swiss banking training and have first professional experience in a similar position? With a committed personality, excellent presentation and a recognized professional conscience, you like contact with customers, teamwork while being able to be autonomous? Then we would be delighted to welcome you to our small team to collaborate in the development of our activities. You want to evolve in a human-sized bank where your commitment makes a difference? Then join us! Claude Anker Vice-President of the Directorate Why Raiffeisen?

We work in a friendly environment

Co-operative values are our pillars. Together. For each other. They shape how we work together and behave toward each other. The culture of mentoring promotes personal proximity and exchanges on an equal footing at all levels of function. We leave room for personal initiative and make sense of our work We invite our employees to actively shape their work and assume responsibilities. The result is a variety of tasks that create real added value – for all people who want to move things.

We encourage personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is an integral part of our culture. We create a space for the professional and personal development of each individual and support our employees in a targeted manner, thanks to a wide range of in-house training and participation in external development costs.

We offer attractive social conditions and benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages over banking products, various discounts from third parties, as well as above average financial stability and old age insurance.

We offer the possibility to buy additional weeks of vacation

In addition to the usual holidays, our employees can take up to two additional weeks of vacation per year.
