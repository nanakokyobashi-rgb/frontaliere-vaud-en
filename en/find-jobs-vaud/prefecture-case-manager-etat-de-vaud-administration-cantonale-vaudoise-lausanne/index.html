Full description

As a case manager in the prefecture of the district of Lausanne, you are involved in the management of all administrative, criminal and civil services in the prefecture. Your mission is part of an evolutionary entity that proposes various tasks.

  • Management of administrative benefits related to various criminal procedures such as contraventions, orders, oppositions to payment arrangements
  • Management of administrative benefits related to the various procedures of the Conciliation Commission for rent leases
  • Reception and information for users at the prefecture counter
  • Administrative monitoring of the common accounts and associations
  • Prefecture accounting records management For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Ms. Yasmina Jordan, Prefectural Administrative Officer at 021 316 41 01
  • CFCs used for trade or equivalent
  • Certification of trainer in company (an asset)
  • Professional experience in criminal matters or in rent lease procedures
  • Interest in public institutions
  • Excellent editorial skills
  • Mastery of common computer tools
  • Meaning of organization and time management
  • Adaptability and flexibility
  • Customer orientation developed, listening and communication

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
