This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Mission

Perform specific tasks related to the administration of the MMS Section in collaboration with the Director and the Section Assistant.

Main tasks and responsibilities

Administrative monitoring •

  • Reception/information and orientation to internal services.
  • Administrative management of mechanical engineering student files.
  • Coordination with the academic service and teachers.
  • Administrative management of student assistants.
  • Accounting management of section budgets

Events and working groups: Organization and coordination of section events: invitations, registration management, organization of catering and verbatim reporting, etc.

Profile

  • An excellent mastery of French and English is mandatory (both in writing and oral).
  • Computer skills with a very good mastery of Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Outlook, etc.
  • Experience in budget and accounting organization.
  • Ability to interact with all levels of professor, lecturers, administrative staff and students in a multicultural/international environment.
  • Autonomy, sense of organization and initiative, rigour and attention to detail.
  • Commercial training (Commercial Employee CFC or equivalent).
