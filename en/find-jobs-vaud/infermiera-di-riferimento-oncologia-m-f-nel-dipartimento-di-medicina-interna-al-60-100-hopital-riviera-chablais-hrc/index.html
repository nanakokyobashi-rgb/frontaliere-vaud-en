Full description

The Riviera-Chablais Hospital, Vaud-Valais (HRC), is a reference intercantonal hospital with more than 2,000 collaborators. Rennaz Hospital has over 350 acute care beds. It is complemented by two Geriatric and Rehabilitation Clinics and two medical clinics. All employees invest on a daily basis to provide quality care for patients, embodying the institution's values of authenticity, exemplaryness and innovation.

The internal medical service consists of 7 units and has a capacity of 110 patients. In particular, it takes care of patients requiring oncological treatment as part of comprehensive and interdisciplinary care.

In order to strengthen our team and to develop patient support within the service, we are looking for an e.

Oncology Reference Nurse M/F in the Internal Medicine Department at 60%-100%

The main tasks of this post are

  • Welcoming and providing care and supervision for medical patients
  • Administer and monitor oncological treatments
  • Contribute to the development of the skills of internal medical staff in the administration of oncological treatments and precautions related to the handling of RMC drugs.
  • Propose improvements and help implement changes in the service
  • Participate in the analysis and prevention of risks associated with the professional handling of RMC drugs
  • Connect with ICS, ICLS and the outpatient cancer department

We ask

  • A nursing degree or equivalent
  • A minimum experience of 2 years in acute care
  • Post-graduate training (CAS) in oncology or equivalent
  • Excellent capacity for adaptation, anticipation and initiatives
  • A motivated, rigorous and dynamic person with a sense of responsibility
