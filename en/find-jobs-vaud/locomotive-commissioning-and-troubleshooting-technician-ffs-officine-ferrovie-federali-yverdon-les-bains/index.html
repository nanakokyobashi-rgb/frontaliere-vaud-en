Locomotive commissioning and troubleshooting technician 100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Locomotive commissioning and troubleshooting technician Yverdon-les-Bains, 01.12.2026 or to be agreed, 100% Apply here

What awaits you.

The staff of the Entreprise en Service team at the Yverdon-les-Bains SBB Workshops are involved in railway vehicles and components. As part of revisions, repairs or modernisations, they carry out checks, troubleshooting and commissioning of railway vehicles such as RABDe500 ICN, RBDe560 DO and Re460 locomotives. Expect a varied and complex job! A strong interest in technology is essential for this position. Experience in the railway field is an advantage. We offer many advantages - Let yourself be convinced: Social benefits. What you can move.

  • You are responsible for the diagnosis and commissioning of rail vehicles and components according to established guidelines.
  • You work regularly with engineers to resolve complex breakdowns.
  • Occasionally, you are involved in the development and implementation of working papers, in collaboration with the Technical Service.

What you bring.

  • Thanks to your professional career, you have acquired an excellent ability to work as a team but also know how to work independently. You are also ready to work with irregular schedules (2x8h or weekend).
  • You are comfortable in troubleshooting and have at least three years of practical experience in this area.
  • You benefit from a CFC or ES level training in the field of electrotechnics, e.g. Automatician/n CFC, Electrician/n CFC, Electrical Engineering Technician/n ES. For foreign diplomas, an official Swiss recognition for one of these trades is to be provided with the application file (legal requirement related to work on live installations).
  • At the level of languages, you master French, German knowledge is a plus.

Your salary and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive salary system and we are actively committed to equal pay. This is why additional and detailed information on the SBB salary system can be consulted in full transparency. This position falls within the E level and gives entitlement to a level 0 regional allowance. Learn more about salary and benefits .
