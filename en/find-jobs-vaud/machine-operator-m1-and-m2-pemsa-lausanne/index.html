Full description

Do you want the freedom of temporary work and the safety of permanent employment? For 30 years we have been joining hundreds of companies in the technical sectors of construction, construction and industry throughout Switzerland, proposing them the best talents. We will not lie to you however: we are demanding. So, if you are a motivated and extremely enthusiastic person, join our team for the position of M1 and M2 machine operator

Your assignment

  • Driving M1 and M2 class construction machines for earthmoving, civil engineering and installation of external areas.
  • Excavating, trenching and levelling.
  • Securing the loading and displacement of materials.
  • Working near existing underground networks and infrastructure.
  • Checking levels, slopes and alignments.
  • Ensuring ordinary maintenance and daily checks of machines.
  • Reporting any anomalies or failures.
  • Working with the foreman and teams on the field.
  • Respect the Suva Directives and safety standards.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Holder of the approved Suva M1 and M2 licenses in Switzerland.
  • Experience proven in the driving of construction machinery, preferably in the field of stoneware or civil engineering.
  • Good mastery of excavation, levelling and filling operations.
  • Ability to read projects and work with precision.
  • Autonomous, reliable, organized and with good team spirit.
  • Able to stay close to underground networks.
  • Careful and responsible in the use of equipment.
  • The possession of other SUVA categories constitutes an advantage.

Do you care? Love and master your profession and want to put your skills at the service of projects that really matter? Then this offer is just for you. From us, your know-how makes a difference and contributes directly to the success of the construction sites.

What we offer: Your contact person for this location is Valentin Colin. He will know how to accompany you, advise you and answer all your position questions. We will only answer applications whose profile matches the description you have requested.
