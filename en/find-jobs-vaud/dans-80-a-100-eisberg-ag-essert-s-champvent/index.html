Eisberg AG is one of the leading manufacturers of salads, fruits and vegetables of the 4th century. Product range available for cooking and consumption in Switzerland. True to our motto "Colour Your Life", our more than 800 employees produce up to 60 tonnes of fresh food every day with passion and commitment at the Dällikon, Dänikon and Essert-sous-Champvent locations. Eisberg AG is proud to be part of the internationally renowned Eisberg Group, which is regarded as the market leader in the production of ready-to-eat salads in Central Europe. The Eisberg Group operates its own state-of-the-art production facilities in Switzerland and Austria.
