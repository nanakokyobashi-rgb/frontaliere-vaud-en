Product Manager — Audemars Piguet

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Under the responsibility of the Senior Director Product, you will play a key role in the definition, deployment and performance of our watch collections. A true interface between the Design, R&D, Industrialization, Quality, Supply Chain, Commercial and Marketing teams, you will manage the entire product life cycle to ensure their consistency with the Manufacture strategy, market expectations and business objectives.

In this context, your main responsibilities will be

  • Lead and develop a Product team, setting priorities, accompanying the increase in skills and fostering a collaborative, stimulating and efficient working environment within the product ecosystem
  • Guarantee and carry the product vision of collections, contributing to the medium- and long-term strategy, the architecture of the offer, its positioning and its overall coherence
  • Pilot the deployment of the Product Plan as well as the technical developments of existing collections, from the expression of need to the final validation, in collaboration with the Design, R&D, Industrialization and Quality teams
  • Analyze the performance of collections and market trends in order to identify opportunities for optimization, innovation and product portfolio evolution
  • Ensure technical excellence of products, ensuring control of specifications, costs, quality, standards and product data management
  • Represent the Product function in cross-jurisdictions and projects, contributing to roadmaps, committees, product reviews and strategic events
  • Support product launches and life cycle, providing the necessary tools, content and expertise to markets and business teams
  • Contribute to the overall performance of the activity, working closely with procurement, Supply Chain and other business functions to ensure availability and competitiveness of the supply
  • Advanced training in Microtechnics, Engineering, Industrial Design or equivalent
  • Minimum 7 years experience in Product Management in the watch industry
  • Excellent knowledge of watchmaking products (movements, complications, materials and industrialization processes)
  • Experience in product development and management of complex technical projects
  • Demonstrated ability to manage, develop and unite teams through natural leadership
  • Strong organizational and priority management capacity
  • Analytical Spirit and Outcome Direction
  • Technical curiosity and sense of innovation
  • Excellent communication skills and cross-cutting collaboration
  • Mastery of PLM, LES and project management tools
  • Mastery of English and French
