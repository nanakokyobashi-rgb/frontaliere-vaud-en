Full description

Our Medical-Social Centres (CMS) offer the Lausanne population high quality multidisciplinary services and their interventions are based on values of humanity and fairness. By joining the Lausanne Care Foundation, you are committed in a concrete way to promoting health and well-being in the canton of Vaud, within expert, motivated and enthusiastic teams, in a varied activity rich in human contacts. Les Peupliers provides day, night and night replacements to the 7 CMS of the Lausanne Care Foundation to guarantee the continuity of our clients' care. In order to complete the team of the CMS of Peupliers, La Fondation Soins Lausanne is looking for the next or to agree:

Nurse Coordinator (M/F)

80% participation rate Undetermined fixed-term contract Your missions

  • Identify, analyze the immediate needs of the client and establish the first intervention plan to optimize continuity of care.
  • Secure clients' return home and coordinate care during care.
  • Provide care during care time.
  • Provide telephone daycare on nights, weekends and public holidays.
  • Collaborate with and respond to client network stakeholders.
  • Respond to acute situations at home as needed.

Your profile and skills

  • Bachelor's degree in nursing or equivalent (foreign diploma recognized by the CRS)
  • Experience in the medical-social field and in interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Knowledge in the care networks of the canton of Vaud
  • Responsibility, priorities and autonomy
  • Essential driving licence and desirable vehicle

We offer

  • A stimulating and warm work environment where the caregiver's autonomy is highlighted
  • A working environment that favours human contact and exchange
  • Exciting work in a pleasant environment for the well-being of clients
  • Fair working conditions and remuneration
  • Conditions of occupational security which extend beyond the minimum benefits provided by law

The benefits of joining us

  • Plan of integration by occupation, offers of rich and varied continuing training, opportunities for vocational development
  • Gender pay equity ( Fair-ON-Pay certification)
  • Remuneration according to the Collective Labour Convention (CCT) of the public health sector of voodoo
  • Favourable offers with preferential rates in service companies around sport, health and welfare and miscellaneous leisure
  • Soft mobility policy and participation in public transport costs
  • Five weeks of vacation per year and six weeks from 50 years
