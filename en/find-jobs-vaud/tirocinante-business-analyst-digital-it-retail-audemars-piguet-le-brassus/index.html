Full description

For our Digital IT Retail & CRM team, we are looking for our future intern for 6 months from February 2027. During this internship, your main mission will be to contribute to the development and improvement of digital solutions supporting customer relationship and user experience.

In this context, you will be responsible for

  • Collaborate with Product Owner, development teams, CRM and Retail business teams to collect and analyze user needs
  • Participate in the formalization of business needs through the writing of user stories, functional specifications and acceptance criteria
  • Take part in the discovery workshops, the functional recipe phases (UAT) and the monitoring of developments until they are put into production
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of digital products and participate in strategic CRM projects within an agile environment
  • You are or are in the process of obtaining a Bachelor or Master in a field related to digital, information systems, business analysis, management or equivalent
  • You have a strong interest in digital, technology and user experience, as well as excellent analytics and synthesis capabilities
  • You are comfortable communicating and collaborating with a variety of contacts, both trades and techniques
  • Curious, proactive and team-minded, any experience in business analysis, CRM, project management or user experience is an asset
  • You have a perfect command of French and a minimum B2 in English

What we offer

  • Health insurance (basic and supplementary)
  • 5 annual holiday weeks and 2 additional weeks between Christmas and New Year thanks to the daily accumulation of additional minutes
  • A contribution to your sports or cultural activities
  • The ability to work remotely depending on the function
  • A business restaurant
  • An advantageous PBL
