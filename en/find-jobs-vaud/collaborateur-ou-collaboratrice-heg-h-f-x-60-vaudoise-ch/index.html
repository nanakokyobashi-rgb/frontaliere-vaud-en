Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. Active in vocational training, it promotes the development of young talent, with every year more than 120 learners trained in our departments. In order to strengthen our Marketing and Customer Experience entity, we are looking for a 60% employee, starting September 1, 2026, for a fixed period of 4 years, as part of a HEG training course on the job. The missions that await you:

  • Participate in studies to deepen the knowledge of our clients and the insurance market
  • Participate in the conduct of surveys to measure Vaudoise client experience (question definition, programming, sending, analysis of results, preparation of reports and presentations)
  • Analyze customer feedback and identify improvement actions
  • Support the team in the activities of competitive monitoring (make comparisons, analyse the market shares of Vaudoise and competition, etc.)
  • Support the administrative activities of the entity (invoicing, budget, etc.) Your profile:
  • You are taking or are going to start HEG-type higher education in business economy
  • You are customer-oriented and open-minded
  • In French, you have excellent written and oral communication. You are also curious, conscientious and have an excellent sense of organization. We offer you:
  • A healthy working environment in a mutual company certified "Great Place to Work"
  • A varied and rewarding work experience
  • A possible opportunity to replace at the end of training Applications may be submitted until 15 August 2026. The HR team is looking forward to receiving your complete application package (CV, cover letter and 3 final transcripts).
