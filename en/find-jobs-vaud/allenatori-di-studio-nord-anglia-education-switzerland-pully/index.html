Full description

WHO WE ARE ?

Collège Champittet, a school of reference in the Lake Geneva region since 1903, offers excellent education to generations of Swiss and foreign students. Its unique spirit revolves around academic performance, multilingualism and values such as respect and trust. The school is part of the Nord Anglia Education family, the world's leading premium schools’ organisation with 89 schools in 32 countries. In this dynamic environment, both local and international, we invite you to join our Boarding Team.

JOB DESCRIPTION

As a Study Coach, you will support and supervise students aged 11 to 18 during evening study sessions twice a week from 6.00 pm, as well as during sports and cultural activities one weekend per month. You will play a key role in fostering a positive, structured and supportive environment that encourages both academic success and personal development.

Profile and competences

  • An IB graduate, studying at University, EPFL or Haute Ecole, in Child & Adolescent Psychology, Sciences, Economics or Literature
  • Ideally with prior experience working with teenagers, understanding their needs and interests
  • A native speaker in French or English with a good command of the other language. Russian, Spanish, Chinese, Japanese or Arabic an asset
  • Enthusiastic, patient, with natural authority, motivated to impart your knowledge to young people.
  • Flexible, integrating easily into a team thanks to good interpersonal skills
  • Swiss or European nationality with a valid work permit
  • Clean criminal records

If you are passionate about education and interested in joining our team, please apply directly online through our website.
