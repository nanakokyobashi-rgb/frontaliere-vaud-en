Full description

In an environment where information systems are at the heart of the performance and continuity of public services, we are looking for a Security products/services line manager. You are piloting a portfolio of major and critical business applications, used by many users of Vaud State services in the field of public security. You will be at the heart of the coordination of specialists in key roles and demanding partners, as well as the management of a substantial budget, to ensure the safety, continuity and evolution of these systems. Do you have a solid experience in leading a large IT entity, a strategic vision and recognized leadership? Then this challenge is for you.

Main missions

  • Manage the line's product/service catalogue and plan its evolution to ensure its safety, efficiency and effectiveness, and respond to the issues of the recipient services and the NSB
  • Ensure that the line develops a new product/service that meets the requirements of computer security, efficiency and efficiency throughout their life cycle
  • Ensure the maintenance, operation and support of line products/services
  • Conduct the teams, activities and resources of the product/service line by ensuring that objectives are met in accordance with the operating rules and values of the NSB
  • Participate in the management of the Products/Services Sector For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact the Director Products and Services Sector, Mr Georges Renaudet, by email georges.renaudet@vd.ch . We are happy to receive your complete file in French (CV, cover letter, work certificates, diplomas).

Profile sought

  • Master in Information Systems or equivalent
  • Management training and management knowledge according to EFQM and ISO 9000
  • Experience in large organisation with medium-sized IT team
  • Public safety knowledge
  • General knowledge of ITIL and good knowledge of recent software development techniques
  • Ability to develop organizational agility by strengthening team autonomy and accountability
  • Ability to prioritize, arbitrate and unite a team in an environment with high availability requirements
  • Strong leadership with an ability to promote cross-cutting collaboration
  • Capacity for communication and collaboration with technical, trade and management partners
  • Global vision and perspective
  • A sense of responsibilities, continuity of service and public service ethics Specific penalties:
  • Perhaps to work outside the usual working hours (night, Saturday, Sunday or holiday)
  • Accreditation Required font
