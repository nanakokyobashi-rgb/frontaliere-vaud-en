Full description

Active in Western Switzerland, we help our clients realize their projects by providing global, efficient and sustainable energy solutions, whether for the production and distribution of electricity and heat, electrical installations, home automation, soft mobility, air conditioning or water treatment. An exemplary employer, we support the development of the skills of our 2,250 employees, including 200 apprentices, as well as the balance between private and professional life.

A local player, Groupe E Tech, thanks to its expertise, is the reference partner for multi-technical projects in the fields of installations, electrical panels and infrastructures, telecommunications, multimedia, solar, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, cold, sanitary, water treatment, as well as the maintenance of installations.

Mission

As a Team Leader, you carry out and commission work related to industrial infrastructure, particularly energy rails. You operationally manage your team in the field in order to guarantee efficient, qualitative and secure execution of the tasks entrusted to you.

Your responsibilities

  • Actively participate in laying and installation work: LV energy rails, cabling, cable trays, connections and electrical equipment.
  • Supervise, motivate and distribute tasks within your team according to site priorities.
  • Adapt execution to field constraints and ensure availability, condition and proper use of equipment.
  • Check the conformity of the work, optimize the use of materials and maintain a clean and safe work area.
  • Strictly enforce safety rules, check the use of PPE and stop work in the event of imminent danger.
  • Carry out measurements, report information to the site manager/works supervisor and contribute to the administrative monitoring of the site.
  • Train, support and evaluate employees, while guaranteeing quality, deadlines and best practices.

Profile

  • Training in the field of electricity or building technology (electrician, locksmith, heating engineer, etc.).
  • Solid field experience (5 years) in high current and/or industrial infrastructure (energy rails, LV/MV).
  • Demonstrated ability to supervise a small team, transmit instructions and maintain cohesion.
  • Excellent technical skills, rigor, organizational skills and responsiveness to unforeseen events.
  • Exemplary attitude: care of equipment, compliance with internal directives, safety and corporate values.
  • Dynamic, autonomous, constructive and solution-oriented person.
  • Driving license.

What we offer you

  • An evolving environment favorable to balance between professional and private life.
  • The social benefits of a modern company, fair treatment, professional development opportunities.
  • A strong spirit of cohesion and a team proud to collaborate: “Together, we move towards success”
