Full description

VZ Banque de Dépôt is owned by VZ Group, Switzerland's leading independent financial services provider. Investments are our passion: we ensure that our customers are able to carry out their transactions efficiently and provide them with attractive conditions. VZ Banque de Dépôt is growing strongly, and we are constantly looking for committed people.

  • As a first step, you support our team of experienced investment specialists in consulting our clients on their investments.
  • After a solid on-the-job training of several months, you gradually take responsibility for customers, accompany them in their investment decisions and develop with them a lasting relationship of trust.
  • You interact regularly with your customers, explain clearly the evolution of the markets and the implications for their investments, and proactively identify their needs.
  • You develop tailor-made placement proposals, present them convincingly and accompany their implementation.
  • You are responsible for the transmission and follow-up of our clients' stock exchanges.
  • You are involved in continuous improvement and automation projects aimed at simplifying our processes and working methods.
  • Experience confirmed in consulting with private clients
  • Banking training completed successfully. An asset: Higher education in finance, banking or economics at a specialized college or university
  • Good knowledge of financial markets and major asset classes
  • Customer friendliness and clear communication in French
  • Very good knowledge of German for internal communication (indispensable)
  • Curiosity, ambition, initiative and team spirit
  • Interest in automation and artificial intelligence to optimize our working methods.
  • With us, you can bring your ideas and constantly develop in a dynamic environment.
  • A competent team, with a strong team spirit and support, which we also cultivate at external events.
  • In the field of continuing training, you are at the right address: we provide you with individual support and promote your professional development through external training, such as AZEK / CFA.
  • Our satisfaction surveys regularly confirm that our employees regard VZ as an extremely positive working environment, in which they can fully flourish. Experience it, too.

Requirements

    • Expérience confirmée dans le conseil en placements auprès d'une clientèle privée

    • Bonne connaissance des marchés financiers et des principales classes d'actifs
