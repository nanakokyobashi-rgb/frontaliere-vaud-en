Data Analytics Engineer Looker (GCP) — Talan

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

We are looking for a Data Analytics Engineer specializing in Looker , capable of structuring and enhancing data so that it is accessible, reliable, and usable by business teams.

At the interface between technical teams and business users, you will contribute to the design of robust data models and the development of analysis tools that facilitate decision-making.

Responsibilities

  • Structure, transform, and prepare data to make it usable by business teams.
  • Design and maintain data models in Looker , notably via LookML .
  • Develop and optimize data transformations with dbt .
  • Write and optimize SQL queries to ensure the quality and performance of processing.
  • Design reliable analysis tools and dashboards adapted to users' needs.
  • Gather business requirements and translate them into relevant analytical solutions.
  • Ensure the consistency, reliability, and maintainability of data models.
  • Collaborate with Data and business teams to encourage the adoption of analytics tools.

Candidate profile

  • Proven experience in Data Analytics Engineering or a similar role.
  • Proficiency in Looker (excluding Looker Studio) and LookML , particularly for data modeling.
  • Very good SQL skills.
  • Practical experience with dbt for data transformation and modeling.
  • Ability to understand business needs and translate them into technical solutions.
  • Analytical mindset, autonomy, and good communication skills.
  • Fluent French essential, daily working language.
  • Knowledge of the Google Cloud Platform (GCP) environment.
