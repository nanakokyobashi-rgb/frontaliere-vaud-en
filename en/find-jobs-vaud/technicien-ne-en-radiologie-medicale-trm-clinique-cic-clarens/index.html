In order to strengthen its team, Clinique CIC Montreux is looking for an e: Medical Radiology Technician (TRM) 80 - 100% Within this function, the main tasks are:

  • Perform diagnostic imaging images on all service devices: MRI, CT, RX, MG, DEXA.
  • Respect medical protocols and meet dosimetry and radiation safety standards.
  • Overall care for patients in collaboration with physicians.
  • Preparation and installation of patients for examinations (possing of venous route, injection of contrast product under the responsibility of the radiologist doctor, preparation of various sterile sets...).
  • Monitoring of various medical imaging protocols and evaluation of the images obtained.
  • To hold a TRM diploma or equivalent recognised by the Swiss Red Cross.
