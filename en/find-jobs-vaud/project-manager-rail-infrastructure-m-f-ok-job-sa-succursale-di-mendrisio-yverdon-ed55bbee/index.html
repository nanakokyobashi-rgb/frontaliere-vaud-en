Project Manager Rail Infrastructure (M/F) back to list To strengthen our teams, we are looking for a (M/F) Rail Infrastructure Project Leader. Job description

Your mission

  • Pilot multidisciplinary railway projects throughout their implementation cycle
  • Define project organisation, set priorities and coordinate different stakeholders
  • Monitoring budgets, timelines, quality, safety and risk
  • Mandate and supervise engineering offices and other specialized project partners
  • Coordinate the undertakings responsible for carrying out the work and monitor the progress of the works
  • Ensure interface with the various partners, authorities and actors involved in the projects
  • Analysis of road projects likely to affect infrastructure or network operation
  • Participate in improving working methods, processes and technical frameworks
  • Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements for railway infrastructure
  • Support projects until completion and implementation

Profile

Profile desired

  • Holder of an engineering degree HES or higher education deemed equivalent
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in conducting railway projects, ideally with a transport company, an engineering office or an infrastructure actor
  • Good knowledge of railway facilities and technical constraints
  • Experience in managing projects involving multiple disciplines and many stakeholders
  • Further training in project management or rail is a real asset
  • Knowledge of Swiss regulations applicable to railway projects and public procurement is particularly appreciated
  • Excellent organizational, planning and priority management capabilities
  • Ability to coordinate and federate internal specialists, agents and external companies
  • Rigorous and structured personality, with a good spirit of analysis and synthesis
  • Clear communication and ease of contact with various interlocutors
