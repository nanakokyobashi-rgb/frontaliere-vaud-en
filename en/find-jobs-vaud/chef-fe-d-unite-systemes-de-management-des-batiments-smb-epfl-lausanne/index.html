Full description

Mission

The Operations department manages and operates the real estate infrastructure and services to EPFL users. It is composed of about 80 employees active in CVSE-MCR techniques, Building Maintenance as well as in concierge and stewardship services.

The newly created Building Management Systems (BMS) unit brings together the RCM and Supervision-Security operating groups. It is currently composed of 8 employees covering:

  • MCR (Building Automation)
  • Supervision of technical and security facilities
  • Hypervision
  • Transmission of alarms
  • Access control
  • Fire
  • Violation
  • DATI
  • CCTV

Main tasks and responsibilities

Your duties and responsibilities as Head of Building Management Systems (BMS) will be as follows:

  • Pilot the Building Management Systems Unit (BMS) by defining priorities, planning activities and ensuring that the objectives of the service are met, while respecting EPFL's values and culture.
  • Ensure the management of the unit's human resources, workload, budget and resources to ensure efficient and sustainable operation.
  • To define the strategy for the evolution of systems and installations in the field, anticipating the needs of EPFL's technical infrastructure.
  • Ensure the smooth running of the Unit's projects to ensure that the cost and time targets are met.
  • Coordinate the operation, maintenance and evolution of associated software and infrastructure.
  • Collaborate closely with the various EPFL services linked to the different areas of activity.
  • Conduct technical and interdisciplinary sessions, validate orders in accordance with current procedures.
  • Participate in technological and regulatory monitoring in its fields to ensure the evolution of solutions according to best practices and current requirements
  • To provide guidance, support and develop the skills of the Unit's staff, in particular by conducting evaluation interviews, identifying training needs and supporting their professional development.
  • Provide picket service if necessary

Profile

  • Training of HES engineer or ES technician in electricity, computing, automation, or equivalent training
  • Experience confirmed in developing or operating technical or security supervisions (SCADA) type PC View, Panorama, Control Maestro for example
  • Good knowledge of Building Automation, Smart Building, mastery of the components of these systems, field communication architectures and protocols (BACnet,Modbus, among others)
  • Good knowledge of IT infrastructure (virtualization, security, containerization, ...)
  • Good knowledge in safety facilities such as fire/breakout, access controls, CCTV
  • Confirmed competence to plan (roadmap, life cycle, resources, ...), analyze and write
  • To develop technical specifications
  • Confirmed ability to manage technical teams
  • Rigourous, autonomous and with a common sense of communication, able to collaboratetransversally with multidisciplinary teams
  • Open mind, listening and proposal strength, you will know how to advance projects with relevance and creativity
  • Very good mastery of French, English level B2
