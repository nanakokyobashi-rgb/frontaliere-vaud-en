Product Owner – Sovereign Data Platform

We are looking for a Product Owner to contribute to the development of the Sovereign Data Platform, transforming complex data into actionable insights that support informed decision-making and deliver measurable value.

ROLE

  • Define and maintain a clear, prioritized product backlog for the Sovereign Data Platform based on business goals, customer needs, market opportunities, and technical dependencies
  • Collaborate with customers, business stakeholders, sales teams, data scientists, architects, and engineering teams to gather requirements and align priorities
  • Translate business needs into actionable user stories, acceptance criteria, analytical requirements, and product increments
  • Own and prioritize the delivery roadmap, ensuring a continuous flow of high-value functionality and measurable business outcomes
  • Drive the development of dashboards, analytical applications, anomaly detection capabilities, predictive insights, and AI-powered recommendations
  • Work closely with UX and engineering teams to ensure intuitive user experiences, effective information architecture, and meaningful data visualization
  • Facilitate communication and alignment across stakeholders, providing transparency on priorities, dependencies, risks, and delivery progress
  • Partner with data engineering, software engineering, QA, DevOps, and data science teams throughout the delivery lifecycle, including discovery, refinement, planning, testing, and release activities
  • Monitor product adoption, customer feedback, and operational metrics to identify improvement opportunities and drive continuous product evolution
  • Promote data-driven decision making and best practices in analytics and insights across the organization

PROFILE

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Business, Data Analytics, Information Systems, or a related field
  • 5+ years of experience as a Product Owner, Product Manager, or in a comparable role within a data, analytics, business intelligence, or software development environment
  • Experience managing and prioritizing product requirements in Agile/Scrum environments
  • Strong understanding of data platforms, analytics, reporting, dashboards, and insights solutions
  • Ability to translate complex business needs into clear, testable, and value-driven product requirements
  • Excellent stakeholder management, communication, and facilitation skills
  • Experience working with multidisciplinary teams including business stakeholders, data engineers, software engineers, UX specialists, and data scientists
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a structured and outcome-oriented mindset
  • Experience with tools such as Jira, Confluence, or similar product delivery platforms
  • Exposure to AI, machine learning, predictive analytics, or decision-support systems is an advantage
  • Experience in government, compliance, track & trace, fuel integrity, tax, or public sector domains is a plus
  • Fluent in English / French is a plus
