Full description

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means more than simply working through tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high demands and have the courage to go new ways. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand and your ideas, initiative and contribution count. So you have the opportunity to really make a difference for our customers, your team and your personal development.

Debt collection, rental rights and debt management are exactly your thing? Then a versatile task awaits you with a lot of personal responsibility, exciting contacts and the opportunity to actively shape processes. We look forward to your application! Tasks: • Responsibility for the collection of dunning and rental interest on real estate portfolios in Western Switzerland in an overarching technical function • Independent implementation of all debt collection measures (in particular recovery, dismissals and expulsions/issuances) in compliance with the rental law and the Debt Recovery and Bankruptcy Act (SchKG) • Processing and management of loss notes and monitoring of outstanding claims • Independent correspondence with tenants, authorities, offices and other external bodies • Technical support for real estate management in all aspects of dunning as well as in the event of dismissals and expulsions outside the actual debt collection process • Cooperation in the preparation, control and release of the monthly reminder run • Audit, management and monitoring of amortisation and payment arrangements • Assumption of deputies within the dunning and debt collection team • Cooperation in the continuous optimisation of debt collection and dunning processes Requirements • Basic commercial training completed • Several years of professional experience in debt collection, debt management or accounting, ideally in the real estate industry • Good knowledge of Swiss tenancy law as well as debt collection and bankruptcy law (SchKG) • Safe handling of MS Office; Knowledge of Garaio REM is beneficial • Very good knowledge of German and French in word and writing • Independent, structured and exact working method • assertiveness, communication strength and pleasure in dealing with different stakeholders We believe in cooperation on equal terms, mutual trust and people who want to take responsibility. Different perspectives help us to develop better solutions – for our customers as well as for us as a team.
