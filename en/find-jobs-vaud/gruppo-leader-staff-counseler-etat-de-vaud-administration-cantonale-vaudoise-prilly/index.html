Full description

The Regional Employment Offices (ORP) of the canton of Vaud attached to the Directorate-General for Employment and Labour Market (DGEM) are responsible for assisting jobseekers in their search for work and supporting employers in the recruitment of staff. They play a key role in the implementation of employment policy and the fight against unemployment. As a member of the Board's management, you support the conduct of the ORP, support the Branch's projects and contribute to the quality of relationships with our partners. You organise and support the activities of the employees of your group, in close connection with the head of the ORP and other staff of the Office, and ensure the development of their skills, while monitoring the quality of the service provided to the clients of the Office and compliance with the legal framework. In addition to your managerial responsibility, you follow and advise job seekers, with the aim of their rapid and sustainable placement in the labour market. You select the appropriate placement strategies for each and have specific measures to develop its employability. You also guarantee compliance with the legal requirements for persons within your portfolio. Thanks to the quality of your candidate proposals, you are a key player for companies looking for staff.

  • Organize and support the activity of an ORP Personnel Advisor Group.
  • Organize, optimize and monitor the quality of the work of his group.
  • To ensure the development of the skills of the staff advisers of his group.
  • Support the Head of the ORP and DGEM management.
  • Provides an ORP Personnel Advisor activity, i.e.:
  • Place each job seeker quickly and sustainably.
  • Establish a reintegration strategy for each jobseeker.
  • Enable and advise each job seeker through counselling and monitoring interviews.
  • To monitor the efforts of each jobseeker with a view to his or her reintegration into employment and to administratively manage the files of the jobseeker. For further information, please consult the Terms of Reference by clicking here or contact Ms Teodora Ciobanu, HR Advisor, at 021 316 61 93.
  • Federal certificate of HR specialist, social insurance specialist or other equivalent.
  • Several years of experience in at least one of the following areas: counselling and placement, socio-professional reintegration or human resources, as well as management.
  • Ability to drive and motivate a team of collaborators by adopting participatory leadership.
  • Ability to initiate and promote organizational change.
  • Flexible and adaptable to change, while inspiring these qualities within the team.
  • Good knowledge of the labour market and the Vaudian economic fabric.
  • Mastery of relational and social skills at the service of results orientation.
  • Stress resistance, rigour and precision in the performance of tasks.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
