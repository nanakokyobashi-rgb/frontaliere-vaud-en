Full description

We are looking for a person motivated to engage in a meaningful mission, serving the most vulnerable. As a professional curator, you have a demanding, useful and deeply humane profession. Every day, you accompany and protect people who are in a position of vulnerability in a variety of social, administrative, financial, legal and health issues. The diversity of people and situations makes the trade rich and offers a stimulating, varied and constantly changing working environment. You work in a structured professional setting, in a department that implements the Conference's inter-cantonal recommendations on the protection of minors and adults (COPMA), in particular those concerning the number of cases per curator. You benefit from the support of internal legal, financial, tax and insurance support. You contribute, on a daily basis, to the prospect of a dignified and autonomous life for all, thus participating in social justice.

  • Carefully manage measures for the protection of adults entrusted by the cantonal justices of the peace, in accordance with the legal and regulatory framework.
  • Manage the assets of the persons concerned and represent them legally.
  • Manage recipient revenues and expenditures.
  • Develop an active partnership with professional networks and beneficiaries' relatives.
  • Create a relationship of trust with beneficiaries.
  • Invest in the initial training necessary for the function and in the continuing training relevant to the development of the service. For more information, please consult the specifications, or watch the videos of this trade "Curatelles et tutelage: A community mission, diverse and rewarding". By joining our service, you will have the opportunity to do telework. Indeed, we encourage flexibility to better reconcile work and personal life. For further information, please contact Iris Martin, Group Leader, at 021 316 90.01.
  • Bachelor's degree in social, legal or financial matters or equivalent.
  • 3 years of professional experience in connection with service missions or significant experience in accompanying vulnerable adults.
  • Further training related to health, addiction, migration, law, budget management and social insurance is welcome.
  • Excellent organizational capacity, administrative and financial rigour.
  • Autonomy, stress resistance, sense of responsibility and priorities.
  • Open mind, listening, flexibility, great ability to adapt and learn.
  • Disclosure of any criminal history and an extract from the Prosecution Office is required for this function.
  • Driver's license.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
