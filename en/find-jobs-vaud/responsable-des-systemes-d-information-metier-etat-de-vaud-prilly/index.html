Full description

We are looking for a Business Information Systems Manager for the 'SI Management and Business Relationship' department of the General Directorate of Digital and Information Systems (DGNSI). If you want to contribute to the evolution of the information systems of the Vaud Cantonal Administration, and if you are particularly interested in the fields of education and cultural affairs, then join us! Missions

  • Under the responsibility of the business relationship manager (RM), define the vision and strategy for the evolution of the business information system to meet the strategic and operational challenges of the beneficiary services and the concerned institutions.
  • Support the beneficiary services in their business or technological monitoring with their partners: confederation, other cantons, etc.
  • Plan, initiate, and supervise the evolutions of the business information system.
  • Document the reference framework of the business information system (processes, functional, application).
  • Participate in the management of the "SI Management and Business Relationship" domain.
  • Profile sought
  • Experience in managing and directing IT projects. Management of several important (or complex) projects in a similar environment (public administration or large company).
  • Master's degree in information systems or equivalent title.
  • Experience in the urbanization of information systems. Having completed projects such as modeling and optimizing processes, or developing master plans for information systems.
  • Experience, or failing that a strong interest, in the field of education and training in Switzerland, as well as sensitivity to the functioning of public or parapublic institutions.
  • Technical knowledge and infrastructure are a strong asset.
  • Stress resistance, patience, and pedagogy.
  • Rigor, autonomy, and precision in work (documentation, monitoring, reporting, planning).
  • What we offer
  • Residence in Switzerland.
  • Position within the Vaud Cantonal Administration (State of Vaud), Switzerland.

  • christophe.robin@vd.ch) is at your disposal.
