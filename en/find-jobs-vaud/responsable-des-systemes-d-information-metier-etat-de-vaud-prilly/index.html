Full description

We are looking for a person in charge of the Business Information System and the Business Information System. If you wish to contribute to the evolution of the SIs of the Cantonal Administration Vaudoise, and if you are particularly interested in the fields of training and cultural affairs, then join us!

  • Under the responsibility of the Trade Relations Officer (RM), define the vision and strategy for the development of the business IS to meet the strategic and operational challenges of the beneficiary services and the institutions concerned.
  • Support beneficiary services in their professional or technological monitoring with their partners: confederation, other cantons, etc.
  • Plan, initialize and oversee developments in the trade IS.
  • Document the trade IS repository (process, functional, application).
  • Participate in the management of the "SI Management and Business Relationship" area. CV in French and letter of application in PDF format. Please also attach the work certificates and diplomas. For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact the head of the trade relationship of the SI Formation, Culture, Christophe Robin (tel. 021 316 04 77 - christophe.robin@vd.ch ) is available.
  • Experience in managing and directing IT projects. Management of several major (or complex) projects in a similar environment (public administration or large enterprise).
  • Master's degree in information systems or equivalent.
  • Experience in urbanization of information systems. Have carried out projects such as modelling and optimisation of processes, or the development of master plans of information systems.
  • An experience, or otherwise a strong interest, in the field of education and training in Switzerland, and a sensitivity to the functioning of public or parastatal institutions.
  • Technical knowledge and infrastructure are a strong asset.
  • Stress resistance, patience and pedagogy.
  • Rigor, autonomy and precision in the work (documentation, monitoring, reporting, planning). Special penalties:
  • May be required to work outside normal working hours (night, Saturday, Sunday or holiday)
  • Home in Switzerland

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
