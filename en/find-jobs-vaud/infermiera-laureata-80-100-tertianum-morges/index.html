Does your heart beat for the elderly? Is the nursing profession your vocation, which allows you not only to improve the quality of life of others, but also to give meaning to your life? For the establishment Tertianum Résidence la Gottaz located in Morges, we are immediately looking for or to arrange a

80-100% qualified nurse

Your tasks

  • Support and care of residents in accordance with our care concept and the care process
  • Taking charge of tasks as a reference person for care
  • Responsibility and control of the care process of assigned guests, including classification of PLAISIR/RAI care needs
  • Collaborate with external stakeholders (doctor, physio, etc.)
  • Electronic documentation of care via CareCoach
  • Planning and support for medical visits
  • Taking charge of daily responsibility with coordination of the daily team
  • Management of case discussions and nursing reports
  • Taking charge of tasks as thematic referent for a specialized subject, and participation in the transversal network on the quality of care

Your profile

  • Completed HES nursing training or equivalent training recognized by the CRS
  • Professional experience in long-term care is an asset
  • RAI/PLEASURE knowledge desired
  • Organized, calm, rigorous, committed and motivated
  • A warm and humorous attitude
  • Enjoyment of taking on varied challenges
  • Good knowledge of French orally and in writing
