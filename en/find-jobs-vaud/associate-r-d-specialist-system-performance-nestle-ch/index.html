Be a force for good and grow through experiences that push you further.

As an Associate R&D Specialist System Performance at Nestlé, your mission is to drive the capability of Nestlé's in-home portioned systems, such as Nespresso and Nescafé Dolce Gusto, to consistently deliver the perfect cup and user experience. You’ll make a difference by collaborating closely with Front End Innovation, Development and Industrialization teams. This is your opportunity to take ownership, grow your expertise, and make a difference at scale.

How you’ll make an impact as an Associate R&D Specialist System Performance

  • Driving system performance to deliver consistent cup quality and an excellent user experience across beverage systems.
  • Building science-based understanding of machine, product and packaging interactions to optimize design decisions.
  • Leveraging models, data analysis and digital tools to accelerate learning beyond physical testing.
  • Providing clear physical explanations of system phenomena to support confident technical decisions.
  • Contributing to troubleshooting, root cause analysis and commissioning support to enable successful project delivery.
  • Strengthening cross-functional collaboration and competitive benchmarking to support innovation and continuous improvement.

What will make you successful

  • Bringing strong knowledge of physics, fluid mechanics and beverage system performance to solve complex technical challenges.
  • Applying a science-based mindset to understand interactions between machine, product and packaging.
  • Using data analysis, modelling and digital tools, including R or Python, to generate insights and guide decisions.
  • Collaborating across product, packaging and machine teams with curiosity, openness and a hands-on approach.
  • Communicating fluently in French and English.
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning.
