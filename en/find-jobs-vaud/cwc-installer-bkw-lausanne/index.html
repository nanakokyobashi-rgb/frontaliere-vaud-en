Who are we?

R. Monnet & Cie SA is an electrical installation company in French-speaking Switzerland, founded in 1932. As a competent partner in the high-voltage and low-voltage power sector as well as in telecommunications, we accompany our clients from consulting to maintenance through the installation. Excellence is our goal – that is why we guarantee top-notch expertise in consulting and installation, as well as the implementation of modern and environmentally friendly technologies. Within the BKW Group, we are cultivating an inclusive welcome culture, open to all.
