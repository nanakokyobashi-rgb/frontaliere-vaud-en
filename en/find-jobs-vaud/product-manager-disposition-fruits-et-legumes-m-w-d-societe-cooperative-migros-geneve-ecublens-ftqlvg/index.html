Full description

Behind each product available in store, there are choices, data and a lot of anticipation. Your turn! You pilot the novel catalogue, coordinate the actors and transform the needs of the field into actions. Your challenge: anticipate, decide and act so that our customers find the right products at the right time.

Luogo di Lavoro: Société coopérative Migros Genève, Migros Marketing Romand, Chemin du Dévent, 1024 Ecublens

Important remarks

The use of women aims at simplicity, but our recruitment is open to all.

Mansioni

  • You pilot the assortment of the Romaned catalogue by following the market trends and expectations of our clients. You identify the products that will make a difference in our stores.
  • You coordinate new product launches, from planning to monitoring results. You connect the different actors and stay on course for every launch.
  • You're piloting branch orders with suppliers. You follow deadlines, anticipate deviations and act quickly so that the products arrive at the right place at the right time.
  • You keep our planning and command tools alive. You ensure the quality of the data produced and ensure that every useful information is reliable and up-to-date.
  • You're accompanying the installation of assortments in stores. You pass on the right information to the subsidiaries and work with the teams to bring the products to life in the field.
  • You plan operations and monitor product availability. You analyze the situation, anticipate needs and adjust actions to support sales and limit breakups.

Requirements

  • 5 years of experience: in product management: preferably in the retail and fruit and vegetable sectors.
  • Studies completed (UK/EFP, HES, ES): in management, marketing or related technical field.
  • You can analyze and decide. You step back, connect information and find simple solutions. You move even when not all the answers are on the table.
  • You like moving projects forward with others. You create links, share information and know how to mobilize different professions around a common goal.
  • You put the client at the centre of your decisions. You understand the expectations of the French market, look for concrete solutions and always keep an eye on the results.
  • French (Very good knowledge)
  • German (Good knowledge)

Cosa offeriamo

Recruitment process

  • Preselection of files: We constantly review the applications received, pre-screen and forward them to the manager. The decision to pursue or eliminate the recruitment process is communicated by telephone or e-mail. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Personality/performance test: Under certain conditions, we take an additional step, the personality test (BIP, scale test or similar) to support the selection. The results of this test are discussed personally and form the basis for the continuation of the selection process. Return time: up to 1 week
  • Professional interview: During the professional interview, the manager and/or other specialized person, or even the team, asks specific questions about knowledge to accomplish the work. If possible, then there is more time to get to know the workstation and team in a broken stick discussion. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • Interview with HR and the specialized department: The interview is conducted in distanciel or face-to-face with the HR person responsible and/or the specialized department. It serves primarily to learn more about personal and professional skills and allows both parties to assess cultural compatibility with the company and the team. Return time: up to 3 weeks
  • References: When we ask for references, we look again at strengths and weaknesses and cause them to be overturned or confirmed by former superiors or collaborators. References may be requested after the first or second round and only with consent. Return time: up to 1 week
  • Offer and contract preparation (digital): The main points of the contract are checked again after our verbal job offer. The establishment of the contract is then launched by mutual agreement before being sent by e-mail for digital signature. Return time: up to 1 week
