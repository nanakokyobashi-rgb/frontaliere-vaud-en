Full description

The cooperative ZFV-Unternehmungen (ZFV) creates, in collaboration with its partners, inspiring meeting places that are enthusiastic about their hospitality and authentic experiences. Founded in 1894 by visionary Zuricho bourgeois women, the ZFV still attaches particular importance to innovation and sustainability. In more than 200 establishments in Switzerland, ZFV employees meet the needs of customers and guests. Areas of activity include restaurants, refectories, staff restaurants, hotels, mobile catering solutions, catering, bakeries and crèches. The cooperative actively contributes to shaping society and takes a stand for the environment, equal opportunities and inclusion. She lives hospitality for a society in which we all want to live. Restaurant of the RTS Ecublens, managed by ZFV from January 2026, will be open five days a week from 7:30am to 4:30pm. The property has 200 seating positions in the restaurant, serves approximately 180 meals a day and consists of a team of 4 people. Every day you will find 3 different and varied menus as well as a buffet of salads, desserts and snacks. A catering service for events is available on request Are you our new team member in French-speaking Switzerland, who will help us as a cook-er?
