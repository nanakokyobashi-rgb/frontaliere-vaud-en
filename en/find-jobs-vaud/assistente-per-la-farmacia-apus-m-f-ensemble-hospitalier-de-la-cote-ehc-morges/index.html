To strengthen our team, we are looking for a

Nursing unit pharmacy assistant - APUS (M/F) at 50-70% Pool

Duration: indefinite Entry into office: as soon as possible Workplace: Morges

WE OFFER YOU

  • A working atmosphere that values exchange within a dynamic team
  • The opportunity to participate in the development of pharmacy activities
  • An opportunity for continuing education
  • 5 weeks of annual vacation, 6 weeks from the age of 50, 10 free public holidays
  • A mobility plan including participation in your non-motorized journey
  • Remuneration based on the valorization of your years of previous professional experience as well as bonuses linked to seniority at the PIC

YOU OWN

  • A CFC pharmacy assistant or equivalent
  • Prior experience in preparing weekly schedules, an asset
  • A strong sense of responsibility, precision and good communication and adaptation skills
  • Availability to work on Wednesdays and every other Saturday
  • Flexibility to supplement this position with hours during vacation replacements

YOUR OBJECTIVES

  • Ensure the preparation of weekly workers in the Surgery and Medicine departments and in the EMS of the Ensemble Hospitalier de la Côte, as well as in the EMS of the Groupement Hospitalier de l’Ouest Lémanique (Rolle).
  • Manage stocks and supply of medications in various departments
  • Interact with medical care teams and answer their questions relating to medications in collaboration with pharmacists
  • Promote medication safety

WE ARE

  • A company with more than 50 employees spread across 7 different professions (F/M: pharmacist, pharmacy assistant, pharmacy assistant in care units, preparer, warehouse worker, delivery driver, administrative assistant).
  • An interhospital pharmacy offering different services to its partners, such as clinical pharmacy, pharmaceutical assistance and the manufacturing of individualized medications (https://pharmpic.ch).
