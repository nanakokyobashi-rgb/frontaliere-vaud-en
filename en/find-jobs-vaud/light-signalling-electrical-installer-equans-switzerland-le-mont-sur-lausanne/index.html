Full description

To support our growth and participate in projects essential for the urban mobility of tomorrow, we are looking for an installer-electrician to strengthen our team in the Road Traffic Regulation Department.

Your Mission

At the heart of light-signalling projects, you contribute to the realization of new installations and the maintenance of signalling systems:

  • Participate in the installation, assembly, commissioning and maintenance of external installations (light signalling, retractable terminals, barriers and site lights)
  • Manage electromechanical installations, commissioning and control of access control and traffic analysis facilities, and traffic control facilities
  • Ensure quality, compliance, timeliness and traceability of the work carried out
  • Strictly apply existing safety regulations and standards, rules and guidelines
  • Actively contribute to continuous improvement by proposing measures to enhance the safety, quality and productivity of interventions
  • Working closely with the various teams in the department
  • Ensure proper maintenance of vehicles, tools and equipment made available
  • Perform interventions throughout the French-speaking Switzerland, occasionally in Ticino, over one or sometimes several days
  • Participate in picket service (approximately once a month)

Your Profile

  • Electrician installer CFC or equivalent training.
  • 4-6 years of successful electrical experience
  • Good command of French in both oral and written terms
  • Good reading skills for electrical diagrams and execution plans
  • A rigorous, reliable and quality-oriented method of work
  • Independent, proactive, pragmatic and solutions-oriented personality
  • Strong team spirit and naturally customer-oriented
  • Essential category B driving licence (trailer and/or heavy vehicle licences are an asset)

Your Opportunity

  • Concrete and visible projects that contribute to the development of safe, efficient and sustainable mobility
  • A stimulating working environment within a committed and passionate team
  • Various missions and real autonomy in your activity
  • Opportunities for continuing training and professional development
  • Prospects for future developments
  • The opportunity to participate actively in the growth and innovation of our department
