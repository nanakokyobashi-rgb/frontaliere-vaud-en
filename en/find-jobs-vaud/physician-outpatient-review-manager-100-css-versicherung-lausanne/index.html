Full description

At the CSS, we are the health partner of about 1.7 million insured people. And maybe soon your partner for your professional future? We would be delighted, because thanks to your medical knowledge, you help us keep the cost of benefits low. We have some interesting offers for you: excellent working hours, teleworking and excellent development opportunities. We are a great team of passionate medical specialists.

Your mission

  • You check and process proof of benefits in your area of competence
  • You write standard correspondence in your field of expertise
  • You provide information to insured persons, benefit providers, agencies and other internal services
  • You participate in group sessions and individual internal interviews
  • You execute special mandates in accordance with the direction of the team leader/fe and the group leader/fe
  • You prepare, sporadically, simple or standardized requests for the medical consultant (or other specialists)

What you bring

  • You benefit from commercial or paramedical training with experience in health insurance. The Federal Health Insurance Patent is an asset
  • You have good expertise in Tarmed/Tardoc
  • You have good computer skills, excellent French writing and knowledge of languages such as German or English is an asset.
  • You have interest in specific contracts
  • Rigor, precision, sense of responsibility, autonomy and team spirit are skills that describe you and contribute to the high quality service for our customers
  • Thanks to your service and customer orientation, you handle the requests of your interlocutors in a competent and courteous manner, in all personal aspects. Customer orientation and thinking about solutions and novelty
  • Work flexibly: Adapts your work every year to your lifestyle, with opportunities for part time and other flexible models such as telework, to name but one.
  • Enthusiasm your purse: Benefits such as bonuses, Reka cheques, preferential health insurance premiums, etc., which can quickly reach several thousand francs per year.
  • Choose your holiday model: Five or six weeks of vacation? You choose. You work 41 or 42 hours a week, respectively.
  • Keep training: We want you to continue training internally or externally and generously support you with time and money.
  • Keep the form: ergonomic work places, promotion of psychic well-being and many other offers related to health.
  • Engage in social matters: Is life good for you? So make others enjoy it and broaden your horizons, alone or as a team.
