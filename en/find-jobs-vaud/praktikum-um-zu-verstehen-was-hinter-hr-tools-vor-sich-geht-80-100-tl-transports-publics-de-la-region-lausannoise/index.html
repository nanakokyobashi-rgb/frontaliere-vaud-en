Full description

Evaluations, onboarding, access management, SuccessFactors and SAP projects: behind each HR tool, there are processes to design, test and adopt. For 12 months, you will be at the heart of this mechanics, in a HR team that accompanies more than 2,000 collaborators. You won't be here to observe. From the first few weeks you will have your own subjects, real responsibilities and a visible impact on the daily lives of our teams.

Internship to understand what's going on behind HR tools (80-100%)

Your Mission

Rotating and improving our HR processes

  • Build and document the forms and objectives of our evaluation process.
  • Support users when they encounter difficulties and help them solve the problem, in conjunction with SI teams.
  • Manage access to our tools (PeopleDoc, LMS) and structure our documentary space.

Contribute to our transformation projects

  • SuccessFactors project: to evolve our announcements and evaluation forms for trainees.
  • SAP project: participate in various projects and discover inside the operation of an ERP.
  • Test new features, trace anomalies, coordinate test sessions, and write procedures related to SI teams.

What you will gain

  • Expertise on SuccessFactors and SAP, two tools in HR and support functions.
  • A complete vision of a digitalisation project, from need to production.
  • A true autonomy, close proximity and a concrete experience, valuable for the continuation of your career.

Your profile

You are a student or a young graduate of a university or high school and you are as interested in human resources as well as in the operation of information systems. You like to understand how things work, test them, and explain them clearly. Organised, autonomous and rigorous, you stay on course when several subjects advance in parallel. You are comfortable with Excel and know how to communicate with both HR and technical profiles. Reading documentation in English is no problem.
