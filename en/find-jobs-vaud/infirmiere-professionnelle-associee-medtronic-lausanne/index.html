A Day in the Life This role is critical to our organisation as it ensures the continuity of Occupational Health services, effective case management, and absenteeism monitoring, all of which are essential to protecting employee wellbeing, supporting a safe return to work, and maintaining operational effectiveness at the Tolochenaz site Responsibilities may include the following and other duties may be assigned.

  • Provides nursing services and first aid to employees or persons who become ill or injured at work facilities and industrial plants.
  • Administers health management programs such as workers' compensation, long-term disability, and wellness programs.
  • Maintains record of persons treated; prepares accident reports; provides health education information; dispenses standard drugs and medicines such as aspirin and cough or cold tablets; refers contagious diseases or serious injuries to company or employee's physician.
  • Must be a Registered Nurse (RN).
  • May work with industrial toxicology and/or hygienists to recognize environmental factors and/or stresses in or from the workplace that may cause sickness, impaired health, or discomfort among the workers.

Required Knowledge and Experience

  • Registered Nurse qualification (Swiss Red Cross recognition required for foreign diplomas) and licence to practice in the Canton of Vaud.
  • CAS in Occupational Health.
  • Minimum 3 years of professional experience, preferably in occupational health or an industrial environment.
  • Strong knowledge of Swiss occupational health regulations, labour law, health protection, and social insurance systems (LAA, AI, APG, etc.).
  • Good understanding of the healthcare network and medico-social resources in the Canton of Vaud.
  • Proven training, communication, interdisciplinary collaboration, and autonomous working skills, with advanced English proficiency for supporting international employees and delivering training sessions.
