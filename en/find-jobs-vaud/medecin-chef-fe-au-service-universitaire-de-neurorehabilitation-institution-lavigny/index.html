Full description

The university neurorehabilitation service of CHUV and the Institution of Lavigny is looking for a Chief Medical Officer at an activity rate of 80% to 100% in order to strengthen his medical team. The date of entry into office is set for 1 January 2027 or to be agreed . The Vaud University Hospital (CHUV) is one of the five Swiss university hospitals. Through its collaboration with the Faculty of Biology and Medicine of the University of Lausanne and EPFL, CHUV plays a leading role in the fields of medical care, medical research and training. The Department of Clinical Neurosciences includes services in neurosurgery, neurology, neuropsychology and neurorehabilitation, the Leenaards Centre for Memory, the Centre for Neuroscience Research and the Spinal Surgery Service. It offers integrated and personalized management for patients suffering from diseases of the central and peripheral nervous system, from brain to muscle. Deployed at several sites, the Lavigny Institution consists of a neurological hospital providing stationary and outpatient services, two departments for adults with disabilities, with an intellectual disability with a physical problem, a specialized school and a workshop and vocational training sector respectively. The University Service of Interinstitutional Neurorehabilitation brings together the neurorehabilitation structures of the CHUV and the Institution of Lavigny, and belongs to the Department of Clinical Neurosciences of the CHUV. The SUN provides state-of-the-art care for neurorelated patients, with special expertise in cognition and behaviour, spasticity, neuromuscular diseases, and neurourology. The SUN covers the entire neurorehabilitation course, with an acute neurorehabilitation unit (2 beds for post-coma patients and a mobile team providing early neurorehabilitation management, a few days from the onset of neurological injury), two hospital units for post-acute neurorehabilitation (65 beds between Lavigny Hospital and 10 beds at CHUV), an outpatient neurorehabilitation centre and day hospital at CHUV, as well as research structures allowing SUN patients access to translational research and technologies including randomized controlled studies. The SUN ensures close links with the Neurology, Neurosurgery, Spinal Surgery and the Leenaards Centre for Memory in the DNC, optimizing the management of patients in need of neurorehabilitation. More than 300 people work in the inter-institutional SUN perimeter. The SUN medical team is based on a single Head of Service at the CHUV and is composed of 5 executive doctors, specialists in neurology and/or physical medicine and rehabilitation, 8 head of clinic and 7 assistant doctors.
