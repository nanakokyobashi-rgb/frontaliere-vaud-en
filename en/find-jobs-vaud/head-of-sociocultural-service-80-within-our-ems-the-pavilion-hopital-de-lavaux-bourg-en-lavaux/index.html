Full description

The Lavaux Hospital is a private foundation recognized in the public interest, a member of the Vaudian Hospital Federation. Located in Cully (Bourg-en-Lavaux), in the heart of the vineyards and close to public transport, school and Lavaux day care. An expansion project is planned for 2027, with services being moved to new premises currently under construction at the hospital site. This project will, in particular, double the capacity of our EMS and open up good prospects for development. Our EMS is a real place of life, where every resident can find his place, maintain his social ties and enjoy a daily life full of meetings, exchanges and activities. To support this vision, all hospital services work hand in hand, in a complementary and interdisciplinary approach, for the well-being and quality of life of residents.

In this perspective, we are looking for a

Head of sociocultural service at 80% Mission

  • Develop the animation program based on the wishes and ideas of the residents, promoting their active participation, their autonomy and the implementation of projects that make sense for them.
  • Coordinate, mentor and support the staff of the socio-cultural service, promoting their commitment, cohesion and skills development.
  • Be a force for proposals and drive new initiatives to renew and enrich the social and cultural life of EMS.
  • Work closely with health care staff and participate in the interdisciplinary project approach to support residents.
  • Monitor activities and documentation in the residents' computerized file.
  • Participation in the supervision of apprentices ESA.

Profile sought Technical expertise

  • HES-SO diploma in sociocultural animation; an ES degree in the field may also be considered.
  • A prior experience in EMS is a serious asset.
  • Experience in a team management or coordination function.
  • Training of trainer in company (FEE), an asset.
  • Good control of common computer tools.
  • Driving licence B

Human skills

  • Excellent organizational capacity and sense of responsibility.
  • Leadership, ability to unite, mobilize and support a team.
  • Creativity, dynamism and strength of proposal.
  • Autonomy, initiative and decision-making.
  • Relationship strength and taste for interdisciplinary team work.
  • Ability to breathe, carry and support change, with a constructive vision and oriented towards the development and evolution of practices.

We offer

  • A position of responsibility within a human-sized institution.
  • A dynamic working environment, valuing proximity, exchanges and interdisciplinary collaboration.
  • The opportunity to contribute to the development of a real place of life for residents.
  • Opportunities for continuing training and professional development.
  • Working conditions governed by the collective labour agreement for the public health sector (CCT San Vaud).

Date of entry: to be agreed
