Full description

With several years of experience in personnel management, accounting management and secretariat, you want to put your skills at the service of a dynamic public heritage institution, recognized for its missions of general interest? As a reference secretary for human resources and accounting within the Vaudese cantonal archives, you ensure the administrative management of fixed and auxiliary staff, as well as the monitoring of the financial and accounting activities of the institution. In addition to these central missions, there are responsibilities related to the reception of users, the management of the secretariat and the economy. You also act as a reference person for occupational health and safety issues. By assuming this essential function, you will play a decisive role in the life of the institution. Your skills, your sense of service and your rigorous and dempathetic approach will be valuable assets to support the development and outreach of the Vaud cantonal archives

  • Provides administrative HR management of the institution's fixed and non-permanent staff and acts as HR respondent.
  • Ensure the financial and accounting management of the unit and act as accounting respondent.
  • Manages economics and orders for equipment.
  • Ensure the reception of users and users, as well as the day-to-day management of the secretariat, in collaboration and complementarity with the Secretary of Unit.
  • Ensure the role of respondent in occupational health and safety and contribute to the implementation of preventive measures within the institution. For further information, please consult the specifications here or contact Ms Delphine Friedmann, Director, in charge of recruitment at 021 316 37 11. Only complete files will be reviewed.

  • Knowledge of the legal and normative framework specific to the Vaud cantonal administration in the HR and accounting fields.
