Role overview

Mission

EPFL's Central Environmental Laboratory (CEL) is looking for a 50-60% scientific assistant to participate in an interdisciplinary research project dedicated to the study of human exposure to chemicals associated with microplastics by the respiratory route.

This project, carried out in collaboration with several Swiss universities, research centres and hospitals, aims to better understand the transfer and fate of chemical additives released by microplastics after inhalation, as well as their potential for lung exposure.
