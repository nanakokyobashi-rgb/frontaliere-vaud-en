Full description

Going to work with pleasure: a more for your health Avenue de Savoie 10, 1003 Lausanne Our diversity is our capital: CONCORDIA brings together people from different cultures, professions and areas of responsibility. Will you also enrich our team soon? For our service center in Lausanne, we look for versatile and open-minded people like you.

Your responsibilities

  • You take over the independent follow-up and telephone advice of our clients in terms of services.
  • You control and process outpatient services according to rates, contracts and insurance coverage.
  • You manage the administrative part of your client portfolio.
  • Your contacts are insured persons, doctors, hospitals, therapists and collaborators from other departments of CONCORDIA.

Your profile

  • You have basic medical training (medical assistant or pharmacy assistant, drug adjunct) combined with continuing business training or professional experience in administration.
  • Or basic commercial training with a sickness fund.
  • Your mother tongue is French and you have good knowledge of German (an asset).
  • You're looking for a varied and demanding activity.
  • You work independently, precisely and quickly.
  • Do you know MS Office well? It's not a problem for you!
  • You like administrative tasks and you're comfortable in correspondence.

Your workplace

Why choose CONCORDIA?

Health is at the heart of our concerns. That is why we are committed to offering our employees a positive and rewarding working environment, characterized by varied tasks, versatile teams and a friendly atmosphere. In addition, we are proud to promote professional development within the company. Having fun working while proving its worth is possible at CONCORDIA. For a rewarding working life is a real plus for health! Why choose CONCORDIA?

Your benefits

Family benefits As part of maternity leave, the salary is paid in full for 14 weeks. CONCORDIA grants additional child and vocational training allowances and covers up to 90% of childcare costs. Health insurance You and your family get reduced health insurance premiums. Warm working environment CONCORDIA favours a friendly working atmosphere.
