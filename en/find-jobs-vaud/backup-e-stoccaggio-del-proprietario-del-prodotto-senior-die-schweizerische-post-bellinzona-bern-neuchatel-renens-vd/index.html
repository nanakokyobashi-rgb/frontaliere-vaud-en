You are interested in the product areas backup, storage, data protection and business continuity in a dynamic environment? Then you are exactly right with us.

You Can Make That Do

  • As Product Owner:in you actively shape the work in a bleak bleak team within the SAFe-Frameworks.
  • The responsibility includes product roadmaps, the budget and the future alignment of the backup and storage product portfolio.
  • Strategic transformation initiatives and the further development of backup, recovery, archiving and storage-Services drive you independently.
  • You can develop the data protection and storage platforms into automated, self-service-enabled service offerings, and coordinate product strategy, engineering skills, and customer requirements.
  • For the implementation of the updated topics along the roadmap, you will give the teams the necessary epics and strategic orientation and ensure the sustainable development of the solutions.

You bring this with you

  • You have several years of experience as Product Owner, Product Manager, or professional manager in a challenging technology or infrastructure environment and will provide a qualification.
  • You actively shape changes and accompany organizations, hybrid teams and customers through transformation, migration and modernization projects.
  • You can translate strategic target images into clear product strategies, roadmaps and concrete added value for customers and the company.
  • You bring together complex interests of stakeholders, specialist departments, suppliers and delivery teams and create sustainable solutions even in challenging change situations.
  • A strong understanding of business-critical platforms, services, as well as security, compliance, resilience and data protection requirements allows you to make sustainable and future-oriented decisions.
  • Agile working methods and collaboration in scaled organizations are familiar to you, and you understand how to align teams to common goals, effectively accompany them and translate technological developments into clear business benefits.
  • Personally, you convince yourself by entrepreneurial thinking, high communication and social skills, analytical strength, and the ability to attract people to change.
  • You have very good knowledge of a national language and very good knowledge of English.
