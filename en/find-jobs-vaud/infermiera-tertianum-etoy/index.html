Nurse Head — Tertianum

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

We are looking for our Tertianum Le Pacific site located in Etoy (80 residents) a Cheffe Nurse or Chief Nurse.

Your Responsibilities

Quality and safety of care

  • Ensuring safe and appropriate care for each resident: care plans, protocols, prevention of falls, escarres and medication errors.
  • Supervise and update care needs assessments (PLAISIR).
  • Monitoring quality indicators and guaranteeing cantonal regulations on care.
  • Promote the internal reporting procedure in case of abuse or dangerous care.

Care team management

  • Supervise the two unit managers and, through them, the entire team.
  • Ensure planning in accordance with the Vaudoise Public Health Commission and the allocation.
  • Recruit, integrate and train collaborators; conduct annual interviews.
  • Acting on absenteeism and team climate, with listening and demand.

Pilotage and resources

  • Monitor monthly staffing, replacement and acting, and care budget.
  • Present these indicators to the monthly review with management, with proposals for correction.

Coordination

  • Work with responding physicians, pharmacy, hospitals and the network that directs residents.
  • Be the interlocutor or contact of families for care issues, including in difficult situations.
  • Collaborate with socio-cultural, hospitality and cooking (regimes, textures, life projects).

Continuity and projects

  • Ensure the clinical and operational continuity of the site in the absence of the Director.
  • Representing care in the residents' life projects approach.
  • Work with La Gottaz Cheffe Nurse to harmonize practices.

Your profile

  • Diploma of nurse (Bachelor HES or ES), recognized in Switzerland; recognition of the Swiss Red Cross for a foreign diploma.
  • Health management training (CAS or DAS in management, or equivalent), completed or ongoing.
  • Confirmed experience as a caregiver, ideally in EMS, geriatrics or psychogeriatrics.
  • Community leadership: presence on the ground, listening, ability to federate and calm, while remaining demanding.
  • Rigueur sur la qualité et la sécurité , et good knowledge of Vaudois cantonal requirements.
  • Organized and methodical, comfortable with schedules, indicators and figures.
  • Autonomous and responsible: you decide at your level and raise a problem with a solution.
  • Clear and caring communication with families, doctors and teams.
  • Tools: computerized care record (careCoach) and planning tool
  • Excel's mastery is an asset.
  • Languages: French
  • German is an asset.

How We Work

  • Decisions based on facts and figures.
  • A weekly point with the director, a monthly review of indicators.
  • Wide autonomy, with clear delegations.
  • The safety of residents and employees as a first priority.

What We Offer

  • A key role, at the heart of a human-sized residence, with garden, animals and a model open to families.
  • Direct support from management and quality manager.
  • A leading group in Switzerland, with prospects for evolution.
  • Conditions according to the CLC of the parapublic health sector Vaudois.

Application

CV, cover letter and copies of diplomas to be deposited on the recruitment portal. The files are treated confidentially.
