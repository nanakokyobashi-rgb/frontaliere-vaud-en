Full description

It is with great passion and love of detail that our employees make GLOBUS the first address of the Swiss Luxury and Premium. They are people like you who make the difference and, with us, give meaning to "the place we love to be".

IT'S WHAT YOU DO DIFFERENCE

  • As a host, you ensure that our customers always feel welcome and in good hands.
  • With your excellent service and personalized advice, you guarantee unique shopping experiences.
  • You support the visual merchandising (including promotions) and the organization of tastings, which awakens the inspiration of our customers as well as the desire to buy our exclusive products.
  • You are responsible for ordering the goods as well as controlling the entries of goods, products and data.
  • You ensure strict compliance with the internal and legal hygiene guidelines and requirements (HACCP).
  • Thanks to your positive aura and your passion for our "delicatessa", you make a decisive contribution to the company's success.

WHAT DISTINCE YOU

  • You are a service oriented person and passionate about quality food products.
  • You like to take on the role of host and live it fully, and your approach is resolutely customer-oriented.
  • You have successfully completed a professional apprenticeship as a retail manager and already have professional experience in fresh products and especially cheeses.
  • You have an irreproachable lifestyle and a neat appearance.
  • You are very reliable, flexible (possible missions from Monday to Saturday) and show initiative.
  • You have excellent knowledge of the French language
  • English and other languages are a major asset in serving our international clientele.

BENEFITS FOR YOU With more than 1,700 other retail enthusiasts, you are an integral part of our fascinating world of high-end and luxury. You are recognized and valued, you work with an exciting and motivated team and we give you our trust so that you can enjoy free time and make a difference with your know-how. If you're an expert in your profession and you're putting your heart into it, many doors will open to develop your career. We will gladly support you in your internal and external trainings.

You can also enjoy the following benefits

  • an exceptional working environment with a horizontal hierarchy in the city center.
  • attractive social benefits .
  • flexible work models and at least 5 weeks of vacation (PS: You can enjoy more free time thanks to the option "unpaid leave" - from 7 to 365 days).
  • up to 50% discount on Globus universe products for you and your family.
  • and much more...
