Full description

The Riviera-Chablais Hospital, Vaud-Valais (HRC), is a reference intercantonal hospital, which brings together more than 2,500 collaborators. Rennaz Hospital has over 350 acute care beds. It is complemented by two Geriatric and Rehabilitation Clinics and two medical clinics. All employees invest on a daily basis to provide quality care to patients, embodying the institution's values of authenticity, exemplaryness and innovation.

The oncology-haematology department is looking for a

100% M/F Medical Secretary

The main tasks of this post are

  • Provide medical secretariat for the oncology-haematology department
  • Writing and formatting of medical correspondence
  • Administrative management of patient files, timeliness
  • Email management, telephone calls
  • Close collaboration with physicians and health care teams and other services
  • Participation in the daily functioning of the service
  • Ensures confidentiality of information and compliance with institutional procedures.

We ask

  • An EFA or an equivalent title
  • Experience as a medical secretary in an oncology department an asset
  • A relational sense (empathy, active listening, service orientation)
  • A developed sense of organization and precision, as well as good quality in the performance of tasks
  • Stress resistance and ability to work under pressure
  • Convenience with MS Office and Office Tools
  • A mastery of French (oral and written) and good knowledge in English an asset

We offer

The HRC offers great job opportunities and career development in all its fields of activity: medical, nursing, medico-technical, hotel, administrative, technical, logistics. In a desire for continuous improvement, skills development is at the heart of the HRC's concerns. The institution offers its employees access to training both internally and externally.
