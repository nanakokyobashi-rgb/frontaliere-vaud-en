Full description

Mission

The School of Computer and Communication Sciences is looking for a full-time lecturer position in Computer Science.

The steady increase in the number of students at EPFL requires strengthening our teaching team for classes taught to Computer Science students.

Main duties and responsibilities

• 80%: Teaching around 2 courses per semester (including at least one bachelor course (with 300+ students) per semester) as well as designing new courses and participating in outreach service activities at the school. • 20%: opportunity to conduct an independent research project or supervise MS students' research projects.

Profile

• PhD in Computer Science or in a related field • Proficiency in English and French • Prior teaching experience (ideally at a university level) • Ability to teach introductory computer science courses • Ability to teach specialized courses in at least one of the following areas: • Machine Learning: including deep learning and reinforcement learning for robotics • Computer Systems: including operating systems and file systems. • Security and Privacy: including systems security and AI security

We offer

At EPFL, we believe that people achieve great results when they feel supported, respected and enabled to succeed. By joining our community, you will help shape the future of society through education, research and innovation.

We offer an inspiring, international and collaborative environment where your work has real impact and your ideas are valued. Combined with flexible ways of working and attractive employment conditions, we aim to create an environment where everyone can contribute, grow and make a difference.

Beyond work, our campuses host a vibrant community and unique environment, enriched by initiatives promoting equality, inclusion, diversity, well-being and sustainability as well as an engaging program of cultural, sports and community activities throughout the year.

The School of Computer and Communication Sciences offers a stimulating, international working environment with a team of dedicated teachers and large classes of highly motivated students. We aim to increase the presence of women amongst its employees, and female candidates are strongly encouraged to apply.
