Mission

In collaboration with researchers.se.s, collaborators.s and other members of EPFL's Swiss Plasma Center (SPC) electronics department, you are involved in the development and maintenance of highly specialized scientific measuring devices for SPC physics experiments, particularly in the framework of the NCCR Genesis project.

Main tasks and responsibilities • The design of measurement electronics: amplification chain and signal processing, from sensor to scanning • AC/DC power development • Implementation and monitoring of developments • Installation and testing of prototypes • Maintenance of existing electronic systems • Technical support and advice to experimenters.rices
