Full description

Following a political and strategic will to strengthen the fight against economic and organised crime, the Financial Brigade of the Vaudian Security Police, in collaboration with the Public Prosecutor's Office, set up a criminal seizure group. The task of this group will be to intensify the confiscation of assets acquired fraudulently by conducting its own investigations and supporting the other brigades of the Vaudian Security Police and the Public Prosecutors' Office (MP). As a specialized criminal law/procedure police officer (APJS), you will participate in investigations and operations to combat economic crime, prepare reports and participate in hearings. You will provide administrative and legal support to investigators and magistrates, propose and implement solutions for seizure and confiscation of criminal assets. You will participate in the training of investigators and magistrates in your field of competence. By joining the CPV, you contribute to a public service committed to serving its population, its security and to maintaining social cohesion.

  • Analyze and return investigative information in a relevant form for judicial purposes.
  • Administratively and legally support investigators and magistrates in the fight against economic crime and the seizure of criminal assets.
  • In his capacity as delegated PYSS, draft and sign reports attached to the judicial file, conduct hearings, participate in police operations and letters rogatory.
  • Identify, develop and propose concepts for the development of new ways of investigating economic crime and seizure of criminal assets.
  • Legally support police officers and work closely with the MP in complex cases.
  • Contribute to close relations and permanent liaison with the School of Criminal Sciences and the UNIL Faculty of Law, EPFL, HEIG-VD and/or other reference schools/institutions in the legal and economic field.
  • Contribute to the legal watch on finance law, banking law, tax law, economic criminal law and economic analysis of law.
  • Participate, in collaboration with the Financial Brigade and MP management, in the establishment of a group specializing in the seizure of criminal assets and work actively on it once established.
  • Provide training related to its specialization to the criminal justice system.
  • Perform permanency services during the week, weekends and holidays.
  • Is equipped with personal safety equipment, except firearms.
