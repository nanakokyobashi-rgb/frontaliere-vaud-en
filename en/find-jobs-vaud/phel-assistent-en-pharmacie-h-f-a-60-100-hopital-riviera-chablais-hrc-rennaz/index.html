Full description

The Pharmacie des Hôpitaux de l'Est Lémanique (PHEL), spread over several sites, is responsible for the supply of medicines and pharmaceutical assistance to hospitals, as well as to various health facilities, in the East of Germany (including the Hospital Riviera-Chablais, Vaud-Valais). The EMS sector of the PHEL is responsible for the supply of medicines and pharmaceutical services to its medical-social institutions (EMS) partners.

For various partner institutions in the Vaud districts of Lausanne, Lavaux-Oron, Riviera-Pays-d-Enhaut and Aigle, we are looking for:

Pharmacy Assistant M/F at 60%-100%

The main tasks of this post are

  • Assistant in Poolist Pharmacy in our partner EMS in the districts of Lausanne, Lavaux-Oron, Riviera-Pays-d-Enhaut and Eagle (30% of the specifications)
  • Pharmacy assistant in a partner EMS still to be defined in the Canton of Vaud: orders for medicines and care equipment, stock management, communications, close collaboration with the healthcare staff (30 to 50% of the specifications)
  • Pharmacy assistant at the Rennaz site for various distribution and billing activities (10 to 20% of the specifications)
  • Participation in continuous improvement and quality documents
  • Miscellaneous administrative and office tasks

We ask

  • A candidate holding the CFC as pharmacy assistant or as equivalent
  • A person with a good team spirit, conscientious, autonomous and well organized, with an openness to the new,
  • A high degree of travel flexibility will be required for this specific position
  • Experience as APUS or in supplying medicines to EMS, as well as the mastery of Tactil billing software, and Word and Excel tools would be an asset

We offer

  • The chance to join a passionate and supportive team, recognized for its excellent social climate.
  • A modern, stimulating and quality oriented environment within a certified hospital pharmacy.
  • Wage conditions as well as benefits according to the HRC Collective Labour Agreement (6 weeks of vacation from 40 years, stamping, etc.).
  • Access to various training courses to enable you to evolve within the company.
  • Exclusive advantages, such as discounts in nearby shops and restaurants.
  • The opportunity to contribute to an essential activity serving the residents of our partner EMS.

Entry into office

On 1 April 2027 or to be agreed

Duration

An indeterminate contract
