Location: Zurich operational area Work model: field service / full-time Contract type: Perpetual

Be a force for good and accelerate change that improves lives and the planet.

As a field sales representative at Nestlé Waters (Suisse) AG, your mission is to successfully position our brands in the catering environment and drive sustainable growth in your region. You make a difference by working closely with your customers and internal teams to build strong relationships and drive impactful sales activities. You will help to further expand our presence in the HORECA channel and strengthen performance in your area. This task offers you the opportunity to actively shape the future of drinks – and to create experiences that enrich the everyday life of many people.

How to develop an impact as a sales representative/in HORECA

  • Acquisition of new customers and establishment of additional catering businesses in your region.
  • Supporting existing customers and expanding long-term partnerships.
  • Development of sales-enhancing concepts and implementation together with your customers.
  • Advising catering companies as well as other relevant channels and beverage retailers on range, solutions and business development.
  • Implementation of defined sales cycle activities to support goal achievement.
  • Carry out promotions and take responsibility for the sales targets of the well-known brands in your area.

What makes you successful

  • Solid experience in sales as well as in-depth knowledge of the catering industry and its processes.
  • Sales-oriented thinking with a focus on sustainable customer relationships.
  • Good knowledge of your region and local restaurants.
  • fluent knowledge of German
  • French is an advantage.
  • Embodiment of our values: Care, Grit, Connection and Ingenuity.
