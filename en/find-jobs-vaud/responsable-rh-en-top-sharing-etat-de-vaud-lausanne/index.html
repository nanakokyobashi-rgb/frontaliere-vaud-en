Full description

We are looking for a HR Manager ready to meet the challenge of exercising a strategic and operational leadership function in top-sharing, in pairs with a HR Manager with experience in job sharing. What's the top-sharing? It is the division of the same position and its responsibilities between two people, who form a true pair of collaborations. The two partners carry the function together, share responsibility and ensure continuity, while working in close coordination. These two people also manage a team together. This model offers a great wealth: two cross-examinations, two ways of thinking and deciding, a way of working that encourages questioning, continuous learning and collective intelligence. But it also requires a particular way of working, because individual success depends on the success of the pair. And let us be honest: this model is not made for everyone. You must like:

  • to question each other, even when you think you are right;
  • communicating a lot, sharing information and not leaving grey areas;
  • receive feedback — and know how to give it with respect;
  • trust and accept that the other does sometimes differently;
  • bring his ideas while at times being ready to change his mind;
  • managing disagreements without turning them into conflicts;
  • putting the collective's success ahead of its personal interests. Anyway, you have to be very good at your job... and even better in the relationship with his pair!
  • To manage the HR entity and participate in the service within its business area as a member of the Executive Committee.
  • Advise and support HR collaborators and managers.
  • Apply development and training processes and ensure the recruitment process for collaborators.
  • Managing workforces and managing time and absences and promoting health and safety at work.
  • Conduct or participate in HR projects, and supervise and coordinate HR Unit projects.
  • Managing the HR team, composed of 4 enthusiastic and professional people

  • Prior experience in human resources of 10 years min., in a public administration an asset, prior experience as HR Manager 5 years.

  • Excellent relational skills: clear and transparent communication, ability to share ideas and manage files in pairs, desire to question each other, ability to give and receive feedback.
