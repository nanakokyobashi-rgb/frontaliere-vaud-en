Full description

This is what awaits you

In this position, you work in the team Services Occupational Pensions in Nyon, which is responsible for the service case handling of pension funds and collective foundations.

This is what you do with us

Your main task is to assess, process and coordinate performance cases of the 2nd pillar.

You are also responsible for processing customer inquiries (by telephone and in writing) in cooperation with various stakeholders (per diem insurance, doctors, employers, disability insurance and accident insurers).

Thanks to your open and solution-oriented personality, you contribute to strengthening our image as “Dear Furniture” by always putting customers first.

This is what you bring with you

  • Commercial/r Employee with EFZ (professional/female with federal professional card is a plus)
  • Some years of experience in the field of social security or occupational pensions
  • Curiosity and interest in pension and social security issues
  • Dynamic, autonomous personality with team spirit
  • Excellent knowledge of the German language in word and writing and good knowledge of the French language

Working with us

The professional pension team is composed of motivated specialists. You work in a pleasant atmosphere characterized by a strong team spirit.

Your workload is 100%, flexible working time models such as home office are open to you. Your place of work can be chosen between Nyon or Zurich. More about our commitment as an employer can be found below.

This is what we offer

Compatibility

We enable flexible working models and offer 20 weeks of maternity leave and 6 weeks of paternity leave, above-average family allowances and a sustainably financed pension fund.

Training and development

We offer a wide range of internal courses as well as participation in possible programs abroad. For job-relevant, external training, we participate generously.

Advantages

Among other things, we offer you up to 25% bonus discount, a half-tax subscription, discounts on mobile phones as well as free subscriptions with flat rate and discounts at cultural events.
