Full description

80-100%, Lausanne / Smart Working

Shape your future with us!

In the Customer Operations sector, you are involved in shaping the customer experience that has suffered a loss and receive benefits. Our clients are at the centre of our concerns and we are looking with them for a solution tailored to their needs. By systematically applying the motto "Customer first", we guarantee very high customer satisfaction.

What's waiting for you

  • Professional management and advice to businesses, insured persons and business partners as part of the administrative management of the PPA
  • Within the framework of the common responsibility of a portfolio, management of priorities and processing of more complex business processes, both at the level of the Provident Fund (such as the establishment of offers, subscription and conclusion of new business, partial liquidation processing, year-end processing, cashing and termination of the contract) and at the level of the insured person (such as risk assessment, buy-back, home ownership promotion, divorce, cash payment and old age pension calculation)
  • Process management in return for our sourcing partner
  • Contribution to continuous improvement of business processes and customer services
  • Coordination and cooperation with internal services such as accounting, benefit service, collection service, our distribution channel and specialized services
  • Coordination with external bodies such as the administrative services of the Confederation, other welfare institutions, lawyers and courts

What you bring

  • Client counselling and referral skills: empathy, solution orientation and rapid response
  • Professional competence: experience in the administrative management of occupational pensions, obtaining a federal patent or the success of a higher education management school are an asset
  • Digital Affinity and Good Computer Skills
  • Mastery of French
  • Foreign language skills (D/E/I); at least one language at a very good level, each additional language would be an advantage
  • Networking, quick understanding with an analytical mind
  • Personal responsibility, decision-making and resilience

What we offer you We are convinced that satisfied employees achieve better performance. That is why we propose salary supplements that bring tangible benefits beyond the ordinary.

Flexibility and freedom: Smart Working (flexible working model), annual working time, five weeks of holidays (six weeks from 50 years), two additional weeks of holidays offered every five years, possibility to buy up to ten additional days of holidays per year

Work and family life: family-friendly meeting hours, 22 weeks of maternity leave, 8 weeks of leave for the other parent, subsidies for day care, company family allowances, days of leave for caring for relatives or in difficult personal situations

Insurance and insurance: an attractive pension fund offering extended overcompulsory benefits and savings options, and up to 30% discount on insurance products

Flexible benefits: AXA Shareplan (staff participation), CHF 500 per year on the Swibeco benefits platform, lunch lunch or lunch card, Smart Working allowance of CHF 200 per year

Mobility: half-rate subscription refund, Reka-Pay up to CHF 1000 per year (20% discount), fleet discount, free electric charging station, AXA bikes to borrow

The right balance at work: free health checkups, health weeks every year, varied physical activity offers (on-line and on-site), massage offers, rest rooms, Employee Care, online health portal, various seminars and events around health prevention and mental health

Development: three days for individual training, wide range of learning and development, free coaching, financial participation in continuing training, promotion of internal mobility, offers for talent development, seminars for people over 55 (AXA Horizon), engagement in communities on different themes, participation in innovative projects (Innoday, AXA Hackathon, UnConference), two days dedicated to volunteer missions (environment and social)
