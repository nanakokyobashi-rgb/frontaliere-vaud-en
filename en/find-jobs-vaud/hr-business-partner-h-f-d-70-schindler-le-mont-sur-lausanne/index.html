We are AS Elevators

AS Lifts is the Swiss partner for the installation, modernization and maintenance of lifts across the country. The diversity and customization of our products distinguish us. Be as unique as we are!

Discover the AS spirit as

HR Business Partner (m/f/d) 70%

Starting at AS Lifts, you will benefit from a respectful and family environment, imbued with a strong team spirit. To learn more about us, consult: video

What's waiting for you

  • Advise and support operational managers on all HR issues
  • Regular presence on Mont-sur-Lausanne, Plan-les-Ouates and La-Chaux-de-Fonds sites
  • Ensure qualitative recruitment for managers and field staff
  • Support and manage the life cycle of employees: onboarding, offboarding, development, disciplinary measures ensuring compliance with legal requirements and our internal directives
  • Implement and monitor the performance management process, including needs analysis, development measures and corrective actions
  • Identify and address cases related to extended absences in collaboration with internal services and external partners
  • Process non-centralized tasks and serve as the first point of contact for general HR requests, coordinating with the Shared Service Center in Ebikon

What you bring

  • Commercial degree (CFC or equivalent) and additional HR training
  • At least 5 years experience in a similar HRBP or HR generalist function
  • MS Office knowledge; SAP/SuccessFactors is an asset
  • Excellent communication skills, customer orientation and ability to work independently and in a structured way
  • Very good language skills in French and German (C1)

Your many advantages

  • Excellent development opportunities: Personal development is at the heart of our culture. We offer various national and international talent programs and support our staff financially and on time for their training and development
  • Work/life balance: 40-hour week, 25-30 holiday days, flexible hours, part-time opportunity and unpaid leave
  • Healthy working environment: High standards of health and safety, internal health management including social counselling and health promotion
  • Employee benefits: generous participation in the public transport and fitness subscription, discount fleet, advantageous purchase of Reka cheques, reduced insurance premiums and various discounts
  • Cultural diversity and inclusion: Strong commitment to diversity and inclusion within the company
