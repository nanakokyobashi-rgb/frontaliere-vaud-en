Your day - your plan! Organize your 40-hour week according to your needs. We attach great importance to your development and offer you exactly what you need. To learn more about us, check out this video

Your new mission

  • Responsibility for managing the company, including several agencies in French-speaking Switzerland (satellite function)
  • Close collaboration with management and other management functions as Finance Business Partner
  • Analysis, presentation and discussion of monthly, quarterly and annual closures
  • Participation in the development of short- and medium-term measures
  • Preparation of medium-term budgets, forecasts and planning and ad hoc analyses
  • Interface between French-speaking Switzerland and the headquarters in Ebikon

Your ideal qualities

  • At least two years of professional experience in operational management control or similar functions, ideally in an industrial environment
  • University degree in business management (Business Administration) or advanced training in business economy and/or continuing training specialized in management control, finance or digitalization
  • Autonomy and ease of communication
  • Pleasure to assume responsibilities and contribute proactively to finding solutions
  • Very good MS Excel mastery, Power BI knowledge is an asset
  • Strong knowledge of ERP systems, SAP knowledge is an asset
  • Current knowledge of French, German and English

Our benefits

  • Excellent development opportunities: Personal development is a reality for us. We provide our employees with various national and international talent programs and support them, both temporally and financially, in their initial and continuing training.
  • Work-life balance: From 25 to 30 days of vacation, flexible hours, possibility of part time and unpaid leave.
  • Healthy work environment: High standards of health and safety and health management at work within the company, offering social counselling and health promotion.
  • Benefits for employees: Participation in public transportation and fitness subscriptions, discounts on the fleet, Reka cheques at reduced prices, premium insurance premiums and various benefits.
  • Cultural diversity and inclusion & diversity within the company.
