Clinique de Montchoisi Description of the company Founded in 1932 and located in the heart of the city of Lausanne, in an exceptional setting facing Lake Geneva and the Alps, the Clinique de Montchoisi has always been one of the most renowned establishments in the canton of Vaud. It brings together the best specialists of national and international renown, especially in its flagship specialties: ophthalmology, orthopaedics, ENT and radiology. The clinic strives to offer excellent care as well as personalized, unique and warm care in a state-of-the-art environment. In 2003, Clinique de Montchoisi joined Swiss Medical Network, one of the two leading Swiss private clinic groups.
