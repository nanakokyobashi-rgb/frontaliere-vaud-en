Full description

Location: Vers-Chez-les-Blanc, Switzerland Work model: Hybrid / Full time Contract type / duration: Permanent

Shape the future through scientific breakthroughs and innovation.

As a Senior Specialist Applied Mathematics & Digital Biology at Nestlé Research, your mission is to apply advanced mathematical modelling, data science, scientific artificial intelligence and digital biology to transform complex health, nutrition, clinical and biological data into predictive, interpretable and experimentally actionable insights. You’ll make a difference by collaborating closely with scientists, data scientists, computational biologists, clinical and translational researchers, nutrition experts, digital teams and external scientific or technology partners. This is your opportunity to turn ideas into sustainable and innovative solutions for people, pets, and the planet.

How you’ll make an impact as a Senior Specialist Applied Mathematics & Digital Biology

  • Leading applied mathematics and digital biology approaches that advance predictive, mechanism-based health and nutrition research.
  • Developing advanced mathematical, statistical and computational models that strengthen scientific decision-making across research programs.
  • Translating complex biological, clinical, nutritional, microbiome and omics data into clear insights that support discovery, validation and innovation.
  • Building artificial intelligence-enabled analytical workflows that help project teams move from descriptive analysis to predictive and decision-ready science.
  • Guiding cross-functional teams in using robust models, reproducible workflows and actionable recommendations to accelerate health-benefit innovation.
  • Lead and strengthen future-ready digital biology capabilities through scalable models, workflows, scientific frameworks, and strategic partnerships that accelerate innovation and reinforce Nestlé’s leadership in digital biology.

What will make you successful

  • Bringing strong expertise in mathematical modelling, statistical learning, machine learning, causal inference, optimisation, predictive modelling and mechanistic modelling.
  • Applying quantitative methods to complex health, nutrition, biological, clinical, microbiome, omics or experimental datasets.
  • Leveraging digital biology approaches (including digital twins, virtual cells scientific, agentic AI and foundation models) to accelerate discovery and innovation
  • Collaborating across research, data science, clinical, nutrition, digital and experimental teams while communicating complex models in a clear and engaging way.
  • Communicating fluently in English.
  • Bringing collaboration, fresh thinking, bold questions, respect, and a positive, wellbeing-oriented mindset.
