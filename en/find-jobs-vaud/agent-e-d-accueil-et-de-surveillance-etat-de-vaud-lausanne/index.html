Full description

What if your next professional challenge took place at the heart of an emblematic monument of the Vaudian heritage? Join the Directorate of Religious Affairs and contribute to the life of the Cathedral of Lausanne by providing a quality reception, coordinating a team and ticketing activities.

  • Hosting Lausanne Cathedral

For further information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Mr Pascal van Griethuysen, Director of Religious Affairs at 021 316 45 76.
  • SWC employed in commerce, hotel communication or equivalent
  • Training in tourism or cultural mediation (an asset)
  • Professional experience in the field of reception and planning of window schedules
  • Attracted for the history of Lausanne Cathedral
  • Good knowledge of English or German
  • Excellent oral expression
  • Customer orientation developed
  • Adaptability and flexibility
