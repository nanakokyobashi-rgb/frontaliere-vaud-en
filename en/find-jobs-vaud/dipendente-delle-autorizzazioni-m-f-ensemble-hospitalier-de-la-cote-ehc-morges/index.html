Full description

Policlinic, emergency, outpatient care, hospitalizations: from birth to old age, the Morges Hospital offers a wide range of care services. The establishment has 240 acute care beds, one maternity, with about 1,500 births per year, and nearly 30,000 emergency consultations. In all, more than 40 medical disciplines have been practiced in its walls for more than 150 years. Surrounded by vineyards, with incredible views of the mountains and the lake, the Morges Hospital welcomes you in a warm and dynamic setting.

We're looking for one for the Morges Hospital

Admissions employee (M/F) at 80-100%

CDD of 6 months, in order to strengthen our Admissions service, as soon as possible

WE OFFER YOU

  • 5 weeks of annual vacation, 6 weeks from the age of 50. Possibility of redeeming an additional week of vacation, as well as 10 holidays offered.
  • Several proposals providing flexibility (unpaid leave, part-time leave, etc.).
  • A mobility plan including participation in your cycling or basketball trip or your public transport subscription, with a view to the environment following an institutional sustainability road plan.
  • Sports activities (Pilates, walking races, discounts at various centres such as several fitness centres or the Bains de Lavey), events for the children of collaborators, a company drink to share moments of conviviality during the beautiful days.
  • Hot drinks offered in the morning and meals at CHF 8.50 in our cafeterias.
  • Remuneration based on the valuation of your previous years of professional experience as well as seniority bonuses within the EHC.
  • Internal transfer facilitated according to your desire for development.
  • Many in-house or certifying training courses available to you.
  • A programme dedicated to managers to support them in acquiring the skills needed to manage their teams.

YOU MAY

  • An EFA of a commercial or medical secretary employee or any other title deemed equivalent
  • An excellent sense of welcome, diplomacy and reserve
  • A first experience in the hospital environment, an asset
  • Availability to work on varying times, including evenings until 8:00 p.m. and weekends, depending on hospital needs
  • Mastering Opale's common IT tools and knowledge, an asset
  • A good team spirit and ability to work as a team as well as independently
  • Good organisational skills and rigour
  • French spoken and written, English spoken, other language an asset

OBJECTIVES

  • Ensure administrative reception and proper follow-up of patient records during hospital stays
  • Enter the data necessary to prepare the Ambulatory or Hospitalization file in OPALE
  • Check with health funds (or other partners) for good parentage of patients admitted to hospital
  • Advertise births and deaths in the hospital to the civil registry office in Morges (birth notification, death notice)
  • Respond to inquiries (telephone and physical)
  • Collaborate with the various services of the hospital and its subsidiaries

WE ARE

  • An institution consisting of acute care and rehabilitation facilities, a private clinic, EMS, medical centres and permanences located in the region of La Côte and the first clinical school of hypnosis dedicated to the care community in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • A network of care allowing many opportunities for internal transformation according to the aspirations and development projects of the collaborators.
  • More than 2,000 employees taking care of patients and residents of the region, sharing values of Responsibility, Proximity, Humanity, Competence, in an agile environment conducive to the realization of projects.
  • A training company with about 70 apprentices in 13 different trades, 200 HES-ES trainees and nearly 300 doctors in training.
