At pfister, we share a vision: we want to move to every home in Switzerland with our products. To this end, we are looking for collaborators to actively support us. Help us bring the approximately 100,000 products of our assortment into the home of our customers. Whether in one of our 18 branches or at the Suhr plant, a unique framework awaits, with attractive products, not to mention esteem and respect for your commitment. Today, nearly 1,400 people work at pfister: do you want to join them?

Your new powers

  • During a training period of 4 years as a 3D polydesigner, through specialists, you learn the profession of three-dimensional realization
  • You're in fact going through the project, the planning, the organization allowing you to realize creative presentations and to promote sales
  • In order to do this, you will be able to get to know the best accessories, materials and lighting installations throughout your learning process.
  • You have a certain flair for colors and shapes
  • In addition, you experience a real pleasure for creating spaces full of originality and ambiance
  • You love handwork and are able to bring all your ingenuity and ability to perform and present decorative objects.
