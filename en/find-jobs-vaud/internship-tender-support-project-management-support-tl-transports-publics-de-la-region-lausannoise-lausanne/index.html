Full description

How do we prepare a tender for a complex project? How do we coordinate the contributions of a multitude of experts to form a coherent file? This professionalizing internship gives you the answer, from inside.

Internship - Tender support & project management support

You will join a multidisciplinary project team and will be involved in the preparation of complex infrastructure projects. You will not be a spectator: your analyses and tools will be used directly to advance the project.

Your missions

Contribute to the preparation of tenders

  • Participate in the development and consolidation of tender documents
  • Check the consistency and compatibility of the perimeters between the different lots

Support project management

  • Develop and use monitoring tools to guide project progress
  • Support the tasks that structure this phase and ensure its smooth running
  • Coordinate and consolidate input from internal stakeholders and follow-up

Your profile

You are at the end of your course or recently graduated from an EPFL Master's degree, HEIG-VD or equivalent, in an engineering or method field. Organized, rigorous and analytical, you can structure and synthesize complex information. You appreciate cross-cutting topics and you like to collaborate with people with diverse expertise. A first immersion in the project environment, infrastructure or tendering is an asset.

What you will gain

  • Immersion in the preparation of tenders, in a multidisciplinary environment
  • Strong methodological, analytical and coordination skills, highly valued in project management
  • A direct contact with many trades and an autonomy that grows over the course
