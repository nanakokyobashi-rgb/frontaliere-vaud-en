Cat. B100 60-100% - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Cat. B100 Denges, immediate or suitable start-up, 60-100% Apply here

What awaits you.

You will find a varied work in a motivated team on interesting construction sites rather in the Neuchâtel region. We are proud to offer attractive working conditions, even in the case of irregular working hours. You see irregular schedules and teamwork as an advantage to enjoy your free time while others work. At home, night and weekend work is entitled to additional pay (e.g. 16 francs per hour for Sunday work). You also benefit from time increases. This means you have more free time than employees who do not work at night or on weekends. To find out what other benefits we offer, such as children's allowances, free AG 2nd class or reduced-cost trips abroad (also for the family), visit our website under "Social Benefits". What you can move.

  • As a motor vehicle driver, you drive construction trains safely, while respecting the regulations in force.
  • You guarantee a high degree of safety within the team and respect the working processes.
  • You are responsible for the preparation and monitoring of trains.
  • Your area of activity also extends to the entry and transmission of data relating to the operation of freight trains using the computer tools at your disposal.
  • You perform services related to our trades according to production needs.

What you bring.

  • You have great motivation, you are ready to take responsibility and you are passionately involved in your tasks. You focus your work on achieving your goals and setting high quality requirements.
  • Your sense of communication allows you to convey complex information in a clear and understandable way and to meet the needs of different stakeholders.
  • Preferably, you have completed an apprenticeship (CFC) in a technical field and you have a cat driver's license. B.
  • You want to invest in your professional development and have successfully completed the B100 locomotive pilot training.
  • You can communicate in French and have knowledge of German.
  • Your view of the colours is perfect (no prot anomaly or deutera) and there is no entry in your criminal record or in the prosecution registry.

Your salary and benefits.

We have a fair and progressive wage system and we are actively committed to equal pay. This is why additional and detailed information on the SBB salary system can be consulted in full transparency. This position falls within the G level and is eligible for a Level 1 regional allocation. Learn more about salary and benefits .
