Full description

Our heart beats for the Swiss gastronomic sector. We are constantly committed to our clients. In our 31 self-service markets, through external service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a varied assortment, attractive own brands, specialized and professional advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Suisse SA is part of the international group Transgourmet. The company is a leader in the delivery and self-service business in Switzerland and has around 2'100 employees, as well as 100'000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals, as well as retail).

Mission

  • Follow-up of demanding customers on the phone or in writing
  • Systematic customer development through active sales and advice (e.g. additional sales, advice on service delivery)
  • Receipt of customer complaints with active search for solutions in the interest of the customer
  • Follow-up of online store customers and advice to our customers on self-service opportunities at Transgourmet
  • Support for internal clients in accordance with the catalogue of benefits and processes
  • Recognize, understand and communicate client needs

Qualifications and qualifications

  • French mother tongue, with very good knowledge of another national language, at the C2 level
  • Completed business or client relationship training or equivalent training
  • Knowledge of food and/or catering is an asset.
  • Use of IT tools
  • Affinity with digital products and systems (online store and apps)
  • High social competence, pleasant and professional communication capacity
  • A careful and precise working method
  • A healthy ability to compete and a high level of workload resistance
  • High flexibility

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Vaud?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Vaud's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Vaud's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
