Together, we make breakfast better

As Global Total Rewards Manager at CPW, you will lead the rewards agenda for a unique global joint venture. You will combine technical expertise with an external perspective to design solutions that work for CPW while balancing the interests of both parent companies. You will operate independently, partner closely with senior leaders, and bring clarity to complex decisions. In our lean team, everyone rolls up their sleeves. We value curiosity, humility, strong relationships, and a sense of humour. This is a chance to make a visible impact on our people, our business, and our future.

How you’ll make an impact as a Global Total Rewards Manager • Shaping global and local total rewards strategies that strengthen competitiveness and meet evolving business and people needs. • Driving a pay-for-performance culture through competitive salary structures and meaningful short- and long-term incentives. • Building understanding and appreciation of total rewards through clear communication, capability building, and effective international mobility support. • Leading annual reward cycles with accuracy, strong governance, and seamless experience for employees and stakeholders. • Strengthening job evaluation and reward governance to ensure consistent, fair, and transparent decisions across the organization. • Ensuring discipline and engagement through people-cost and incentive budgets in partnership with Finance and Human Resources teams across regions and markets.

What will make you successful • Comprehensive, in-depth experience across the full total rewards portfolio. • Strong business and financial understanding to connect reward strategy with people priorities and sustainable value creation. • Broad experience in advanced data analysis, metrics, budgeting, and spreadsheet skills to turn complex insights into clear decisions. • Demonstrating strong learning agility, quickly navigating complexity and adapting your approach while influencing senior stakeholders across functions, markets, and partner organizations. • Communicating fluently in English. • Demonstrating CPW’s values of collaboration, commitment, courage, and capability, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.
