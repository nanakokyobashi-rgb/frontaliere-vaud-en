Full description

Under the direct responsibility of the head of the prototyping workshop, your main mission is to train and manufacture mechanical parts.

In this context, your activities will include

  • Performing programs, settings and manufacturing follow-ups on traditional machine or on 5-axis CNC, according to station instructions and in compliance with AP quality criteria
  • Providing factual feedback on its activities
  • Collaborate on improvement projects
  • You have a micromechanic CFC or equivalent training as well as an experience of at least 5 years in the machining of watchmaking/prototyping components on Bumotec S-191 machines.
  • You are comfortable with the computer environment and master industrialization on AlphaCam and Inventor software.
  • You also control the means of control.
  • Organized, flexible, autonomous and rigorous, you also show a good team spirit and easy integration.
  • You are the strength of proposals for continuous improvement or new processes.
