Full description

The LEB links Lausanne to Gros-de-Vaud and supports the development of a growing region. By 2035, he plans to introduce a 10-minute pace between Lausanne and Echhallens in order to offer ever more attractive and sustainable mobility.

This development is based on several major infrastructure projects, including the underground installation of the line under the village of Etagnières and the renewal of technical installations between Assens and Etagnières. To fully integrate environmental issues into these transformations, we are looking for a person who is ready to put his expertise at the service of concrete and sustainable projects.

Environmental Project Leader - LEB (m/f)

Your Mission

  • You lead the environmental components of the large and complex projects of the LEB, under the responsibility of the Strategic Program Manager.
  • You ensure environmental monitoring of impact assessments, integrate environmental requirements into infrastructure projects and support project leaders in implementing sustainable solutions.
  • You represent the field of environment and energy with internal and external partners.
  • You ensure that environmental aspects are integrated into specifications, tender and site monitoring, and contribute to the Infrastructure Directorate's environmental strategy, continuous improvement and team awareness.

Your profile

  • You have a HES or university training in environmental engineering, or equivalent training, complemented by a specialization in sustainable development.
  • You justify from 3 to 5 years of experience in an environment-related field, with proven experience in project management, ideally in infrastructure or public transport.
  • You have mastered project management and good knowledge of environmental standards and regulations in Switzerland.
  • You master oral and written French
  • German is an asset.
  • Your analytical spirit, synthesis capacity, relationship ease and sense of collaboration allow you to interact effectively with different stakeholders.
  • IPMA, PMP or Prince2 certification is a plus.
