Engineer Process Industrialization & CNC Processing (M/F) back to list In order to strengthen our teams, we are looking for a Process Industrialization & CNC Processing Engineer (M/F). Job description

Your mission

  • Pilot continuous improvement projects to enhance quality, productivity and industrial performance
  • Analyze existing processes and equipment to identify opportunities for optimization and reliability
  • Develop, develop and industrialize new manufacturing processes for technical parts or prototypes
  • Define manufacturing strategies, process parameters and tool solutions adapted to customer requirements
  • Program and optimize CNC machining centres for high performance technical materials
  • Conduct technical feasibility and industrialization studies
  • Monitoring projects from needs analysis to industrial validation
  • Work closely with the departments Production, Quality, Methods, Development and Project Management
  • Develop and implement manufacturing standards and good operational practices
  • Providing technical support for complex production or quality issues

Profile

Profile desired

  • HES, EPF or equivalent engineering training in mechanics, microtechnics or similar fields
  • Initial training in mechanical machining, ideally Polymechanical CFC or equivalent training
  • Experience in industrialisation and optimization of manufacturing processes
  • Solid knowledge in CNC machining and process development
  • Experience in managing industrial projects
  • Mastery of continuous improvement tools; a Lean or Six Sigma certification is a real asset
  • High analytical capacity and methodical approach oriented results
  • Excellent relational skills and a taste for transversal work
  • Initiative, autonomy and responsibility
  • Good professional English (minimum B2)
