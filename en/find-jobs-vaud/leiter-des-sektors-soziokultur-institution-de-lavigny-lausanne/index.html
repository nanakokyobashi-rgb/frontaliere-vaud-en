Full description

Head of the sociocultural sector, 80% to 100% Deployed at several sites, the Lavigny Institution consists of a neurological hospital with outpatient consultations on two sites, a housing department for adults with disabilities, intellectual disability or physical problems, a specialised school and a workshop and vocational training sector. The site of Plein Soleil in Lausanne, is a unique place of life and care in the Canton of Vaud. It offers short-term or long-term accommodation, socio-cultural activities, workshops and day-time reception for people with neurological diseases or brain damage. Would you like to put your managerial experience and support expertise at the service of a project that really places the person at the centre? Do you want to change practices, work with committed professionals and develop an accompaniment that goes beyond the walls of the institution?

Join Full Sun as

Head of the Sociocultural Sector

At the heart of the life of Plein Soleil, the socio-cultural sector plays an essential role: enabling each person to remain an actress of his or her life, to make choices, to maintain his or her ties, to develop his or her capacities and to find his or her place in the community. Member of the management team and directly attached to the Director, you provide strategic, organizational and managerial leadership for the socio-cultural sector (15.4 EFA). You are the guarantor of the sociocultural accompaniment model of Plein Soleil, its coherence and its evolution. As such, you will be responsible for:

  • Set the course, by changing the offer of accompaniment around self-determination, quality of life, social participation and openness to the outside world;
  • To grow teams by developing management based on trust, clarity, feedback and accountability;
  • Develop interdisciplinarity, working closely with nurses, doctors, therapists and other professionals around the resident's life project;
  • Open Plein Soleil by developing links with relatives and external partners;
  • Supporting change by piloting projects, changing practices and contributing to the continuous improvement of the organisation
  • We are looking for a person who combines accompaniment expertise, managerial experience and ability to rise, while being able to transform a vision into concrete actions.

You have

  • higher education in the social, socio-educational, health, management or equivalent fields;
  • a confirmed experience in team management;
  • experience in the medical-social and/or accompaniment of persons with disabilities;
  • experience in conducting projects or accompanying change.

Knowledge of neurodisability is an important asset. You are comfortable in interdisciplinary environments, know how to engage different professional cultures and combine listening, decision-making capacity and frame posture. What we offer you

  • A high-impact position within a specialised and committed institution in an environment where care, therapy, support and social life meet daily.
  • A framework function that allows you to participate in the orientations of Plein Soleil and to contribute concretely to the evolution of its accompaniment model, for the benefit of the people welcomed.
  • And above all, the possibility to join professionals who are committed to making Plein Soleil a place of life, relationships and possibilities.
