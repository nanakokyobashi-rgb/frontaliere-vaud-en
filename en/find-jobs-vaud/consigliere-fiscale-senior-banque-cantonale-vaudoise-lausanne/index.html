Full description

What if your professional history continues at the BCV? Put your managerial ambitions at the service of sound and sustainable performance by joining the canton of Vaud's first universal bank, one of the strongest banks in the world, and its 2,000 collaborators. Take part in the success of an institution rated AA by Standard & Poors since 2011 and contribute to the growth of the Vaud economic fabric as well as to the attractiveness and vitality of our region. For our Strategy and Organization department, we are looking for one or more:

Senior Tax Advisor Your main missions

  • Personalized, value-added advice to Private Banking clients on taxation issues in close collaboration with their advisors and other industry specialists (e.g. estate consultancy, financial planning, foresight consultancy)
  • The realization of mandates on important issues in the context of preparation for the transfer of enterprise, the structuring of the heritage, the holding of real estate, etc...
  • Support for advisors in developing their client portfolio and acquiring prospects
  • The promotion of the bank and the heritage council in particular through the animation of customer events and the writing of articles, internal or external documentation
  • Support in the Bank's strategic and operational projects related to the Client Heritage Board Your Profile:
  • Holder of Swiss tax training
  • Professional experience confirmed for several years (+10 years) in the field of tax advice to private and commercial customers
  • Ability to communicate with clients in a clear and solution-oriented manner
  • Ability to make presentations to an audience with a true sense of communication
  • Spirit of analysis, fitness for organisation and sense of customer service,
  • Knowledge and curiosity for digital tools
  • Perfect mastery of editorial and oral French
  • Good knowledge of English
  • Positive and dynamic personality with the ability to work both independently and as a team

If this position is for you, apply now and participate in the promising future of the BCV. Let's write three stories together: your career story, your bank story, our region story.
