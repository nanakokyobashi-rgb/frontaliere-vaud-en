Full description

In order to complete its team of the Psychiatric Service of Welcome to Emergency and Liaison (SPAUL) within our Service of Psychiatry and Psychotherapy of Children and Adolescents (SPEA), the Foundation of Nant is putting to the competition a position of

Nurse in pedopsychiatric liaison at 80% THE MISSION SPPEA takes care of young people aged 0-18 and their families in the Vaudois East. It guarantees accessible, continuous and child-friendly psychiatric care. The mission of SPAUL is to receive, evaluate, take care of all patients in crisis requiring emergency psychiatric intervention and to guide them in the care system. As a liaison nurse, your primary mission will be to follow up with patients in child-psychiatric liaison for patients hospitalized at HRC.

WE OFFER

The SPAUL covers all liaison activities and psychiatric emergencies in the Vaud area. He is attached to the Nant Foundation and is the main interlocutor for the psychiatry of the HRC.

Working within this team is a diversified activity within an interdisciplinary team.

It is also a unique opportunity to participate in the co-construction of care projects in the child psychiatry of East Vaudois. WE SEEK A person with experience in child psychiatry who holds a Bachelor of Science HES-SO in Nursing, recognized by the Red Cross and with a good knowledge of the health environment of Eastern Vaudois. Having a CAS in community health or liaison would be an asset.

We want a person able to collaborate on interdisciplinary teams , to prevent and/or defuse crisis situations , as well as to evaluate, orient and contain while offering suitable care . Relationship ease to engage in an individual and group relationship process, as well as good communication and negotiation skills are expected. Experience in a similar position , good autonomy , ability to manage unforeseen and stress , as well as interdisciplinary analysis and coordination skills are essential to working in this environment.

Finally, some flexibility is required in order to enrol in an evolving position currently under construction.
