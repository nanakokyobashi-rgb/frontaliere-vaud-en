Full description

About Our Client Our customer is a multi-activity, French-speaking Swiss group recognized for the quality of its services, its proximity to its customers and its ability to evolve in a constantly changing environment. Present through several complementary entities, the group pursues a development strategy based on innovation, operational performance and sustainable value creation. Its model is based on a strong entrepreneurial culture, proximity management and a permanent willingness to satisfy its customers. As part of the strengthening of its management team, our client is looking for his future or his future Administrative/Ve and Financial/Ear Director. The post is based in the Canton of Vaud.

Job Description Strategic management and support of the Directorate-General

  • Support the Directorate General in the development of the various units and contribute actively to the definition and implementation of the group's strategy.
  • Act as a true partner of the Directorate-General by providing a financial, economic and operational vision for strategic decisions, assessing the profitability of activities, identifying value creation levers and making recommendations aimed at sustainable improvement of performance, particularly in the context of development, investment and transformation projects. Performance control and management discipline
  • Develop annual budgets, financial forecasts and medium-term financial plans.
  • Develop and monitor performance indicators for rigorous management of the activity.
  • Support and challenge managers in analysing their results, monitoring their objectives and implementing corrective action plans.
  • Promote a culture of accountability, budgetary discipline and continuous improvement at all levels of the organization.
  • Supervise all financial, accounting and administrative functions of the group.
  • Ensure the reliability of financial information as well as the quality of reporting to the Directorate-General and governance bodies.
  • Provide proactive management of cash, cash and financial flows.
  • Ensure proper allocation of financial resources and control of economic risks.Governance, compliance and risk management
  • Ensure compliance with the tax, legal and regulatory obligations applicable to the different entities of the group.
  • Ensure the preparation and quality of financial documentation for the Board of Directors.
  • Supervise the legal aspects of the group.
  • Ensure the strengthening of internal control processes.Transformation and operational excellence
  • Contribute to the optimization and automation of administrative, financial and management processes.
  • Develop scoreboards and decision support tools to strengthen performance management.
  • Participate in transformation projects to improve operational efficiency, process quality and reliability of management data.
  • Develop and foster a culture of implementation, rigour and monitoring of commitments within the organization.

The Successful Applicant Training and experience

  • University training in finance, accounting, business administration or equivalent discipline.
  • Experience confirmed from 15 to 20 years minimum in financial and administrative management functions in multi-company environments in Switzerland.
  • Strong experience in team management and support of operational leaders.
  • Excellent knowledge of the accounting, tax, financial and regulatory principles applicable in Switzerland.
  • Experience in managing strategic projects, organizational transformation or performance improvement.
  • Experience in the implementation, evolution and optimization of management, reporting and decision-making tools.Technical and analytical skills
  • Ability to analyse complex financial data, learn relevant lessons and make strategic recommendations.
  • Excellent control of financial planning, budgeting, management control, reporting and performance management.
  • Strong focus on results and ability to translate financial indicators into concrete and measurable action plans.
  • Mastery of management tools and financial information systems, with a special sensitivity for automation, digitization and continuous process improvement.Strategic vision and execution capacity
  • Ability to understand environmental developments in order to anticipate opportunities and risks for the organisation.
  • Ability to combine strategic vision with operational excellence in delivery.
  • Ability to support the Directorate General in defining, adapting and implementing the corporate strategy.
  • Habits of piloting cross-cutting projects involving multiple stakeholders and ensuring their implementation in accordance with objectives, deadlines and resources.
  • Entrepreneurial, pragmatic and solutions oriented.Leadership and cross-cutting management
  • Recognized leadership to mobilize teams around common goals and foster a culture of accountability, trust and performance.
  • Ability to federate diverse profiles, develop talents and support change.
  • Ability to constructively challenge leadership and promote a culture of management discipline and continuous improvement.
  • Strong influence and ease in matrix or complex environments requiring close collaboration between different functions.
  • Management courage, ability to make decisions and enforce organizational priorities and commitments.Rigueur, management discipline and performance orientation
  • Recognized for its demands, analytical rigour and high sense of responsibility.
  • Ability to establish robust processes, clear management standards and rigorous performance monitoring.
  • Strong culture of indicator leadership, compliance with commitments and achievement of results.
  • Ability to structure, simplify and improve business processes in order to sustainably enhance organizational efficiency.
  • Constant focus on quality, reliability of data and risk management Communication and interpersonal relationships
  • Excellent communication, listening and persuasion skills.
  • Ability to tailor his speech to a variety of interlocutors, from the Board of Directors to operational managers.
  • Relationship building to build trust with various internal and external stakeholders.
  • Diplomacy, finesse and situational intelligence in the management of sensitive or complex subjects.
  • Ability to popularize financial issues and make issues understandable to non-specialist interlocutors.
