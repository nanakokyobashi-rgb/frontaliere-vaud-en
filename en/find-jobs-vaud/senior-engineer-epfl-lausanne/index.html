Full description

Context and Mission

The Directorate of Information Systems (DSI) is responsible for the implementation of EPFL's digital strategy: IT strategy and master plan, digitalisation of business processes, security, central infrastructure, core applications and coordination with the departments of the faculties.

Attached to the DSI's VPO-SICS Head of Service and placed under the operational supervision of the IA Product Manager and Team Leader of the AI4ADMIN programme, the holder ensures, in pairs with the AI Engineer of the team, the transition to production of the prototypes of AI developed for the administration of EPFL. He or she is the technical reference point of the team for architecture, industrialisation and the exploitation of solutions.

He or she designs, deploys and operates solutions based on robust, scalable and secure open source LLM (assistants, RAG systems, agents) that meet the institution's cyber security and data protection requirements. He or she works closely with DSI teams (infrastructure, operation, security, architecture and integration) and with user units to integrate solutions into their environment and ensure their adoption.

He or she implements the standards of industrialization (MLOPS, IC/CD, observability, evaluation) which will enable the team to deliver new cases of use in a reproducible manner.

Main tasks and responsibilities

Main activities

  • Industrialization and production of IA prototypes — Transforming, with AI Engineer, internal prototypes into tested, documented and sustainable services, developing backend APIs and services, integrating them into EPFL's IS and deploying them on Kubernetes with DSI teams.
  • Architecture, scalability and operation of open source LLMs — Define architecture and technical standards, design data pipelines and indexation, and exploit LLMs by optimizing latency, costs and availability, with mechanisms of IC/CD, infrastructure as code, supervision and recovery.
  • Cybersecurity, Data Protection and Responsible IA — Integrate security from the design stage, ensure compliance with the nLPD, EPFL guidelines and the principles of the AI Act, and coordinate technical validation prior to production with the relevant RSSI, DPO or DPO and DSI teams.
  • Quality, evaluation and continuous improvement of LLM solutions — Automate evaluations and non-regression tests, optimize prompts, RAG channels and, if relevant, fine tuning models, then track quality and production biases and integrate user feedback into backlog.
  • Management of complex projects — Pilot IA projects from needs identification to deployment in a secure environment.

Secondary tasks

  • Agile Work and Collaboration with Trades and ISD — Participate in agile activities and technical ticket tracking with the IA Product Manager, coordinate work with ISD and suppliers, and support user units in the deployment stress analysis.
  • Knowledge transfer and sustainability — Document solutions, formalize operating guides, reusable standards and templates, train IA and operating teams, and monitor open source LLM and MLOps practices.

Profile sought

Your profile

  • Master's degree in computer science, engineering, data science or equivalent technical field; A PhD is an asset.
  • At least five years of experience in software engineering or machine learning, including at least two years of production and operation of ML or LLM systems.
  • Kubernetes (CKA/CKAD) certifications, cloud or security: an asset.

Qualifications required

  • Development of LLM applications: RAG architectures, rapid engineering, fine tuning (LoRA/QLoRA), agents and orchestration (LangChain, LlamaIndex, LangGraph or equivalent), model evaluation and benchmarking.
  • LLM open source (Llama, Mistral, Qwen, Apertus...) and their commissioning (vLLM, TGI, Ollama or equivalent), Hugging Face ecosystem and PyTorch, optimization of inference on GPU.
  • Vector databases and semantic search (pgvector, Qdrant, Weaviate, Milvus, OpenSearch...), hybrid search and re-ranking.
  • Excellent mastery of Python, development of API (FastaPI, REST, OpenAPI), automated tests, Git; experience of data engineering (pipelines ingestion, ETL, SQL, orchestration of workflows).
  • Docker, Kubernetes (Helm), CI/CD (GitLab CI, GitHub Actions or Azure Pipelines), infrastructure as code (Terraform, Ansible), observability (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry, Langfuse or equivalent).
  • MLOps/LLMOps and DevOps practices: versioning models, data and prompts, continuous deployment, tracking drift and production performance.
  • Application and infrastructure security (OWASP Top 10 and OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications, IAM, SSO/OIDC, secret management); knowledge of the nLPD, the AI Act and the principles of responsible AI (fairness, transparency, explanation, human supervision).
  • Software development in agile methods (Scrum, Kanban) and practice of Jira or d-Azure DevOps.

Personal and social skills

  • Relationship, openness and positive attitude: easily connects with colleagues and partners, addresses difficulties constructively and actively contributes to a caring and motivating team dynamic.
  • Excellent team spirit: a taste for teamwork, code review and knowledge sharing.
  • Proactivity and responsibility: supports solutions to production and beyond, anticipates problems and proposes improvements.
  • User orientation, ease of contact with ISD trades and technical teams, ability to explain simply technical topics.
  • Technical leadership: ability to make architectural choices, to set standards and to bring his colleagues into competence, without hierarchical responsibility.
  • Rigor, concern for quality and safety, autonomy in the organisation of work.
  • Pragmatism and ability to prioritize in a rapidly changing technological environment.
  • Excellent command of French (level C2 or mother tongue) and very good command of English (level C1 minimum), both oral and written
  • German is an asset.
