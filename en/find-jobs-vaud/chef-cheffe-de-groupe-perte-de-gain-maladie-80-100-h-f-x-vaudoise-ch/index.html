Full description

A strong and forward-looking employer, Vaudoise is one of the leading insurers in Switzerland. True to its cooperative nature and values, it encourages the creation of a healthy, inclusive and inspiring work environment, ideal for developing your talent. Within the Department of Personal Insurance, and in order to strengthen the loss of sickness gain (PGM) service, we are looking for a group leader Loss of sickness gain between 80 and 100%, based in Lausanne. The role includes leading a team of specialised managers and managing claims files. The missions that await you:

  • Provide your technical support and advice to your employees in the files
  • Organize and coordinate the work of your group: team sessions, bilateral sessions, organization of replacement plans, etc.
  • Actively participate in weekly senior management sessions and work closely with the Chief of Service and other group leaders
  • Train your employees (10 people) and ensure the proper application of management rules
  • Plan, organize and monitor the processing of your team's benefits and participate actively in improving and simplifying processing processes
  • Deal autonomously and proactively with some of the claims from your own client portfolio (starting with the opening and in close collaboration with medical consultants and our Care Managers)
  • Maintain regular contact with partners, insured persons, employers and caregivers Your profile:
  • Federal patent in insurance or equivalent
  • Experience confirmed in the area of PGM services
  • Experienced leader, recognized for your ability to mobilize teams, create a positive working atmosphere and foster employee development, while making effective decisions in demanding environments
  • A French mother tongue, a very good mastery of Italian is an asset.
  • Easy with the usual IT tools (MS Office) If you are also an invested, organized leader, proposal strength and strong customer orientation and have a positive character, then you are probably the person we are looking for! We offer you:
  • A healthy working environment in a shared enterprise labeled "Best Employer"
  • Multiple social benefits, including flexibility in the organisation of work and numerous employee discounts
  • Team management and accountability
  • Human values focused on the well-being of employees We look forward to receiving your complete application package digitally (with curriculum vitae, cover letter, copies of work certificates and diplomas).
