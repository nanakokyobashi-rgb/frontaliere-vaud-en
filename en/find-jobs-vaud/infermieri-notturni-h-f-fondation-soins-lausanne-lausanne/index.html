Role overview

Our Medical-Social Centres (CMS) offer the Lausanne population quality multidisciplinary services and their interventions are based on values of humanity and fairness.

Les Peupliers provides day, night and night replacements to the 7 CMS of the Lausanne Care Foundation in order to guarantee the continuity of the care of our clients.
