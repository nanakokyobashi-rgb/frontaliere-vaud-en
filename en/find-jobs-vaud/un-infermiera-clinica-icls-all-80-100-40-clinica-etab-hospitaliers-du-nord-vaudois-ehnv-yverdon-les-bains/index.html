Full description

THE PEDIATRICS DEPARTMENT OF YVERDON IS LOOKING FOR

MAIN MISSIONS

You actively contribute to the development of clinical expertise in pediatrics and to the continuous improvement of quality, safety, and coordination of care. You perform an advanced clinical role combining direct patient practice and clinical leadership.

Your main responsibilities

  • Ensuring care for complex pediatric situations
  • Ensure the quality and continuity of care throughout the patient pathway: pediatric emergencies, inpatient units, child psychiatry, neonatology, and collaboration with the maternity department
  • Integrate evidence and expert recommendations into daily practices
  • Support the development of the team's skills (coaching, training, support)
  • Develop, implement, and evaluate clinical projects related to institutional priorities
  • Participate in the development and optimization of clinical pathways
  • Contribute to monitoring quality indicators and clinical governance
  • Work closely with the medical team, interprofessional partners, and hospital pediatric networks
  • Working in tandem with the local environment in a dynamic of collaboration and continuous improvement

SKILLS – REQUIRED

  • Nursing degree recognized by the Swiss Red Cross
  • Pediatric experience desired
  • Specialized training in pediatrics (CAS or equivalent training), an asset
  • Holder of a Master's degree in Nursing Sciences or equivalent clinical training, or a desire to train
  • Proven clinical leadership and change management skills
  • Strong interest in quality, care safety, and continuous improvement
  • Analytical skills, autonomy, initiative, and sense of responsibility
  • Excellent interpersonal, communication, and interprofessional skills
  • Adaptability, flexibility, and the ability to handle complex situations
  • Proficiency in common computer tools

WE OFFER

  • A strategic position with strong clinical impact
  • The opportunity to contribute to the development of clinical practices in pediatrics
  • A rewarding connection between clinical practice, projects, and leadership
  • A stimulating environment fostering innovation and collaboration
  • Opportunities for continuing education and professional development
  • A salary according to the scale of the Federation of Vaud Hospitals (FHV)
  • Excellent social benefits
  • Main place of activity: Yverdon
