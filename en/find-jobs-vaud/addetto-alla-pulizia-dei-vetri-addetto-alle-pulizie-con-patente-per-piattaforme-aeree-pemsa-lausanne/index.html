Your assignment

  • Perform cleaning and maintenance work on site, with particular attention to the washing and cleaning of windows;
  • Perform glass, stained glass, panoramic windows and stained glass surfaces using appropriate equipment and products;
  • Ensure the cleanliness and restoration of glass surfaces, façades and various installations;
  • Perform cleaning work at altitude using an aerial platform;
  • Perform the cleaning operations in the American and French for the cleaning of the windows;
  • Use and properly handle professional cleaning equipment and equipment specific for work at altitude;
  • Be in possession of the license/authorization to guide aerial platforms (type IPAF or equivalent);
  • Ensure strict compliance with the safety standards for work at altitude and the use of air platforms;
  • Punctually perform the cleaning of the different working areas and structures of the yard;
  • Keep the equipment, windows and the work area clean and in good condition;
  • Collaboration with the different teams present at the construction site;
  • Respect quality requirements, internal procedures and established deadlines.

And now we talk about you?

  • Consolidated experience in industrial cleaning, on site or in glass cleaning;
  • Experience in the washing and maintenance of windows, panoramic windows and stained glass surfaces;
  • Mastery of American and French cleaning techniques;
  • Experience in work at altitude, preferably on air platform;
  • License / authorization to guide aerial platforms (IPAF or equivalent), mandatory;
  • Good knowledge of equipment, products and professional cleaning techniques;
  • Deficiency in the use of specific cleaning equipment at altitude;
  • Self-contained, accurate, meticulous and attentive to the finishes;
  • Good physical form and ability to work in different yard environments;
  • Respect for safety standards, in particular those relating to work at altitudes;
  • Serious, reliable and punctual;
  • Ability to work both alone and on team;
  • Driving licence, advantage;
  • Available in short term.
