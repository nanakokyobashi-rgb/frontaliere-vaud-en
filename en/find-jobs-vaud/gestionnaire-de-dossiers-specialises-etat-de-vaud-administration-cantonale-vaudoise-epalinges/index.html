Full description

Within the Directorate-General for the Environment (DGE), the Directorate for the Industrial, Urban and Rural Environment (DIREV) is responsible for the protection of the population and the environment from pollution, damage and nuisance caused by human activities. As a specialist case manager in the canton's contaminated sites management team, your main mission is to support the section's project managers in the administrative and financial management of their projects. You thus contribute to the general tasks of the section, by writing, sending and classifying mails concerning notifications of registrations to the cadastre of polluted sites and decisions relating thereto. You also assist them in monitoring cantonal and federal grants, as well as monitoring and committing operating and investment budgets. Autonomous, pragmatic and with a good team spirit, are you motivated to put your technical skills at the service of environmental protection? Join us! For more details, you can refer to the specifications by clicking on this link . For more technical questions, you can contact Mr Sébastien Catimel, Head of the Polluted Sites and Special Waste section, at 021 316 59 15. Environmental issues are at the heart of your concerns, you want to meet a motivating and meaningful challenge and seek to put your skills at the service of the voodoo community, so you are the person we want to meet! Please send us your file in a single PDF document for documentary purposes.

  • Support the Section's project managers in the administrative and financial management of their projects.
  • Contribute to the section's general missions.
  • Manage and update the database in connection with the cadastre of polluted sites using the dedicated management tool.
  • Exchange and follow-up of the section's documents with the management, including those for the department, the Council of State and the Grand Council.
  • Contribute to the efficient management of the services of the Division secretariat and its sections.
  • SWC trade employee and specific training and/or higher or technical school.
  • Additional technical training related to the financial management of projects.
  • 3 years of professional experience in administrative management.
  • Excellent mastery of French. Writing and speaking. Interest in technical areas. Adaptability, flexibility and ease of teamwork. Very good ability to organize and manage your time, spirit of mutual help and collaboration. Rigor and discretion.
  • Good knowledge of the functioning of the voodoo administration, institutions and administrative procedures. Interest and knowledge of environmental protection. Access with IT tools (MS Office and databases) and numbers.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
