Full description

Do you want a job where you can make a difference and take responsibility? With you, we develop the next generation of our reporting platform and create a scalable data basis for data-driven decisions. Become part of an innovative young team in Renens (Lausanne).

You can do that.

  • You develop and operate modern data pipelines on AWS from raw data ingestion to curated data layer.
  • Together with the team, you will implement batch and streaming solutions with Apache Spark on AWS Glue and EMR Serverless as well as Apache Kafka.
  • You are actively shaping the evolution of our data lakehouse architecture with Apache Iceberg, AWS Lake Formation and Amazon Athena.
  • Using AWS Step Functions and Amazon EventBridge, you can orchestrate complex data and processing workflows.
  • You make an important contribution to the high quality and stability of the platform through monitoring, observability and automated controls.
  • You use CloudWatch, Grafana and OpenLineage to ensure transparency and traceability along the entire data processing.
  • Together with internal stakeholders, you accompany solutions from the first design through implementation to operation according to the principle "You build it, you run it".

You bring that with you

  • Several years of experience in developing productive data pipelines with Scala (2.12) and Apache Spark (3.x) distinguishes you
  • You know best practices for performant, testable and maintainable applications with Scalatest, Scalafmt and ScalaFix.
  • In-depth knowledge of SQL and Apache Iceberg enables you to securely handle ACID transactions, table formats and schema evolution in modern data lakehouse architectures.
  • In the AWS environment, you have practical experience with at least three relevant data services such as AWS Glue, Amazon EMR Serverless, Amazon Athena, AWS Lambda, AWS Step Functions, Amazon S3 or AWS Lake Formation.
  • Cloud infrastructures are described and managed with Terraform (from version 1.2) and bring a good understanding of the entire Infrastructure-as-Code lifecycle.
  • Ideally, you could already gain experience with Apache Kafka, OpenLineage, AWS Lake Formation or modern monitoring and reporting solutions such as Grafana, Amazon QuickSight or Power BI.
  • The pleasure of working with interdisciplinary teams, an independent working method and the ability to convey technical issues in an understandable way round off your profile.
  • You communicate stylishly in German or French
  • You use English safely in the technical environment.
