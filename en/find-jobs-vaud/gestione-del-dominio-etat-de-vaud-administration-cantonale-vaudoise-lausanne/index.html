Full description

The Architecture and Buildings Directorate is responsible for the planning, programming, development, construction and maintenance of all buildings in Vaud State. In order to complete her team, she is looking for an estate manager for Prisons Park. As such, you supervise all construction projects in this area and guarantee the quality of the services, the financial objectives and the deadlines.

  • Monitoring the affairs of the field, ensuring at all times complete information on the progress of the projects
  • Coordinate and lead the project team in the field, support them in the implementation of projects, organize the work, promote team spirit and ensure their motivation
  • Representing the Master of Works and defending the interests of the State of Vaud during all phases of the projects
  • As an area manager, actively participate in the life of management, support the Director of Architecture and Buildings in his or her missions and act as a relay to his or her team by carrying out and maintaining the direction of management within his or her field.
  • Represent the Architecture and Buildings Directorate at meetings with partners, third parties, other departments of Vaud State and working groups. For further information, you can consult the specifications here or contact Mr Claudio Iglesias, Director of Architecture and Buildings at 021 316 73 21.
  • Master in architecture, engineering or equivalent, complete with training in project management
  • Professional experience of at least 10 years in project management
  • Excellent knowledge in real estate project management and organizational management
  • Leadership, autonomy, decision-making, listening and organization
  • Ability to create and maintain positive exchanges within stakeholder and specialist networks, a sense of responsibility and ethics in the public service.

Position in the Cantonal Administration of Vaud (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offers-emploi.vd.ch.
