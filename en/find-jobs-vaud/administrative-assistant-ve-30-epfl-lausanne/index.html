Mission

The Wood Construction Laboratory (IBOIS) aims to develop a new generation of wood constructions using a wide range of wood products, ranging from natural resources to processed products, such as laminated-paste wood.

As administrative assistant.ve you ensure the administrative management of the laboratory as a whole in support of the professor and team members (20%).

On the other hand, you ensure the administrative management of a post-graduate course (10%)

Laboratory administrative management

Employee Management

  • Preparation of files, reception and information to employees
  • Absence management
  • Transfer and removal of funds

  • Management of accreditation rights

Budget management

  • spirit of analysis and synthesis
