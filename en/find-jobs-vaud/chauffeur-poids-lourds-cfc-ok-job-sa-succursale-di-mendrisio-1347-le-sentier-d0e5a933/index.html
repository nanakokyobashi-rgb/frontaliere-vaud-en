CFC Heavy Duty Driver back to list Mandated by one of our customers in the northern Vaud region, specializing in freight transport, we are looking for an experienced CFC heavyweight driver (M/F). Job description

Your mission

  • To ensure the transport of goods on Swiss territory in accordance with the deadlines and customer requirements
  • Carry out the loading, unloading and stowage of goods
  • Transport conventional goods and dangerous goods according to current authorizations
  • Control the general condition of the vehicle and conduct daily safety checks
  • Ensure strict compliance with road regulations, ADR/SDR and safety standards
  • Complete transport documents and ensure strict monitoring of deliveries
  • Representing the company with clients with professionalism and courtesy
  • Assist in maintaining cleanliness and condition of the equipment entrusted

Profile

Profile desired

  • Heavy-duty driver CFCs required
  • Resident in the northern region of Vaud
  • Swiss driver's license up to date, all categories necessary to perform the function
  • CPAO valid and current for Switzerland (mandatory)
  • ADR/SDR certificate valid for the carriage of dangerous goods in Switzerland
  • Authorizations required for the transport of dangerous, flammable or sensitive products
  • Experience confirmed as a truck driver in Switzerland
  • Excellent knowledge of the Swiss road network and current regulations
  • Court case blank and irreproachable circulation file
  • Master's degree in oral and written French
  • High sense of responsibility, autonomy and rigour
