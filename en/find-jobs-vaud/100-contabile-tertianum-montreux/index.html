100% accountant

Your tasks

  • Autonomous management of the accounting of several establishments (general ledger and debtors)
  • Establishment of monthly accounting statements according to CO and Swiss GAAP FER, including reconciliations
  • Analysis and plausibility of monthly financial statements
  • Management and monitoring of debtors (reminders and disputes)
  • Privileged contact, including advice and assistance to establishment directors

Your profile

  • You hold the federal certificate of specialist in finance and accounting or training considered equivalent
  • You have several years of experience in a similar position, ideally in the service, audit or fiduciary sector.
  • You have the ability to prioritize tasks, meet deadlines and work effectively in a fast-paced environment.
  • You are detail-oriented, with a high degree of accuracy and integrity.
  • You have strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to collaborate cross-functionally and interact with stakeholders at all levels.
  • You have a perfect command of IT tools (Excel, knowledge of SAP ERP FI)
  • French mother tongue and very good knowledge of English and German (an asset)
