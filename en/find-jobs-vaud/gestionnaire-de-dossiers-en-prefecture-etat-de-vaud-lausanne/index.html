Full description

As a case manager at the Morges district prefecture, you participate in managing all administrative, criminal, and civil services of the prefecture. Your mission is part of a dynamic entity that offers a variety of tasks.

  • Management of administrative services related to various criminal proceedings such as fines, orders, and objections to payment arrangements
  • Management of administrative services related to the various procedures of the conciliation commission regarding leases
  • Welcome and user information at the prefecture counter
  • Administrative monitoring of joint accounts and associations
  • Management of the prefecture's accounting records For more information, please consult the specifications by clicking here or contact Mrs. Valérie Faine, administrative manager of the prefecture, at 021 557 91 27.
  • CFC commercial employee or title deemed equivalent
  • Professional experience in the legal field (court registry, law firm, legal sector of a state service)
  • Additional training in lease law or criminal law
  • Very good knowledge of Vaud institutions and municipal institutional functioning
  • Excellent writing skills
  • Proficiency in common computer tools
  • Sense of organization and time management
  • Adaptability and flexibility
  • Developed customer orientation, listening, and communication

Position within the Vaud Cantonal Administration (State of Vaud), Switzerland. Online application on the official career portal offres-emploi.vd.ch.
