Full description

The Swiss Medical Network Management Academy launches the SMN Internship, an 18-month course designed to offer young graduates a unique immersion in the Swiss Medical Network. For 18 months, participants benefit from an immersive and cross-sectional experience in the three language regions and in five different professional fields to allow you to discover the different professions and institutions as well as the strategic vision of the Swiss Medical Network.

Your profile

  • Be a young graduate and wish to start your career in a stimulating, dynamic and training environment.
  • Have a minimum B1 level in a second national language and a desire to further develop your language skills during the program.
  • Be available for mobility between different regions of Switzerland.
  • Demonstrate motivation, intellectual curiosity, professional commitment, flexibility and adaptability.

Our offer

  • A unique opportunity to develop professional skills through concrete experiences and responsible missions in different areas of activity.
  • Immersion in several strategic functions of the Swiss Medical Network and its institutions, providing a comprehensive understanding of the health ecosystem.
  • An intercultural experience in the different language regions of Switzerland, promoting the development of language skills and openness to diverse working environments.
  • Personalized support throughout the program, including regular follow-up, constructive feedback and a mentor dedicated to your professional development.
  • Participation in cross-cutting projects with high added value, with visibility among the group's management teams.
  • The development of a strong professional network through interactions with collaborators, experts and & #xa0
  • Top Managers of the Swiss Medical Network.
  • Participation in networking events, conferences and meetings with group leaders.
  • The opportunity to actively prepare your professional development within the Swiss Medical Network at the end of the program.

Swiss Medical Network, one of the main private groups of clinics and hospitals in Switzerland, offers first-rate hospital care to its Swiss and international patients. The group is distinguished by its quality services, state-of-the-art equipment and entrepreneurial environment.
