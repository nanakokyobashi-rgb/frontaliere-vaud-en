Full description

About us

Joining the Groupement Hospitalier de l'Ouest Lémanique (GHOL) is to integrate a regional hospital of human size, recognized for the quality of its care and attention to the well-being of patients. In Nyon, our teams combine professional requirements, proximity and spirit of collaboration. In this context, we are strengthening our brigade and looking for a junior kitchen assistant, driven by healthy nutrition and quality dietetic cuisine.

Your mission

Within the central kitchen, you integrate a brigade of about 20 employees working in continuous hours. A true link between medical prescriptions and hospital food for our patients, you contribute every day to a safe, adapted and appetizing diet, fully participating in the care journey. As a junior kitchen assistant, you assist the kitchen manager in the daily organization of the production and distribution of meals for patients, employees and external customers. You are actively involved in production and gradually develop your managerial and managerial skills within the catering department. In your new function, you will be responsible for the following main tasks:

  • Actively participate in the hot, cold and dietetic production of meals and monitor the quality of preparations before distribution.
  • Ensure compliance with HACCP specifications, kitchen chef requirements and standards.
  • Participate in the development of menus and recipes, orders and controls of goods.
  • Ensure rigorous management of raw materials and contribute to reducing food waste.
  • Divide tasks according to the instructions of the chef des cuisines and support employees in the organisation of their daily work.
  • Participate in the reception and integration of new employees and accompany apprentices in their practical training.
  • Participate in production planning, monitoring staff needs and organizing special events and services (banking, cocktails, etc.).
  • Monitor temperature readings, the use of the Epack hygiene and the proper functioning of the equipment, and report any non-compliance or breakdown.
  • Ensure the transmission of information between the hierarchy and the teams.
  • Replace employees in the various production sectors on time and perform certain tasks of the kitchen chef during his short absences.
  • To be the driving force for improving the quality of meals and service.
  • Participate in service meetings, inventories, audits and working groups.

Profile required

Your success is guaranteed by one/one,

  • Cookery or cookery CFC in dietetics; further training in management or team management.
  • 2 to 5 years experience in collective cooking, hospital catering or community catering; first team coordination experience desired.
  • Good command of culinary techniques and basic knowledge of diets and modified textures.
  • Very good knowledge of HACCP standards in hospitals.
  • Stock and order management.
  • Mastery of common computer tools (Word, Excel, business software like Logimen).
  • Organizational sense, initiative and ability to work as a team.
  • Ability to transmit instructions and team spirit, and willingness to develop your management skills.
  • Excellent collaboration, sense of service, respect for professional secrecy, respectful communication, flexibility and adaptability.

Motivated to move towards managerial responsibilities, you want to move forward alongside the chef and his sub chefs. A true team player, you contribute to a positive and collaborative working climate.

What we offer

A first coordination experience within a brigade of about 20 employees, with the support of the chef des cuisines and his deputy chefs. An evolving position that allows you to gradually develop your managerial and managerial skills, with access to internal and external training. A human-sized regional hospital where proximity and spirit of collaboration count.
