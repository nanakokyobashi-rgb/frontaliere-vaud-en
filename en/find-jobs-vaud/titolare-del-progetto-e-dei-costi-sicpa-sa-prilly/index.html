Full description

To strengthen our Finance & Risk department, we are looking for an:

PROJECT & COST CONTROLLER

(12 months fixed-term contract)

Your mission will consist of partnering with project leaders and local finance teams to oversee government project profitability throughout deployment and operations across Swiss and local entities.

ROLE

  • Consolidate customer and designated internal project financials across entities; monitor revenue, costs, investments, assets, consumables and headcount monthly against budgets and project charters.
  • Explain variances, assess financial risks and provide profitability, investment and resource analyses to support project decisions.
  • Support local finance teams with project monitoring and closings, validate revenue recognition and ensure visibility over Swiss invoicing, assets and consumables.
  • Coordinate budgets, forecasts and system submissions with project teams; incorporate actual performance into Business Finance model updates.
  • Ensure consistent centre-of-excellence and intercompany cost allocations; back up project transfer pricing calculations and SAP input.
  • Track intercompany fixed-asset margin eliminations and coordinate related depreciation adjustments with Consolidation.

PROFILE

  • Bachelor degree in Finance, Business Administration, or equivalent
  • Minimum 5-8 years experience ideally in controlling, reporting or related sector
  • Proficiency in Excel and PowerBI are a major asset
  • Experience in SAP/S4Hana ERP
  • Analytical mind-set and detail-oriented person.
  • Proactive and forward-thinking, with a focus on continuous improvement and innovation
  • Team Spirit & (internal) customer oriented
  • Fast learner, works independently with ability to multi-task
  • Fluent in English, French and Spanish would be an asset

WHY JOIN US?

  • Join a global leader in trust technologies with a mission that matters.
  • Be at the strategic heart of a financially sound and innovation-driven company.
  • Collaborate with high-level experts and work on impactful international projects.
  • Operate in a multi-cultural, high-integrity environment where autonomy and ownership are encouraged.
  • Monitor project actuals against planned budgets and escalate risks where appropriate.
