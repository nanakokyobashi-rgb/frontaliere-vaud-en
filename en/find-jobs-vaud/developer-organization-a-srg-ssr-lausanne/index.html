Role overview

In the Organization and Process team you will perform the following tasks:

  • development, start and accompaniment of organizational models oriented to the future, with particular attention to the structure (organizational design);
  • processing and management of partial roles and organigrams at company level;
  • analysis and definition of priorities for organizational change, contributing to the continuous improvement of organizational structures and organs;
  • advice to executive persons on organizational issues and accompaniment of strategic projects;
  • participation in the implementation and development of organizational management at company level.

Requirements

  • Training at a professional university and proven professional experience in organizational development.
  • Solid specialist skills in organizational management.
  • Good familiarity with business process management issues.
  • Excellent oral and written knowledge of German and French.
  • Sense of communication, persuasive ability and convincing personality.
  • Your skills also include analytical and conceptual skills as well as an autonomous, structured and reliable working approach.
