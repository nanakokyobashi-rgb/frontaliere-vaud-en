Full description

As a Physiotherapist, you are responsible for managing the physiotherapy activities of the Genolier site while actively participating in patient care. Clinical activities

  • Provide care for outpatients and inpatients.
  • Ensure a level of clinical excellence and quality of care.
  • Manage complex clinical situations and participate in the development of multidisciplinary treatment plans.
  • Develop specialized expertise to meet the needs of the medical network and patients.
  • Ensure close coordination with physicians, surgeons, occupational therapists and other health professionals.

Management and leadership

  • To supervise, accompany and develop the team of physiotherapists of the site.
  • Participate in the recruitment, integration and retention of employees.
  • Promote a collaborative, motivating and continuous improvement work environment.
  • Organize and coordinate the activity to ensure efficient and quality management.
  • Identify training needs and support the professional development of teams.

Development of activity

  • Participate actively in the definition and implementation of MotionLab Genolier's development strategy.
  • Develop collaborations with medical specialists, Swiss Medical Network clinics and external partners.
  • Contribute to the creation of new services and innovative care pathways.
  • Promote the image and positioning of MotionLab in the region.
  • Participate in institutional projects to enhance the quality, efficiency and growth of the activity.

Quality and innovation

  • Define and evolve support protocols, standards and recommendations.
  • Ensure the quality and consistency of the treatments delivered by the team.
  • Monitor performance indicators and contribute to continuous improvement of performance.
  • Participate in the integration of new technologies and innovative therapeutic approaches.

Training

  • Swiss diploma in physiotherapist or title recognized by the Swiss Red Cross.
  • CAS, DAS or further training in management, leadership or management are an asset.
  • Specialised training in a clinical field (sport, musculoskeletal, neurology, pelvi-perineology, rehabilitation, manual therapies, etc.) is an advantage.

Experience

  • Confirmed clinical experience in physiotherapy.
  • First supervisory experience or strong interest in managerial responsibilities.
  • Experience in developing activities or projects is an asset.

Personal skills

  • Natural leadership and ability to unite a team.
  • Excellent relational and communication skills.
  • A sense of organization and management of priorities.
  • Entrepreneurial spirit and orientation results.
  • Autonomy, flexibility and sense of responsibility.
  • Strong interest in innovation, new technologies and multidisciplinary approach.
  • Willed to contribute actively to the development of MotionLab.

We offer

  • A function with responsibilities with real autonomy.
  • The opportunity to participate in the strategic development of MotionLab Genolier.
  • A multidisciplinary environment of excellence within the Swiss Medical Network.
  • Opportunities for continuing training and professional development.
  • Modern infrastructure and state-of-the-art equipment.
  • A dynamic, committed and innovative team.
  • The possibility of actively contributing to the evolution of physiotherapy and rehabilitation practices.

Join MotionLab and participate in the development of a referral centre that combines clinical excellence, innovation and performance for our patients.

Our approach is based on clinical expertise, innovation and multidisciplinary collaboration between physiotherapists, medical specialists and other health professionals. Integrated with the Swiss Medical Network since 2024, MotionLab enjoys privileged access to one of Switzerland's largest private health networks. As part of the development of our activities on the Lemanic Arc, we are looking for a Responsible Physiotherapist for our Genolier site. This position represents a unique opportunity to combine high-level clinical activity with management, strategic development and innovation responsibilities in a dynamic and fast-growing environment.
