Full description

Founded in 1910, the Collège Beau Soleil is one of Switzerland's most renowned private schools. As a prestigious international institution, we welcome students aged 11-18 from over 60 different nationalities and offer them holistic boarding education. Our College joined the Nord Anglia Education Group in 2011, the world leader in high-end schools, bringing together more than 80 international schools around the world.

Our employees benefit from a dynamic, family-friendly work environment on a modern campus. They also take advantage of the opportunities arising from our membership in a global organization dedicated to education and professional development.

Alpine Beau Soleil College is looking for

Cleaning and logistical support employee – 80%

From 04/01/2027

Your Mission

As a member of our Housekeeping team, you ensure the daily logistical support of Alpin Beau Soleil College and contribute to the smooth functioning of the boarding schools and various campus buildings.

This 80 per cent shift runs from Monday to Friday, from 7:30 a.m. to 2:45 p.m. (3:00 p.m. on Friday), with 30 minutes of daily break. It includes the collection and distribution of laundry, waste management and supply of products and supplies to teams. It involves regular travel on campus and handling tasks. The driving licence category B is indispensable.

Under the supervision of the Housekeeping Manager, you organize your tasks independently and collaborate with the laundry, boarding and maintenance teams.

Your Responsibilities

  • Collection and distribution of linen, uniforms and bed linen
  • Daily collection of external waste
  • Receipt, storage and distribution of goods and supplies
  • Internal deliveries between buildings and services
  • Routine maintenance and periodic inspection of laundry equipment
  • Maintenance of order and cleanliness of work spaces, in accordance with safety instructions

Your profile

  • Minimum one year experience in the cleaning sector
  • Experience in logistics, laundry or appreciated supply
  • Mastery of French
  • English and other languages an asset
  • Good physical condition for movement and handling
  • Organizational sense and ability to work as a team and independently
  • Reliability, timeliness, versatility and flexibility
  • Good presentation and welcoming attitude

How to Apply

Please send us your complete dossier (CV, diplomas, certificates and cover letter) via our website https://www.beausoleil.ch/careers .

Deadline for application: 15 November 2026.

The College is committed to protecting and promoting the well-being of children. The selected candidate will be required to provide an extract of his or her criminal records.
